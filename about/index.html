About AI Tools Optimizer

AI Tools Optimizer helps everyday people, creators and small businesses understand AI and choose the tools that actually save them time and money.

I'm Andrew. I test AI tools hands-on, write plain-English guides, and keep a curated directory so you don't have to sift through hundreds of options yourself.

How we review tools

  • We use each tool on real tasks before rating it.
  • Ratings consider output quality, ease of use, pricing and support.
  • Affiliate partnerships never change a rating.
  • Reviews are re-checked regularly and dated.

How the site is funded

The site is free to read. It's supported by display ads, affiliate links (clearly disclosed) and our own ebooks.
