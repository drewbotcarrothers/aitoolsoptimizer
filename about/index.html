About AI Tools Optimizer

AI Tools Optimizer is an independent website that helps everyday people, creators and small businesses understand AI and choose AI tools that fit their work and budget.

Who runs the site

The site is created and run by Andrew. It is a small independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the AI companies listed here.

What you'll find here

  • AI tools directory: 104 tools across 17 categories, with pricing in US dollars, free plan details, key features, use cases and alternatives.
  • Frontier AI model comparison: release dates, context windows, open-weight status and API prices.

How the information is researched

  • Pricing, plans and features are checked against each company's official website and announcements. Where a page lists its sources, they are linked at the bottom.
  • Each tool page shows the date its details were last checked. AI products change quickly, so always confirm current pricing on the official site before you buy.
  • We don't publish star ratings or scores unless they come from our own testing.
  • Affiliate relationships never decide whether a tool is listed or how it is described.

How the site is funded

The site is free to read. It may be supported by display advertising (Google AdSense) and affiliate links. See the Disclaimer for details.

Corrections and contact

Spotted an outdated price or a mistake? Email contact@aitoolsoptimizer.com and it will be reviewed and corrected.
