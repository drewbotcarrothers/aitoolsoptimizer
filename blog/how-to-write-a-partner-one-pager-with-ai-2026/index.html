How to Write a Partner One-Pager with AI (2026): What You May Say Together

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

A partner one-pager written with AI is a single page both companies can hand to a buyer: the joint offer, who it is for, the proof each side already has, and the sentence each side is allowed to say. The model may compress notes you paste. It may not invent a customer, a discount, or a metric. The internal brief that assigns the work is how to write a partner brief with AI. This page is the leave-behind. If the two disagree, the one-pager is wrong, or the brief is, and a person decides before anyone sends it.

The product page that is not a partner page is how to write a product one-pager with AI. The team that runs the motion is best AI tools for partner marketing. The seller’s one-offer sheet is how to write a sales one-pager with AI.

What not to delegate

Do not ask the model for a joint pipeline number. Do not put the other company’s pricing on the page unless they wrote the sentence. Do not send the draft from a personal account if the partnership is under NDA. The factual check is how to fact-check AI writing.

FAQ

How is this different from a partner brief?

The brief says what each team will do and by when. The one-pager is what a buyer can read. The brief can contain internal dates the buyer should not see. Do not paste the brief and call it the one-pager.

How is this different from a product one-pager?

The product page is your product, your proof, your owner. The partner page has two names and a split. A sentence that only one side can defend does not belong on it.

Can the model design the page?

It can suggest a section order. Canva can lay out type after the words are frozen. A generated illustration of the other company’s product is not an asset you have the right to use unless they gave it to you.

Who signs off?

One named person on each side. A shared chat is not a signature. Save the approved file. If either side edits it later, the other side sees the diff before it goes out again.
