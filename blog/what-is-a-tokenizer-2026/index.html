What Is a Tokenizer? (2026): The Meter Before the Model

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

A tokenizer is the program that splits text into the pieces a model counts, before the model writes anything. Those pieces are tokens. The model does not read “words” as your invoice does. It reads the tokenizer’s inventory. Two models can split the same paragraph into different counts, so a price per million tokens is not comparable until you know which tokenizer ran.

The unit on the bill is what AI tokens are. How many of them fit in one request is what a context window is. The thing being counted is what an LLM is.

This page does not publish a token count for a sample sentence. A rule of thumb you did not run through that vendor’s tokenizer is a guess. Prices below are US list prices from directory pages checked October 16, 2026. Published October 24, 2026.

The output is tokenized too. A long answer costs more than a short one on the same model, even when the prompt was identical. Asking for brevity is a cost control. It is also an editing choice. The person who checks the short answer is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

Why the same paragraph is not one price

ChatGPT Plus is $20 a month. Go is $8. Free includes text chat with limits on tools. Those seats do not show you a tokenizer. You feel the meter as a usage cap, not as a line item. The ladder is ChatGPT Go vs Plus vs Pro.

Claude Pro is $20 a month, or $17 billed annually. The directory records a context window up to 1 million tokens depending on the model. That is a capacity number, not a promise that your file will be cheap. A long contract still has to fit, and a long answer still spends the seat’s usage. The long-document comparison is Claude vs Gemini for long documents.

DeepSeek is where the tokenizer becomes an invoice. Flash is $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens at peak, and half of that off-peak, on a cache miss. A worked sketch in the review uses those rates. Your tokenizer and your cache behavior decide the real invoice. The review is DeepSeek review. The cache, which is not the tokenizer, is what a context cache is.

Gemini Free comes with a Google account. Google AI Plus is $4.99 a month. Google AI Pro is $19.99. You are buying usage of a hosted assistant, not a token line you can audit from the seat. If you call an API, use that API’s tokenizer and that API’s price. Do not divide a seat price by an imagined token count and call it a rate.

Image models can use a different meter. GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst, on the ChatGPT Images page, is $8 per million image input tokens and $30 per million image output tokens on standard processing. Do not compare that to a chat token. The warning is the same one on Leonardo pricing: a credit in an image app is not a chat token.

What to do with the idea

Paste the real prompt into the vendor’s own counter when the bill matters. If they do not publish a counter, run one small job and read the usage line. Then multiply. A blog post that says “about 750 words is 1,000 tokens” is a sketch for English prose on one family of models. It is a bad sketch for code, for JSON, and for a language the inventory splits finely.

A shorter prompt is not always a better prompt. Cutting the source that the answer must follow is how you get a fluent invention. The failure mode is what an AI hallucination is. Keep the facts. Cut the preamble.

FAQ

Is a token a word?

Often it is a piece of a word, a word, or a bit of punctuation. The inventory belongs to that model. This page will not give you a words-to-tokens ratio to paste into a budget.

Do I need to know the tokenizer to use ChatGPT?

No. You need it when you are comparing API prices, fitting a file into a window, or explaining a bill. A Plus seat hides the meter inside a usage cap.

Can I change the tokenizer?

Not on a hosted model. The tokenizer ships with the model. Choosing another model is choosing another tokenizer, another window, and another price.

Does quantization change the token count?

No. Quantization changes how weights are stored. The prompt is still split the same way. The definition is what quantization is.
