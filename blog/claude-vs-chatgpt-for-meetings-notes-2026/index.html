Claude vs ChatGPT for Meeting Notes (2026): The Transcript You Paste

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

Use Claude when the notes have to follow a long transcript plus a Project that already holds your template. Use ChatGPT when the notes are a short recap in the chat you already pay for. Neither one joins the call. A recorder does that. The model only rewrites what you paste, and you delete anything the room did not say.

What each seat is doing in this job

Claude. Pro is the long-paste seat. Put last month’s notes format in a Project: decisions, owners, open questions. Free can hold a short paste with limits. Max at $100 or $200 is for people who already hit Pro, not for “better meeting judgment.” The directory does not print a Team price. The review is Claude review. The container is Claude Projects vs ChatGPT Projects.

ChatGPT. Plus at $20 is enough for a recap you will paste into the doc yourself. Go at $8 can hold a shorter one and may include ads. Business at $20 a user a month billed annually, for two or more users, is the admin seat and does not train on business data by default. A personal Plus account is a different promise if the transcript has customer names. The ladder is ChatGPT Go vs Plus vs Pro.

The recorder, which is not either chatbot. Fathom Free includes unlimited recordings and summaries when a bot is allowed. Team is $15 a user a month billed annually, with a two-user minimum, when the notes must live in folders. Granola does not put a bot on the call. It uses your computer audio. Business is $14 a user a month for unlimited history. Basic is free with 30 days of history. Tell people they are being captured. The cards are Fathom pricing explained and Granola vs Fathom.

The record. Notion Business is worth it when the decision log already lives there. Generate in Claude or ChatGPT, then paste the approved text. The chat is not the system of record. That distinction is what a system of record for AI is.

A prompt that holds the line:

“Here is the transcript and our template: decisions, owners, open questions. Use only the transcript. If a decision is unclear, write Unclear. Do not invent dates, owners, or dollar amounts. Do not add a next step nobody said.”

A decision the model upgraded from a joke is a hallucination. The person who publishes the note is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

How to choose

If the transcript is long and the template is stable, start with Claude Pro. If you already live in ChatGPT and the recap is a page or less, stay there. Add a recorder when the failure is “we did not write anything down,” not “the prose is flat.”

FAQ

Which writes better notes?

This page did not measure that. The transcript you paste, and whether you delete invented decisions, matter more than the logo. A beautiful recap of a meeting that did not happen that way is a defect.

Can I connect the model to my calendar and let it file notes?

You can draft. You should file the note yourself until the template has been boring for a month. An auto-file will eventually assign an owner who was not in the room.

Is Gemini a third option?

Yes, when the meeting notes should land in a Google Doc. Google AI Pro is $19.99 a month. That bundle is Gemini vs ChatGPT for Google Workspace. It is still not a recorder.

What about a team of five?

Five Claude Pro seats at the monthly price are 5 × $20 = $100, with no printed Team admin price on the directory card. Five ChatGPT Business seats on the annual card are also 5 × $20 = $100, with admin controls. The recorder is extra: Fathom Team at $15 is 5 × $15 = $75 a month on the annual rate, and the plan has a two-user minimum. Buy the recorder first if the notes do not exist.
