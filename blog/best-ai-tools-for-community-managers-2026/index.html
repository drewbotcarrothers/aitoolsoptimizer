The reply

Most community time is a reply that has to be warm and accurate. Claude fits a long thread or a guideline you will revise in a Project. ChatGPT Plus fits when the same seat also makes a small image or holds a custom GPT of your macros. Go at $8 is the lighter ChatGPT step. Free is enough to learn which one you edit less. The writing split is ChatGPT vs Claude for writing. The email-shaped version of a reply is how to write customer emails with AI.

Grammarly is the pass in the box where you already type. It will not invent the moderation decision. A ban, a warning, and a refund stay with the person, under the guideline you pasted. The comparison is Grammarly vs ChatGPT for editing. The voice, so five moderators do not sound like five brands, is how to create a brand voice with AI.

Put the guidelines in Notion AI if that is the home people will open. A rule that lives in a chatbot will be paraphrased differently every week. The build is how to build an internal wiki with AI. The card is Notion AI pricing explained.

FAQ

What should a community manager pay for first?

The place the week breaks. Replies: Claude or ChatGPT. Guidelines nobody can find: Notion. Off-brand visuals: Canva. Calls you cannot remember: Granola or Fireflies. One seat. Add a second when the first is in use.

Can AI post for me?

It can draft. A person posts. A wrong rule, a joke that lands badly, or a promise about a product will sit in the community after you have closed the laptop. The control is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

Do I need a marketing platform like Jasper?

Not to answer members. Jasper Pro at $59 a seat billed yearly is a brand system for a marketing team. Buy it when several people publish campaigns and the voice note keeps drifting. The comparison is Jasper vs ChatGPT for marketing.

How do I plan the month?

A calendar of what you will actually say, not fifty generated posts. The workflow is how to build an AI content calendar. Member questions that repeat become FAQ pages, not a longer chatbot.
