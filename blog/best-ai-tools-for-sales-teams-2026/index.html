Best AI Tools for Sales Teams (2026): Lists, Calls, and the CRM

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

A sales team needs a list it has the right to contact, a record of the call, and a CRM that stays the source of truth. AI helps research a public page, draft a first email from facts you supply, and summarize a call you already recorded. It does not create permission to email strangers, and it does not know which deal is real. Win rates on a vendor’s homepage are the vendor’s story. This page does not repeat them as measurements.

Prices are US list prices excluding tax, recorded October 16, 2026, except Perplexity Pro, which is from an October 24, 2026 read. Gong has no public price. It is a quote: per-user licenses plus a platform fee.

The list

Apollo combines a contact database, sequences, a dialer, and AI research. Free is a real start: 900 credits a seat a year, two sequences, and Gmail. Paid plans are $49, $79, and $119 a seat a month billed annually for Basic, Professional, and Organization. Organization has a three-seat minimum. Credits are pooled, granted up front, and do not roll over. Every seat on the account is on the same plan. Annual billing saves about 24% versus monthly, per Apollo’s page.

Clay is the spreadsheet when the enrichment logic is the work. Launch at $167 a month billed annually is the first paid floor. Seats are unlimited. The invoice follows actions and data credits, including marketplace providers. Bring-your-own-key columns still create a bill at that provider. Do not treat unlimited seats as unlimited enrichment.

Instantly Growth at $47 a month is the sender: 5,000 emails, 1,000 contacts, unlimited accounts and warmup on the outreach tab. Bundles start higher. The Hypergrowth and Light Speed tiers are volume plans. Warm a secondary domain. Keep the main domain off cold outbound. The tool will send what you load. It will not make a scraped list lawful.

The call and the CRM

Fathom Free is the default notetaker if the buyer has been told the bot is there. Business at $25 a user a month billed annually is the plan that maps fields into the CRM and adds Deal View. Premium at $16 annual is the individual step for richer summaries, without the CRM writeback. Reps should correct the fields. A summary can miss the budget and invent a next step the buyer did not say.

Gong is the platform when a revenue team wants capture, coaching, and forecast in one quoted system. There is no free plan and no public list price in our directory. Integrating the existing stack is described as free. The Anthropic figures sometimes cited on Gong’s site are Gong’s case study. We did not reproduce them as a benchmark. A small team that wanted notes does not need this quote. Fathom or Fireflies does that job. The bot comparison is Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom.

HubSpot’s free CRM is the system of record if you do not already have one. Breeze is the AI layer: assistant and agents, metered with HubSpot Credits, with extra credits listed at $10 per 1,000. Starter was a limited-time offer from $7 or $10 a seat when the tool page was checked October 16, 2026. Treat that as a promotion you must see at checkout, not as a permanent price. Professional and Enterprise are a different budget. Set a credit cap before an agent runs.

Drafts, research, and approval

Perplexity prepares a public brief: pricing page, careers page, recent announcement. Open the links. The method is how to use Perplexity for market research. Claude or ChatGPT at $20 a month can draft the email from that brief and from your offer. The draft is not the send.

Lindy and Relevance AI are the agent products if a follow-up routine is truly repetitive. Lindy Team starts at $49.99 a month for 5,000 credits. Relevance Pro is $19 a month billed annually, and the free plan is retired. Team on Relevance jumps to $234 a month annual. Keep a person on send. The setup rule is how to create an AI agent without coding. Lindy’s product page is Lindy review.

Moving a stage in the CRM after a human confirms it is Zapier or Make. That comparison is Zapier vs Make vs n8n.

A team that stays out of a platform quote

Two reps: HubSpot’s free CRM, Apollo Free until credits hurt, Fathom Free, and a chatbot you already pay for. Add Instantly Growth at $47 when sending volume is the bottleneck and the list is clean. Add Fathom Business when the CRM fields are the hour you want back. Add Clay when the same enrichment is rebuilt by hand every week. Ask Gong for a quote when forecasting and coaching across the whole revenue team is the purchase, not when one rep wanted a transcript.

Marketers who share the list and the site are on best AI tools for marketers. Recruiters who share the sequencer and the notetaker are on best AI tools for recruiters.

FAQ

What should a small sales team buy first?

A CRM, even the free HubSpot tools, and Fathom Free for calls you have disclosed. Add Apollo or Instantly when outbound is a weekly motion with a list you may use. Add Gong when you are ready to take a custom quote.

Is Clay or Apollo the list tool?

Apollo is the database plus sequences in one seat, from $49 a seat on Basic annual. Clay is the custom enrichment table, from $167 a month on Launch annual, with unlimited seats and a credit meter. Teams use both. Start with the one that matches the job you did by hand last week.

Can an AI send the follow-up?

It can draft it. A person should send it until the drafts have been right for a month. Payment and contract language stay human.

Does Fathom replace Gong?

Fathom records and summarizes, and Business writes CRM fields. Gong is a revenue platform with coaching and forecast, priced as a quote. Replacing Gong with Fathom is a scope cut, not a coupon.
