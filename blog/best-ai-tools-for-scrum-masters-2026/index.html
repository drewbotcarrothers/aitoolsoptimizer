Best AI Tools for Scrum Masters (2026): The Agenda, the Retro, the Board

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

A scrum master should buy the tool that matches the ceremony they already run: the agenda, the notes, or the board. An agent that writes velocity and a morale score from a transcript is not a facilitation tool. It is a rumor with a chart. This page is the sprint. The manager next to the team is best AI tools for engineering managers. The product partner is best AI tools for product managers. The coach who drafts a note from what people said, and does not add a maturity score, is best AI tools for agile coaches.

The agenda and the retro

Write the agenda before you invite the model to be creative. The workflow is how to create meeting agendas with AI. A retro has its own shape: what to keep, what to change, and an owner. That workflow is how to run a retro with AI. A quarterly target is not a retro. If the team is setting numbers, the workflow is how to write OKRs with AI, and the number still comes from a person.

Claude is the better first draft when you are revising a long planning doc. ChatGPT Plus is the better first draft when the scrum master also lives in that seat for other work. What not to automate

Do not ask a model to score a person’s performance from standup transcripts. Do not publish a velocity number the model computed from a messy board. Do not add a second bot because the first summary felt thin. Facilitation is the job. The draft is the aid. The line is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

FAQ

What should a scrum master pay for first?

The ceremony that is actually breaking. Lost notes: Granola, Fireflies, Fathom, or Otter. Aimless planning: Claude or ChatGPT, used with the agenda workflow. A board nobody trusts: Notion or ClickUp, whichever is already the system of record. One tool, then a second only after the first is in the ritual.

Is Fathom’s free plan enough?

The pricing explainer records unlimited recordings and transcripts on Free, with Premium at $16 annually for the advanced summary and action-item lines. If Free’s summary is enough and you paste actions into the board the same day, you do not need a second paid notetaker.

Can AI run the standup?

It can draft a prompt for what each person might update, from the board you pasted. It cannot replace the conversation. An unread generated standup is a status report. Call it that, or do not send it.

How many AI tools belong in one retro?

One notetaker, one writing seat, and the board. A fourth tool that “finds insights” will be a second opinion nobody asked for. The retro guide is enough structure.
