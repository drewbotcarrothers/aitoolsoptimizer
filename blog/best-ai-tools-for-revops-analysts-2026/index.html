What you are paying for

Notion Business at $20 a seat holds the dictionary. Each metric has one formula, one owner, and one source system. A “revenue” that means bookings in one row and collections in another is two metrics. The model may format the row. It may not merge them. The home of the fact is what a system of record for AI is. A named input shared by a model and a report is what an AI feature store is.

Hex Professional at $36 per editor is the notebook when the warehouse is the source. Team is $75 per editor a month. Community is free with an agent trial and small compute. The invoice, including hourly compute, is Hex pricing explained.

Julius Plus is the path for an export someone already pulled. Do not ask it to estimate the rest of the funnel outside the file. The review path for analysts who live in sheets is best AI for Excel and Google Sheets.

Claude Pro writes the weekly sentence from figures you paste: what changed, by the definition in the dictionary, and what you are not claiming. A cause you did not test stays out. The review is Claude review.

HubSpot is where the opportunity sits. The analyst reads it. A narrative that disagrees with the CRM is a bug in the narrative or a bug in the CRM, and a person decides which. The Starter offer is HubSpot pricing explained.

A prompt that holds the line:

“Write the weekly note from the metric definitions and the figures I pasted. Use only those figures. Do not add a cause, a forecast, or a recommendation. If a definition and a figure disagree, write Conflict and stop.”

What not to automate

Do not auto-publish the sentence into the board deck. The board packet is a different file, and the figures there have to be the ones you pasted: Gamma vs Plus AI for board decks. Do not let an assistant change a stage definition to make a chart smoother. Stage changes are sales ops. Do not treat a finance close as a CRM report. The books are best AI tools for finance ops.

FAQ

How is this different from the RevOps roundup?

That page is the operational stack: enrich a list, keep the CRM, route a record, chart the funnel. This page is the analyst who defines the metric and writes the sentence. One person may do both. The tools and the failure modes differ. A Clay credit and a metric dictionary are not the same purchase.

Can the model reconcile CRM and warehouse?

It can format a diff you already computed. It cannot decide which system is right. Write Conflict, then the owner of each source decides. A blended number with no owner is how the board sees two revenues.

Do we need Hex and Julius?

No. Hex when the warehouse is the source. Julius when you have a file and no warehouse seat. Two tools charting the same metric is the drift you are trying to avoid.

Can this note include a forecast?

Only a forecast a person who is allowed to forecast has already signed, pasted as a figure. This page does not build the forecast. A sentence that “implies” the quarter will land somewhere is the sentence to delete.
