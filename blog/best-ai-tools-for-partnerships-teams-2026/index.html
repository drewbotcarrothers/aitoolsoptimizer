What to use for each artifact

The research. Perplexity Free cites sources with a weekly cap. Pro adds Computer, premium data such as PitchBook and Statista, and model choice. Max at $167 a month billed annually is the heavy tier. A partner brief does not need Max. The cost against a drafting seat is Perplexity Pro vs ChatGPT Plus cost. The review is Perplexity review.

The brief. Claude Pro holds a long source pack in a Project. ChatGPT Plus holds a repeating brief template. The structure that holds the line: who they are, who we are, the customer we both serve, what each side will do, what we will not promise, and the next meeting. If a revenue share is not in your notes, the model writes Not agreed. The container is Claude Projects vs ChatGPT Projects. The person who sends the brief is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

The record. Notion Business is the wiki for the brief and the meeting notes. HubSpot’s free CRM is the system of record for the partner company and the deal stage, if that is the CRM you run. The deck. If the partner will edit a PowerPoint or Google Slides file, start with Plus AI. If a link is enough, Gamma Plus is the lower seat. The choice is Plus AI vs Gamma. What not to automate

Do not let a model score “strategic fit” and then sort the pipeline by that score. Do not let it offer a discount, a marketplace fee, or an exclusivity term. Do not publish a co-marketing claim the partner has not seen. The RFP-style response, when a partner sends a questionnaire, is how to write an RFP response with AI. Same rule: only the answers you can source.

FAQ

What should a two-person partnerships team buy first?

Perplexity Free, then Pro if the weekly cap hits. Claude or ChatGPT for the brief. The CRM you already have. Gamma or Plus AI when a deck is actually requested. Clay when the list is the job and RevOps is not already running it.

Is a partner brief the same as a sales one-pager?

The sales one-pager asks a buyer to take a step. The partner brief asks both companies what they will do. Using the buyer page as the partner page will read as a pitch. Write the mutual sections on purpose.

Can AI negotiate the terms?

It can list the open terms you pasted. A person negotiates. A generated “standard revenue share” will leak into an email. Leave economics blank until someone with authority fills them in.

Where do meeting notes go?

Into the partner record, after you delete anything the model upgraded into a decision. The capture choice is Claude vs ChatGPT for meeting notes. The recorder is separate from the chatbot.
