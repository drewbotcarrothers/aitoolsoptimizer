What Is AI Rollforward? (2026): Back to the New Version, After the Fix

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

AI rollforward is the controlled return to a newer prompt or model after you rolled back, and only after the check that forced the rollback passes again. Users are on the last known-good version. The fix exists. Rollforward is the decision to put that fix in front of people, usually a small slice first. Shipping the same broken prompt again because the incident channel went quiet is not a rollforward. It is a repeat.

The first time a new prompt meets a small slice of users is what an AI canary release is. The switch you use to show or hide it is what AI feature flags are. A candidate that sees live inputs and does not answer the user is what a shadow deployment for AI is. The duplicate requests in that setup are what AI shadow traffic is.

The version id is what prompt versioning is. The case you re-run is what prompt unit testing is. If the original failure had no saved case, write the case from the incident before you roll forward. A story in Slack cannot fail a build.

A route that sends traffic back to the known-good model when the new one errors is what AI fallback routing is. Fallback is a runtime behavior. Rollforward is a release decision. You can use both. You should not confuse them in the incident note.

What to write down

Write the version you left, the version you are returning to, the check, the slice, the owner, and the condition that sends you backward again. The checklist that holds those gates for an ordinary release is how to write a release checklist with AI. Rollforward uses the same gates, with the failed check named in the first row.

Do not widen the slice because a dashboard looks calm and nobody re-ran the case. Calm is not the check.

FAQ

Is rollforward just a second canary?

A canary is how you expose a version to a slice. Rollforward is why you are exposing this version: you already retreated, and the original check now passes. The mechanism can be a canary flag. The decision is the return.

Can we roll forward without a saved case?

You can ship. You cannot call it a rollforward on this page. Write the case from the incident first. If you cannot reconstruct the input, say the evidence is missing and keep the known-good version until you can.

Who is allowed to widen the slice?

The owner you named before the return, which is often the person who approved the rollback. A model that says the fix “looks good” is not that owner. The person who must sign a release is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

Does shadow traffic replace the re-run?

Shadow traffic can show you the new output on live inputs before users see it. It does not replace the original check. Re-run the case, then decide whether a shadow period is worth the second bill.
