Gemini vs Claude for Spreadsheet Audits (2026): The Sheet, or the Rules

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

Use Gemini when the audit is a Google Sheet you can open, and the check is whether the cells match rules you paste. Use Claude when those rules have to be the same rules as last month’s audit, kept in a Project. The model may point at a cell, repeat a value, and show arithmetic you asked for. It may not invent a total, a cause, or a “corrected” number. Cleaning messy text is Gemini vs ChatGPT for spreadsheet cleanup. Writing a formula is Gemini vs ChatGPT for spreadsheet formulas. This page is the audit: the rules, the cells, and the mismatches.

An audit line that says the revenue “should be” a different number has left the audit and written a forecast. If the rule is “flag totals that do not equal the sum of the rows,” the draft shows the cell, the sum, and the difference. It does not explain why the business missed a target.

What you are checking

Gemini is the Google Sheet path. Free exists with a Google account, on the October 16 card. Plus at $4.99 is the first paid step on that card. Pro at $19.99 is the higher seat. The invoice is Gemini pricing explained. The model still only knows the cells you gave it.

Claude Pro is the standing-rules path. Change the rule in the Project when the definition changes, then rerun. Do not keep a second definition in a chat from March. The review is Claude review.

Julius Plus is the upload when you want a chart of the mismatches you already listed. Free has a daily credit allowance on that card. The invoice is Julius AI pricing explained.

Hex Professional is the notebook when the number was calculated upstream. Community is free, with a smaller history, on that card. Cite the cell you checked, not a rounded cousin.

A prompt that holds the line:

“Audit this sheet against the rules I pasted. For each mismatch, cite the cell and the value. If a cell is empty, write Empty. Do not invent a replacement value. Do not explain the business cause.”

What not to delegate

Do not paste a payroll file into a personal account your policy forbids. Do not ask which rows “look wrong” without a rule. Do not let a chart title state a conclusion the cells do not state. The sample you would need before calling the audit done, if this is a model change rather than a sheet, is what an AI eval sample size is.

FAQ

Which one catches more errors?

We did not measure that. Gemini fits a Google Sheet and a rule you paste. Claude fits rules that must match last month. The audit is done when every mismatch cites a cell you can open.

Can the model fix the sheet?

It can draft a list of cells that failed a rule you wrote. A person changes the sheet, or decides the rule was wrong. A model that overwrites the total has ended the audit.

Do I need Julius or Hex?

Only if the picture or the upstream query is the thing you must show. The audit can be a list in the chat you then file. Two tools that both “round” the same cell will disagree. Cite one cell.

Who signs the audit?

The person named on it. A clean list of mismatches is not a finding that the books are fine, and it is not a finding that they are not. This page is not an assurance engagement.
