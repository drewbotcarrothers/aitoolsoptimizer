Best AI Tools for HR Teams (2026): The Handbook, the Training Video, the Interview Notes

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

HR teams need a handbook people can find, a training video they can update, and interview notes a candidate was told about. They do not need a model that scores an employee or ranks a resume. This directory does not include an HRIS. Keep the system of record you already run. Use the tools below for the writing, the video, and the notes around it.

A model drafts from a policy you supply. It does not know your jurisdiction. This page is not legal advice. Have counsel read anything an employee must follow. The hiring-side neighbor, which is the recruiter’s stack rather than the HR team’s, is best AI tools for recruiters.

The handbook and the policy

Paste the rule you mean into Claude or ChatGPT. Ask for a plainer version that does not add benefits, deadlines, or exceptions you did not write. Delete any sentence that creates a promise. The editing standard is how to write a blog post with Claude. Grammarly is the pass after that. Free covers the grammar basics. Pro at $12 a member a month billed annually is the tone seat. The buying note is is Grammarly Pro worth it.

Put the approved page where employees already look. Notion Business at $20 a seat is the full Notion AI plan, including the agent features on that tier. Plus at $10 does not include that full set. The ladder is Notion AI pricing explained. If the company runs projects in ClickUp, the comparison is Notion AI vs ClickUp Brain. ClickUp Brain² starts at $9 a user a month on the directory page. Do not stand up a second wiki to get an AI button.

Do not ask a model to “write an employee score” or to infer performance from messages. That is a different, riskier product, and it is not a recommendation on this page.

Training video

Synthesia is the avatar path for a module you would otherwise postpone because nobody can film. Basic is free and watermarked, with 10 minutes. Starter is $29 a month, shown as $19 on yearly billing, with 1,250 credits. One-click translation, SCORM, and SSO are Enterprise lines on the page we checked. The bill is Synthesia pricing explained. The steps are how to make training videos with Synthesia.

If you filmed a person, edit the recording in Descript. Hobbyist at $16 annual is watermark-free 1080p. Business at $50 annual is the plan with translation and dubbing in 30+ languages. The bill is Descript pricing explained. Script the module from the approved handbook page, not from a model’s guess at your policy.

Interviews and the calendar

Tell the candidate. Fathom Free is unlimited recordings when a bot is acceptable. Granola is the path when a bot would make a candidate cautious. Otter is the live transcript, with a 30-minute cap on Basic and a 90-minute cap on Pro. Those caps are Otter pricing explained. The comparison is Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom. The no-bot split is Granola vs Fathom.

The notes feed a scorecard a person fills in. They do not feed an automated hire or no-hire. The recruiter article says the same thing and is the page to send a sourcer who also needs outbound.

Reclaim Lite is free and includes scheduling links. Starter is $10 a seat a month. Yearly billing saves 20% on the page we checked. Use it to protect focus time around interview loops on Google or Outlook. It does not replace the HRIS if offers and stages live there.

A small HR stack

One HR generalist: the assistant you already pay for, Notion or the wiki you have, Synthesia Basic until a module is real, Fathom Free or Granola Basic, Grammarly Free. Add Synthesia Starter when you will publish without a watermark. Add Otter Pro or Fathom Premium when the free cap, not a demo, gets in the way. Add Reclaim Starter when scheduling links on Lite are not enough.

That is a writing and video layer beside the HRIS. It is not a talent-intelligence suite.

FAQ

What AI tool should HR buy first?

A writing seat and a place for the handbook. Training video and a notetaker come after you have a policy worth recording and a candidate who has been told about notes.

Can AI screen employees or candidates?

A person can use a model to summarize notes against criteria they wrote. A model should not be the decision. Talk to counsel before any automated feature touches candidates or performance. This page does not recommend a score.

Synthesia or Descript for training?

Synthesia when nobody is on camera and an avatar is acceptable. Descript when you filmed a person and want to edit the transcript. Starter versus Hobbyist is $29 versus $16 annual, and the products are not substitutes.

Is Otter’s free plan enough for interviews?

Enough for a conversation under 30 minutes, inside 300 minutes a month. A typical hour-long interview needs a paid Otter cap or a different notetaker.
