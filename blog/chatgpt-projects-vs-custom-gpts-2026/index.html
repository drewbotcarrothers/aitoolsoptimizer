ChatGPT Projects vs Custom GPTs (2026): A Folder, or a Reusable Assistant

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

Use a ChatGPT Project when the work is a pile of chats and files that belong together. Use a custom GPT when the work is a reusable assistant with instructions you want to open again, or hand to someone else. Projects are a folder with memory of the effort. A custom GPT is a starting point that begins a new chat as that assistant.

Both show up on the Plus line of the directory card checked October 16, 2026. Plus is $20 a month. Go is $8 and is described there as more messages, uploads, images, and memory. The card lists projects, custom GPTs, and scheduled tasks as Plus additions. If a Go or free account shows one of the buttons, use it. Budget Plus when you need the plan that names both. Instructions you rely on are still what a system prompt is, whether they sit on the project or on the GPT. They guide the model. They do not block a document that says something else. That limit is what prompt injection is.

How to pick for a real week

A spec that grows. Put the notes, the rejected scope, and the latest draft in a Project. Each new chat should see those files so you stop re-attaching them. The writing workflow after the notes exist is how to draft a product spec with AI.

A format you repeat. A custom GPT that knows the release-note headings, the banned words, and two examples will beat a Project you have to re-explain. Paste one finished example. That is what few-shot prompting is. The release-note steps are how to write release notes with AI.

Notes that are the system of record. A Project is a working set. It is a poor wiki. If the team lives in Notion, the AI seat is Notion AI. The comparison is Notion AI vs ChatGPT for notes. Notion Business is $20 per seat per month on the October 16 card. ChatGPT Plus is also $20, and it does not become the wiki.

FAQ

Do I need Plus for both?

The directory card checked October 16, 2026 puts projects and custom GPTs on Plus at $20. Go at $8 is the cheaper step for messages and uploads. Check the account you have. If the control is missing, Plus is the plan that lists it.

Can a custom GPT see every chat in a Project?

Treat them as separate containers. Put the files the assistant needs on the GPT. Put the running thread in the Project. If a file must be in both, you are maintaining two copies, and one of them will go stale.

Which one is better for a team?

A custom GPT when many people should start from the same instructions. A Project when a few people are continuing the same body of work. Business is the plan that adds admin controls around either one.

Will either one stop the model from inventing a price?

No. Paste the price and the date, and tell it to leave a blank when the source is missing. The check is how to fact-check AI writing.
