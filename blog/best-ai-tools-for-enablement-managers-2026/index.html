Best AI Tools for Enablement Managers (2026): The Note, the Deck, the Quiz

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

An enablement manager should use AI to draft a coaching note from a call they are allowed to use, a deck they will present, and a quiz that only asks what the approved script already says. The library, the battlecard, and the shared call archive are the team’s system: best AI tools for enablement teams. This page is the manager who has to walk into Monday with one note and one deck. The script the quiz must follow is how to create a demo script with AI.

What you are paying for

Claude Pro at $20 holds the transcript in a project and drafts the note: what the rep said, what the script says, and the gap, only where both texts support it. The review is Claude review.

Gamma Plus at $9 is the deck. Pro is $18 a seat a month if you need the larger credit pool. The invoice is Gamma pricing explained. A generated chart with a number you did not paste is the slide you delete.

Fathom is the recorder when a bot is acceptable. If the room will not accept a bot, write the note yourself and paste only that. The invoice is Fathom pricing explained. The no-bot option on another card is Granola vs Fathom.

ChatGPT Plus at $20 writes the quiz. Go at $8 may include ads. A quiz you will give a rep is a poor place for an ad. The review is ChatGPT review.

Notion Business is the seat only if the checklist lives there and you do not already have it through the team. The invoice is Notion AI pricing explained.

A prompt that holds the line:

“Draft a coaching note from the transcript and the script I pasted. Quote both. Do not score the rep. Do not add a claim. Then stop. I will ask for a quiz only from the script, in a separate step.”

What not to automate

Do not rank reps. Do not email a recording the participant did not expect. Do not let the deck update the script. The script changes in its own review. The manager presents the current one.

FAQ

How is this different from the enablement team roundup?

That page is the system: the script library, the battlecard, the shared archive. This page is one manager’s Monday: a note, a deck, and a quiz. Use the team page when you are buying for the function. Use this page when you are the person in the room.

Can I live on free plans?

Claude Free and ChatGPT Free can draft one note and one quiz from a paste. Gamma’s free credits do not refresh, on the directory card. Fathom Free is the recorder if a bot is allowed and the free tier still matches the pricing article. The paid step is the one you repeat every week.

What if I cannot record?

Then Fathom is the wrong tool for that call. Write what you heard, and paste only that into Claude. A note that remembers a sentence you did not write down is the sentence to delete.

Who approves the quiz?

You do, against the script, before a rep sees it. A question with no line in the script is teaching a guess. Delete it.
