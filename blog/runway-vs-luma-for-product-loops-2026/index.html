Runway vs Luma for Product Loops (2026): A Short Clip of a Thing You Own

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

Use Runway or Luma to draft a short looping clip from a product photo you have the right to use. Then you watch every frame before it runs as an ad. Their credits are different units. A lower monthly price is not a cheaper second of video. We did not score which loop looks more like the product. The general pair is Runway vs Luma.

The invoices are Runway pricing explained and Luma AI pricing explained. The still before the motion is how to generate product photos with AI.

How to spend the first credits

Runway lets you spend 125 credits before a subscription. That is the right first loop if you are unsure the shot is worth a subscription. Standard at $12 annual is the first refill: 625 credits a month. The directory says all models and an agentic collaborator are on that row. It does not say those credits equal a specific number of seconds. Do not divide $12 by 625 and call it a price per loop. The review is Runway review.

Luma starts at Plus. $30 a month or $300 a year buys 10,000 credits and, on the card, commercial use and guest collaborators. Pro at $90 multiplies Luma Agents usage by four, on the card. If you only need one loop and you can live with Runway’s free credits, pay Luma after you know you want that tool. The wider shelf is best AI video generators.

A cheaper video seat, if the loop is an ad and the budget is the point. Kling AI Standard is $6.99 the first month, then $8.80 a month, on the directory card. That comparison is Runway vs Kling for ads. It is a third product. It does not make Runway credits and Luma credits comparable.

A prompt that holds the line:

“Animate this product photo as a three-second loop. Keep the label text, colors, and shape unchanged. No extra products. No people. No claims on screen. If you cannot keep the label, stop.”

Then you watch it frame by frame. A melted logo is a failed clip. The storyboard habit before you generate motion is how to storyboard with AI.

What not to delegate

Do not upload a photo you do not have rights to. Do not accept a loop that invents a certification badge, a “before and after,” or a result the product does not deliver. Do not buy Max or Ultra to fix a bad still. Fix the still. Motion makes a wrong label more expensive.

The person who approves the export is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

FAQ

Which one makes a better product loop?

We did not score clips. Pick Runway if you want a free credit taste and a $12 annual entry. Pick Luma if you will start at $30 and the Plus pool is the product you want. Watch the label either way.

Are the credits the same?

No. Runway’s 625 and Luma’s 10,000 are not a ratio you can shop. Each vendor meters its own generations. Compare the plan you can afford to the number of loops you will actually review.

Is a free plan enough to ship an ad?

Runway’s free credits are one-time. Enough to see a draft. A campaign that needs revisions belongs on a paid row, and on a plan whose commercial terms you have read. Luma’s card does not list a free plan.

What if the product has to show a screen recording instead?

Use the recording. A generated loop of a UI you did not capture will invent buttons. That is a different video, and it is the wrong tool for a changelog.
