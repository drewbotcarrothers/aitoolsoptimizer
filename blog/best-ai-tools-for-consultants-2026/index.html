Best AI Tools for Consultants (2026): Research, the Deck, and the Notes

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

A consultant should pay for the research source, the deck format the client will open, and notes from the working session. A model can draft the memo from material you checked. It cannot sign the recommendation. If a figure is not on a page you opened, it does not go in the deck.

Prices are US list prices excluding tax. Most come from directory pages checked October 16, 2026. Perplexity’s plan detail also appears in an October 24, 2026 pricing article. Nothing here is a utilization study. The adjacent solo stack is best AI tools for freelancers.

Research before slides

Perplexity is the public-web pass. The steps are how to use Perplexity for market research. Pro at $17 billed annually is optional until the free allowance blocks a real week. The bill is Perplexity pricing explained. The split with a general assistant is ChatGPT vs Perplexity for research.

Papers are a different corpus. Consensus Pro is $12 a month on a $144 year. Elicit Plus is $11 a user a month billed annually on the non-Industry card. The workflow is how to research with Elicit and Consensus.

Then the memo. Paste only the claims you kept. Ask Claude or ChatGPT to draft from that list and to mark gaps. The editing rule is how to write a blog post with Claude. Grammarly is the surface pass after the facts are right. Whether Pro is worth $12 annual is is Grammarly Pro worth it.

Julius is the chart step when the evidence is a spreadsheet, not a web page. Plus is $16 a month billed annually for 2,000 monthly credits plus a daily allowance. The spreadsheet shelf is best AI for Excel and Google Sheets. Do not upload a client’s confidential model to a new tool until the engagement letter allows it.

The deck and the notes

Pick the deck tool from the file, not from a template gallery. The comparison is Gamma vs Beautiful.ai vs Plus AI. Gamma’s credits are Gamma pricing explained. Beautiful.ai’s annual-versus-monthly gap is Beautiful.ai pricing explained. A consultant who presents twice a month and can send a link will feel Gamma Plus at $9. A consultant whose client opens PowerPoint will feel Beautiful.ai or Plus AI. Build the outline before you generate. A generated deck of twelve vague slides is a rewrite, and on Gamma it also spent credits.

Notes: tell the client when a bot or a recorder is on. Fathom Free is the unlimited recording plan when a bot is acceptable. Granola is the local notes path when it is not. Business at $14 a user removes the 30-day history cap, and the workspace shares one plan. The split is Granola vs Fathom. The wider shelf is best AI meeting notetakers.

Notion Business at $20 a seat is the shared findings wiki if the team already writes there. The seat detail is Notion AI pricing explained. The wiki is not the client deliverable. The deck is.

A small stack

One independent consultant: Perplexity Free, Claude or ChatGPT on the plan you already have, Gamma Plus or the Beautiful.ai trial, Granola Basic or Fathom Free, Grammarly Free. Add a paid seat when a limit is real. That is tens of dollars, not a research platform. A firm that also sells outbound should keep that work on best AI tools for sales teams, not in the same inbox as client memos.

FAQ

What AI tool should a consultant buy first?

The one that matches the deliverable a client is waiting on. Usually that is a writing seat you already trust, plus a deck tool in the file format you send. Research can start on Perplexity’s free tier.

Gamma or Beautiful.ai for client work?

Gamma Plus at $9 if a link or a doc is acceptable. Beautiful.ai Pro at $14.50 annual, or $45 monthly, if you need PowerPoint and unlimited generation on the seat. The format article is linked above.

Can I record client calls?

Only with a practice the client has agreed to, and only where the law allows it. This page is not legal advice. Granola is the option when a bot should not join. Fathom is the option when a bot is fine.

Is Julius a replacement for an analyst?

It makes charts and answers from data you upload, inside a credit plan. You still check the number that will be in the recommendation. Plus is $16 a month billed annually.
