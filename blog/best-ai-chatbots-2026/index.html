Why your choice of chatbot matters

All three assistants are capable, but each has strengths. ChatGPT is the most versatile, Claude shines with long documents, and Gemini fits neatly into Google Workspace.

Quick verdict

  • All-rounder: ChatGPT
  • Long documents & writing: Claude
  • Google users: Gemini
  • Research with sources: Perplexity

How to choose

Try the same three real tasks in each free tier and pick the one whose answers you edit least.
