Notion AI vs Grammarly (2026): The Page, or the Sentence

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

Use Notion AI when the writing lives in Notion and you want the model on that page, that database, and that meeting note. Use Grammarly when the pain is the sentence, in the apps you already type in, and the facts are already yours. Notion AI is a workspace feature. Grammarly is an editor that follows you. Buying both on day one is how a team pays $20 and $12 to fix one comma.

What the money buys

Notion. Free includes a trial of Notion AI, such as generating docs and autofilling databases. Plus is $10 a seat a month for small teams. Business is $20 a seat and adds Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, and search across connected apps. That is the full AI tier. Custom Agents run on credits: free to try, then $10 per 1,000 credits on Business and Enterprise. Enterprise is sales. The card is Notion AI pricing explained. The review is Notion AI review.

Notion will draft a page that sounds finished and is missing the number you meant. Paste the source. Delete the sentence that adds a policy. A page in Notion is still a page you publish. The fact pass is how to fact-check AI writing.

Grammarly. Free covers grammar and spelling. Pro is $12 a member a month billed annually, which is $144 a year, or $60 per quarter, or $30 billed monthly. Pro adds full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustment, and 2,000 AI prompts. Enterprise, through Superhuman Go, adds unlimited AI prompts, roles, and data loss prevention. The directory does not print an Enterprise dollar. Pro replaces the old Business plan and supports up to 149 seats. Grammarly is part of Superhuman. The review is Grammarly review. Grammarly will not know that the flag name is wrong, the discount is outdated, or the customer asked for the other tier. Run it after the facts are right. It is a poor place to ask for the first draft of a case study. That draft is a chat, from notes you paste. The method is how to write a blog post with Claude.

A sensible order

If the team already lives in Notion, try the Free AI trial on one real page. Move the people who publish to Business when the trial stops the work. Do not buy Plus and expect the agent. Leave Grammarly Free on for the sentence, including inside Notion if the extension runs there.

If the team does not live in Notion, do not adopt Notion to get a writing assistant. Grammarly Free, then Pro at $12 annual if rewrites are the weekly habit, is the smaller bet. A $20 chat seat is the bet when you are producing the draft, not polishing it. The writing aisle is best AI writing assistants.

Monthly Grammarly at $30 is $360 a year, against $144 annual. The directory’s own verdict is to avoid monthly billing if you can. This page agrees with the arithmetic. It does not agree that everyone needs Pro.

FAQ

Does Notion Business include Grammarly?

No. Business includes Notion’s AI on the workspace. Grammarly is a separate product that checks text where its extension or app runs. You can use both. You do not get one by paying for the other.

Is Grammarly Pro enough for a wiki?

It is enough for the sentences in the wiki. It will not search your Notion workspace, autofill a database, or keep meeting notes on the page. That is the Business seat, if Notion is the system of record.

Can I write the page in ChatGPT and edit it in Grammarly?

Yes. That is often the right pair when Notion is not where the page lives. Plus is $20. Grammarly Free may be enough after that. Pro is the rewrite seat, not a second drafter.

Which one is better for students?

Grammarly Free on work you wrote yourself. Notion only if the school already uses it. A student stack that does not start with a workspace seat is best AI tools for students. Your school’s rules outrank either product.
