invideo AI Review (2026): Starter at $10 a Seat, Billed Annually

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

invideo AI turns a prompt into a video draft and meters the generative work in credits. The compare table on the pricing page prints annual prices: Starter $10 per seat per month, Plus $30, and Max $75. Starter’s own card shows $20 a month against that $10 annual price, and says you save $120 a year per seat. Credits do not roll over.

Those figures were read from invideo.io/pricing on October 24, 2026. Plus and Max monthly prices were $60 and $150 when we checked the same page on October 16, 2026, which is double the annual rates. The October 24 page still frames annual plans as 50% off. Confirm the monthly toggle before you quote it. We did not score picture quality. The video shelf is best AI video generators.

invideo’s FAQ says a credit is used for generation, not for downloading, and that model prices follow their API rates and can change without notice. The timeline editor, color, audio, and 3D tools are listed on every plan, including Starter. Guest seats are not on Starter. Multiplayer caps rise with the plan.

What the credit is not

A credit is not a minute of timeline editing. invideo’s planner treats timeline hours as separate from generative credits, and says editing on Starter does not spend the credit pool. The bill surprises people when they generate, not when they trim. Price one finished video: script, images, and the Seedance shots. If that one video is 200 credits, Starter’s 400 is two of them. Plus is the plan when that count is a weekly job.

CapCut is the editor for footage you already have. The CapCut review is that seat. Runway is a generative studio with its own credit, in the Runway review. Opus Clip cuts long video into shorts. Google Flow is Google’s Veo studio. YouTubers choosing a stack should read best AI tools for YouTubers.

Commercial use and model licenses are not the subscription price. invideo says models are available at their API pricing inside the credit. Check the model you will actually render before you promise a client the file.

Who should pay

Try one video and read the credit receipt before you pick a pool. Pay for Starter annual if 400 credits and the timeline cover the month and you will still be here next month. The monthly card is $20. Pay for Plus when agents and the larger Seedance set are the job. Pay for Max when the credit log says Plus ran out. Skip invideo for a podcast edit or a screen recording.
