Editing, by the shape of the footage

Descript is the editor when the video is mostly someone talking and you want the timeline to follow the transcript. Free is 60 media minutes a month and does not require a card. Hobbyist is $16/person/month billed annually or $24 monthly, with 10 media hours, 400 AI credits, and watermark-free 1080p. Creator at $24/person/month annual adds 4K. The plan-by-plan breakdown is the Descript review.

CapCut is the editor when the job is captions, templates, and a vertical cut, including on a phone. The US reference price for Pro is $19.99/month or $179.99/year. CapCut says the price on the site is a reference and the final price appears after you sign in, and it can differ by region. Believe the number in your account. Details are in the CapCut review.

Use both only if you already do. A channel that lives in one editor should not pay for the other “just in case.”

Shorts, thumbnails, and generated shots

Opus Clip Free includes 60 credits a month, and those clips stop being exportable after 3 days. Starter at $15/month is monthly billing only, with 150 credits and no watermark. Pro is $29/month or $14.50/month billed annually. If you post Shorts every week, Free will fight you. The review is Opus Clip.

Thumbnails fail when the words are wrong. Ideogram is the generator sold for text in images. Free is 30 credits a week. Plus is $20/month or $15/month if you pay $180 for the year. Canva is the layout tool when you already know the title and you are placing it on a face or a product. Canva’s AI features are reviewed in Canva Magic Studio.

HeyGen Creator at $29/month is the presenter plan: 1080p and watermark removal. Free is three videos a month, up to one minute each, and it keeps a watermark. Training and course presenters are compared in HeyGen vs Synthesia.

Generated B-roll is optional. Runway Standard is $12/month billed annually for 625 credits, which Runway equates to about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5. invideo AI Starter is $10/seat/month billed annually ($20 monthly) and is a prompt-to-draft tool, not a timeline. A publishing pass that AI does not get to skip

  1. Write the claim list before the script. Every number needs a page you can link in the description.
  2. Generate the voice on a plan whose license matches the video. Free ElevenLabs is for a private test.
  3. Cut the silence and the repeated sentence yourself, even if the editor offers a filler-word button.
  4. Read the thumbnail out loud. If a word is misspelled, fix it before you upload.
  5. Watch the first three seconds of each Short. A virality score is a sort order.

FAQ

What AI tools do YouTubers actually need?

A script assistant you already have for free, a voice plan with a commercial license if you narrate, one editor, and a Shorts tool once you cut clips every week. Thumbnails can stay on a free Ideogram or Canva plan until text or brand layout blocks you.

Is ElevenLabs required for YouTube?

No. It is the default when you want generated narration and a commercial license, which starts at $6/month. If you record your own voice, skip it and edit in Descript or CapCut.

Can I use Suno music on YouTube?

Suno includes commercial rights on Pro and Premier, not on Free. Pro showed at $11/month, or $8/month with yearly billing, when we checked. Confirm the license text at checkout before a monetized upload.
