What you are paying for

Figma Professional is the system. Starter is free, with 150 AI credits a day up to 500 a month. The identity file is the record. A generated board that never lands in that file is a mood, not a brand. The review is Figma review.

Firefly Standard at $9.99 is the stills row when you want the credit pack and Adobe’s recorded claim that outputs can be used commercially unless a feature is marked as a personal-use beta. That is Adobe’s wording on the directory card, not a clearance from this site. Pro at $19.99 includes 4,000 credits and Express Premium. The ladder is Adobe Firefly pricing explained.

Midjourney Basic is the exploration row when the gallery rules fit the work. Companies with more than $1,000,000 in gross annual revenue need Pro or Mega for commercial use, on that card. Do not explore an unreleased identity on a public plan. The ladder is Midjourney pricing explained.

Krea Basic is the cheap look pass. Free is 100 compute units a day. The invoice is Krea pricing explained.

Canva Pro resizes an approved layout. It does not hold the identity for a second designer. Business is $250 a person a year when the team must share paid assets under an admin. The seat rule is Canva pricing explained. Express, when the still should be Firefly inside a template, is Adobe Express pricing explained.

A one-time generated logo package is Looka at $35 for the Basic Logo Package, and you pay to download. It is a starting file, not a system. The invoice is Looka pricing explained.

A prompt that holds the line:

“Explore three stills from the color, type notes, and subject I pasted. Do not draw a logo, a wordmark, or a slogan. Do not add a product claim. Leave space for type. If a constraint is missing, make a quieter image, not a busier one.”

What not to automate

Do not generate the logo and ship it. Do not let a campaign still invent a tagline. Do not treat a Canva Brand Kit as the source of truth if Figma is the system, or the reverse, unless one owner picks one file. Magnific’s upscaler is a real tool, and the USD price was not on the directory card. The reason is Magnific pricing explained. Leave it out of a US forecast until you read USD on the page.

FAQ

How is this different from the brand-team page?

Brand teams on this site apply a voice and hand off a file the company can edit. Brand designers own the visual system and the stills that have to match it. A copy tool is their page. Figma and a generator are this one.

How is this different from design ops?

Design ops keeps intake, libraries, and templates from drifting. Brand design makes the identity those templates have to follow. If the pain is the request queue, use the design-ops page. If the pain is the mark and the campaign frame, use this one.

Which generator should we standardize on?

We did not score looks. Standardize the system file first. Use Firefly when the commercial-use note on Adobe’s card is the requirement you need. Use Midjourney when you accept its plan rules, including Stealth and the revenue line. Use Krea for a cheap pass. Put the winner in Figma.

Can AI set the brand colors?

It can show options inside constraints you named. The palette you publish is a decision, with contrast you checked and an owner. A generated palette with no one responsible is a second brand.
