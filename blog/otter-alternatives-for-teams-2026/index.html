Otter Alternatives for Teams (2026): Seats, Caps, and a Shared Library

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

A team should leave Otter when the billed-user cap, the conversation length, or the missing admin control blocks the workspace you actually have. Stay on Otter Business when unlimited meetings, a 4-hour conversation cap, and up to 25 billed users match the team, and you want that contract. The individual version of this question, which is about minute caps and bots, is Otter.ai alternatives.

Otter Pro is $16.99 a user a month, or $8.33 billed annually, with 1,200 minutes, 90 minutes per conversation, and a workspace cap of 5 billed users. Business is $30, or $19.99 annually, with unlimited meetings, a 4-hour conversation cap, and up to 25 billed users. Enterprise is custom. The invoice is Otter.ai pricing explained.

What the team plan changes

Otter Business is the upgrade when Pro’s 90-minute conversation cap or the 5-user workspace is the wall. The directory also lists custom AI workflows, 200 AI Chat queries a user a month, and up to 6,000 imported minutes a user a month. Promotions were running during the October 16 check, so checkout can be lower. Do not budget the promotion as the renewal price. The review is Otter.ai review.

Fathom Team is the shared layer on top of a free recorder. Free already lists unlimited recordings. Team adds comments, folders, keyword alerts, and shared playlists. Business, at $25 a user annually or $34 monthly, is the CRM tier, also with a two-user minimum. Buy Team when the library is the pain. Buy Business when the CRM field is the pain. How a team should switch

Pick one standing meeting and one customer call. Run them for two weeks on the candidate, with consent from the room. Compare the admin questions, not the adjectives: Can we see everyone’s notes? Can we delete a recording? Does the bot join? What happens at user 6 and user 26?

Otter Enterprise is the conversation when 25 billed users is not enough or you need a control the Business card does not list. The directory does not print an Enterprise dollar. Ask for it. Do not multiply Business by a guess.

Notes are not the system of record for a decision. File the decision in the ticket or the doc. That distinction is what a system of record for AI is.

FAQ

Is Otter Business required for any team?

No. A team of three with short calls can live on Pro until the 5-user cap or the 90-minute cap hurts. A team of ten cannot put ten billed users on Pro.

Which alternative is cheapest for ten people?

On the annual list prices above, Granola Business is $140 a month and Fathom Team is $150, against Otter Business at $199.90 and Fireflies Business at $190. Cheapest is not the same library. Granola has no bot and puts SSO on a higher tier. Fathom’s free plan already records. Match the constraint, then the total.

Do we need a bot?

If the room refuses a bot, Granola is the path to test. If you need a shared recording the whole team can open, a bot product is the usual shape. Get consent either way.

Can AI notes replace the recap email?

They can draft it. A person sends it, after deleting anything the transcript does not support. The habit is how to write customer emails with AI.
