Otter vs Fireflies for Standups (2026): A Short Meeting, Then the Note

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

For a short standup, Otter Pro at $8.33 a user a month billed annually fits when you want a template and the 90-minute conversation cap is longer than the meeting. Fireflies Pro at $10 a user a month billed annually fits when you need to search what the team said across many standups. Tell people a bot is in the room. We did not score transcription accuracy.

What you are paying for

Otter Basic is a real free tier for a short meeting: 300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation. A team that standups for 15 minutes can stay there until the minute cap or the conversation cap binds. Pro is the template-and-minutes step. Business is the longer meeting and the advanced workflows. Enterprise is custom. The tool page is Otter.

Fireflies Pro at $10 annual is the search step: ask across meetings, not only inside today’s note. Business at $19 annual is the analytics and admin step the knowledge base described for that tier. The directory’s pricing table does not itemize every analytics feature in one cell, so this page does not invent a feature list beyond the plan names and prices. Read the current knowledge base before you buy Business for a single checkbox. The tool page is Fireflies.

Granola Business at $14 is the path when the team will not accept a bot. Basic keeps 30 days of history. A standup archive you need for a quarter is the paid history, or a note you paste into the tracker the same day. The invoice is Granola pricing explained.

Fathom Free includes unlimited recordings on the directory card, with a bot. Premium is $16 a month billed annually. Use Fathom if that is already the company standard. Do not add a third bot to the same standup. One recorder, agreed in advance.

A prompt that holds the line, after the transcript exists:

“From this standup transcript, list blockers, owners, and dates that were actually said. If an owner was not named, write Unassigned. Do not invent a decision. Do not add a task that was only a joke or a side comment. Keep yesterday’s unresolved items only if they appear in this transcript.”

The person who accepts the action is what human-in-the-loop AI is. A task nobody said is what an AI hallucination is.

What the note is for

The note feeds the tracker. It does not replace the tracker. If the team runs the work in ClickUp, the task lives there after a person creates it. ClickUp Brain² starts at $9 a user a month on the directory card, on top of a ClickUp plan. A transcript is not a task until someone assigns it.

Do not record a standup that includes a performance conversation, a medical update, or a customer secret the whole channel should not have. Stop the bot. Those topics are the wrong input for a shared summary. The written record you do keep, for ordinary blockers, should match what was said. That habit is what a system of record for AI is.

FAQ

Which transcribes a standup more accurately?

We did not measure accuracy. Read the note before you assign work. Names and dates fail first. Fix those by hand.

Is the free plan enough?

Otter Basic can cover a short standup inside the 30-minute conversation cap and the monthly minutes. Fireflies Free exists and includes Talk dictation on every plan. Pay when you need Otter’s longer cap and templates, or Fireflies search across meetings.

Do we have to announce the bot?

Yes. Tell the people in the meeting, including anyone who joins late. A surprise recorder is a trust problem before it is a product problem. If someone opts out, stop the bot or reschedule the topic.

Can the summary write the status report?

It can draft bullets from accepted notes. The status report still needs the owner. A weekly rewrite from raw transcripts will promote side comments into commitments. Filter first.
