How to Create a Sales Discovery Script with AI (2026): Questions, Not a Pitch

By Andrew · October 24, 2026

A sales discovery script written with AI is a short list of questions you will ask, the facts you must leave with, and the lines you will not say. The model can order the questions and cut a monologue. It cannot invent the buyer’s problem, their budget, or a competitor weakness. Discovery is the meeting before the demo. If you already know the slide you want to show, you are writing a different script.

A script that answers its own questions is a pitch. Delete any line that tells the buyer what their problem is. You can hypothesize in your prep notes, labeled as a hypothesis. You do not recite it as a fact. A fabricated pain is a hallucination said out loud.

A prompt that holds the line:

“Draft a discovery script of questions only, from these facts we need and these lines we must not say. Do not describe the buyer’s pain. Do not mention a price I did not paste. Do not add a competitor insult. Leave a blank line under each question for notes. End with the next step I wrote.”

The person on the call is what human-in-the-loop AI is.

After the call

Write what they said, in their words, under the question that prompted it. The model may file a quote you paste. It may not infer budget from a joke, or authority from a job title you did not confirm. Empty fields stay empty. That empty field is the reason for the next email.

The demo script, if they ask for one, uses only the pains they stated. If they stated none, the demo is premature. Enablement’s version of the file the rep clicks is best AI tools for enablement teams. Getting the file into the system the rep already opens is best AI tools for revenue enablement.

FAQ

How long should discovery be?

Long enough to fill the facts you listed, and short enough that you listen. A 20-question script you race through is a survey. Cut questions that do not change what you do next. The model can propose cuts. You decide which fact is required.

Can AI role-play the buyer?

Yes, as practice, if you label it practice. A simulated buyer will invent constraints. Do not paste that fiction into the CRM as if it were the account. The CRM row is for the real call.

Should pricing be in the discovery script?

Only the sentence you are allowed to say, which might be “I will confirm and send the page.” An invented range is a quote. If the page is not approved, it is not in the script. The page workflow is linked above.

Is a call recording required?

No. Notes a person took are enough for this workflow. Recording follows consent and your policy. A script does not imply a recorder.
