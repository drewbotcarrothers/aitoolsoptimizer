Best AI Voice Generators: ElevenLabs Review + Alternatives

By Andrew · October 17, 2026

ElevenLabs is the default AI voice generator for narration, dubbing, and voice agents. The free plan is 10,000 credits a month and does not include a commercial license. Starter at $6/month is the first plan that does. Choose Murf when you want a published API rate card, Descript when you are editing recordings you already made, and Speechify when you want to listen rather than publish a voice.

Prices were last verified October 16, 2026. We did not run a blind listening test, so this article does not rank “naturalness” with a score. It ranks fit against published licenses, credits, and prices.

Who Starter is for: a creator publishing monetized videos who can live inside 30,000 credits. Who Creator is for: anyone who needs a professional clone. Do not expect that feature on Free or Starter. Who Pro and above is for: API products and teams, not a single YouTube channel, unless the credit math says otherwise.

ElevenLabs is also the voice layer in front of avatar video. If the face matters as much as the voice, compare HeyGen and Synthesia instead of buying a higher ElevenLabs tier alone.

What to do with it

  1. Write the script out loud. Short sentences survive synthesis better than clauses inside clauses.
  2. Generate on Free. If you will publish commercially, move to Starter before you post, because Free does not include the commercial license.
  3. Clone a voice only with permission from the person, a written release if money is involved, and a disclosure where the platform asks for one. Cloning yourself for your own channel is a different situation from cloning a client or a public figure.
  4. Keep the project file. Regenerating a paragraph later spends credits again.

Murf publishes API rates that are easy to forecast:

New API accounts get $10 in credit in the plan description we checked, with 5 concurrent requests. Purchased credit does not expire. A 1,000-character script at studio quality is about $0.03, so the $10 credit is a large number of short reads. Enterprise API adds options such as higher concurrency and professional voice cloning.

Murf’s Help Center describes a free Studio workspace with 10 minutes of generation and no downloads. We could not verify current Studio subscription prices, because that tab did not render. Use Murf’s pricing page for Studio seats, and use the API table above only for API work. Do not treat ElevenLabs Starter ($6) and an unknown Murf Studio price as equivalent.

Descript, when the voice is yours

Descript is an editor that happens to have strong audio tools: Studio Sound, filler-word removal, and transcription-based editing. It is the wrong product if you have no recording and want a synthetic narrator. It is the right product if you recorded the take on a laptop.

Speechify, for ears rather than microphones

Speechify Premium is $29/month on monthly billing, with 1,000+ natural voices and 60+ languages. Free is 10 voices at up to 1.5× speed and text-to-speech only. The company says yearly billing saves 60% versus monthly. Until you see the annual charge, budget the $29 figure or read the checkout total. Speechify is for students and anyone who would rather hear a document. It is not the commercial voiceover stack.

Music is a different subscription

Suno makes songs. Free is 50 credits a day without commercial rights. Pro is $8/month billed yearly. Buying ElevenLabs to get a soundtrack is the wrong SKU; ElevenLabs Starter does include commercial use of its music features, which is still not a full song studio. Pick one.

FAQ

Is ElevenLabs free for YouTube?

You can generate on the free plan. ElevenLabs’ pricing page puts the commercial license on Starter ($6/month) and above, not on Free. If the video earns money or promotes a product, budget Starter and read the current license.

What is the best ElevenLabs alternative?

For API voice at a published per-character rate, Murf. For repairing your own voice recordings, Descript. For listening to text, Speechify. For a face plus a voice, HeyGen. There is no single alternative that copies every ElevenLabs tier at a lower confirmed price.

Can I clone someone else’s voice?

Only with their permission. A tool that allows the upload is not permission. Disclose synthetic voices where a platform, client, or local rule requires it.
