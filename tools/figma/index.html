Figma review

Collaborative interface design platform with an AI agent, Figma Make prototyping and Dev Mode.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Figma → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Figma is the industry standard for interface design and collaboration, and its AI features now cover everything from generating designs to building working prototypes with Figma Make. The free Starter plan is enough to learn; Professional at $16 per Full seat suits small teams, and cheaper Dev and Collab seats keep costs down for non-designers. Non-designers who need social graphics should look at Canva instead.

Key takeaways

  • Starter is free with unlimited drafts and 150 AI credits per day (up to 500 per month).
  • Professional: Full seat $16/mo (3,000 AI credits), Dev seat $12/mo (500 credits), Collab seat $3/mo.
  • Organization (billed annually): Full $55, Dev $25, Collab $5 per month; Enterprise: Full $90, Dev $35.
  • AI add-ons are available on all plans, and extra AI credits can be bought pay-as-you-go or by subscription.
  • Figma's agent can add motion, document design systems and implement feedback on the canvas.

What Figma is

Figma is a collaborative design platform that runs in the browser (with desktop apps) where teams design interfaces, build prototypes, manage design systems and brainstorm on whiteboards. Its signature is real-time multiplayer: designers, developers and product managers work in the same file at once.

Figma now calls itself an “AI-native canvas,” where “teammates and AI agents work in the same space with shared context.” You can ask Figma’s agent to add motion to designs, document a design system or implement feedback, and use Figma Make to turn ideas into working apps, websites and products.

Key features

Design and prototyping

Vector editing, auto layout, components and variants, plus interactive prototypes with transitions. Newer tools include textures, brush strokes for type and type on a path.

AI agent

Generate and refine designs, add motion, document systems and apply feedback directly on the canvas.

Figma Make

Describe an idea and get a working prototype or app you can iterate on.

Dev Mode and MCP server

Inspect designs, get code-ready specs and connect AI coding tools through Figma’s MCP server (advanced inspection on paid plans).

Design systems

Shared libraries, variables and components keep products consistent.

Best use cases

  • App design: a startup designs its mobile onboarding flow and tests it as a clickable prototype.
  • Design systems: a company maintains shared components used across web and mobile products.
  • Handoff: developers inspect spacing, tokens and code snippets in Dev Mode.
  • Rapid ideas: a PM uses Figma Make to turn a feature idea into a working prototype for user testing.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at figma.com.
  2. Start from a UI kit or template in the Community.
  3. Learn frames, auto layout and components first.
  4. Invite collaborators with Collab seats for comments.
  5. Try Figma’s AI and Figma Make on a small project and watch your credit usage.
  6. Upgrade to Professional when you need shared team libraries.

Tips and tricks

  • Master auto layout; it makes designs responsive and easy to update.
  • Give developers Dev seats, not Full seats, to save money.
  • Name layers and components clearly so AI and teammates understand them.
  • Use variables for colors and spacing to theme designs quickly.

Example Figma Make prompt:

Build a mobile habit tracker app with a home screen showing today’s habits as checkable cards, a weekly streak chart and an add-habit form. Use a calm blue palette and rounded cards.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Best-in-class real-time collaboration
  • Free plan for learning and personal projects
  • Seat types keep costs low for developers and reviewers
  • Strong developer handoff with Dev Mode and MCP server
  • Huge community of templates, UI kits and plugins

👎 Cons

  • Costs rise quickly on Organization and Enterprise plans
  • AI credits add another meter to manage
  • Overkill for simple marketing graphics
  • Steeper learning curve than template editors

Who should (and shouldn't) use Figma

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Design apps, websites or product interfaces
  • Work on a team with designers, developers and PMs
  • Maintain a design system across products

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need quick social posts or flyers (consider Canva or Adobe Express)
  • Want a no-code app builder only (consider Lovable or v0)
  • Need print layout tools

Figma alternatives

  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))
  • Adobe Express — All-in-one design app for social posts, flyers, videos and PDFs, with Firefly generative AI built in. (from $9.99/mo (Premium))
  • v0 — Vercel's AI builder that turns prompts into working apps and publishes them in seconds. (from $30/user/mo (Plus))
  • Lovable — AI software engineer that builds websites and web apps from chat, with hosting via Lovable Cloud and team workspaces. (from $25/mo for 100 credits (Pro); $21/mo billed annually)

FAQ

Is Figma free?

Yes. The Starter plan is free with unlimited drafts, UI kits and templates, and 150 AI credits per day up to 500 per month.

How much does Figma Professional cost?

Figma Professional costs $16 per month for a Full seat, $12 for a Dev seat and $3 for a Collab seat. Full seats include 3,000 AI credits per month and Dev seats 500.

What are Figma AI credits?

AI credits are consumed by Figma's AI features. Each seat includes a monthly allowance, and teams can buy additional shared credits pay-as-you-go or by subscription.

What's the difference between Full, Dev and Collab seats?

Full seats get every design and AI tool, Dev seats focus on Dev Mode and developer tools, and Collab seats are for people who mainly view, comment and whiteboard.

How much do Figma Organization and Enterprise cost?

Billed annually, Organization is $55 per Full seat, $25 per Dev seat and $5 per Collab seat per month. Enterprise lists $90 per Full seat and $35 per Dev seat per month.
