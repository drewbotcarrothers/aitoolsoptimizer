What Intercom Fin is

Fin is an AI customer service agent from Intercom. It reads your help content, policies and data, then answers customers and resolves their issues across chat, email, voice, social and Slack. With Procedures, Fin can follow multi-step instructions for complex cases, such as checking order status or processing a request, and hand off to a human or workflow when appropriate.

The latest generation, Fin 4, is described as “a major step toward a fully agentic customer experience,” powered by Intercom’s Apex AI models built for customer service. Fin improves through a flywheel of Train, Test, Deploy and Analyze.

Key features

Resolutions across channels

Best use cases

  • Ecommerce: answer order, shipping and returns questions instantly.
  • SaaS: resolve how-to questions and onboard new users.
  • Customer success: route complex accounts to the right teammate with context.
  • After hours: provide 24/7 support without extra staff.

How to get started

  1. Start a free trial at fin.ai.
  2. Connect your help center and knowledge sources.
  3. Test Fin’s answers on real past questions.
  4. Set up handoff rules and a few Procedures.
  5. Deploy on one channel, then expand.

Tips and tricks

  • Improve your help content; Fin’s answers depend on it.
  • Review unresolved conversations weekly to find content gaps.
  • Start with your top 20 questions and measure resolution rate.
  • Forecast costs using expected monthly resolutions times $0.99.

Who should (and shouldn't) use Intercom Fin

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Handle high volumes of repetitive support questions
  • Want pricing tied to resolutions
  • Use Intercom or another major help desk

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need a simple website chatbot on a tiny budget (consider Tidio or Chatbase)
  • Are fully committed to Zendesk's native AI (consider Zendesk AI)
  • Have very low support volume

Intercom Fin alternatives

  • Zendesk AI — AI agents, Copilot and automation built into Zendesk's customer service suite. (from $19/agent/mo paid yearly (Support Team); AI Agents from Suite Team $55)
  • Tidio (Lyro) — Live chat, ticketing and Lyro, an AI agent that resolves customer conversations. (from $24.17/mo billed annually (Starter))
  • Chatbase — Build AI agents trained on your data to handle support and sales across chat, email, WhatsApp, Slack and voice. (from $32/mo (Hobby, billed yearly); $40 monthly)
  • HubSpot (Breeze AI) — Agentic customer platform with a free CRM, Breeze AI agents for marketing, sales and service, and usage-based HubSpot Credits. (from Free; Starter currently from $7/seat/mo (limited-time offer))

FAQ

How much does Intercom Fin cost?

Fin costs $0.99 per outcome, billed as part of a $49 per month base plan that includes 50 outcomes. Additional outcomes are $0.99 each.

What counts as an outcome?

An outcome is a Resolution (the customer confirms Fin resolved the issue or doesn't ask for more help) or a Procedure handoff (Fin completes a configured Procedure that ends in a handoff).

Does Fin work with Zendesk or Salesforce?

Yes. Fin works natively in Intercom and on other help desks including Salesforce, Freshdesk, HubSpot and Zendesk.

Which channels does Fin support?

Fin supports voice, chat, email, social, Slack and more.

How do I stop Fin charges?

Pause Fin in the Deploy section. It will stop replying to customers, so no new outcomes are billed.
