What Plus AI is

Plus AI is an AI presentation maker that lives inside the tools most companies already use: Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint. You describe the presentation or upload a document, and Plus generates a full deck of native, editable slides. You can then insert single slides in a specific format, rewrite text or remix a slide’s layout—for example turning a paragraph into three columns.

Because the output is a real Slides or PowerPoint file, decks fit corporate templates, sharing and approval workflows without exporting or rebuilding.

Key features

Deck generation

Create AI presentations in Google Slides and PowerPoint from a prompt.

Slide-level control

Insert single slides, rewrite and remix layouts.

Document to deck

Upload PDF, docx or txt files and use 100K+ character prompts (Pro and up).

AI images

Generate images on Pro; advanced image models on Team.

Branding and presets

Team plans customize logos, colors and fonts, share templates and save custom AI instructions so Plus remembers company context.

AI Agent

The Max plan targets power users and teams who use the AI Agent every day.

Best use cases

  • Consultants: turn a report into a client deck.
  • Sales teams: produce branded proposals quickly.
  • Managers: create monthly business reviews.
  • Trainers: build course slides from outlines.

How to get started

  1. Sign up with Google or Microsoft at plusai.com.
  2. Install the Slides add-on or PowerPoint add-in.
  3. Describe your deck or upload a document.
  4. Review the outline, then generate slides.
  5. Remix slides and apply your template.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with an outline for better structure.
  • Remix instead of regenerate to save credits.
  • Save presets for recurring deck types.
  • Set brand templates before rolling out to a team.

Example prompt:

Build a 12-slide quarterly business review from the attached PDF: highlights, KPIs vs targets, wins, risks, customer feedback and next-quarter priorities.

Who should (and shouldn't) use Plus AI

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Turn documents into presentations
  • Keep teams on-brand

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want a standalone web deck tool (consider Gamma or Pitch)

Plus AI alternatives

  • Gamma — AI design partner that generates presentations, documents, websites and social posts from a prompt. (from $9/seat/mo (Plus))
  • Beautiful.ai — AI presentation software with auto-aligning Smart Slides that keep decks polished and on-brand. (from $14.50/mo (Pro, billed annually); $45 monthly)
  • Pitch — Collaborative presentation software with AI slide generation, analytics and deal rooms. (from $10/seat/mo billed yearly (Plus))
  • Napkin AI — Turn text into infographics, diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps and slides. (from $9/person/mo (Plus))
  • Microsoft Copilot — Microsoft's AI assistant: free for everyone, built into Microsoft 365 apps on paid plans. (from Free; Microsoft 365 Personal $9.99/mo)

FAQ

Is Plus AI free?

No free plan is offered to new users, but every plan starts with a 7-day free trial that includes 1,000 AI credits. Only new customers are eligible for the trial.

How much does Plus AI cost?

Billed annually, Basic is $10, Pro $20, Team $30 and Max $200 per user per month. Billed monthly, they're $15, $25, $40 and $240.

Does Plus AI work in PowerPoint?

Yes. Plus AI creates and edits presentations in both Google Slides and PowerPoint.

How many credits does a task use?

Tasks typically use between 40 and 170 credits depending on complexity. Basic includes 1,500 credits per month, Pro 3,000, Team 6,000 and Max unlimited.

Can Plus AI make a deck from a document?

Yes. Pro and higher support document uploads (PDF, docx, txt) and prompts of 100K+ characters.

Can I use my brand?

The Team plan lets you customize branding with your logo, colors and fonts, create team-wide templates and share presets.
