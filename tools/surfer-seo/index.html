Surfer SEO review

Plan, write, optimize and track content for Google and AI search.

⚡ Quick verdict

Surfer remains one of the best tools for turning keyword data into concrete writing guidance, and it now tracks how brands show up in ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews. Standard at $99 a month (yearly) suits most content teams; Pro adds daily AI prompt tracking and internal linking at scale. It's not cheap, and it works best alongside a broader SEO suite.

Key takeaways

  • Standard: $99/month billed yearly (saving $240/year) with 360 documents to create or optimize and 25 AI prompts tracked weekly.
  • Pro: $182/month billed yearly (saving $444/year), adding 50 daily-refreshed AI prompts, 1-click internal linking and content ideas and coverage gaps.
  • AI Search Analytics: $82/month billed yearly (saving $156/year) for AI visibility tracking with 50 prompts and 5 Brand Spaces.
  • Monthly billing costs more; Enterprise options are available through sales.
  • Includes integrations, team collaboration, a plagiarism checker and rank-drop detection.

What Surfer SEO is

Surfer is an AI SEO platform that helps teams “win AI search—not guess it.” Its core is a Content Editor that analyzes what ranks for a keyword and turns it into writing guidance—terms, structure and length—with a live score and one-click optimization. Surfer has expanded into AI visibility: tracking how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google’s AI Overviews and other AI models.

Key features

Content Editor and optimization

Create or optimize documents with real-time guidance and 1-click content optimization.

AI Search Analytics

Track AI prompts, mention gaps and brand sentiment across AI models, organized in Brand Spaces with Brand Knowledge.

Internal linking

1-click internal linking suggestions on Pro.

Content ideas and coverage gaps

Find topics you’re missing versus competitors (Pro).

Monitoring

Rank-drop detection to catch decaying content.

Team tools

Collaboration, integrations and a plagiarism checker.

Best use cases

  • Content marketers: optimize every new blog post before publishing.
  • SEO agencies: refresh client pages that dropped in rankings.
  • Brands: monitor visibility in AI answers.
  • Editors: standardize briefs for freelance writers.

How to get started

  1. Choose a plan at surferseo.com.
  2. Create a Content Editor document for a target keyword.
  3. Write or paste your draft and follow the guidance.
  4. Add internal links and check coverage gaps (Pro).
  5. Set up AI prompts to track brand visibility.

Tips and tricks

  • Don’t chase 100/100—write for readers first.
  • Refresh pages flagged by rank-drop detection.
  • Track buyer-intent prompts in AI Search Analytics.
  • Use coverage gaps to plan content clusters.

Example AI prompt to track:

“What’s the best CRM for a 10-person real estate team?”

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Clear, data-driven writing guidance
  • AI visibility tracking built in
  • Team collaboration
  • Rank-drop alerts
  • Integrations with writing tools

👎 Cons

  • No free plan
  • Expensive for solo bloggers
  • Optimizing to scores can make content formulaic
  • Not a full SEO suite

Who should (and shouldn't) use Surfer SEO

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Content teams producing many articles
  • Agencies optimizing client content
  • Brands tracking AI search presence

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need full keyword and backlink research (consider Semrush)
  • Need AI writing first (consider Writesonic or Jasper)
  • Have a tiny budget (consider Frase)

Surfer SEO alternatives

  • Semrush — SEO, AI search visibility, content, local, social and advertising tools on one data platform. (from $117.33/mo billed annually (SEO plan))
  • Frase — SEO and GEO content platform that finds topics, writes drafts in your voice, publishes and monitors pages for AI search. (from $39/mo billed yearly ($49 month to month))
  • Writesonic — Track and improve your brand's visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and more. (from $79/mo billed annually (Starter))
  • Jasper — AI platform for marketing teams: on-brand content with Canvas, marketing agents, brand IQ and AI search visibility. (from $59/seat/mo billed yearly; $69 monthly (Pro))
  • Copy.ai — GTM AI platform for sales and marketing teams, with multi-model chat and automated content and prospecting workflows. (from $24/mo (Chat, billed annually); $29 monthly)

FAQ

Does Surfer SEO have a free plan?

No free plan was listed on Surfer's pricing page during our check.

How much does Surfer SEO cost?

Billed yearly, Standard is $99/month (saving $240 a year) and Pro is $182/month (saving $444). AI Search Analytics is $82/month billed yearly. Monthly billing costs more.

What is AI Search Analytics?

It tracks your brand's visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews and other AI models, with 50 AI prompts refreshed daily, mention gap and brand sentiment, 5 Brand Spaces and Brand Knowledge.

How many articles can I optimize?

Standard includes 360 documents to create or optimize.

What does Pro add?

Everything in Standard plus 50 AI prompts tracked daily, 1-click internal linking, and content ideas and coverage gap analysis.

Surfer or Semrush?

Surfer focuses on writing and optimizing content; Semrush is a broader SEO and competitive data suite. Many teams use both. See our Semrush review.
