Microsoft Copilot review

Microsoft's AI assistant: free for everyone, built into Microsoft 365 apps on paid plans.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Microsoft Copilot → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Copilot is the obvious AI assistant if you live in Microsoft 365: it drafts in Word, analyzes in Excel, builds PowerPoint decks and summarizes Outlook and Teams. The free version is a capable chatbot, and Microsoft 365 Premium ($19.99/month) is the plan for heavy personal AI use. Businesses can add Copilot Business from $18 per user per month (annual, promotional). Outside the Microsoft ecosystem, standalone chatbots can be better value.

Key takeaways

  • Copilot is free with a signed-in Microsoft account, with usage limits on some features.
  • Microsoft 365 Personal is $9.99/month and Family $12.99/month (up to 6 people; AI for the subscription owner only).
  • Microsoft 365 Premium is $19.99/month with advanced Copilot chat and extensive usage.
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot Business starts at $18/user/month paid yearly (promotional, originally $21).
  • Bundles: Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot from $23.50 and Business Premium with Copilot $32 per user/month paid yearly.

What Microsoft Copilot is

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft’s AI assistant. On its own, at copilot.microsoft.com and in the Copilot apps, it’s a chatbot that helps you research, create images and get answers—free with a Microsoft account. With a Microsoft 365 subscription, Copilot also shows up inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, where it can draft, summarize and analyze your own documents and email.

For organizations, Microsoft 365 Copilot adds work-grounded AI with enterprise security, adoption analytics and model choice. Microsoft is also rolling out Copilot Notebooks to Copilot Chat users.

Key features

Copilot chat

Ask questions, brainstorm and research. Paid consumer plans raise usage limits, and Premium adds advanced Copilot chat with extensive usage.

Copilot in Office apps

Draft and rewrite in Word, analyze data and build formulas in Excel, turn outlines into PowerPoint decks, and summarize threads in Outlook and meetings in Teams.

Visual assistance

Get help from what’s on your screen or camera, with higher usage on Premium.

Image creation

Generate images in Copilot, with higher usage than free on paid plans.

Business controls

Business plans add identity and access management, analytics to measure adoption and impact, and LLM model choice.

Best use cases

  • Office workers: draft reports in Word and summarize long email threads.
  • Analysts: explain and chart Excel data.
  • Managers: get Teams meeting recaps and action items.
  • Households: plan, write and create images with the free app.

How to get started

  1. Go to copilot.microsoft.com and sign in with a Microsoft account.
  2. Try chat and image creation for free.
  3. If you have Microsoft 365, open Word or Excel and click Copilot.
  4. Upgrade to Premium if you hit usage limits.
  5. Businesses can start a trial of Copilot Business.

Tips and tricks

  • Reference files in Microsoft 365 (“summarize /Q3 report”).
  • Ask for structure: tables, bullet summaries, slide outlines.
  • Iterate on drafts instead of starting over.
  • Check numbers in Excel analysis before sharing.

Example prompt:

Using the attached sales spreadsheet, find the three regions with the biggest quarter-over-quarter decline, chart them, and draft a five-bullet summary for my manager.

Who should (and shouldn't) use Microsoft Copilot

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Use Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook daily
  • Want AI with enterprise data protection
  • Already pay for Microsoft 365

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Work mainly in Google Workspace (consider Gemini)
  • Need cited research answers (consider Perplexity)
  • Want a coding agent (consider GitHub Copilot)

FAQ

Is Microsoft Copilot free?

Yes. Copilot is free with a signed-in Microsoft account, with some capabilities subject to usage limits and more available through paid plans.

How much does Copilot cost for individuals?

Microsoft 365 Personal is $9.99/month, Family is $12.99/month and Premium is $19.99/month, each including Copilot with increasing usage. Microsoft also lists a Microsoft 365 Pro plan at $99.99/month (monthly billing) with the highest usage, though it showed as unavailable in our market.

How much is Copilot for business?

Microsoft 365 Copilot Business starts at $18 per user/month paid yearly (shown as reduced from $21). Business Standard with Copilot starts at $23.50 and Business Premium with Copilot is $32 per user/month paid yearly.

Does Copilot work in Word and Excel?

Yes. Paid Microsoft 365 plans add Copilot in apps such as Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams.

Can my family share Copilot?

Microsoft 365 Family covers 1 to 6 people on up to 5 devices each, but its AI features are for the subscription owner only.

What's the difference between Copilot and GitHub Copilot?

Microsoft Copilot is a general assistant and Office companion; GitHub Copilot is a coding assistant for developers. See our GitHub Copilot review.
