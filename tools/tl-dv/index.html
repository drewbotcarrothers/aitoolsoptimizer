What tl;dv is

tl;dv (“too long; didn’t view”) is an AI meeting recorder and note taker. It joins your calls, records and transcribes them, and delivers AI-generated summaries right after each meeting. tl;dv describes itself as the place where teams “capture knowledge, find answers, and automate meeting workflows,” sending meeting insights to the apps you already use.

Its strengths are post-call automation—logging notes and outcomes in your CRM, drafting follow-ups—and analysis across many meetings, such as tracking feature requests across customer calls.

Key features

Recording and transcription

Unlimited recordings and transcription in 30+ languages, even on Free.

AI notes

Instant summaries with custom templates—sales frameworks like MEDDIC, Smart AI Topics or your own format, in many languages.

CRM and follow-ups

Automatically log call notes and outcomes; AI drafts follow-up emails and next-step tasks.

Ask AI

Ask questions about your meetings.

Multi-meeting insights and reports

Analyze many calls at once and receive scheduled AI reports (Business).

Sales coaching

AI playbook monitoring and objection handling (Business).

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at tldv.io and connect your calendar.
  2. Let tl;dv join your next Google Meet, Zoom or Teams call.
  3. Review the AI notes and transcript.
  4. Connect your CRM.
  5. Upgrade for unlimited AI notes or multi-meeting reports.

Tips and tricks

  • Create note templates for each meeting type.
  • Tell participants they’re being recorded.
  • Schedule weekly reports on topics like objections.
  • Use Ask AI to find quotes quickly.

Example Ask AI question:

Across last month’s discovery calls, what were the top three objections about pricing, and which accounts raised them?

FAQ

Is tl;dv free?

Yes. The Free plan includes unlimited video recordings and unlimited transcription in 30+ languages, email and calendar integrations and mobile apps, with 10 meetings of AI notes and 10 Ask AI queries.

How much does tl;dv cost?

tl;dv shows prices in local currency. From our location the pricing page showed Pro at C$27 per seat per month billed annually and Business at C$39. We couldn't confirm USD prices, so check tldv.io. Enterprise is custom.

Does tl;dv update my CRM?

Yes. tl;dv automatically logs call notes and CRM outcomes after every call and drafts follow-up emails and next-step tasks.

Can tl;dv analyze multiple meetings?

Yes. Business includes unlimited multi-meeting AI insights and scheduled AI reports sent to your inbox, such as feature-request tracking across customer calls.

Which languages does tl;dv support?

Transcription works in 30+ languages, and AI notes can be customized—for example MEDDIC notes in Japanese.

Does tl;dv offer enterprise security?

Enterprise adds SCIM provisioning, custom SSO, privately hosted AI, organization activity logs and org-wide automations.
