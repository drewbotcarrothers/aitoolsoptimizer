Adobe Express review

All-in-one design app for social posts, flyers, videos and PDFs, with Firefly generative AI built in.

freemium⏱ 5 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Adobe Express → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Adobe Express is the easiest way into Adobe's ecosystem: a Canva-style template editor with Firefly generative AI, Adobe Stock and Photoshop/Illustrator syncing. The free plan is solid for occasional use, and Premium at $9.99 a month adds 250 generative credits, premium templates and brand kits. It is especially attractive if you already use Creative Cloud, which often includes Express Premium.

Key takeaways

  • Free plan includes core tools and a limited number of free daily AI generations; Premium costs $9.99 per month with 250 generative credits.
  • Firefly AI features are designed to be commercially safe, trained on licensed and public-domain content.
  • Premium adds 220,000+ premium templates, 200M+ Adobe Stock assets, brand kits and unlimited social scheduling.
  • Express Premium is included with Creative Cloud Standard, Creative Cloud Pro and most single-app plans.
  • K-12 schools can get Adobe Express for Education with Premium features at no cost.

What Adobe Express is

Adobe Express (formerly Adobe Spark) is Adobe’s quick-design app. It runs in the browser, on iOS and Android, and can be installed on desktop as a Progressive Web App. You start from a template or a blank canvas and create social posts, Reels and TikToks, flyers, resumes, presentations, web pages and PDFs.

What sets it apart from other template editors is its connection to the rest of Adobe: Firefly generative AI is built in, Adobe Stock and Adobe Fonts are available inside the editor, and Photoshop or Illustrator files can be added and kept in sync.

Key features

Firefly generative AI

Generate images from text, generate templates, remove or replace objects, and create text effects using Adobe’s Firefly models. Adobe says these models were trained on licensed content such as Adobe Stock and public-domain content where copyright has expired, which is why it describes them as “designed to be commercially safe.” Generative features use monthly credits.

Templates and assets

Premium unlocks more than 220,000 premium static and video templates, over 200 million Adobe Stock photos, videos and audio files (excluding Premium and editorial collections), and a large Adobe Fonts library.

Editing tools

Remove backgrounds from photos and videos, resize designs for different platforms, animate text and elements, animate characters from audio, generate QR codes and edit or convert PDFs.

Brands and collaboration

Create brand kits with logos, colors and fonts, use Creative Cloud Libraries, comment and co-edit in real time. You can set up multiple brands, though full brand setup is available on the web only.

Content Scheduler

Plan and publish posts directly to social platforms, with unlimited scheduling on Premium.

Best use cases

  • Small business marketing: a bakery creates a weekly specials post, resizes it for Instagram Stories and Facebook, and schedules both in one session.
  • Creators: a YouTuber designs thumbnails and short promo clips from templates, using generative fill to extend a background.
  • Students and teachers: a class builds posters and digital portfolios in the free K-12 edition.
  • Design teams: designers build assets in Photoshop, and marketers adapt them in Express without breaking the brand.

How to get started

  1. Go to adobe.com/express and sign up free with an Adobe, Google or Apple account.
  2. Pick a template category, such as Instagram post, flyer or video.
  3. Replace text and images, or use Generate image to create artwork from a prompt.
  4. Set up a brand kit (web) with your logo, colors and fonts.
  5. Use Resize to adapt the design to other formats (Premium).
  6. Download, share a link or schedule the post with Content Scheduler.

Tips and tricks

  • Write specific prompts: include subject, style, lighting and composition.
  • Watch your credit balance before batch generating; standard edits don’t all use credits.
  • Check your Creative Cloud plan first; you may already have Premium.
  • Link Photoshop assets so updates flow into every Express design.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Generous free plan with real AI features
  • Firefly models trained on licensed content, with commercial-use guidance
  • Syncs .psd and .ai files with Photoshop and Illustrator
  • Built-in social scheduling, PDF tools and video editing
  • Included with many Creative Cloud plans at no extra cost

👎 Cons

  • Generative credits run out quickly on heavy AI use
  • Full brand setup is web-only
  • Annual prepaid plans are non-refundable after 14 days
  • Less layout flexibility than professional tools like Illustrator or InDesign

Who should (and shouldn't) use Adobe Express

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Need social posts, flyers and short videos fast without design training
  • Already pay for Creative Cloud and want quick templates
  • Care about commercially safe AI image generation

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need professional print layout or vector illustration
  • Generate hundreds of AI images a month (a Firefly plan fits better)
  • Prefer the larger template marketplace of Canva

Adobe Express alternatives

  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))
  • Adobe Firefly — Adobe's generative AI app for images, video, audio and vectors, with partner models and commercially safe Firefly models. (from $9.99/mo (Firefly Standard))
  • Figma — Collaborative interface design platform with an AI agent, Figma Make prototyping and Dev Mode. (from $16/mo per Full seat (Professional, billed annually))
  • Looka — AI logo maker and brand kit with social assets, business cards and an AI-generated website. (from $35 one-time (Basic Logo Package))

FAQ

Is Adobe Express free?

Yes. The free plan includes templates, core editing tools and a limited number of free daily generative AI uses. Premium features such as background removal, bulk resize and premium templates require a paid plan.

How much is Adobe Express Premium?

Adobe lists Adobe Express Premium at $9.99 per month in the US, with monthly or annual prepaid billing options.

How many AI credits does Adobe Express Premium include?

Premium includes 250 Firefly generative credits per month. Free users get a limited number of free daily generations that refresh each day.

Can I use Adobe Express AI images commercially?

In general, yes. Adobe says outputs from its generative AI features can be used commercially unless a feature is marked as a beta that is for personal use only.

Is Adobe Express included with Creative Cloud?

Yes. Adobe Express Premium is included with Creative Cloud Standard and Creative Cloud Pro, and with most single-app plans except InCopy, Substance and Acrobat Standard.

Is Adobe Express free for teachers and students?

K-12 schools can use Adobe Express for Education, which gives Premium access at no cost with safe search and admin controls. Some universities also provide Premium through school accounts.
