Otter.ai review

AI meeting agent that transcribes, summarizes and answers questions about your meetings.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew

⚡ Quick verdict

Otter is one of the original AI note-takers and still one of the easiest: it joins your calls, transcribes live and delivers summaries and action items you can chat with. The free Basic plan is fine for occasional meetings (30 minutes per conversation). Pro at $8.33 a month billed annually is good value for individuals, and Business adds unlimited meetings for teams. Promotions change often, so check the live price.

Key takeaways

  • Basic: free forever with 300 meeting-transcription minutes per user per month and 30 minutes per conversation.
  • Pro: $16.99/user/month monthly or $8.33/user/month billed annually, with 1,200 minutes and 90 minutes per conversation.
  • Business: $30/user/month monthly or $19.99/user/month billed annually, with unlimited meetings and 4-hour conversations.
  • Otter was running additional promotions during our check (e.g., 50% off Pro); prices are in USD.
  • Otter AI Chat, an MCP server for AI assistants and a Zapier integration are available.

What Otter.ai is

Otter.ai is an AI meeting assistant. Its agent joins your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams calls (or records in person through the app), transcribes the conversation in real time with speaker labels, and produces a summary with action items when the meeting ends. Afterwards you can search, share or ask Otter AI Chat questions like “what did we decide about the launch date?”

Otter now also offers an MCP server, so AI assistants can pull context from your meetings, plus a Zapier integration and custom AI meeting templates and workflows.

Key features

Live transcription

Real-time transcripts with speakers, highlights and comments.

Summaries and action items

Automatic recaps with action items after each meeting.

Otter AI Chat

Query a single meeting or many; limits scale with your plan.

Custom AI workflows and templates

Tailor summaries for sales, interviews or stand-ups; advanced workflows on Business.

Integrations

Google Calendar, Zoom/Meet/Teams, Zapier and an MCP server for AI assistants.

Import and export

Import recorded audio/video; export mp3, txt, pdf, docx and srt.

Best use cases

  • Sales reps: capture call details and next steps automatically.
  • Managers: share stand-up summaries with absent teammates.
  • Researchers and journalists: transcribe interviews.
  • Students: record and search lectures.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at otter.ai and connect your calendar.
  2. Let the Otter agent auto-join your next meeting.
  3. Review the transcript and summary afterwards.
  4. Ask Otter AI Chat a follow-up question.
  5. Upgrade if you need longer meetings or more minutes.

Tips and tricks

  • Tell attendees that Otter is recording.
  • Tag speakers early to improve labels.
  • Use custom templates for recurring meeting types.
  • Connect the MCP server to use meeting context in your AI assistant.

Example AI Chat question:

Across this week’s customer calls, list every feature request, who asked for it and how urgent they said it was.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Accurate real-time transcripts
  • Free plan available
  • Ask questions across meetings with AI Chat
  • Works with Zoom, Meet and Teams
  • MCP server for AI assistants

👎 Cons

  • Free plan caps conversations at 30 minutes
  • Frequent promotions make pricing confusing
  • Bots joining calls can bother attendees
  • AI Chat queries are limited on lower plans

Who should (and shouldn't) use Otter.ai

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Want searchable transcripts of every meeting
  • Need quick summaries and action items
  • Record lectures or interviews

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want bot-free notes (consider Granola)
  • Need sales coaching analytics (consider Gong)
  • Need video highlights (consider tl;dv)

FAQ

Is Otter.ai free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever with 300 meeting-transcription minutes per user per month, up to 30 minutes per conversation, and 20 AI Chat queries per month.

How much does Otter.ai cost?

Pro is $16.99 per user per month on monthly billing or $8.33 on annual billing. Business is $30 per user per month monthly or $19.99 annually. Enterprise is custom. Otter was running promotions during our check, so the checkout price may be lower.

What's the difference between Pro and Business?

Pro includes 1,200 transcription minutes per user and 90-minute conversations; Business includes unlimited meetings and in-app recordings, 4-hour conversations, custom AI workflows and up to 25 billed users per workspace.

Does Otter work with Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes. Otter can join and transcribe meetings on Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams.

What is Otter AI Chat?

Otter AI Chat lets you ask questions about your meetings and get answers. Monthly query limits are 20 per user on Basic, 50 on Pro and 200 on Business.

Can I import recordings?

Yes. Basic allows 3 lifetime imports per user, Pro allows 10 per month, and Business includes up to 6,000 imported minutes per user per month.
