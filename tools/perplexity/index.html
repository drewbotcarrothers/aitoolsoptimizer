What Perplexity is

Perplexity is an AI-powered answer engine. Ask a question and it searches the web, reads the results and writes a concise answer with numbered citations you can click to verify. Instead of relying on a single model, Perplexity orchestrates several—including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude and NVIDIA Nemotron—and picks the best for each query.

Perplexity has grown into a broader platform: Deep Research for long reports, Perplexity Computer for agentic tasks, file and app creation, and the Comet browser with its built-in assistant.

Key features

Cited answers

Every response links its sources so you can check claims.

Deep Research

Multi-step research that compiles longer, sourced reports; Max raises the limits.

Perplexity Computer

An agent that carries out tasks; Max includes maximum Computer usage and extra credits.

Model selection and Model Council

Choose a preferred model per query on Pro and Max, or compare answers across models with Model Council.

Premium data sources

Pro adds sources such as PitchBook and Statista.

Comet

Perplexity’s browser, where advanced models power Comet Assistant on paid plans.

Best use cases

  • Students: get an overview of a topic with sources to read.
  • Analysts: gather market sizes and competitor facts quickly.
  • Journalists: fact-check claims against multiple sources.
  • Shoppers: compare products with reviews and specs.

How to get started

  1. Go to perplexity.ai and ask a question.
  2. Sign up to save threads and use more features.
  3. Click citations to verify key claims.
  4. Try Deep Research for a bigger question.
  5. Upgrade to Pro for Computer, premium data and model choice.

Tips and tricks

  • Ask follow-ups in the same thread to keep context.
  • Specify sources or dates (“only 2026 sources”).
  • Use Spaces to organize projects.
  • Compare models on important questions.

Example prompt:

Compare the three largest US e-bike brands by 2026 price range, battery capacity and warranty, in a table, citing manufacturer pages where possible.

Who should (and shouldn't) use Perplexity

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Research topics quickly with sources
  • Compare products, companies or markets
  • Replace many traditional searches

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need peer-reviewed literature search (consider Elicit or Consensus)
  • Want a writing-first assistant (consider Claude)
  • Need deep Office integration (consider Microsoft Copilot)

Perplexity alternatives

  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Google Gemini — Google's AI assistant for chat, research, image and video generation, built into Gmail, Docs and Android. (from $4.99/mo (Google AI Plus))
  • Consensus — AI search engine for peer-reviewed research that gives cited, evidence-based answers. (from $12/mo (Pro, $144 billed annually); $20 monthly)
  • Elicit — AI research assistant that searches, summarizes and extracts data from scientific papers, including systematic reviews. (from $11/user/mo (Plus, billed annually))
  • Claude — Anthropic's AI assistant for writing, analysis, research, coding and long-running work, with Claude Code included on paid plans. (from $17/mo (Pro, annual; $20 monthly); Max from $100)

FAQ

Is Perplexity free?

Yes. The Free plan includes access to top AI models with limited weekly usage and cited answers.

How much does Perplexity Pro cost?

Perplexity's pricing page lists Pro at $17 per month billed annually. Max is $167 per month billed annually. Monthly billing costs more; check the pricing page for current monthly rates.

How much is Perplexity Enterprise?

Enterprise Pro is $40 per user per month ($400 per year), and Enterprise Max is $325 per user per month ($3,250 per year). You can mix Pro and Max users in one organization.

What is Perplexity Computer?

Perplexity Computer is an agent included with Pro and Max that completes tasks for you; Max includes maximum Computer usage plus monthly and bonus credits.

Which models does Perplexity use?

Perplexity orchestrates several top models, including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude and NVIDIA Nemotron, and picks the best one for each query. Pro and Max let you choose a preferred model.

Why choose Max over Pro?

Max is designed for heavy, sustained work, with higher limits on Deep Research, asset generation and file uploads, more video creation per month, several Model Council runs and priority access to new features.
