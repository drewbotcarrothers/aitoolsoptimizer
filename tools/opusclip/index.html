OpusClip review

Turn long videos into viral-ready shorts and publish them everywhere in one click.

⚡ Quick verdict

OpusClip is the best-known tool for turning one long video into a week of shorts: ClipAnything picks highlights from any genre, AI reframe keeps subjects centered, and captions and scheduling are built in. The free plan lets you test it (clips expire after 3 days), Starter at $15 a month removes the watermark, and Pro at $14.50 a month on annual billing is the best value for regular creators.

Key takeaways

  • Free: $0 with 60 credits per month; free clips can't be exported after 3 days.
  • Starter: $15/month (monthly billing only) with 150 credits, Virality Score, animated captions in 20+ languages and no watermark.
  • Pro: $29/month or $14.50/month billed annually ($174/year) with 300 credits, rollover for 2 months, a 2-seat team workspace and 6 social connections.
  • Business: custom credits, seats, brand assets, dedicated storage and API access.
  • Annual billing saves up to 50%.

What OpusClip is

OpusClip turns long videos into short clips and publishes them to social platforms in one click. Upload or link a podcast, webinar, stream or vlog, and its AI finds the most engaging moments, cuts them into vertical clips, adds animated captions and gives each one a Virality Score so you know which to post first.

Its headline models are ClipAnything, which OpusClip says works on any genre rather than just talking-head podcasts, and AI reframe, which resizes video for any platform while keeping moving subjects centered with object tracking.

Key features

AI clipping

ClipAnything detects highlights in interviews, vlogs, gaming, sports and explainers.

Virality Score

Ranks clips by predicted performance.

Captions and reframe

Animated captions in 20+ languages and AI reframing with optional manual tracking.

Editing

Take full editing control or let AI handle it—trim, swap captions and add B-roll.

Brand templates and teams

Save fonts, colors, logos, intros and outros; collaborate in team workspaces.

Publishing and API

Schedule and post to social platforms, or integrate OpusClip with your CMS via the API.

Best use cases

  • Podcasters: turn each episode into 10+ shorts.
  • YouTubers: feed Shorts, Reels and TikTok from long videos.
  • B2B marketers: cut webinar highlights for LinkedIn.
  • Agencies: produce on-brand clips for multiple clients.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at opus.pro.
  2. Paste a YouTube link or upload a video.
  3. Choose clip length and genre, then generate.
  4. Review clips by Virality Score and edit captions.
  5. Connect social accounts and schedule posts.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with strong audio—it drives highlight detection.
  • Set a brand template before bulk exporting.
  • Review the first seconds of each clip for a hook.
  • Repost top performers in different formats.

Example clipping instruction:

Find moments where the guest shares a surprising statistic or a personal story, 30–60 seconds each, with bold yellow captions.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Works on any genre, not just podcasts
  • Fast end-to-end: clip, caption, publish
  • Smart reframing for vertical video
  • Free plan to test
  • Affordable annual Pro

👎 Cons

  • Credits limit how much video you can process
  • Free clips expire after 3 days
  • Starter is monthly only
  • AI picks still need human review

Who should (and shouldn't) use OpusClip

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Post short clips daily from long content
  • Run social for a podcast or channel
  • Want captions and scheduling in one place

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need full long-form editing (consider Descript)
  • Need generative video (consider Runway or Pika)
  • Only edit occasional phone videos (consider CapCut)

OpusClip alternatives

  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))
  • CapCut — Free video editor for desktop, mobile and web with AI captions, effects, templates and AI video generation. (from $19.99/mo (Pro); $179.99/yr)
  • invideo AI — AI video creation platform with frontier image and video models and agents, priced by credits per seat. (from $10/seat/mo billed annually (Starter); $20 monthly)
  • Runway — AI creative suite for video, images and audio with Gen-4.5 and models from other labs. (from $12/mo billed annually (Standard))
  • HeyGen — AI video platform for realistic avatars, voice cloning and video translation in 175+ languages. (from $29/mo (Creator))

FAQ

Is OpusClip free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 60 credits per month. Clips on the free plan stop being exportable after 3 days.

How much does OpusClip cost?

Starter is $15 per month (monthly billing only) with 150 credits. Pro is $29 per month, or $14.50 per month billed annually ($174 a year), with 300 credits. Business is custom.

What is a credit in OpusClip?

Credits are used to process video into clips. Plans include a monthly allowance, and on Pro unused credits roll over for 2 months; Pro users can add more credit packs.

What is ClipAnything?

ClipAnything is OpusClip's AI clipping model that finds highlight moments in any genre—vlogs, gaming, sports, interviews and explainers—not just video podcasts.

Which languages are supported?

OpusClip supports English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese and 20 more languages, with animated captions in 20+ languages.

Can OpusClip post to social media?

Yes. OpusClip can publish clips to social platforms in one click; Pro includes 6 social account connections.
