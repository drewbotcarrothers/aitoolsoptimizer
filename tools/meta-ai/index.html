Meta AI review

Meta AI is the most widely distributed free assistant, built into the apps billions already use. Heavy agent work now points to Muse, Meta's separate agent with $20 and $100 plans. Privacy-conscious users should review Meta's data settings first.

What Meta AI is

Meta AI is Meta’s AI assistant. You can use it at meta.ai, in the standalone Meta AI app, and inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger and Threads—in search bars, group chats and posts—as well as on Meta’s AI glasses.

In 2026 Meta rebuilt its AI efforts around Muse Spark, the first model in the Muse family from Meta Superintelligence Labs. Meta describes it as a natively multimodal reasoning model with support for tool use, visual chain of thought and multi-agent orchestration. Muse Spark now powers Meta AI, replacing the earlier Llama-based assistant.

Meta also launched Muse, a separate personal AI agent that can take actions, connect to other apps and services, set reminders and keep ongoing conversations.

Key features

Chat and reasoning

Ask questions, plan, write and brainstorm. Contemplating mode runs several reasoning agents in parallel for hard problems and is rolling out gradually on meta.ai.

Voice

Meta says you can talk naturally with Meta AI in the app—interrupt, switch topics or swap languages—while it generates images and pulls up recommendations from Reels, maps and more.

Live AI

Point your camera at the world and ask about what you see in real time, a feature that came to the app from Meta’s AI glasses.

Inside Meta apps

Meta is testing side chats in group conversations (a private answer grounded in what the group is discussing) and @meta.ai mentions in Threads.

Image generation

Create images from prompts in chat.

Best use cases

  • Everyday help: recipes, travel ideas and quick answers in WhatsApp.
  • Social creators: generate images for posts and stories.
  • Group planning: a quick private answer in a busy group chat.
  • On the go: ask about a landmark or a household fix with your camera.

How to get started

  1. Open meta.ai or download the Meta AI app.
  2. Sign in with a Meta account (Facebook, Instagram, email or phone).
  3. Try a text question, then voice mode.
  4. Use the camera for live AI help.
  5. If you need an action-taking agent, try Muse’s free tier.

Tips and tricks

  • Be specific about format and length.
  • Use voice for hands-free brainstorming.
  • Verify facts before relying on answers.
  • Review privacy settings in your Meta account.

Example prompt:

Plan a three-day weekend in Chicago for two people on a $600 budget, with one museum, one architecture tour and dinner ideas near the Loop.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Free with a Meta account
  • Available inside apps you already use
  • Multimodal: voice, images and camera
  • Strong new reasoning model
  • Works on Meta AI glasses

👎 Cons

  • Privacy concerns for some users
  • Fewer pro and workplace features than ChatGPT or Copilot
  • Muse agent plans are limited by region
  • Features roll out unevenly by country

Who should (and shouldn't) use Meta AI

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Want a free assistant inside WhatsApp or Instagram
  • Create quick images for social posts
  • Ask questions about what your camera sees

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need workplace integrations (consider Microsoft Copilot)
  • Need cited research answers (consider Perplexity)
  • Need strict data controls for business use

FAQ

Is Meta AI free?

Yes. Meta AI is free to use at meta.ai, in the Meta AI app and across Meta's apps. Meta's separate personal agent, Muse, is free with a usage limit and has paid plans.

What model does Meta AI use?

Meta AI now uses Muse Spark, a natively multimodal reasoning model with tool use, visual chain of thought and multi-agent orchestration, developed by Meta Superintelligence Labs.

What is Contemplating mode?

Contemplating mode orchestrates multiple agents that reason in parallel for harder problems. Meta says it is rolling out gradually on meta.ai.

How much does Muse cost?

Muse is free with a usage limit. The Power plan is $20 per month with 500M Muse tokens per week, and the Maximum plan is $100 per month with 3B tokens per week. Muse is in limited testing and not available everywhere.

Where can I use Meta AI?

At meta.ai, in the Meta AI app and in WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Messenger and Threads, plus Meta's AI glasses.

Do I need a Facebook account?

You sign in with a Meta account, which you can create with a Facebook or Instagram account or with an email address or phone number.
