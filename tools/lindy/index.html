Lindy review

AI teammate that plugs into your tools, learns how you work and handles real tasks across inbox, sales, marketing and ops.

freemium⏱ 3 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew

⚡ Quick verdict

Lindy is one of the most approachable AI agent platforms: you describe a role, connect your tools and it starts handling routines like inbox triage, pipeline nudges and weekly reports. The free $50 in credits is a generous trial, and Team at $49.99 a month pools credits across everyone. Heavy automations can burn through credits, so start with a few high-value tasks.

Key takeaways

  • Free: $50 in credits, no credit card, credits last 7 days.
  • Team: from $49.99/month for 5,000 credits, scaling up to 1,750,000 credits ($9,999.50).
  • Top up anytime at $10 per 1,000 credits; credits are pooled across the team.
  • Team includes model choice per task, computer use and live onboarding.
  • Enterprise adds HIPAA compliance with a BAA, SSO and SCIM, and audit logs.

What Lindy is

Lindy describes itself as “your AI teammate that plugs into your tools, learns how you work, and takes real work off your plate.” Instead of building workflows step by step, you “hire” Lindy for a role—paid ads, account management, client reporting, SEO, lead generation, inbox and calendar, data analysis and more—and it gets to work in Slack, email and your other apps.

Lindy’s homepage examples show what that looks like: a daily ad snapshot with ROAS flags, a pipeline pulse that drafts a nudge for a stalled deal, a weekly competitor report and month-end reconciliation that flags a duplicate charge.

Key features

Roles and routines

Assign recurring work on a schedule, such as daily reports at 8:00 AM.

Inbox and calendar

Triage email, draft replies and manage meetings.

Computer use

Lindy can operate a computer to complete tasks in apps without integrations (Team).

Model choice

Pick the model for each task.

Integrations

Connect Slack, email, calendars, CRMs and ad platforms; use the Chrome extension.

Best use cases

  • Founders: get a daily brief of pipeline, ads and inbox priorities.
  • Agencies: automate client reporting.
  • Sales: draft follow-ups for deals that go quiet.
  • Finance ops: flag anomalies during month-end close.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free and claim $50 in credits.
  2. Pick a role or describe a task.
  3. Connect the tools it needs (email, Slack, CRM).
  4. Run it a few times and review the output.
  5. Schedule it as a routine and upgrade to Team.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with one high-value routine before adding more.
  • Require approval for emails or payments at first.
  • Monitor credit usage for the first weeks.
  • Give clear success criteria in each task.

Example instruction:

Every weekday at 8 AM, summarize yesterday’s Meta and Google ad spend, flag any campaign more than 50% over target CPA, and post the summary in #marketing.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Easy setup in minutes
  • Generous free credits
  • Pooled team credits
  • Computer use for tasks without APIs
  • HIPAA option for Enterprise

👎 Cons

  • Credit consumption can be hard to predict
  • Free credits expire after 7 days
  • Complex workflows need careful setup
  • Agents still need oversight for important actions

Who should (and shouldn't) use Lindy

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Want an assistant that handles recurring busywork
  • Run a small team without an ops hire
  • Need agents connected to Slack, email and CRM

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need deterministic workflow automation at scale (consider Zapier, Make or n8n)
  • Want to build custom agent teams for enterprise (consider Relevance AI)
  • Only need a chatbot

Lindy alternatives

  • Zapier — No-code automation across 9,000+ apps, with AI agents, Copilot, MCP, Tables and Forms. (from $19.99/mo billed annually (Professional))
  • Make — Build automated workflows and AI agents visually across 3,500+ apps. (from $9/mo billed annually for 10,000 credits (Core))
  • n8n — Fair-code workflow automation with AI agents—self-host it or use n8n Cloud. (from €20/mo billed annually (Starter, 2.5K executions))
  • Relevance AI — Build an AI workforce of agents for go-to-market and operations work—no code required. (from $19/mo billed annually (Pro))
  • Manus — General-purpose AI agent that researches, builds websites and slides, and runs scheduled tasks using credits. (from $20/mo for 4,000 credits (Pro))

FAQ

Is Lindy free?

Lindy offers $50 in free credits with no credit card required. The free credits last 7 days.

How much does Lindy cost?

The Team plan starts at $49.99 per month for 5,000 credits, with larger tiers such as 15,000 credits for $99.99 and 45,000 for $299.99. Top-ups cost $10 per 1,000 credits.

What can Lindy do?

Lindy can triage email, manage calendars, run daily or weekly routines like ad and pipeline reports, draft follow-ups, research competitors and use a computer to complete tasks.

Are credits shared across a team?

Yes. On Team, credits are pooled across your whole team.

Is Lindy HIPAA compliant?

The Enterprise plan includes HIPAA compliance with a signed BAA, plus SSO, SCIM and audit logs.
