Motion review

AI that plans your day and your team's projects, automatically scheduling tasks on the calendar.

paid⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew

⚡ Quick verdict

Motion is the most ambitious AI planner: it doesn't just list tasks, it schedules them into your calendar and re-plans when things change, then extends that to team projects, docs and meetings. It's paid only (after a free trial), with Pro AI at $19 per seat a month and Business AI at $29. It works best if you commit to managing all your work in it.

Key takeaways

  • No free plan, but a free trial you can cancel anytime.
  • Pro AI: $19 per seat/month with 7,500 AI credits per seat per month, unlimited storage and mobile/desktop apps.
  • Business AI: $29 per seat/month with 15,000 credits, central billing and priority support.
  • Paying annually saves 33% according to Motion's pricing page.
  • AI projects, tasks, calendar, meetings, docs, notes, reports and workflows in one app.

What Motion is

Motion is an AI productivity platform that bills itself as an all-in-one workspace for “AI Projects, AI Tasks, AI Calendar, AI Meetings, AI Docs, AI Notes, AI Reports, AI Workflows, and more.” Its core idea: a due date isn’t a plan. Motion takes everything on your plate and schedules it into real time slots on your calendar, then re-plans automatically when meetings move or priorities change.

For teams, Motion builds an optimal plan for every member based on tasks, deadlines, assignees and dependencies, and keeps statuses and reports up to date. Motion claims projects get done 32% faster by eliminating idle time between tasks.

Key features

AI calendar and prioritization

Motion’s AI detects your most urgent and important tasks and schedules them around your meetings, warning you when something is at risk.

AI project builder

Describe a project and add docs; Motion builds the whole project with tasks, deadlines, assignees and stages. Motion says it’s over 90% accurate out of the box, needing only a review.

Task capture

Forward emails from Gmail, Outlook or iCloud, convert Zoom, Meet or Teams meetings and Slack or Teams messages into tasks, or use Siri.

Team visibility

Automatic status updates, next-step assignment, delay flags and project reports.

Collaboration

Comment and ask questions inside projects, tasks and docs so discussion stays with the work.

Best use cases

  • Consultants and freelancers: fit client work around meetings.

  • Founders: turn meeting notes into assigned tasks.

How to get started

  1. Start a free trial at usemotion.com.
  2. Connect your Google or Outlook calendar.
  3. Set working hours and add tasks with durations and deadlines.
  4. Let Motion schedule your day and review the plan.
  5. Invite teammates and create a project from a description.

Tips and tricks

  • Always add durations—Motion schedules by time.
  • Mark hard deadlines so they’re protected.
  • Forward emails instead of retyping tasks.
  • Review the plan each morning for a minute.

Example project prompt:

Plan the launch of our new pricing page over four weeks: copywriting, design, legal review, engineering and an email announcement, assigned to Sam, Priya and Leo with a June 30 deadline.

Who should (and shouldn't) use Motion

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Juggle many tasks and meetings
  • Want deadlines protected automatically
  • Run small-team projects without a PM

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Just need focus-time blocking on Google Calendar (consider Reclaim)
  • Want a free notes workspace (consider Notion)
  • Prefer manual planning

Motion alternatives

  • Reclaim.ai — AI scheduling that defends focus time, habits and meetings on your Google or Outlook calendar. (from $10/seat/mo (Starter))
  • ClickUp Brain — ClickUp's built-in company AI that knows your tasks, docs, chat and connected apps and can run work with agents. (from $9/user/mo (Brain² AI))
  • Notion AI — AI built into your Notion workspace: Notion Agent, Custom Agents, AI Meeting Notes and enterprise search. (from $20/seat/mo (Business, includes full Notion AI))
  • Microsoft Copilot — Microsoft's AI assistant: free for everyone, built into Microsoft 365 apps on paid plans. (from Free; Microsoft 365 Personal $9.99/mo)
  • Read AI — AI meeting notes, enterprise search and an AI assistant, Ada, across meetings, email and messages. (from $15/user/mo billed annually (Pro))

FAQ

Is Motion free?

No. Motion doesn't have a free plan, but you can start a free trial and cancel anytime.

How much does Motion cost?

Pro AI is listed at $19 per seat per month with 7,500 AI credits per seat, and Business AI at $29 per seat per month with 15,000 credits. Motion says paying annually saves 33%.

What are Motion AI credits?

AI credits power Motion's AI features. Plans include a monthly allowance per seat; Motion lists additional credit pricing of 25 cents per 100 credits on Pro AI and 19 cents per 100 on Business AI.

How does Motion schedule tasks?

Motion looks at your projects and tasks—their priorities, dependencies, deadlines and durations—and builds a plan on your calendar. When a task is at risk of missing its deadline, it warns you.

Can Motion create tasks from email?

Yes. You can forward Gmail, Outlook or iCloud emails, turn Zoom, Meet or Teams meetings and Slack or Teams messages into tasks, or add tasks with Siri.

Motion or Reclaim?

Motion is a full planning and project platform; Reclaim focuses on smart scheduling inside Google or Outlook calendars and has a free plan. See our Reclaim review.
