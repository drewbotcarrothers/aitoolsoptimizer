Kling AI review

AI video and image generator from Kuaishou, with Kling VIDEO 3.0, a creative studio and an API.

freemium⏱ 3 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Kling AI → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Kling AI offers some of the best value in AI video: Pro's 3,000 monthly credits cover roughly 150 720p videos, and quality from Kling VIDEO 3.0 is competitive with pricier tools. Pricing has quirks—first-month discounts, then higher renewal prices—so read the plan page carefully. It's a strong pick for frequent creators; occasional users can start on the free Basic tier.

Key takeaways

  • Basic is free forever, with no monthly credits.
  • Standard: 660 credits/month, $6.99 first month then $8.80/month ($10 shown).
  • Pro: 3,000 credits, $25.99 first month then $32.56/month ($37 shown); about 150 720p videos.
  • Premier: 8,000 credits ($64.99 then $80.96); Ultra: 26,000 credits ($127.99 then $159.99).
  • Kling VIDEO 3.0 at 4K costs about 30 credits per second, per Kling's guide.

What Kling AI is

Kling AI is a generative video and image platform developed by Kuaishou. It turns text prompts and images into short cinematic videos and images, and has become one of the most popular AI video tools thanks to strong motion quality and competitive pricing. Its global site has moved to kling.ai.

The latest model family, Kling VIDEO 3.0, supports high-resolution output up to 4K. Kling offers a creative studio for creators and a developer platform for API access.

Key features

Text and image to video

Generate clips from prompts or animate reference images.

Kling VIDEO 3.0

The latest video model, with credit costs scaling by length and resolution.

Image generation

Create images alongside video in the same credit system.

Best use cases

  • Social creators: produce eye-catching short clips for Reels and TikTok.
  • Marketers: animate product photos into short ads.
  • Filmmakers: prototype scenes and camera moves.
  • Developers: add video generation through the API.

How to get started

  1. Sign up at kling.ai.
  2. Start on Basic to explore the interface.
  3. Choose Standard or Pro for monthly credits.
  4. Test prompts at 720p before rendering in higher resolutions.
  5. Track credit costs per generation.

Tips and tricks

  • Use good source images; better inputs save credits.
  • Test at lower resolution before 4K.
  • Describe camera movement clearly (pan, dolly, orbit).
  • Note renewal prices before subscribing.

Example prompt:

A red vintage convertible driving along a coastal cliff road at golden hour, drone shot slowly following from behind, waves crashing below, cinematic color grading

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Strong video quality for the price
  • Free Basic plan
  • Large credit allowances on higher plans
  • Developer API available
  • Low cost per credit on bigger plans

👎 Cons

  • First-month discounts make pricing confusing
  • Renewal prices are higher than the intro price
  • 4K video consumes credits quickly
  • Free plan has no monthly credits

Who should (and shouldn't) use Kling AI

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Make AI video regularly
  • Want good value per clip
  • Build video features via API

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need avatar presenters (consider HeyGen or Synthesia)
  • Want deep editing tools (consider Runway or Descript)
  • Prefer simple flat pricing

Kling AI alternatives

  • Runway — AI creative suite for video, images and audio with Gen-4.5 and models from other labs. (from $12/mo billed annually (Standard))
  • Google Flow — Google's AI creative studio for cinematic video scenes and images, powered by Veo, Gemini Omni Flash and Nano Banana. (from Free (50 credits/day); Google AI Plus $4.99/mo)
  • Luma AI — Creative AI platform with Ray 3.2 video, Uni-1 brand-aware image model and Luma Agents for creative teams. (from $30/mo (Plus) or $300/yr)
  • Pika — An AI creative platform with 25+ apps for video, image and sound, powered by leading gen-media models. (from $10/mo (Starter, 900 credits))
  • Higgsfield — Creator-focused AI video and image platform with many models, effects, ad tools and team workspaces. (from See higgsfield.ai/pricing (prices not verifiable at time of review))

FAQ

Is Kling AI free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free forever but includes no monthly credits.

How much does Kling AI cost?

Standard is $6.99 for the first month then $8.80 per month, Pro is $25.99 then $32.56, Premier is $64.99 then $80.96, and Ultra is $127.99 then $159.99. Kling shows list prices of $10, $37, $92 and $180.

How many videos do Kling credits buy?

Kling estimates 660 credits ≈ 33 720p videos (Standard), 3,000 ≈ 150 (Pro), 8,000 ≈ 400 (Premier) and 26,000 ≈ 1,300 (Ultra).

How many credits does Kling VIDEO 3.0 use?

Kling's guide uses 30 credits per second as a reference for 4K Kling VIDEO 3.0 output.

Does Kling have an API?

Yes. Kling offers a developer platform for API access.
