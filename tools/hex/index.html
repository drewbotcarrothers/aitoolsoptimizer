Hex review

Analytics platform for humans and agents: SQL and Python notebooks, the Hex agent, interactive data apps and self-serve exploration.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Hex → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Hex is one of the best modern analytics workspaces: it blends SQL, Python and no-code cells with a capable AI agent, and turns analyses into polished, interactive apps that business users can explore. The free Community plan is fine for learning; Professional at $36 per editor suits solo analysts, and Team at $75 adds the scheduling, sharing and unlimited apps most companies need.

Key takeaways

  • Community is free with a Hex agent trial, any data source, all cell types and small compute.
  • Professional: $36 per editor/month with the Hex agent, up to 5 published apps and medium compute.
  • Team: $75 per editor/month with unlimited apps, scheduled runs, alerts and a 14-day free trial.
  • Enterprise adds SSO, audit logs, BYOK, HIPAA and embedded analytics add-ons.
  • Medium compute is included on paid plans; larger and GPU profiles cost $0.32-$4.06 per hour.

What Hex is

Hex describes itself as “the analytics platform for humans and agents.” It’s a collaborative workspace where analysts and data scientists write SQL and Python (or use no-code cells), ask the Hex agent to do the heavy lifting, and publish results as interactive data apps and dashboards.

Business users can then explore data themselves, asking questions in plain English grounded in semantic models, while data teams keep governance and observability. Hex highlights customers like LangChain using it to let their whole team self-serve data.

Key features

Notebooks with every cell type

Mix SQL, Python, charts and no-code cells in one project, connected to any data source.

Hex agent

Ask questions, generate queries, scaffold apps and debug SQL; included from Professional up.

Data apps

Turn analyses into interactive apps and dashboards with visual exploration and drill-down (Team).

Semantic models and Context Studio

Ground AI answers in trusted definitions.

Scheduling and alerts

Scheduled runs, alerts and shared components on Team and Enterprise.

Governance

Audit logs, OIDC SSO, BYOK, HIPAA and single-tenant options on Enterprise.

Best use cases

  • Revenue reporting: track subscription revenue with interactive dashboards.
  • Forecasting: build next-quarter projections in Python.
  • Self-serve: let sales and marketing ask questions of governed data.
  • Ad-hoc analysis: answer one-off questions quickly with the agent.

How to get started

  1. Sign up for the free Community plan at hex.tech.
  2. Connect your warehouse or upload a dataset.
  3. Ask the agent a question or write your first SQL cell.
  4. Add a chart and publish it as an app.
  5. Start a Team trial to try schedules and sharing.

Tips and tricks

  • Define semantic models so AI answers stay consistent.
  • Use parameters to make apps interactive for stakeholders.
  • Stick to included compute unless jobs need more memory.
  • Review generated SQL before sharing numbers.

Example agent prompt:

Using the orders table, show monthly revenue by product line for the last 12 months, flag any month with a drop greater than 10%, and build a line chart.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Combines notebooks, BI and AI in one tool
  • Free plan for learning
  • Beautiful, interactive published apps
  • Connects to any data source
  • Enterprise-grade security options

👎 Cons

  • Per-editor pricing adds up for large teams
  • Advanced compute costs extra
  • Professional caps published apps at 5
  • Requires some SQL or Python skills to get the most out of it

Who should (and shouldn't) use Hex

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Work on a data team with SQL or Python skills
  • Want to share interactive analyses with stakeholders
  • Need AI-assisted self-serve analytics

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need a quick spreadsheet-style AI analyst (consider Julius AI)
  • Want simple charts for occasional use
  • Have no data warehouse or database

Hex alternatives

  • Julius AI — AI workspace to analyze data, research, and create charts, presentations, reports, websites, images and video. (from $16/mo billed annually (Plus); $20 monthly)
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Notion AI — AI built into your Notion workspace: Notion Agent, Custom Agents, AI Meeting Notes and enterprise search. (from $20/seat/mo (Business, includes full Notion AI))
  • Claude — Anthropic's AI assistant for writing, analysis, research, coding and long-running work, with Claude Code included on paid plans. (from $17/mo (Pro, annual; $20 monthly); Max from $100)

FAQ

Is Hex free?

Yes. The Community plan is free for hobbyists and includes a Hex agent trial, connections to any data source, all cell types and small compute.

How much does Hex cost?

Professional is $36 per editor per month and Team is $75 per editor per month (with a 14-day free trial). Enterprise is custom.

What is the Hex agent?

The Hex agent is Hex's AI assistant that can answer data questions, write SQL and Python, scaffold apps and help debug queries. It's included on Professional and above.

How does Hex compute pricing work?

Medium compute is included on paid plans. Team and Enterprise can opt into larger profiles billed per minute, from $0.32 per hour (Large) up to $4.06 per hour for an A10G GPU.

Does Hex support Python?

Yes. Hex notebooks support SQL, Python and no-code cells together.
