Midjourney review

High-quality artistic AI image generation.

Overview

Leading AI image generator known for artistic, high-quality images (V8).

Key features

  • V8/V8.1 models
  • Style references
  • Moodboards
  • HD mode
  • Web editor

Best use cases

  • Concept art
  • Marketing visuals
  • Illustrations
  • Mood boards

Pricing

Paid. From $10 (Basic); $8/mo annual (USD).

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Outstanding aesthetic quality
  • Strong style control
  • Active community

👎 Cons

  • No free tier
  • Learning curve for parameters

Midjourney alternatives

  • ChatGPT Images (formerly DALL·E) — OpenAI's image generation inside ChatGPT, now powered by GPT Image models after DALL·E's retirement. (from Included with ChatGPT Free (limited), Go $8, Plus $20)
  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))

Sources

This is an editorial review based on research of official Midjourney pages and documentation, not hands-on benchmark testing. Prices are in USD and were checked on October 15, 2026; vendors change plans often, so confirm on the official pricing page before buying.
