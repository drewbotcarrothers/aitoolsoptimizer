Suno review

Make full songs from a prompt, then edit them in a browser-based DAW.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Suno → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Suno is the most popular AI song generator for a reason: a short prompt can produce a full track with vocals, and its newest model sounds fuller and more detailed. Suno Studio and stem export make it useful for real musicians, not just novelty songs. The free plan is fun but has no commercial rights or downloads; Pro adds commercial use for about the price of a streaming subscription.

Key takeaways

  • Free: $0 with 50 credits per day, free models, standard features and no commercial rights or monthly downloads.
  • Pro: $11/month billed monthly (the page shows $8/month with yearly billing) with 2,500 credits, 20 song downloads, commercial rights and Pro features.
  • Premier: $35/month billed monthly with 10,000 credits and every feature unlocked; yearly billing saves up to 20%.
  • Pro and Premier can record and create with your own voice and train custom models.
  • Suno Studio is a browser-based DAW for arranging, editing and mixing songs.

What Suno is

Suno makes AI music creation accessible to everyone: whether you have a melody in your head, lyrics you’ve written or just a feeling, you describe it and Suno generates a complete song—instruments, arrangement and vocals. Suno says its newest model is its most capable yet, “with fuller vocals, tighter arrangements and more of the detail you asked for.”

Suno has also grown into a production tool, with Suno Studio, voice recording, stems, remixing and custom models.

Key features

Song generation

Create full tracks from a prompt or your own lyrics.

Suno Studio

A browser-based DAW to arrange, edit and mix every part of a song.

Your voice

Record a few seconds of your voice and hear it sing anything you write (Pro and Premier).

Stems

Split songs into clean, time-aligned stems for your own DAW.

Remix and restyle

Change a song’s style, rewrite lyrics or rebuild it around your recording.

Custom models

Train a model on your recordings so every song starts from your sound.

Best use cases

  • YouTubers and podcasters: original intro music and beds.
  • Songwriters: turn lyrics into a demo.
  • Producers: generate stems to rework in a DAW.
  • Marketers: quick jingles for campaigns (with commercial rights).

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at suno.com.
  2. Describe a style, mood and topic, or paste lyrics.
  3. Generate a couple of versions and pick a favorite.
  4. Open it in Studio to edit sections.
  5. Upgrade to Pro for downloads and commercial use.

Tips and tricks

  • Name genre, tempo and instruments in the prompt.
  • Write your own lyrics for more control over the song.
  • Iterate on sections rather than regenerating whole songs.
  • Export stems for final mixing.

Example prompt:

Upbeat indie-pop song about a summer road trip along the coast, female vocals, jangly guitars, 120 BPM, catchy singalong chorus.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Full songs with vocals in minutes
  • Browser DAW for real editing
  • Stems export to your own DAW
  • Affordable Pro tier
  • Free daily credits

👎 Cons

  • Free plan has no commercial rights
  • Download limits on Pro
  • Quality varies by prompt and genre
  • Copyright questions around AI music remain

Who should (and shouldn't) use Suno

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Create songs from lyrics or ideas
  • Make original background music
  • Musicians sketching demos

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need voiceovers (consider ElevenLabs or Murf)
  • Want a different AI music engine (consider Udio)
  • Need precise MIDI composition

Suno alternatives

  • Udio — Create, extend, remix and edit AI songs with Voices and Styles. (from $10/mo (Standard) or $8/mo billed annually)
  • ElevenLabs — Lifelike AI voices for text to speech, voice cloning, dubbing, music, sound effects and conversational voice agents. (from $6/mo (Starter); Creator $22)
  • Murf AI — Studio-quality AI voiceovers, a fast text-to-speech API, dubbing and voice agents. (from API from $0.01 per 1,000 characters; Studio plans on pricing page)
  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))
  • Speechify — Listen to anything with text to speech, type by talking, and ask a voice AI assistant. (from $29/mo billed monthly (Premium))

FAQ

Is Suno free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 50 credits per day and access to free models, but no commercial rights, no monthly song downloads and no add-on credit purchases.

How much does Suno cost?

During our check, Suno's pricing page showed Pro at $11/month and Premier at $35/month, with yearly billing saving up to 20% (Pro was shown at $8/month yearly). Taxes are calculated at checkout.

Can I use Suno songs commercially?

Commercial use rights are included on Pro and Premier, not on the Free plan.

What is Suno Studio?

Suno Studio is a browser-based DAW for arranging, editing and mixing every part of a song.

Can Suno sing in my voice?

Yes. On Pro and Premier you can record a few seconds of your voice and have it sing anything you write, and you can train a custom model on your own recordings.

Can I export stems?

Yes. Suno can pull songs apart into clean, time-aligned stems you can bring into your own DAW; Pro includes two stem separation types.
