ClickUp Brain review

ClickUp's built-in company AI that knows your tasks, docs, chat and connected apps and can run work with agents.

⚡ Quick verdict

ClickUp Brain is most valuable for teams that already live in ClickUp: because it sees your tasks, docs, chat and connected apps, it can answer 'what's blocking the launch?' without you explaining context. At $9 per user per month it bundles access to several premium models. Teams not on ClickUp should judge ClickUp itself first, since Brain's value comes from that context.

Key takeaways

  • Brain² AI starts at $9 per user per month, with unlimited Brain² conversations and 1,500 AI Super Credits.
  • Everything AI costs $28 per user per month and adds Notetaker, AI Fields, Automations and Super Agents.
  • ClickUp plans: Free Forever, Unlimited $7 and Business $12 per user per month billed yearly ($10 and $19 monthly).
  • Switch between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other models in one subscription.
  • ClickUp says your data is never used to train third-party AI models.

What ClickUp Brain is

ClickUp is an all-in-one work platform for tasks, docs, chat, whiteboards and dashboards. ClickUp Brain, now branded Brain², is the AI built into it. Because it lives inside your workspace, it can see tasks, docs, chat, calendar, email and connected apps, so you can ask “What did the design team decide about the onboarding flow?” or “Write this week’s status update for Project Atlas” without pasting context.

ClickUp describes Brain² as company AI that “self-improves, routes to the best models, and coworks to complete your work.” It routes requests to models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, and you can pick a model yourself.

Key features

Contextual answers

Ask questions about anything in your workspace and get answers that respect permissions: Brain² only surfaces information you already have access to.

Multiple models, one subscription

Switch between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others with a click; each gets full workspace context.

Persistent memory

Brain² learns your role, workflows and tone, and you can view, edit or delete preferences. You can import memories from ChatGPT or Claude.

AI Fields, Automations and Super Agents

Fill custom fields with AI, trigger AI automations and deploy Super Agents that handle multi-step work. These use AI Super Credits.

AI Notetaker

Included in Everything AI, it records and summarizes meetings into tasks and docs.

Best use cases

  • Status reporting: a project manager asks Brain for a weekly summary of progress, blockers and overdue tasks.
  • Catching up: after vacation, ask “What changed on the Q3 launch while I was out?”
  • Doc drafting: turn a meeting’s notes into a project brief with tasks assigned.
  • Automation: an AI field categorizes incoming support tasks by urgency.

How to get started

  1. Sign up for ClickUp (Free Forever) or open your existing workspace.
  2. Open Brain from the sidebar and try it on a real project question.
  3. Set your preferences so Brain² learns your tone and role.
  4. Add Brain² AI to your plan from billing when ready.
  5. Connect apps such as calendar and email for better context.
  6. Try one AI automation or Super Agent on a repetitive workflow.

Tips and tricks

  • Ask about specific spaces or lists to get tighter answers.
  • Use different models for different jobs, such as one for writing and one for analysis.
  • Monitor Super Credits if you build many automations.
  • Keep tasks and docs updated; Brain is only as current as your workspace.

Example prompt:

Summarize the Website Redesign list: what shipped this week, what’s blocked and who owns each blocker. Format as a short Slack update with bullet points.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Understands your actual tasks, docs and chats
  • Access to several frontier models in one subscription
  • Brain² conversations don't consume credits
  • Strong privacy commitments and compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA)
  • Conversations are private from admins

👎 Cons

  • Only worthwhile if your team uses ClickUp
  • Advanced features use AI Super Credits, which can run out
  • Full AI stack costs $28 per user per month
  • ClickUp itself has a learning curve

Who should (and shouldn't) use ClickUp Brain

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Already manage projects in ClickUp
  • Want status reports and summaries without chasing people
  • Want one AI subscription that covers several models

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Use another project tool (consider Notion AI or Motion)
  • Only need a standalone chatbot
  • Are a solo user who doesn't need team context

ClickUp Brain alternatives

  • Notion AI — AI built into your Notion workspace: Notion Agent, Custom Agents, AI Meeting Notes and enterprise search. (from $20/seat/mo (Business, includes full Notion AI))
  • Motion — AI that plans your day and your team's projects, automatically scheduling tasks on the calendar. (from $19/seat/mo (Pro AI))
  • Reclaim.ai — AI scheduling that defends focus time, habits and meetings on your Google or Outlook calendar. (from $10/seat/mo (Starter))
  • Microsoft Copilot — Microsoft's AI assistant: free for everyone, built into Microsoft 365 apps on paid plans. (from Free; Microsoft 365 Personal $9.99/mo)

FAQ

How much does ClickUp Brain cost?

ClickUp says Brain² AI starts at $9 per user per month and includes unlimited Brain² conversations, premium models and 1,500 AI Super Credits. The Everything AI plan with Notetaker, AI Fields, Automations and Super Agents costs $28 per user per month.

Is ClickUp Brain free?

ClickUp's Free Forever plan users can try Brain² before committing, but ongoing access is a paid add-on.

What are AI Super Credits?

AI Super Credits (1,500 per month included) are used for advanced capabilities such as Super Agents, AI Automations and AI-powered fields. Regular Brain² conversations don't use credits.

Does ClickUp use my data to train AI?

ClickUp says your data is never used to train third-party AI models, with zero-retention policies for AI subprocessors, and Brain² only surfaces information you already have permission to see.

Can I choose the AI model in ClickUp Brain?

Yes. You can switch between models such as Claude, GPT and Gemini, and each gets full access to your workspace context.
