What Leonardo.Ai is

Leonardo.Ai is a generative AI platform for creating images and videos. Originally popular with game developers and concept artists, it now serves designers, marketers and content creators with a mix of high-quality models, fine control and production features. Leonardo is owned by Canva.

Key strengths include personal AI models you can train on your own images for consistent characters or styles, a Realtime Canvas for sketch-to-image editing, and generous relaxed-mode generation on higher plans.

Key features

Image generation

Generate in many styles with control over composition and quality.

Video generation

Create short videos from images or prompts; Ultimate includes unlimited relaxed video for select models.

Personal AI models

Train custom models (up to 50 on Ultimate).

Realtime Canvas

Draw and see AI-enhanced results instantly.

Teams

Shared token pools, private team generations and unlimited collections.

Best use cases

  • Game studios: generate consistent character and environment concepts.
  • Marketers: produce campaign imagery quickly.
  • Ecommerce: create lifestyle product shots.
  • Creators: make short animated clips for social.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at leonardo.ai.
  2. Generate images with a preset style.
  3. Try Realtime Canvas with a rough sketch.
  4. Train a personal model on 10-20 consistent images.
  5. Upgrade to Premium for unlimited relaxed generation.

Tips and tricks

  • Use relaxed mode for drafts to save fast tokens.
  • Train models on consistent images (same lighting and style).
  • Bank tokens in light months for busy ones.
  • Reuse seeds and settings for consistent series.

Example prompt:

Isometric fantasy tavern interior, warm candlelight, wooden beams, adventurers at tables, detailed game art style, soft shadows

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Fair pricing with a useful free tier
  • Unlimited relaxed generation on Premium and up
  • Train personal models for consistent style
  • Unused tokens can bank up to 3x the monthly allowance
  • Team plans with private generations

👎 Cons

  • Token system takes time to understand
  • Relaxed mode is slower
  • Best models consume tokens quickly
  • Prices exclude tax

Who should (and shouldn't) use Leonardo.Ai

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Create game, fantasy or concept art
  • Want to train custom style models
  • Work in a small creative team

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need precise text in images (consider Ideogram)
  • Want simple design templates (consider Canva)
  • Need enterprise IP indemnity (consider Adobe Firefly)

FAQ

Is Leonardo.Ai free?

Yes. The free plan is designed for casual creators who want to explore AI art.

How much does Leonardo.Ai cost?

On monthly billing, Essential is $12, Premium is $30 and Ultimate is $60 per month, excluding tax.

What are fast tokens?

Fast tokens pay for generations at full speed. Essential includes 8,500 per month, Premium 25,000 and Ultimate 60,000, and unused tokens can accumulate in a token bank.

Does Leonardo offer unlimited generations?

Premium includes unlimited image generation for select models at a relaxed pace, and Ultimate adds unlimited relaxed video generation for select models.

Does Leonardo have team plans?

Yes. Team Starter is $24 per seat ($72 per month) and Growth is $48 per seat ($144 per month), with shared tokens and private team generations.
