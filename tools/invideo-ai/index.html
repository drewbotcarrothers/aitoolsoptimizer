What invideo AI is

invideo AI is a video creation platform that turns a prompt into a finished video draft and gives you access to leading image and video models in one place. Recent plans highlight models like Nano Banana Pro for images and Seedance 2 for video, plus “frontier agents” that handle more of the creative work.

It’s built for creators, marketers and small teams who want to publish videos quickly without traditional editing skills.

Key features

Prompt to video

Describe your video and let invideo generate a complete draft you can refine.

Image and video models

Use popular frontier models under one credit balance.

Agents

Plus and higher include access to frontier agents (Lite, Pro and Ultra tiers).

Seats and collaboration

Per-seat plans, with free guest seats on Plus.

Best use cases

  • YouTubers: produce explainer or listicle videos from a topic.
  • Marketers: generate product promos and social ads.
  • Small businesses: create videos without hiring an editor.
  • Designers: generate images for thumbnails and posts.

How to get started

  1. Sign up at invideo.io.
  2. Describe your video: topic, length, audience and platform.
  3. Review the generated draft and edit with prompts.
  4. Export and check credit usage.
  5. Choose annual billing if you’ll use it regularly.

Tips and tricks

  • Be specific about length and format (e.g., 60-second vertical).
  • Draft with cheaper models, then use premium models for key shots.
  • Edit the script before generating visuals.
  • Check rights and licensing for commercial use.

Example prompt:

Create a 60-second vertical video explaining three ways to save on groceries, upbeat tone, female voiceover, captions on screen, ending with a call to follow for more tips.

Who should (and shouldn't) use invideo AI

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Create social and YouTube videos regularly
  • Want images and video in one subscription
  • Have a small team sharing work

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need avatar presenter videos (consider HeyGen or Synthesia)
  • Edit long filmed footage (consider Descript or CapCut)
  • Need cinematic control over every shot (consider Runway)

invideo AI alternatives

  • HeyGen — AI video platform for realistic avatars, voice cloning and video translation in 175+ languages. (from $29/mo (Creator))
  • Synthesia — Create training and business videos with AI avatars, dubbing, roleplays and surveys. (from $29/mo billed monthly (Starter))
  • Runway — AI creative suite for video, images and audio with Gen-4.5 and models from other labs. (from $12/mo billed annually (Standard))
  • CapCut — Free video editor for desktop, mobile and web with AI captions, effects, templates and AI video generation. (from $19.99/mo (Pro); $179.99/yr)
  • OpusClip — Turn long videos into viral-ready shorts and publish them everywhere in one click. (from $15/mo (Starter, monthly only))

FAQ

How much does invideo AI cost?

Starter is $20 per seat per month or $10 billed annually, Plus is $60 or $30 annually, and Max is $150 or $75 annually.

How many videos can I make?

It depends on the model. invideo says Starter's 400 monthly credits cover about 160 Nano Banana Pro generations or 15 Seedance 2 fast videos, and Plus's 2,000 credits about 800 Nano Banana Pro generations or 52 Seedance 2 videos.

Does invideo AI offer team seats?

Yes. Plans are priced per seat, and Plus includes 10 free guest seats with 190 credits each. Starter has no guest seats.

Which AI models does invideo use?

invideo offers frontier models such as Nano Banana Pro for images and Seedance 2 for video, alongside its own agents.

Is there a discount?

Annual billing is half the monthly price, and invideo frequently runs extra offers for new sign-ups.
