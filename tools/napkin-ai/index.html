Napkin AI review

Turn text into infographics, diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps and slides.

⚡ Quick verdict

Napkin is one of the fastest ways to turn a paragraph into a clean diagram: paste text, pick from suggested visuals and tweak colors and shapes. The free plan's 500 weekly credits cover light use, Plus at $9 per person a month adds unlimited PPT and SVG export, and Pro adds custom branding. It complements, rather than replaces, a full design or presentation tool.

Key takeaways

  • Free: $0 forever with 500 AI credits per week and unlimited visual editing.
  • Plus: $9 per person/month with 10,000 AI credits per month and unlimited PPT and SVG export.
  • Pro: $22 per person/month with 30,000 credits, exclusive designs, unlimited custom branding, your own fonts and credit top-ups.
  • Yearly billing saves 25%; roughly 1 credit is charged per word selected for generation.
  • New Slides (beta), Mind Maps, Flowcharts and an API extend the core text-to-visual tool.

What Napkin AI is

Napkin AI is a visual communication platform: you write or paste text, and Napkin generates the most relevant visuals for it—infographics, diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps and data charts. You pick the one that best expresses your idea, then reshape every element, change colors to match your brand and export it.

Napkin has expanded beyond single visuals with Slides (beta) for presentation-ready decks, dedicated Mind Maps and Flowcharts, and an API for automating visual generation at scale. It works alongside tools like Google Docs, Microsoft Word, Notion and Confluence.

Key features

Text to visual

Highlight text and Napkin proposes multiple visual layouts.

Full editing

Take visuals apart and change shapes, icons, text and colors freely.

Slides, mind maps and flowcharts

Turn ideas into decks, structured mind maps or clear process flows.

Branding

Match your palette on any plan; Pro adds unlimited custom branding and font uploads.

Export and API

Export to PPT, PNG, PDF or SVG, or generate visuals programmatically via the API.

Best use cases

  • Bloggers and LinkedIn writers: add a diagram to every post.
  • Consultants: visualize frameworks for decks.
  • Teachers: turn lesson notes into mind maps.
  • Ops teams: document processes as flowcharts.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at napkin.ai.
  2. Paste a paragraph or outline.
  3. Select text and click to generate visuals.
  4. Choose a layout and edit colors and labels.
  5. Export to PNG or PPT and drop it into your doc.

Tips and tricks

  • Select only key sentences to save credits.
  • Write in lists or steps for cleaner flowcharts.
  • Set brand colors once for consistency.
  • Try several suggestions before editing.

Napkin AI alternatives

  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))
  • Gamma — AI design partner that generates presentations, documents, websites and social posts from a prompt. (from $9/seat/mo (Plus))
  • Beautiful.ai — AI presentation software with auto-aligning Smart Slides that keep decks polished and on-brand. (from $14.50/mo (Pro, billed annually); $45 monthly)
  • Plus AI — Create and edit AI presentations directly inside Google Slides and PowerPoint. (from $10/user/mo billed annually (Basic))
  • Pitch — Collaborative presentation software with AI slide generation, analytics and deal rooms. (from $10/seat/mo billed yearly (Plus))

FAQ

Is Napkin AI free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever and includes 500 AI credits per week plus unlimited visual editing.

How much does Napkin AI cost?

Plus is $9 per person per month and Pro is $22 per person per month. Paying yearly saves 25%. Enterprise plans are available through sales.

What are Napkin AI credits?

Credits measure usage for generating visuals, with approximately 1 credit charged per word you select for generation. Napkin notes this may change for more complex features.

Can I export Napkin visuals?

Yes. Visuals can be exported as PPT, PNG, PDF or SVG; Plus and Pro include unlimited PPT and SVG export.

Can I use my brand colors and fonts?

You can customize colors on any plan; unlimited custom branding and uploading your own fonts are part of Pro.

Are there student discounts?

Napkin says it doesn't currently offer discounts to nonprofits or students, but the free plan covers most lightweight use.
