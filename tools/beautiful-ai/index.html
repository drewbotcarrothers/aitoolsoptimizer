What Beautiful.ai is

Beautiful.ai is a web-based presentation maker built around Smart Slides: layouts that automatically realign, resize and animate content as you add it. Instead of nudging text boxes, you pick a layout (a timeline, comparison, chart or image grid) and type; the slide handles spacing and hierarchy.

On top of that sits an AI workflow. Beautiful.ai says it is designed to “keep you in the driver’s seat”: you start with a prompt, an outline or source material, refine the slide-by-slide outline, choose a theme and image style, then generate. Customers quoted on its site include CVENT and IDC.

Key features

AI presentations

Prompt a topic, paste a full outline or add files and links for context. Edit the outline before slides are created, then iterate by switching Smart Slide layouts while keeping your copy. AI image generation, a writing assistant and language translation are included.

Smart Slides

More than 300 layouts for data, comparisons, quotes, timelines and diagrams. Charts and graphs are data-linked, and built-in animations add polish without manual keyframes.

Brand control

Define colors, fonts and logos once; shared and locked themes keep every team member’s decks consistent.

Presenting and sharing

Present live, record voice-overs, share secure links with permissions, track views and slide engagement, or export to PDF and editable PowerPoint.

Best use cases

  • Sales decks: a rep generates a first draft from a product one-pager, then swaps layouts until the story flows.
  • Startup pitches: a founder pastes an outline and uses data-linked charts for traction slides.
  • Quarterly reports: an analyst updates charts each quarter without rebuilding slides.
  • Training: an HR team records voice-over walkthroughs for asynchronous onboarding.

How to get started

  1. Start a 14-day free trial at beautiful.ai.
  2. Set up your theme: brand colors, fonts and logo.
  3. Choose Create with AI and paste your topic or outline.
  4. Edit the generated outline slide by slide before generating.
  5. Swap Smart Slide layouts until each slide makes one clear point.
  6. Share a tracked link or export to PowerPoint or PDF.

Tips and tricks

  • Fix the outline first. It’s faster to restructure an outline than restyle slides.
  • Feed source material (files or links) for more accurate content.
  • Use one idea per slide; Smart Slides look best with concise text.
  • Check view analytics to time follow-ups after sending a deck.

Example AI prompt:

Create a 10-slide pitch deck for a B2B scheduling app for dental clinics. Include problem, solution, market size, product demo, business model, traction, competition, team, roadmap and ask. Keep each slide under 40 words.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Smart Slides keep spacing and alignment consistent automatically
  • AI lets you edit a slide-by-slide outline before generating
  • Unlimited AI content generation on every seat
  • Editable PowerPoint export and PDF export
  • Brand locking and shared themes for teams

👎 Cons

  • No permanent free plan
  • Monthly Pro pricing ($45) is steep
  • Smart Slide constraints can feel limiting for custom layouts
  • Web-only app

Who should (and shouldn't) use Beautiful.ai

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Build sales, investor or client decks every week
  • Want a team's decks to look the same without a designer
  • Prefer guardrails over a blank canvas

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need full freeform design control
  • Make one presentation a year (a free tool will do)
  • Need offline desktop editing

Beautiful.ai alternatives

  • Gamma — AI design partner that generates presentations, documents, websites and social posts from a prompt. (from $9/seat/mo (Plus))
  • Pitch — Collaborative presentation software with AI slide generation, analytics and deal rooms. (from $10/seat/mo billed yearly (Plus))
  • Plus AI — Create and edit AI presentations directly inside Google Slides and PowerPoint. (from $10/user/mo billed annually (Basic))
  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))

FAQ

How much does Beautiful.ai cost?

Pro is $14.50 per month billed annually, or $45 per month billed monthly. Team is $40 per user per month billed annually or $50 billed monthly. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Does Beautiful.ai have a free plan?

No permanent free plan, but there is a 14-day free trial of Pro or Team. Students with a valid .edu email can get a free year through the education plan.

Can Beautiful.ai export to PowerPoint?

Yes. Paid plans include PowerPoint import and editable PowerPoint export, plus PDF export.

Does Beautiful.ai use AI?

Yes. You can prompt a topic, paste an outline or add source files and links, refine a slide-by-slide outline, then generate slides with AI images, writing help and translation.

Is Beautiful.ai good for teams?

Yes. The Team plan adds shared asset libraries and locked brand themes so every member's decks stay consistent. Enterprise adds SAML SSO.
