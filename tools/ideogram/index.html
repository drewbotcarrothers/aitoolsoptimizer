What Ideogram is

Ideogram is an AI image generator built with a particular strength: putting accurate, readable text into images. That makes it a favorite for posters, flyers, social graphics, YouTube thumbnails, logo concepts and ad mockups, where many image models still garble lettering.

Beyond generation, Ideogram includes editing tools (remove backgrounds and objects), consistent Characters, collections and sharing, JSON prompting for precise control, an iOS app and an API. Its documentation is unusually thorough, with guides on prompt structure, text in images and fixing common problems.

Key features

Text in images

Put the words you want in quotes and Ideogram renders them as typography in the design.

Editing tools

Edit images, remove backgrounds and objects, and upscale (these use credits).

Characters and collections

Keep characters consistent and organize work in collections.

Batch generation

Generate many variations at once on Pro.

Private generation

Plus and higher keep your generations private.

API and pay as you go

Developers can integrate Ideogram; personal accounts can fund a wallet without a subscription.

Best use cases

  • Event promotion: generate a concert poster with the band name and date.
  • Small business: draft logo concepts and storefront signage ideas.
  • Creators: make thumbnails with bold, readable titles.
  • Marketers: produce several ad variations with different headlines.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at ideogram.ai.
  2. Write a prompt with the exact text in quotes.
  3. Choose an aspect ratio and style.
  4. Generate, then refine or edit the best result.
  5. Upgrade to Plus if you need privacy and more credits.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep on-image text short; fewer words render more reliably.
  • Describe font style and placement (“bold sans-serif title at top”).
  • Use JSON prompting when you need precise control.
  • Check the cost on the run button before generating with partner models.

Example prompt:

Retro travel poster of Lake Louise at sunrise, flat illustration style, the text “LAKE LOUISE” in bold cream letters at the top and “Banff National Park” smaller at the bottom

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Excellent text rendering
  • Free weekly credits
  • Unlimited slow-queue generation on paid plans
  • Clear, documented pricing
  • API and pay-as-you-go options

👎 Cons

  • Free images aren't private
  • Edits, upscales and reference images always cost credits
  • Monthly credits don't roll over
  • Less painterly than some art-focused tools

Who should (and shouldn't) use Ideogram

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Need text-heavy graphics like posters and thumbnails
  • Want quick logo or brand concepts
  • Prefer predictable credit pricing

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want artistic, stylized imagery first (consider Midjourney)
  • Need full design layout tools (consider Canva)
  • Need video generation as the main use

  • FLUX (Black Forest Labs) — Black Forest Labs' FLUX models for image, video and audio generation via API, playground and open weights. (from From $0.008 per image (FLUX 3 Klein, 768×768); no subscription)
  • Leonardo.Ai — AI image and video generation platform with personal model training, Realtime Canvas and team plans, now part of Canva. (from $12/mo (Essential, monthly))
  • Adobe Firefly — Adobe's generative AI app for images, video, audio and vectors, with partner models and commercially safe Firefly models. (from $9.99/mo (Firefly Standard))
  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))

FAQ

Is Ideogram free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 30 credits a week. When they run out, wait for next week's credits or buy more.

How much does Ideogram cost?

Plus is $20 per month or $15 per month billed yearly, Pro is $60 per month or $42 yearly, and Team is $30 per user per month or $20 yearly, in USD before tax.

What happens when my Ideogram credits run out?

On Plus, Pro and Team you can keep generating with Ideogram models in a slower queue with no limit. Custom and partner models, reference images, edits and upscales still need credits.

Why is Ideogram good for text in images?

Ideogram is known for rendering legible text, and its docs include dedicated guidance on prompting text in images.

Does Ideogram have an API?

Yes. Ideogram offers an API, and pay-as-you-go credits can be used for eligible image, video and API usage.
