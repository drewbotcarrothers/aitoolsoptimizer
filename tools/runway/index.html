Runway review

AI video generation and editing suite.

Overview

Pro-grade AI video generation and editing platform (Gen-4.5).

Key features

  • Gen-4.5
  • Credits system
  • Editing tools
  • Image generation

Best use cases

  • Text/image-to-video
  • AI video editing
  • Ads & music videos
  • Film previsualization

Pricing

Freemium. From $12 (Standard, annual); ~$15 monthly (USD).

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Cutting-edge video generation
  • Many editing tools in one place
  • Browser based

👎 Cons

  • Credits run out quickly
  • Clips are short

Runway alternatives

  • Synthesia — Create videos with AI avatars from a script. (from $18 (Starter, annual); $29 monthly)
  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))

Sources

This is an editorial review based on research of official Runway pages and documentation, not hands-on benchmark testing. Prices are in USD and were checked on October 15, 2026; vendors change plans often, so confirm on the official pricing page before buying.
