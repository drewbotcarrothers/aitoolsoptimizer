Runway review

AI creative suite for video, images and audio with Gen-4.5 and models from other labs.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Runway → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Runway is the professional's AI video studio: its own Gen-4.5 model sits alongside top third-party models, with editing, upscaling, voice clones and an agentic collaborator in one workspace. The free tier is only a taste (125 one-time credits); Standard at $12 a month annually gives about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 video, so serious users will want Pro or Max. Credits go fast with premium models, so plan shots carefully.

Key takeaways

  • Free: $0 with 125 one-time credits, a selection of models and 5 GB storage.
  • Standard: $12/month billed annually ($15 monthly) with 625 credits/month—about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 or 104 seconds of Gen-4 Turbo.
  • Pro: $28/month annually ($35 monthly) with 2,250 credits, a custom voice clone, Runway MCP, unlimited 4K upscaling and 100 GB storage.
  • Max: $76/month annually ($95 monthly) with 9,500 credits, 1-month rollover, 20 parallel generations and early access to new models.
  • Yearly billing gives 2 months free; Team, Enterprise and API options are available.

What Runway is

Runway is one of the pioneers of AI video. Its creative suite is an all-in-one cloud platform to generate and edit video, images and audio in one workspace, for individuals and teams. Alongside its own Gen-4.5 and Gen-4 models, Runway now offers leading third-party models—Seedance, Kling, Nano Banana Pro, Wan and more—plus an agentic creative collaborator that helps plan and produce work.

Runway describes its broader mission as building “Real-World Intelligence,” with an API platform for developers and GWM-1, a General World Model for simulation.

Key features

Video generation

Text- and image-to-video with Gen-4.5, Gen-4 and Gen-4 Turbo, plus third-party models.

Image generation

Gen-4 Images and other models such as Nano Banana Pro.

Agentic creative collaborator

An AI collaborator to help develop and execute creative ideas (all paid plans).

Voice and audio

Custom voice clones (1 on Pro, up to 3 on Max).

Upscaling

Unlimited 4K video upscaling and Topaz AI Upscale on Pro and above.

Workflow tools

Runway MCP, projects, Brand Kits (Max) and an API.

Best use cases

  • Filmmakers: previsualize scenes and generate B-roll.
  • Agencies: produce ad variations quickly.
  • Musicians: create music-video sequences.
  • Designers: animate stills and upscale to 4K.

How to get started

  1. Sign up at runway.com and use the free credits.
  2. Generate a few images to lock in a look.
  3. Animate the best one with Gen-4 Turbo to save credits.
  4. Render finals with Gen-4.5.
  5. Upscale to 4K and assemble in your editor.

Tips and tricks

  • Draft with cheaper models, finalize with Gen-4.5.
  • Use reference images for consistent characters.
  • Describe camera motion explicitly.
  • Track credits per shot before big projects.

Example prompt:

Slow dolly-in on a lone cyclist crossing a foggy suspension bridge at dawn, golden backlight, cinematic 35mm look, gentle film grain.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Strong in-house video models
  • Many top third-party models in one place
  • Professional editing and upscaling
  • MCP and API for workflows
  • Credit rollover on Max

👎 Cons

  • Free tier is one-time only
  • Credits run out quickly on premium models
  • Best results need learning and iteration
  • Higher tiers get expensive

Who should (and shouldn't) use Runway

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Produce cinematic AI shots
  • Agencies creating ads at scale
  • Filmmakers doing previs and VFX

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need avatar presenters (consider Synthesia or HeyGen)
  • Want quick social edits (consider CapCut)
  • Need text-based editing (consider Descript)

Runway alternatives

  • Luma AI — Creative AI platform with Ray 3.2 video, Uni-1 brand-aware image model and Luma Agents for creative teams. (from $30/mo (Plus) or $300/yr)
  • Pika — An AI creative platform with 25+ apps for video, image and sound, powered by leading gen-media models. (from $10/mo (Starter, 900 credits))
  • Google Flow — Google's AI creative studio for cinematic video scenes and images, powered by Veo, Gemini Omni Flash and Nano Banana. (from Free (50 credits/day); Google AI Plus $4.99/mo)

FAQ

Is Runway free?

Yes, in a limited way. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 125 one-time credits, a selection of generative models and 5 GB of storage.

How much does Runway cost?

Billed annually, Standard is $12/month, Pro $28/month and Max $76/month. Billed monthly they are $15, $35 and $95. Team, Enterprise and API pricing are separate.

How many videos do Runway credits buy?

Runway says Standard's 625 monthly credits equal about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5, 104 seconds of Gen-4 Turbo or 78 Gen-4 images at 1080p. Pro's 2,250 credits equal about 187 seconds of Gen-4.5.

Which models can I use in Runway?

Runway's own Gen-4.5 and Gen-4 models plus models such as Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Nano Banana Pro and Wan 3.0. Paid plans include access to all AI video and image models.

Do Runway credits roll over?

On Max, unused credits roll over for one month. Pro and above can top up credits anytime.

What is Runway's world model?

Runway is also building General World Models; GWM-1 powers tools for policy inference, evaluation and synthetic data generation through photorealistic simulation.
