Jasper review

AI platform for marketing teams: on-brand content with Canvas, marketing agents, brand IQ and AI search visibility.

paid⏱ 3 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Jasper → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Jasper has moved upmarket from a copywriting app to a marketing platform with agents, brand governance and AI search visibility tracking. Pro at $59-$69 per seat per month suits individual marketers who want brand voices and workflow agents; Business adds custom agents, Jasper Grid and enterprise governance. Solo writers who just need drafts will find general chatbots cheaper.

Key takeaways

  • Pro: $69 per seat/month, or $59 billed yearly (about 20% off), with a 7-day free trial.
  • Pro includes Canvas, agents for core marketing workflows, 2 Brand Voices, 5 Knowledge assets and 3 Audiences.
  • Business (custom pricing) adds GEO, translation and deep research agents, a no-code agent builder and Jasper Grid.
  • Jasper measures brand presence and citation rates across major AI answer engines.
  • Business includes unlimited Brand Voices, Knowledge and Audiences plus admin controls.

What Jasper is

Jasper is an AI platform designed for marketers. It began as one of the first AI copywriting tools and has since grown into a system for producing on-brand marketing content at scale, with “purpose-built agents that execute end-to-end marketing workflows” and governance that keeps every asset aligned with your brand.

A newer focus is AI search: Jasper helps you “win the new front door of search” by measuring how your brand performs across major AI answer engines, identifying content gaps and generating content AI tools are more likely to cite.

Key features

Canvas

A workspace for creating and refining on-brand content quickly.

Marketing agents

Agents for core workflows on Pro, and complex ones (GEO, translation, deep research) on Business.

Brand IQ

Brand Voices analyze your tone and set rules; Knowledge assets and Audiences give the AI context about products and personas.

AI visibility

Track brand presence, citation rates and sentiment across AI answer engines.

Jasper Grid and custom agents

Systematic content execution at scale and a no-code builder for custom agents (Business).

Best use cases

  • Campaign launches: turn a brief into on-brand emails, ads and landing page copy.
  • SEO and GEO: create content designed to rank and be cited by AI.
  • ABM: personalize content for target accounts.
  • Localization: translate campaigns with Business agents.

How to get started

  1. Start the 7-day Pro trial at jasper.ai.
  2. Set up a Brand Voice from your existing content.
  3. Add Knowledge assets about your products.
  4. Define your key Audiences.
  5. Run an agent on a real campaign brief and review the output.

Tips and tricks

  • Invest in Brand Voice setup; it drives quality across every asset.
  • Keep Knowledge current when products or pricing change.
  • Review AI visibility monthly to guide content priorities.
  • Edit for facts and claims before publishing.

Example brief:

Create a launch email, three LinkedIn posts and two Google ad headlines for our new expense app for freelancers. Audience: US freelancers. Tone: friendly, confident. Key benefit: track expenses in 10 seconds.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Built specifically for marketing teams
  • Strong brand voice and governance controls
  • Agents for end-to-end workflows
  • AI search visibility tracking
  • Free 7-day trial

👎 Cons

  • Expensive compared with general chatbots
  • Pro limits Brand Voices and Audiences
  • Advanced agents require Business
  • No free plan

Who should (and shouldn't) use Jasper

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Lead content for a marketing team
  • Need every asset to stay on brand
  • Want to track AI search visibility

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Write occasionally (consider ChatGPT or Claude)
  • Need deep SEO research data (consider Semrush)
  • Write fiction (consider Sudowrite)

Jasper alternatives

  • Copy.ai — GTM AI platform for sales and marketing teams, with multi-model chat and automated content and prospecting workflows. (from $24/mo (Chat, billed annually); $29 monthly)
  • Writesonic — Track and improve your brand's visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and more. (from $79/mo billed annually (Starter))
  • Frase — SEO and GEO content platform that finds topics, writes drafts in your voice, publishes and monitors pages for AI search. (from $39/mo billed yearly ($49 month to month))
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Claude — Anthropic's AI assistant for writing, analysis, research, coding and long-running work, with Claude Code included on paid plans. (from $17/mo (Pro, annual; $20 monthly); Max from $100)

FAQ

How much does Jasper cost?

Jasper Pro is $69 per seat per month billed monthly or $59 per seat per month billed yearly. Business pricing is custom.

Does Jasper have a free plan?

No, but Pro comes with a 7-day free trial.

What does Jasper Business add?

Business adds agents for complex workflows like GEO, translations and deep research, a no-code custom agent builder, Jasper Grid, unlimited Brand Voices, Knowledge and Audiences, admin controls and dedicated support.

What is Jasper Canvas?

Canvas is Jasper's platform for accelerated, on-brand content creation, where you create and refine marketing assets with AI.

Can Jasper help with AI search (GEO)?

Yes. Jasper measures how your brand performs across major AI answer engines, monitors citation rates and helps generate content designed to be cited.
