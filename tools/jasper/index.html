Overview

AI marketing content platform with brand voice, agents and campaign workflows for marketing teams.

Key features

  • Canvas
  • Marketing agents
  • Brand Voices
  • Knowledge assets
  • Audiences

Best use cases

  • Blog & ad copy
  • On-brand campaigns
  • Marketing agents
  • Content repurposing

Pricing

Paid. From $59 (Pro, annual); $69 monthly (USD).

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Brand voice controls
  • Marketing templates
  • Team collaboration

👎 Cons

  • Pricey for individuals
  • Overlaps with general chatbots

Jasper alternatives

  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Grammarly — AI writing assistant for clear, correct text. (from $12 (Pro, annual); $30 monthly)
