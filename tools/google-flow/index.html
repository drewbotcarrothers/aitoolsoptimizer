Google Flow review

Google's AI creative studio for cinematic video scenes and images, powered by Veo, Gemini Omni Flash and Nano Banana.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Google Flow → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Google Flow puts Google's best video and image models in one creative studio, and the free 50 daily credits make it easy to experiment. Serious video work needs a Google AI plan: Pro's 1,000 monthly credits go fast at 100 credits per Veo 3.1 Quality clip, while Ultra subscribers get Veo 3.1 Lite clips free. It's excellent value if you already pay for Google AI Pro.

Key takeaways

  • Everyone gets 50 Google Flow credits per day; unused daily credits don't roll over.
  • Monthly bonus credits: AI Plus 200, AI Pro 1,000, AI Ultra $100 10,000, AI Ultra $200 25,000.
  • Veo 3.1 Lite costs 10 credits per clip (free for Ultra), Fast 20 and Quality 100; editing with Gemini Omni Flash costs 40.
  • Non-subscribers may be unable to generate video during peak hours (about 2-5 PM UTC).
  • Subscribers in supported regions can buy additional AI credits.

What Google Flow is

Google Flow is Google’s AI creative studio for making cinematic scenes and stories. Built by Google Labs, it brings Google’s generative video and image models into one workspace where you can generate clips from text or images, edit generated or uploaded videos, create images and get help from the Google Flow Agent.

Flow runs on credits. Everyone gets a daily free allowance, and Google AI subscribers receive monthly credits on top, making Flow one of the perks of Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra.

Key features

Veo 3.1 video

Generate 4, 6 or 8-second clips with Veo 3.1 Lite, Fast or Quality, trading speed and cost for quality.

Gemini Omni Flash

Create 4-10 second videos at 360p or 720p and edit uploaded and generated videos of any length and resolution.

Images

Generate images (with Nano Banana models) for concepts, frames and assets.

Flow Agent

Get help planning and creating scenes; available even to free users during peak hours.

Credit management

Track remaining credits, refresh dates and usage history (subscribers), and share AI credits with your family group.

Best use cases

  • Ads: create several short product clips to test on social media.
  • Filmmakers: visualize a scene or storyboard before shooting.
  • Educators: illustrate historical or scientific concepts with short clips.
  • Marketers: generate B-roll for explainer videos.

How to get started

  1. Go to labs.google/flow and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Check your credit balance under your profile picture.
  3. Start with Veo 3.1 Lite to test ideas cheaply.
  4. Refine prompts, then switch to Quality for final shots.
  5. Subscribe to a Google AI plan for more credits and peak-hour access.

Tips and tricks

  • Draft cheaply, finish in Quality: test with Lite or Fast first.
  • Describe camera moves and lighting explicitly.
  • Use your first generation wisely; it starts your daily refresh clock.
  • Generate outside peak hours if you’re on the free tier.

Example prompt:

Slow dolly-in on a steaming cup of coffee on a rainy cafe windowsill at dawn, warm interior light, raindrops on glass, shallow depth of field, soft jazz ambience

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Free daily credits for everyone
  • Access to Google's leading video models
  • Bundled with Google AI subscriptions
  • Credits can be shared with a Google One family group
  • Agent helps plan and create scenes

👎 Cons

  • High-quality clips consume credits quickly
  • Peak-hour limits for free users
  • Unused credits don't roll over
  • Credit costs differ by model and can change

Who should (and shouldn't) use Google Flow

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Already subscribe to Google AI Pro or Ultra
  • Make short cinematic clips or ads
  • Want to experiment with AI video for free

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need long-form video editing (consider Descript or CapCut)
  • Want avatar presenter videos (consider Synthesia or HeyGen)
  • Need predictable unlimited generation

FAQ

Is Google Flow free?

Yes. Everyone receives 50 Google Flow credits per day. Without a subscription, video generation may be unavailable during peak hours (roughly 2 PM to 5 PM UTC), but you can still generate images and use the Flow Agent.

How many credits do Google AI plans include?

On top of the daily 50, Google AI Plus adds 200 credits a month, Pro 1,000, Ultra $100 10,000 and Ultra $200 25,000.

How many credits does a Veo video cost?

Per generation, Veo 3.1 Lite costs 10 credits (0 for Ultra subscribers), Veo 3.1 Fast costs 20 for non-Ultra subscribers, and Veo 3.1 Quality costs 100. Some features create multiple generations per request.

Do Flow credits roll over?

No. Unused daily and monthly Google Flow credits don't roll over. Purchased AI credits may expire after a set period.

How much are Google AI plans?

Google AI Plus is $4.99 per month, Pro is $19.99 and Ultra is $99.99 or $199.99 per month in the US.
