Zendesk AI review

AI agents, Copilot and automation built into Zendesk's customer service suite.

paid⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Zendesk AI → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Zendesk AI is a safe, powerful choice for established support teams: AI agents resolve common requests, Copilot assists human agents, and everything sits on one of the most mature help desks. AI Agents start with Suite Team at $55 per agent a month (yearly), Copilot is a $50 add-on, and AI agent usage is billed per automated resolution, so total cost depends on volume. Smaller teams may find simpler tools cheaper.

Key takeaways

  • Support Team: $19 per agent/month paid yearly for email, ticketing, routing and analytics.
  • Suite Team: $55 per agent/month paid yearly, adding AI Agents, Knowledge Base, Action Builder and omnichannel routing.
  • Suite Professional: $115 per agent/month paid yearly, adding Admin Copilot, App Builder, Writing Tools and skills-based routing.
  • Copilot add-on: $50 per agent/month paid yearly; Contact Center: $83 per agent/month.
  • AI agent usage is billed per automated resolution; annual billing saves up to 20%. Zendesk + Forethought adds self-learning AI agents.

What Zendesk AI is

Zendesk is one of the most widely used customer service platforms, and Zendesk AI is the set of AI capabilities built into it. AI Agents resolve customer requests automatically across channels, Copilot assists human agents, Admin Copilot helps administrators optimize operations, and tools like Action Builder let AI take actions in other systems.

Zendesk has also introduced Zendesk + Forethought, self-learning AI agents that improve in real time and can integrate with existing setups without migration.

Key features

AI Agents

Resolve common requests automatically, billed per successful automated resolution.

Copilot

AI assistance for agents (add-on).

Admin Copilot and App Builder

Help admins optimize workflows and build apps (Suite Professional).

Knowledge Base and Writing Tools

Help center content for self-service and AI writing assistance.

Routing

Omnichannel routing on Suite Team; skills-based routing on Suite Professional.

Contact Center

Voice and complex contact-center features as an add-on.

Best use cases

  • E-commerce support: automate order-status and returns questions.
  • SaaS support: deflect how-to questions with AI and knowledge base.
  • Contact centers: unify voice, chat and email.
  • Large teams: route tickets by skill and assist agents with Copilot.

How to get started

  1. Start a free trial at zendesk.com.
  2. Connect your channels (email, chat, messaging).
  3. Build out your Knowledge Base.
  4. Turn on AI Agents for top request types.
  5. Add Copilot and monitor automated resolutions.

Tips and tricks

  • Start with your top 10 intents.
  • Keep knowledge articles current for better AI answers.
  • Review AI conversations weekly.
  • Model total cost: seats + add-ons + resolutions.

Example AI agent instruction:

For “where is my order” requests, look up the order by email, share the tracking link and expected delivery date, and hand off to a human if the order is more than 5 days late.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Mature, full-featured help desk
  • AI agents built in from Suite Team
  • Pay per successful automated resolution
  • Strong omnichannel and routing
  • Enterprise-ready

👎 Cons

  • Costs add up with seats, add-ons and usage
  • Copilot is extra
  • Complex for very small teams
  • Pricing structure takes time to model

Who should (and shouldn't) use Zendesk AI

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Support teams already on Zendesk
  • Organizations needing omnichannel and AI
  • Contact centers

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Small shops needing simple chat (consider Tidio)
  • Teams wanting an AI-first help desk (consider Intercom Fin)
  • Need a docs chatbot only (consider Chatbase)

Zendesk AI alternatives

  • Intercom Fin — AI customer service agent that resolves conversations across chat, email, voice and social, priced per outcome. (from $49/mo base plan including 50 outcomes)
  • Tidio (Lyro) — Live chat, ticketing and Lyro, an AI agent that resolves customer conversations. (from $24.17/mo billed annually (Starter))
  • Chatbase — Build AI agents trained on your data to handle support and sales across chat, email, WhatsApp, Slack and voice. (from $32/mo (Hobby, billed yearly); $40 monthly)
  • HubSpot (Breeze AI) — Agentic customer platform with a free CRM, Breeze AI agents for marketing, sales and service, and usage-based HubSpot Credits. (from Free; Starter currently from $7/seat/mo (limited-time offer))
  • Gong — Revenue AI operating system that captures customer interactions, analyzes deals and automates next steps with AI agents. (from Custom pricing (contact sales))

FAQ

Does Zendesk have a free plan?

No free plan is listed, but Zendesk offers free trials of its plans.

How much does Zendesk AI cost?

Paid yearly, Support Team is $19 per agent/month, Suite Team $55, and Suite Professional $115. AI Agents are included from Suite Team, Copilot is a $50 per agent/month add-on, and AI agent usage is billed per automated resolution.

What is an automated resolution?

Automated resolutions are the unit Zendesk uses to bill AI agent usage: you pay only for customer requests that were successfully resolved by AI.

What does Copilot do?

Copilot provides AI assistance to your entire support team. It's an add-on at $50 per agent/month paid yearly.

What is Zendesk + Forethought?

Zendesk added Forethought's self-learning AI agents, which learn and improve in real time and integrate with your current setup, whether or not you use Zendesk.

What's the difference between Support and Suite plans?

Support plans focus on email and ticketing essentials; Suite plans unify support channels and add AI Agents, Knowledge Base, Action Builder and omnichannel routing.
