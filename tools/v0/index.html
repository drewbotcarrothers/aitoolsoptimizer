What v0 is

v0 is Vercel’s AI app builder. You describe what you want—a dashboard, a booking app, a landing page—and v0 generates a working application you can preview, edit and publish as a live website in seconds. Under the hood it writes real code you can sync to GitHub, so developers can keep working on it in their own tools.

v0 also offers Design Mode for visual edits, reusable design systems (colors, typography and styles across projects) and integrations with your favorite tools and APIs.

Key features

Prompt to app

Generate full-stack applications and UI from natural language.

Design Mode

Edit visually without writing prompts for every change.

Design systems

Define colors, typography and styles to reuse across projects.

GitHub sync and deploy

Sync code to GitHub and deploy to Vercel’s infrastructure.

Team collaboration

Shared chats, projects and credit pools with centralized billing on Plus and above.

ChatGPT connection

ChatGPT Plus and Pro users can cover eligible responses from OpenAI models with their ChatGPT plan.

Best use cases

  • Founders: prototype an MVP and share a live URL.

How to get started

  1. Sign in at v0.app with a Vercel account.
  2. Describe your app in a few sentences.
  3. Refine with follow-up prompts and Design Mode.
  4. Sync to GitHub.
  5. Publish to Vercel.

Tips and tricks

  • Describe pages, data and users up front.
  • Use design systems for consistent branding.
  • Iterate in small steps to save credits.
  • Review generated code before production.

Example prompt:

Build a SaaS pricing page with three tiers, a monthly/annual toggle, an FAQ accordion and a dark mode, using our brand color #4F46E5.

FAQ

Is v0 free?

Yes. New users on the Free plan get $5 in one-time credits and a 7-message daily limit, plus Design Mode, GitHub sync and deployment to Vercel. Existing Free users keep their $5 monthly allowance.

How much does v0 cost?

Plus is $30 per user per month and Business is $100 per user per month; both include $30 of monthly credits per user. Enterprise is custom.

What happened to v0 Premium?

The $20/month Premium plan is being sunsetted and is no longer available to new users.

How do v0 credits work?

Credits work like a prepaid balance. Generations draw from it and pause when it runs out. Unused monthly credits roll over and expire after 65 days; purchased credits expire one year after purchase and need an active paid plan to use.

Can I use my ChatGPT subscription with v0?

Yes. ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscribers can connect their account to cover eligible AI responses from supported OpenAI models, though a v0 credit balance is still required.

What does Business add over Plus?

Business adds training opt-out by default (data opt-out) on top of Plus team features like shared projects, shared credit pools and centralized billing.
