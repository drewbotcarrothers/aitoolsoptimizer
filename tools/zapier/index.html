What Zapier is

Zapier is the best-known no-code automation platform. You build Zaps—automated workflows where a trigger in one app (“new form submission”) starts actions in others (“add to CRM, send Slack message”)—across 9,000+ apps. No code is needed, and the editor is designed for clarity.

Zapier has added a full AI layer: Agents that work across your apps, Zapier Copilot to help build automations, and Zapier MCP to connect AI assistants to thousands of apps. The core suite also includes Tables (databases) and Forms.

Key features

Zaps

Single- and multi-step automations with triggers and actions.

Logic tools

Filters, Paths and Formatter for branching and data cleanup—these don’t count as tasks.

Agents and Copilot

AI agents that act across apps, and Copilot to help create Zaps.

Zapier MCP

Connect AI tools to apps through the Model Context Protocol, code-free.

Tables and Forms

Store data and collect inputs inside Zapier.

Monitoring

Alerts with context when something fails, plus custom error handling.

Best use cases

  • Sales: send new leads from forms to your CRM and Slack.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at zapier.com.
  2. Pick a template or describe a workflow to Copilot.
  3. Choose a trigger app and event.
  4. Add actions and test each step.
  5. Turn it on and monitor task usage.

Tips and tricks

  • Use Filters to avoid wasting tasks.
  • Use Paths instead of separate Zaps.
  • Add error alerts for critical workflows.
  • Try MCP to let your AI assistant act in your apps.

Example Zap:

When a new row is added to the “Leads” table, use AI to summarize the company, create a HubSpot contact, and post a summary to #sales in Slack.

FAQ

Is Zapier free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 100 tasks per month, unlimited Zap workflows and access to the core suite, including Tables, Forms and AI tools.

How much does Zapier cost?

Professional is $19.99/month and Team is $69/month, both billed annually after a 14-day free trial. Enterprise is custom.

What is a Zapier task?

A task is an action your Zap successfully completes—for example, creating a new contact in your CRM. Filters, Paths and Formatter steps don't count toward task usage.

What happens if I go over my tasks?

On Professional, your automations keep running and you're charged per task for completed tasks over your limit.

What is Zapier MCP?

Zapier MCP lets you connect AI assistants to thousands of apps code-free, so AI tools can take actions through Zapier.

Zapier vs Make vs n8n?

Zapier is easiest with the most apps; Make is more visual and often cheaper per action; n8n can be self-hosted and charges per execution. See our Make and n8n reviews.
