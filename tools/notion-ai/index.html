Overview

AI built into Notion for writing, search, meeting notes and custom agents across your workspace.

Key features

  • AI writing
  • Enterprise search
  • Meeting notes
  • Custom Agents (credits)

Best use cases

  • Notes & docs
  • Workspace search
  • Meeting notes
  • Custom agents

Pricing

Freemium. From $20 per member (Business, annual) for full AI (USD).

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Works where your notes live
  • Q&A across workspace
  • Handy summaries

👎 Cons

  • Add-on cost
  • Only as good as your docs

Notion AI alternatives

  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Grammarly — AI writing assistant for clear, correct text. (from $12 (Pro, annual); $30 monthly)
