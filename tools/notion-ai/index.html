What Notion AI is

Notion AI is built into Notion, the connected workspace for docs, wikis, projects and databases. Notion describes it as “your built-in teammate inside Notion,” working directly in your pages, tasks and databases using context from your workspace and connected apps.

Over the past year Notion AI has shifted from writing assistance to agents. Notion Agent takes on entire tasks, and Custom Agents automate recurring team work—“answer questions, prioritize tasks, and write reports—all while you sleep.”

Key features

Notion Agent

Chat with the agent to complete multi-step work: research a topic, draft a doc, build a database or update dozens of pages, using Notion, connected apps and the web.

Custom Agents

Set a trigger or schedule and the agent handles recurring work—answering questions in Slack, routing tasks or posting project updates. One person builds it and the whole team benefits. Admins control who can create agents.

AI Meeting Notes

Automatic meeting notes and follow-ups without a bot joining the call.

Enterprise search

Search across Notion and connected apps such as Slack and GitHub.

Model choice and controls

Switch workflows to different models or providers without losing context, with a bird’s-eye view of AI actions and permissions.

Best use cases

  • Team leads: auto-generate weekly project updates from task databases.
  • Support and ops: let an agent answer common questions in Slack.
  • Product teams: turn meeting notes into specs and tasks.
  • Everyone: find answers across Notion, Slack and GitHub.

How to get started

  1. Open your Notion workspace and try AI on any page.
  2. Ask Notion Agent to draft or organize something real.
  3. Upgrade to Business to unlock Agent and Meeting Notes fully.
  4. Build a Custom Agent for one recurring task.
  5. Monitor credit use before rolling agents out widely.

Tips and tricks

  • Point the agent at specific pages for better context.
  • Keep databases tidy so autofill works well.
  • Start Custom Agents small and add human review.
  • Use verified pages so answers cite up-to-date docs.

Example prompt:

Review this week’s tasks in the Product Roadmap database, summarize what shipped, what slipped and why, and create a new page “Weekly update” with a table of owners and next steps.

FAQ

Is Notion AI free?

The Free plan includes a trial of Notion AI, such as generating docs or autofilling databases. The full Notion AI experience, including Notion Agent and AI Meeting Notes, is on the Business plan.

How much does Notion AI cost?

Notion lists Plus at $10 per seat per month and Business at $20 per seat per month. Business includes Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and search across connected apps. Enterprise pricing is through sales.

What are Custom Agents?

Custom Agents automate recurring work for a team—like answering questions in Slack, routing tasks and sharing project updates—on a trigger or schedule, 24/7. They run on Notion credits, priced at $10 per 1,000 credits after a free trial.

What is Notion Agent?

Notion Agent completes complex, multi-step tasks using context from your Notion workspace, connected apps and the web, and can create and edit pages and databases.

Does Notion train AI on my data?

Notion says it has contractual agreements prohibiting its AI subprocessors from using customer data to train models, and no data is stored with LLM providers (30-day retention for non-Enterprise).

Can Notion AI take meeting notes?

Yes. AI Meeting Notes on the Business plan automate notes and follow-ups without a bot joining your call.
