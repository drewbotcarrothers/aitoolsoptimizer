HubSpot (Breeze AI) review

Agentic customer platform with a free CRM, Breeze AI agents for marketing, sales and service, and usage-based HubSpot Credits.

⚡ Quick verdict

HubSpot is one of the easiest all-in-one CRMs to adopt, and its Breeze AI agents now handle real work like answering customers, prospecting and researching accounts. The free CRM is generous, and Starter is very affordable, especially during promotions. Costs rise significantly at Professional and Enterprise, and AI usage is metered through HubSpot Credits, so set spending limits early.

Key takeaways

  • Free CRM and free tools for forms, landing pages, live chat and more.
  • Starter Customer Platform: currently $7 or $10 per seat per month depending on plan (limited-time offer, up to 65% off).
  • HubSpot plans include credits; additional HubSpot Credits are listed at $10 per 1,000.
  • Breeze agents include a customer agent, prospecting agent and data agent built on your CRM data.
  • Credit spend controls let you cap monthly usage and set feature-level limits.

What HubSpot is

HubSpot is a customer platform that brings marketing, sales, customer service, content, data and commerce software together on one CRM. It’s known for being easy to adopt, with a free CRM that many startups use as their first system of record.

HubSpot now describes itself as an “agentic customer platform.” Its AI, Breeze, includes an assistant and a team of AI agents for marketing, sales and customer service—built on your CRM data so they have context. Examples include an AI customer agent that resolves queries 24/7, a prospecting agent that identifies and engages high-value leads, and a data agent that answers questions about customers.

Key features

Free CRM and tools

Contacts, deals and free builders for websites, landing pages, forms, chatbots and live chat.

Breeze agents

Customer, prospecting and data agents that do work across the platform.

Breeze Assistant

AI help for drafting content, summarizing records and answering questions.

AEO tools

Track and improve your brand’s visibility in AI search results.

Credits and spend controls

Usage-based HubSpot Credits with monthly maximums and feature-level limits.

Best use cases

  • Startups: capture leads from website forms into a free CRM.
  • Sales teams: use the prospecting agent to find and engage leads.
  • Support teams: deploy the customer agent for 24/7 answers.
  • Marketers: run email campaigns and track AI search visibility.

How to get started

  1. Create a free HubSpot account.
  2. Import contacts and set up your deal pipeline.
  3. Add forms and live chat to your website.
  4. Try Breeze Assistant on everyday tasks.
  5. Upgrade to Starter when you need more automation, and set credit limits before enabling agents.

Tips and tricks

  • Clean your CRM data first; agents are only as good as the data behind them.
  • Set credit caps before turning on agents.
  • Start with one agent and measure results.
  • Use HubSpot Academy for free training.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Generous free CRM
  • All-in-one marketing, sales and service
  • AI agents grounded in your CRM data
  • Credit spend controls from day one
  • Huge marketplace and learning resources

👎 Cons

  • Professional and Enterprise get expensive
  • Credits add a usage-based cost layer
  • Promotional prices are temporary
  • Can be more than very small teams need

Who should (and shouldn't) use HubSpot (Breeze AI)

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Want one platform for marketing, sales and service
  • Are a startup or small business starting with a CRM
  • Want AI agents tied to customer data

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need a dedicated cold email tool (consider Instantly)
  • Want only a help desk AI agent (consider Intercom Fin or Zendesk AI)
  • Need deep SEO research (consider Semrush)

HubSpot (Breeze AI) alternatives

  • Semrush — SEO, AI search visibility, content, local, social and advertising tools on one data platform. (from $117.33/mo billed annually (SEO plan))
  • Intercom Fin — AI customer service agent that resolves conversations across chat, email, voice and social, priced per outcome. (from $49/mo base plan including 50 outcomes)
  • Zendesk AI — AI agents, Copilot and automation built into Zendesk's customer service suite. (from $19/agent/mo paid yearly (Support Team); AI Agents from Suite Team $55)
  • Apollo.io — B2B contact database, outreach sequences and AI sales agents in one go-to-market platform. (from $49 per seat/mo (Basic, billed annually))
  • Instantly — Cold email platform with unlimited sending accounts and warmup, a 450M+ B2B lead database and AI sales agents. (from $47/mo (Growth outreach plan))

FAQ

Is HubSpot free?

Yes. HubSpot offers a free CRM and free tools including website, landing page, form, chatbot and live chat builders.

How much does HubSpot Starter cost?

HubSpot is currently advertising Starter at $7 or $10 per seat per month depending on plan, up to 65% off, as a limited-time offer. Check hubspot.com for current pricing.

What are HubSpot Credits?

Credits are used when features and agents do work for you across HubSpot. Plans include credits, and you can buy more in packs or pay as you go; HubSpot lists $10 per 1,000 credits.

What is Breeze?

Breeze is HubSpot's AI, including Breeze Assistant and AI agents for marketing, sales and customer service, such as a customer agent, prospecting agent and data agent.

Can I control AI spending in HubSpot?

Yes. You can set a maximum monthly credit limit and feature- or action-level limits.
