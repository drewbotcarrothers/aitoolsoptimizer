What Writesonic is

Writesonic began as an AI writing tool and has repositioned around AI search visibility, also called GEO (generative engine optimization). As more buyers ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity instead of searching, Writesonic shows where your brand is mentioned—or ignored—in AI answers, and helps fix it.

Writesonic calls its approach the AI Search Loop: track, prioritize, act and measure. “Most AI search tools show you the gap and stop. Writesonic runs the fix and proves the lift.”

Key features

AI visibility tracking

Track prompts and answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Muse Spark.

Sentiment analysis

See how AI describes your brand (Growth).

Action Center

Agentic workflows that run on-page and off-page fixes.

AI articles and site audits

Create optimized articles and audit thousands of pages for technical issues.

Ads monitoring

Monitor ads alongside organic AI visibility.

Best use cases

  • B2B marketers: see whether AI recommends you for buyer prompts.
  • Agencies: report AI visibility to clients.
  • Content teams: publish articles that close visibility gaps.

How to get started

  1. Start a free trial at writesonic.com.
  2. Add your brand, competitors and key buyer prompts.
  3. Review where AI mentions competitors but not you.
  4. Prioritize fixes and generate content.
  5. Measure changes in visibility over time.

Tips and tricks

  • Track prompts buyers actually ask, not just keywords.
  • Compare against 3–5 competitors.
  • Earn citations on sites AI already trusts.
  • Re-check monthly to measure lift.

Example tracked prompt:

“What are the best payroll tools for US startups with under 50 employees?”

Who should (and shouldn't) use Writesonic

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Measure brand visibility in AI answers
  • Agencies offering GEO services
  • Teams combining SEO and AI search

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need a cheap AI writer (consider QuillBot or ChatGPT)
  • Need fiction help (consider Sudowrite)
  • Need classic SEO data depth (consider Semrush)

Writesonic alternatives

  • Surfer SEO — Plan, write, optimize and track content for Google and AI search. (from $82/mo billed yearly (AI Search Analytics) or $99/mo yearly (Standard))
  • Semrush — SEO, AI search visibility, content, local, social and advertising tools on one data platform. (from $117.33/mo billed annually (SEO plan))
  • Jasper — AI platform for marketing teams: on-brand content with Canvas, marketing agents, brand IQ and AI search visibility. (from $59/seat/mo billed yearly; $69 monthly (Pro))
  • Copy.ai — GTM AI platform for sales and marketing teams, with multi-model chat and automated content and prospecting workflows. (from $24/mo (Chat, billed annually); $29 monthly)
  • Frase — SEO and GEO content platform that finds topics, writes drafts in your voice, publishes and monitors pages for AI search. (from $39/mo billed yearly ($49 month to month))

FAQ

Is Writesonic free?

There's no free plan listed, but paid plans offer a free trial.

How much does Writesonic cost?

Billed annually, Starter is $79/month, Basic is $199/month and Growth is $399/month. Annual billing saves 20%. Enterprise is custom.

Is Writesonic still an AI writer?

Writesonic now focuses on AI search visibility (SEO + GEO). It still includes AI articles—25 per month on Basic and 50 on Growth—but as part of fixing visibility gaps.

Which AI platforms does Writesonic track?

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and Muse Spark.

What is the Action Center?

The Action Center runs on-page and off-page actions to fix visibility gaps; Growth includes a trial of 5 off-page and 5 on-page actions per month.

What is the AI Search Loop?

Writesonic's approach: track, prioritize, act and measure—showing where AI ignores you, fixing it with content, citations and technical work, and proving the lift.
