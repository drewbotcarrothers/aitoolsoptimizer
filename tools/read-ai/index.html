⚡ Quick verdict

Read AI goes beyond note-taking: it summarizes meetings, email and messages, coaches you on how meetings went, and lets you ask what was decided with citations. The free plan (5 meetings a month) is a good trial, and Pro at $15 per user a month billed annually adds unlimited transcripts and premium integrations. Key takeaways

  • Free: $0 with 5 meeting transcripts per month, unlimited enterprise search, summaries, a meeting coach and 20+ languages.
  • Pro: $15/user/month billed annually or $19.75 monthly, with unlimited transcripts, 100 file upload credits and premium integrations.
  • Enterprise: $22.50/user/month annually or $29.75 monthly, adding audio/video playback, video highlights and 200 upload credits.
  • Enterprise+ adds HIPAA compliance, SAML & SCIM, domain capture and custom retention (5+ licenses; talk to sales).
  • Works across Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams, with apps for Windows, macOS, Android and iOS.

What Read AI is

Read AI’s pitch is simple: “Every meeting should have outcomes.” It joins meetings on Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams, records and transcribes them, and produces summaries enriched with context from your email and calendar, plus AI agents that recommend what happens next.

Read AI is independent and platform-agnostic, so it also summarizes email and messaging and provides enterprise search across connected tools. Ask Read answers questions like “what was decided and who owns it?” with citations, and Ada acts as an AI executive assistant.

Key features

Meeting summaries and transcripts

Automatic recaps, action items and searchable transcripts in 20+ languages.

Ask Read and enterprise search

Ask about decisions, owners and open items across meetings and messages, with citations.

Speaker coach

A personalized meeting coach with feedback on how meetings went.

Topic Readouts

Summaries organized by topic across conversations.

Playback and highlights

Audio and video playback and video highlights on Enterprise.

Integrations

Basic integrations on Free; Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Confluence, Zapier and webhooks on Pro.

Best use cases

  • Managers: catch up on missed meetings in minutes.
  • Sales teams: push call notes to Salesforce or HubSpot.

  • Executives: get summaries of email and chat alongside meetings.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at read.ai and connect your calendar.
  2. Let Read AI join your next meeting.
  3. Review the summary and coaching feedback.
  4. Try Ask Read on a past decision.
  5. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited transcripts and integrations.

Tips and tricks

  • Let attendees know Read AI is present.
  • Use Ask Read before weekly planning.
  • Connect your CRM on Pro to avoid manual notes.
  • Review coaching metrics to improve meetings.

Example Ask Read question:

What did we decide about the Q3 launch date in the last three product meetings, and who owns the open items?

FAQ

Is Read AI free?

Yes. The Free plan is always free with no credit card and includes 5 meeting transcripts per month, unlimited enterprise search, summaries for meetings, email and messaging, a personal meeting coach and basic integrations.

How much does Read AI cost?

Pro is $15 per user per month billed annually ($19.75 monthly). Enterprise is $22.50 per user per month annually ($29.75 monthly). Enterprise+ is available through sales.

What is Ask Read?

Ask Read lets you ask what was decided, who owns it and what's still open, with citations to the source meetings and messages.

What is Ada?

Ada is Read AI's AI executive assistant that works alongside you to handle tasks and manage your workflow.

Which platforms does Read AI support?

Read AI works with Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams, and has apps for Windows, macOS, Android and iOS.

Which integrations are on Pro?

Pro adds premium integrations including Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Confluence, Zapier and webhooks.
