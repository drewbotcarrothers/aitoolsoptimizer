Murf AI review

Studio-quality AI voiceovers, a fast text-to-speech API, dubbing and voice agents.

Murf is a polished choice for business voiceovers—training modules, explainers and ads—with an editor that makes timing and emphasis easy, and its Falcon API publishes low per-character prices. What Murf AI is

Murf AI is a voice AI platform used to create studio-quality voiceovers with 200+ AI voices. Murf says more than 10 million developers and creators use the platform. It’s organized into three products:

  • Murf Studio — a web editor for writing scripts, choosing voices, adjusting pitch, speed and emphasis, and syncing narration with video or slides.
  • Murf API — text-to-speech, voice changer and translation endpoints, including the low-latency Falcon model for conversational agents.
  • Murf Dub — translation and dubbing of video and audio into 20+ languages.

Murf also offers Murf Agents, multilingual AI agents that handle inbound and outbound calls and chat and escalate to a human when needed.

Key features

Voice library

200+ AI voices across languages and styles; the API lists 150+ voices in 35+ languages.

Studio editor

Build voiceover projects, organize them in folders and fine-tune delivery.

Falcon real-time TTS

Time-to-first-audio under 100 ms for voice agents. Murf Falcon 2 targets human-sounding, real-time conversations.

Voice cloning and Voice Changer

Clone a voice or transform recordings into lifelike AI voices.

Dubbing and translation

Localize content at scale with Murf Dub and the translation API.

Best use cases

  • Learning designers: narrate e-learning modules consistently.
  • Marketers: voice explainer videos and ads.
  • Developers: add real-time speech to voice agents.
  • Global teams: dub English videos into Spanish and more.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at murf.ai.
  2. Paste a script into Studio and audition voices.
  3. Adjust emphasis, pauses and speed.
  4. Upgrade to download and use voiceovers commercially.
  5. Developers can grab an API key and test Falcon with the free credit.

Tips and tricks

  • Write for the ear: short sentences and clear punctuation.
  • Add pauses between sections for natural pacing.
  • Test a few voices with the same paragraph.
  • Use Falcon for agents and studio-quality voices for media.

Example script line:

Welcome to module three. In the next five minutes, you’ll learn how to file an expense report—and the two mistakes that cause most rejections.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Natural, business-friendly voices
  • Very low published API prices
  • Low-latency Falcon model for agents
  • Dubbing and voice cloning in one platform
  • Free tier to test voices

👎 Cons

  • Studio prices weren't visible to us on the official page
  • Free workspace doesn't allow downloads
  • Fewer creative/character voices than some rivals
  • Team collaboration limited to higher tiers

Who should (and shouldn't) use Murf AI

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Produce training and explainer voiceovers
  • Build real-time voice agents on a budget
  • Localize videos into other languages

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need expressive character voices for games (consider ElevenLabs)
  • Want listening/reading tools (consider Speechify)
  • Need avatar video (consider Synthesia or HeyGen)

Murf AI alternatives

  • ElevenLabs — Lifelike AI voices for text to speech, voice cloning, dubbing, music, sound effects and conversational voice agents. (from $6/mo (Starter); Creator $22)
  • Speechify — Listen to anything with text to speech, type by talking, and ask a voice AI assistant. (from $29/mo billed monthly (Premium))
  • Synthesia — Create training and business videos with AI avatars, dubbing, roleplays and surveys. (from $29/mo billed monthly (Starter))
  • HeyGen — AI video platform for realistic avatars, voice cloning and video translation in 175+ languages. (from $29/mo (Creator))
  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))

FAQ

Is Murf AI free?

There's a free Studio workspace with 10 minutes of voice generation (downloads aren't available), and new API users get $10 of free credit.

How much does the Murf API cost?

Falcon text-to-speech for real-time agents is $0.01 per 1,000 characters; studio-quality TTS is $0.03 per 1,000 characters; Voice Changer is $0.10 per minute; Translation is $0.02 per 1,000 characters. API credit never expires.

How much are Murf Studio plans?

We couldn't confirm Studio subscription prices because that section of Murf's pricing page didn't load during our check. See murf.ai/pricing for current Studio plans.

What is Murf Falcon?

Falcon is Murf's TTS model optimized for conversational AI and real-time voice agents, with time-to-first-audio under 100 ms. Murf Falcon 2 powers agents that respond in real time.

Can Murf dub videos?

Yes. Murf Dub translates videos and audio into 20+ languages with natural voices.

Does Murf support voice cloning?

Yes. Murf offers voice cloning, and professional voice cloning is listed for enterprise API customers.
