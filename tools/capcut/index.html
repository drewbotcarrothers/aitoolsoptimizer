What CapCut is

CapCut is a video editor from ByteDance, the company behind TikTok. It runs on Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and in the browser, and it is built for the way social video is made today: vertical formats, quick cuts, captions, trending effects and templates.

The basic editor is free. Paid plans unlock premium templates and features and add monthly AI credits for generative tools, including AI video generation with the models listed on CapCut’s pricing page.

Key features

Editing and templates

A multi-track timeline with transitions, effects, filters, keyframes and speed controls, plus a large library of templates you can drop your clips into.

AI helpers

Auto captions (transcribe audio to text), background removal, text-to-speech and AI voices, and tools that add voice to video. Many free tools work without credits; generative features use credits.

AI video generation

Generate short clips from text or images. CapCut’s pricing lists different models and per-second costs, with lower-resolution options that use fewer credits.

Collaboration

Teams plans add shared spaces, a brand kit and Pro features for every seat. CapCut’s help center lists seat options of 10, 15 or 30.

Best use cases

  • Short-form content: a fitness coach cuts a 60-second Reel from a longer workout video, adds auto captions and a trending template.
  • E-commerce promos: a shop owner turns product photos into a quick promo with text animations.
  • Talking-head videos: a consultant records on a phone, removes the background and adds captions.
  • AI b-roll: a creator generates a few seconds of AI footage to fill a gap in a story.

How to get started

  1. Download CapCut for desktop or mobile, or open the web editor at capcut.com.
  2. Start a new project or pick a template.
  3. Import your clips and trim them on the timeline.
  4. Add auto captions and review the transcript for errors.
  5. Export in the aspect ratio of your platform (9:16 for TikTok and Reels).
  6. Upgrade to Pro only if you need premium templates or AI generation.

Tips and tricks

  • Check the price in your account on the platform where you’ll subscribe.
  • Proofread auto captions, especially names and brand terms.
  • Use lower-resolution AI previews to save credits, then generate the final clip.
  • Hook viewers in the first two seconds with a bold caption.

Example AI video prompt:

Slow cinematic push-in on a steaming cup of coffee on a wooden table by a rainy window, cozy warm lighting, 5 seconds, vertical 9:16

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Strong free plan for social video
  • Available on desktop, mobile and web
  • Huge library of trendy templates and effects
  • AI captions and background removal save time
  • Seamless for TikTok-style vertical video

👎 Cons

  • Pricing varies by platform and region, and desktop can cost more than mobile
  • AI generation consumes credits that can run out
  • Teams can only be purchased through CapCut Online
  • Not built for long-form, professional color or audio work

Who should (and shouldn't) use CapCut

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Post short videos to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube Shorts regularly
  • Want quick captions and effects without a learning curve
  • Edit on your phone and computer interchangeably

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Edit feature films or broadcast content
  • Need transcript-based editing of podcasts (consider Descript)
  • Want AI avatars as presenters (consider Synthesia or HeyGen)

CapCut alternatives

  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))
  • OpusClip — Turn long videos into viral-ready shorts and publish them everywhere in one click. (from $15/mo (Starter, monthly only))
  • invideo AI — AI video creation platform with frontier image and video models and agents, priced by credits per seat. (from $10/seat/mo billed annually (Starter); $20 monthly)
  • Runway — AI creative suite for video, images and audio with Gen-4.5 and models from other labs. (from $12/mo billed annually (Standard))

FAQ

Is CapCut free?

Yes. CapCut's free plan includes free templates and basic editing tools on desktop, mobile and web. Premium templates, effects and AI credits require a paid plan.

How much does CapCut Pro cost?

CapCut lists Pro at $19.99 per month or $179.99 per year in the US. CapCut notes that displayed prices are for reference and final prices, taxes and offers appear after you sign in.

What is CapCut Ultra?

Ultra is CapCut's higher individual tier with more monthly AI credits and exclusive AI features on top of everything in Pro.

How much is CapCut Teams?

CapCut says Teams pricing varies by region, number of seats and billing cycle, with annual plans typically about 20% cheaper. Teams can only be bought through CapCut Online.

Does CapCut have AI video generation?

Yes. Paid plans include monthly AI credits for AI video generation using models listed on CapCut's pricing page, and you can buy more credits.
