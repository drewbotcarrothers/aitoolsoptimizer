What MagicSchool is

MagicSchool is “an AI education platform for every classroom and learner.” For teachers, it offers a large library of AI tools that save time on planning and admin—lesson materials, presentations, report card comments and more—plus an assistant called Raina. For students, it provides “safe, district-aligned AI” that helps them explore, create and build AI literacy. For district leaders, it adds oversight, privacy and professional development.

MagicSchool emphasizes trust: it’s SOC 2-certified, FERPA/COPPA-compliant and says it does not use student or teacher data to train AI.

Key features

Teacher tools

Generate lessons, differentiated materials, rubrics and report card comments.

Presentation Generator

Recently relaunched to create classroom-ready slides.

Raina

An AI assistant for educators, with prompt packs such as a back-to-school set of 20 prompts.

Student tools

Guardrailed AI experiences that teachers can launch for students.

District controls

Customization, oversight and onboarding on Enterprise.

Best use cases

  • Lesson planning: draft a unit plan aligned to your standards.
  • Differentiation: rewrite a text at several reading levels.
  • Admin: draft report card comments faster.
  • AI literacy: let students use AI safely under teacher oversight.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at magicschool.ai with your school email.
  2. Browse tools by category and try a lesson planner.
  3. Use Raina for open-ended questions.
  4. Launch a student tool with your class.
  5. Upgrade to Plus or talk to your district about Enterprise.

Tips and tricks

  • Give grade level and standards in every request.
  • Review outputs for accuracy and bias before using them.
  • Save favorite tools for quick access.
  • Share templates with colleagues.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Free plan for teachers
  • Built specifically for K-12
  • Strong privacy and compliance
  • Student tools with guardrails
  • Professional development resources

👎 Cons

  • Many tools can feel overwhelming at first
  • District pricing isn't public
  • Outputs still need teacher review
  • Less useful outside K-12

Who should (and shouldn't) use MagicSchool

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Teach K-12 and want time-saving AI tools
  • Lead a school adopting AI safely
  • Want student-facing AI with oversight

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need a one-on-one tutor for your child (consider Khanmigo)
  • Teach in higher education primarily
  • Want flashcards and study sets (consider Quizlet)

MagicSchool alternatives

  • Brisk Teaching — Free AI browser extension that helps teachers create materials, give feedback and differentiate inside Docs, Slides, YouTube and the LMS. (from Free for teachers; Premium and Intelligence plans are custom-priced)
  • Khanmigo — Khan Academy's AI tutor for learners and free AI teaching assistant for teachers, with tools for families and districts. (from $4/mo or $44/yr (learners and families))
  • Quizlet — Flashcards, practice tests, Learn mode and AI study guides for students and teachers. (from See quizlet.com/upgrade (price not confirmed))
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)

FAQ

Is MagicSchool free?

Yes. MagicSchool is forever free for individual teachers exploring AI.

How much does MagicSchool Plus cost?

MagicSchool Plus costs $8.33 per user per month billed annually or $12.99 per month billed monthly, in USD.

Is MagicSchool safe for students?

MagicSchool says it's SOC 2-certified and FERPA/COPPA-compliant, and that it doesn't use student or teacher data to train AI.

Is there a district plan?

Yes. Enterprise offers custom pricing for schools and districts that need customization and oversight at scale, with white-glove onboarding.

What can teachers create with MagicSchool?

Teachers can create lesson materials, presentations, report card comments and more, and use the Raina AI assistant with prompt packs.
