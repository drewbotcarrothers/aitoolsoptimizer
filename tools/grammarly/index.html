Grammarly review

AI writing assistant for grammar, clarity, tone and rewrites that works across your apps, now part of Superhuman.

freemium⏱ 3 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew

⚡ Quick verdict

Grammarly is still the easiest way to catch mistakes and polish writing across every app you use. The free plan handles grammar and spelling well; Pro at $12 per month (billed annually) adds full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustments and 2,000 AI prompts, which is worth it for heavy writers. Avoid monthly billing at $30. For long-form AI drafting, a chatbot like ChatGPT or Claude does more.

Key takeaways

  • Free plan covers grammar and spelling for mistake-free writing.
  • Pro: $12/member/month billed annually ($144/year), $60 quarterly or $30 monthly.
  • Pro adds full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustment and 2,000 AI prompts.
  • Enterprise, delivered through Superhuman Go, adds unlimited AI prompts, roles and permissions, and data loss prevention.
  • Grammarly Pro replaces the old Business plan and supports up to 149 seats.

What Grammarly is

Grammarly is an AI writing assistant that checks your writing as you type—in your browser, email, documents and chat apps—and suggests fixes for grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity and tone. Over the years it has added generative AI, so it can also rewrite whole sentences, adjust tone and draft text from prompts.

Grammarly is now part of Superhuman, a suite of AI tools that includes Superhuman Go, Grammarly, Coda and Superhuman Mail. For organizations, Grammarly is delivered through Superhuman Go with proactive AI that works in every app and tab.

Key features

Correctness

Catch grammar, spelling and punctuation mistakes in real time.

Clarity and tone

Make sentences concise and adjust tone to sound confident, friendly or formal.

Full-sentence rewrites (Pro)

Rewrite awkward sentences in one click.

Generative AI

Draft, reply and brainstorm with AI prompts (2,000 on Pro, unlimited on Enterprise).

Team tools

Brand tones, analytics, roles and permissions, and data loss prevention for organizations.

Best use cases

  • Professionals: send clear, polished emails and reports.
  • Students: proofread essays before submission (follow your school’s AI policy).
  • Non-native speakers: write confidently in English.
  • Teams: keep brand tone consistent across customer communications.

How to get started

  1. Create a free account at grammarly.com.
  2. Install the browser extension and desktop app.
  3. Set your writing goals (audience, formality).
  4. Write normally and review the underlined suggestions.
  5. Upgrade to Pro (annual) if you want rewrites and more AI prompts.

Tips and tricks

  • Set goals per document so tone suggestions fit the audience.
  • Don’t accept everything; keep your voice where it matters.
  • Turn off Grammarly for sensitive documents if your policy requires it.
  • Choose annual billing to save 60% vs. monthly.

Example AI prompt:

Rewrite this paragraph to sound confident but friendly, under 80 words, for a client who missed a payment deadline.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Works almost everywhere you write
  • Strong free plan
  • Clear, explainable suggestions
  • Annual Pro pricing is reasonable
  • Team features for brand consistency

👎 Cons

  • Monthly Pro billing is expensive
  • AI prompt cap on Pro
  • Suggestions can flatten personal voice
  • Not a full long-form writing tool

Who should (and shouldn't) use Grammarly

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Write lots of emails, reports or essays
  • Are writing in English as a second language
  • Want consistent tone across a team

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need long creative drafting (consider Sudowrite or Claude)
  • Mainly need paraphrasing on a budget (consider QuillBot)
  • Can't use cloud-based writing tools

Grammarly alternatives

  • QuillBot — AI paraphraser, grammar checker, summarizer, AI detector and humanizer in one writing toolkit. (from $8.33/mo billed annually (Premium))
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Claude — Anthropic's AI assistant for writing, analysis, research, coding and long-running work, with Claude Code included on paid plans. (from $17/mo (Pro, annual; $20 monthly); Max from $100)
  • Jasper — AI platform for marketing teams: on-brand content with Canvas, marketing agents, brand IQ and AI search visibility. (from $59/seat/mo billed yearly; $69 monthly (Pro))
  • Writesonic — Track and improve your brand's visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and more. (from $79/mo billed annually (Starter))

FAQ

Is Grammarly free?

Yes. Grammarly Free helps make your writing mistake-free at no cost.

How much does Grammarly Pro cost?

Grammarly Pro costs $12 per member per month billed annually ($144 per year), $60 per quarter, or $30 billed monthly, in USD.

What does Grammarly Pro add?

Pro adds full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustments and 2,000 generative AI prompts on top of everything in Free.

Is Grammarly part of Superhuman?

Yes. Grammarly is now part of Superhuman, a suite that includes Superhuman Go, Grammarly, Coda and Superhuman Mail. Enterprise Grammarly is delivered through Superhuman Go.

Does Grammarly have a team plan?

Grammarly Pro replaces the Business plan and supports up to 149 seats. Larger organizations can use Enterprise.
