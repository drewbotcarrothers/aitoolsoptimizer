Khanmigo review

Khan Academy's AI tutor for learners and free AI teaching assistant for teachers, with tools for families and districts.

⚡ Quick verdict

Khanmigo is one of the most trustworthy AI tutors for students because it's designed not to just hand over answers: it asks questions and guides learners to work things out. At $4 a month for families, it's inexpensive, and teachers get the planning tools free. Older students who need a general-purpose assistant may still want ChatGPT or Claude alongside it.

Key takeaways

  • Free for teachers: lesson planning, rubrics, exit tickets and student work summaries.
  • Learners and families: $4/month or $44/year (sales tax not included).
  • Parents can add up to 10 children, view interaction history and get moderation alerts.
  • District Tools add student access, data, SSO and rostering via Clever or ClassLink (pricing on request).
  • Built by the education nonprofit Khan Academy and tied to its content library.

What Khanmigo is

Khanmigo is “an AI-powered personal tutor and teaching assistant from trusted education nonprofit Khan Academy.” For learners, it acts like a patient tutor: when a student types a homework question, Khanmigo asks guiding questions so they discover the answer themselves. For teachers, it’s a free assistant that cuts planning time.

Because it’s tied to Khan Academy’s library, Khanmigo can tutor across math, science, writing, coding, SAT prep and more, with privacy and safety controls designed for schools and families.

Key features

Socratic tutoring

Personalized help that builds understanding rather than giving away answers.

Writing Coach

Guided prompts and immediate feedback for writing and debate practice.

Teacher tools

Lesson plans, rubrics, exit tickets, learning objectives and student work summaries.

Family controls

Add up to 10 children, view their chat history and get moderation alerts.

District tools

Classroom access, data dashboards, SSO and automatic rostering.

Best use cases

  • Homework help: a student gets guided through an algebra problem step by step.
  • Lesson planning: a teacher generates a differentiated lesson and exit ticket.
  • Homeschooling: parents use it as a tutor and monitor progress.
  • Writing: students practice essays with feedback.

How to get started

  1. Teachers: sign up free at khanmigo.ai.
  2. Families: create a parent account and choose monthly or annual.
  3. Add your children and review settings.
  4. Have students start with a subject they’re working on.
  5. Check history and alerts regularly.

Tips and tricks

  • Encourage students to explain their thinking to Khanmigo.
  • Teachers: paste your unit objectives for better lesson plans.
  • Pick annual billing to save a little.
  • Review chat history together with younger kids.

FAQ

Is Khanmigo free?

Khanmigo is free for teachers. Learners and families pay $4 per month or $44 per year, plus sales tax.

How is Khanmigo different from ChatGPT?

Khanmigo is an AI tutor and teaching assistant from Khan Academy. It challenges students to think critically and solve problems without giving direct answers, and it's built around Khan Academy's content.

How many children can I add?

With a monthly or annual subscription on your parent account, you can enable access for up to 10 children.

What can teachers do with Khanmigo?

Teachers can generate standards-aligned, differentiated lesson plans, summarize student work, create learning objectives, rubrics and exit tickets, and refresh content knowledge.

Is there a district plan?

Yes. Khanmigo District Tools include access for teachers and students, district and school data, SSO and rostering through Clever or ClassLink, and a customer success manager. Pricing is on request.
