Krea review

Creative AI suite for real-time image generation, video, upscaling, LoRA training, Nodes workflows and the Krea Agent.

⚡ Quick verdict

Krea is one of the most versatile creative AI suites: real-time generation for fast iteration, a wide model library for images and video, powerful upscaling and node-based workflows. The free daily units are enough to explore, and Pro at $21 per month (yearly) unlocks all video models and a commercial-use-friendly toolset for working creatives.

Key takeaways

  • Free: 100 compute units per day, real-time models, Krea Agent and Krea 2, no credit card.
  • Basic: $9/month or $5/month yearly, 5,000 units (about 64 Nano Banana 2 images or 20 Seedance 2.0 videos).
  • Pro: $35/month or $21/month yearly, 20,000 units, all video models and upscaling up to 22K.
  • Max: $105/month or $63/month yearly, 60,000 units (about 250 Seedance 2.0 videos).
  • Paid plans include a commercial license and unlimited relaxed generations on supported in-house models.

What Krea is

Krea is a creative AI platform that gathers leading image and video models in one interface and adds its own tools for iteration and production. It became known for real-time generation—images update as you type or draw—and has grown into a full suite with upscaling, LoRA fine-tuning, video style transfer, node-based workflows and an agent. Krea says it’s trusted by 40 million creatives, enterprises, agencies and studios.

Its in-house models (Krea 2 and the newer Krea 3) sit alongside third-party models such as Nano Banana, Seedance and Veo, all paid for with compute units.

Key features

Real-time generation

Iterate instantly as you type or sketch.

Images and video

Generate with many models; Pro unlocks all video models.

Upscale and enhance

Upscale up to 2K on free and up to 22K with paid upscalers like Topaz Standard.

LoRA training

Personalize models with your own characters, styles or objects.

Krea Nodes, App Builder and Agent

Build visual workflows, turn them into reusable apps, or brief the Krea Agent to plan and iterate across models.

Plugins, API and MCP

Use Krea in Photoshop, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, 3D tools and chat assistants.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at krea.ai.
  2. Try real-time generation with a simple prompt.
  3. Explore the model picker for images and video.
  4. Upscale a favorite result.
  5. Upgrade to Pro for all video models and more units.

Tips and tricks

  • Use real-time mode to find a composition, then render with a premium model.
  • Train a LoRA for consistent brand characters.
  • Use relaxed generations to save units on paid plans.
  • Pick yearly billing to save 40%.

Example prompt:

People running through a city park at dawn, risograph poster style, two-color print in teal and orange, grainy texture

Who should (and shouldn't) use Krea

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Iterate visually and quickly on concepts
  • Want access to many models without separate subscriptions
  • Need upscaling and LoRA training

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want a simple one-model tool (consider Midjourney or Ideogram)
  • Need avatar videos (consider HeyGen)
  • Need only template-based design (consider Canva)

FAQ

Is Krea free?

Yes. The free plan includes 100 compute units per day, real-time models, the Krea Agent and Krea 2, with no credit card required.

How much does Krea cost?

Basic is $9 per month or $5 per month billed yearly, Pro is $35 or $21 yearly, and Max is $105 or $63 yearly. Business and Enterprise are also available.

What do Krea compute units buy?

Krea estimates Basic's 5,000 units cover about 64 Nano Banana 2 generations or 20 Seedance 2.0 videos; Pro's 20,000 about 257 images or 83 videos; Max's 60,000 about 771 images or 250 videos.

Can I use Krea images commercially?

Krea's paid plans include a commercial license to use generated content for marketing, products and client work.

Does Krea work in Photoshop?

Yes. Krea offers plugins to generate and edit images in Photoshop, animate in After Effects and create video in DaVinci Resolve, among others.
