Luma AI review

⚡ Quick verdict

Luma AI stands out because it builds its own models: Ray 3.2 gives frame-level direction and continuity across cuts, and Uni-1 keeps brand style and characters consistent. Agents that research, generate and refine across media make it more of a creative studio than a single generator. It's priced for professionals, starting at $30 a month.

  • Plus: $30/month or $300/year, 10,000 credits, Luma and third-party models, commercial use.

  • Ultra: $300/month or $3,000/year, 150,000 credits and 15x agent usage.
  • Team and Enterprise add shared credits, SSO, analytics and custom fine-tuning.
  • Ray 3.2 offers frame-by-frame control and continuity for cinematic video.

What Luma AI is

Luma AI is a creative AI company that builds its own models and a platform for creative teams. “Speed like this doesn’t come from a wrapper,” Luma says. “We build the models underneath Luma.” Its mission statement goes further, aiming to build general intelligence that can generate, understand and operate in the physical world.

For creators, the headline models are Ray 3.2, which lets you “direct video the way you would direct a shoot,” and Uni-1, which “understands your brand as deeply as it generates for it,” carrying style and character references. Luma Agents research, generate and refine across video, image, audio and text.

Key features

Ray 3.2 video

Frame-level direction, continuity across cuts and cinematic finishing.

Uni-1

Brand intelligence at the model level, with style and character references.

Luma Agents

Explore many creative directions quickly across media types.

Third-party models

Plans include Luma and third-party image and video models.

Collaboration

Edit access for guest collaborators; Team plans add projects, sharing and SSO.

Best use cases

  • Agencies: generate many campaign directions before a client meeting.
  • Brands: keep characters and style consistent across assets.
  • Filmmakers: previsualize scenes with directed camera work.
  • Marketers: produce short cinematic product videos.

How to get started

  1. Create an account at lumalabs.ai.
  2. Subscribe to Plus to get credits.
  3. Upload brand references for Uni-1.
  4. Brief an agent or generate with Ray 3.2.
  5. Invite collaborators to review.

Tips and tricks

  • Provide style and character references for consistency.
  • Think like a director: specify shots, angles and transitions.
  • Let agents explore broadly before refining one direction.
  • Choose yearly billing to save up to 20%.

Who should (and shouldn't) use Luma AI

  • Produce brand content at volume
  • Want directed cinematic control
  • Work in an agency or creative team

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

FAQ

How much does Luma AI cost?

Plus is $30 per month ($300 per year), Pro is $90 per month ($900 per year) and Ultra is $300 per month ($3,000 per year). Team and Enterprise pricing is by contact.

Is there a free Luma plan?

Luma's pricing page lists Plus, Pro and Ultra individual plans and business plans; no free plan was listed when we checked.

What is Ray 3.2?

Ray 3.2 is Luma's video model that lets you direct every frame, hold continuity across cuts and finish in cinematic quality.

What are Luma Agents?

Luma Agents research, generate and refine across video, image, audio and text while keeping your brand consistent. Pro includes 4x and Ultra 15x the agent usage of Plus.

Can I use Luma content commercially?

Yes. Commercial use is included starting with the Plus plan.
