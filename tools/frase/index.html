What Frase is

Frase describes itself as “the content operating system for AI search.” It checks your site, finds what your buyers are searching for, and writes drafts in your voice. You approve the ones you like and the pages go live, then Frase keeps watching them, flagging decay in Google and tracking whether AI assistants mention you.

That loop—discover, draft, publish, monitor—is what separates today’s Frase from the content-brief tool many marketers remember. It’s built for a world where buyers ask ChatGPT or Google’s AI answers as often as they click blue links.

Key features

Topic discovery and audits

Frase surfaces “the searches your buyers are already making that nobody in your category has answered well,” and audits existing pages.

Drafts in your voice

AI-written articles tailored to your brand, with SEO and GEO scores to guide edits.

Publishing and agent mode

Publish to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity, Wix or FraseCMS. The agent can publish on its own when you allow it.

AI Visibility

Track mentions in ChatGPT and Google AI on every plan, with Perplexity added on Professional and Claude and Gemini on Scale. Professional also adds AI crawler monitoring.

Content Guard

Monitors pages for Google decay so you know what to refresh.

Team tools

Content calendar, internal linking suggestions, channel repurposing and exportable client reports on higher tiers. Frase Answers is a free conversational widget for your site.

Best use cases

  • Solo founders: publish a steady stream of buyer-focused articles for one site.
  • In-house teams: run a content calendar with internal linking suggestions.
  • Agencies: manage up to 10 client domains with exportable reports.
  • Refresh programs: use Content Guard to prioritize pages losing traffic.

How to get started

  1. Start the free 7-day trial (no card required).
  2. Connect your site and run the first audit.
  3. Review suggested topics and approve a few drafts.
  4. Edit drafts with your own expertise and examples.
  5. Connect your CMS and publish.
  6. Check AI Visibility and Content Guard weekly.

Tips and tricks

  • Add original data and experience to every draft; it helps both rankings and AI citations.
  • Keep agent publishing off until you trust the drafts.
  • Prioritize decaying pages before writing new ones.
  • Use Frase Answers to learn what visitors ask, then write content for it.

Example brief instruction:

Write a buyer’s guide for small dental clinics choosing practice management software. Answer pricing, migration and HIPAA questions directly in the first paragraph of each section, and include a comparison table.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Covers both traditional SEO and AI search optimization
  • Publishes directly to popular CMSs
  • Monitors pages for ranking decay
  • Clear plan limits and a no-card trial
  • Agent can run the loop autonomously when you allow it

👎 Cons

  • No free plan
  • Article caps on lower tiers
  • AI drafts still need editing and expertise
  • Less backlink and competitive data than full SEO suites

Who should (and shouldn't) use Frase

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Want an automated, approval-based content pipeline
  • Care about visibility in ChatGPT and Google AI answers
  • Manage several sites or clients

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need deep backlink and PPC data (consider Semrush)
  • Only need occasional blog posts (consider Jasper or Writesonic)
  • Have no budget for a monthly subscription

Frase alternatives

  • Surfer SEO — Plan, write, optimize and track content for Google and AI search. (from $82/mo billed yearly (AI Search Analytics) or $99/mo yearly (Standard))
  • Semrush — SEO, AI search visibility, content, local, social and advertising tools on one data platform. (from $117.33/mo billed annually (SEO plan))
  • Writesonic — Track and improve your brand's visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and more. (from $79/mo billed annually (Starter))
  • Jasper — AI platform for marketing teams: on-brand content with Canvas, marketing agents, brand IQ and AI search visibility. (from $59/seat/mo billed yearly; $69 monthly (Pro))

FAQ

How much does Frase cost?

Frase Starter is $39 per month billed yearly or $49 monthly, Professional is $103 yearly or $129 monthly, and Scale is $239 yearly or $299 monthly. Enterprise is custom.

Does Frase have a free plan?

No, but there's a free 7-day trial that doesn't require a credit card.

What is GEO in Frase?

GEO, or generative engine optimization, means optimizing content to appear in AI answers. Frase scores content for GEO and tracks your AI Visibility in tools like ChatGPT and Google AI.

Which CMSs does Frase publish to?

Frase publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Sanity and Wix, or you can host content on FraseCMS.

What is Content Guard?

Content Guard watches your pages for Google ranking decay: 10 pages on Starter, 75 on Professional and 300 on Scale.
