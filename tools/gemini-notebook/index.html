Gemini Notebook review

Google's AI research and thinking partner (formerly NotebookLM), grounded in your sources, with Audio and Video Overviews.

⚡ Quick verdict

Gemini Notebook is one of the most useful free AI tools available: it answers questions using only the sources you upload, with citations, and turns dense material into Audio Overviews you can listen to. The free tier covers most individual needs; Google AI Pro raises limits about 5x, and Workspace and Google Cloud add business-grade data protection. It's a research companion, not a general web search engine.

Key takeaways

  • Free to use in the browser with a Google account in supported countries.
  • Google AI Plus ($4.99/mo) adds more Audio Overviews and notebooks; Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo) offers 5x more.
  • Included in all Google Workspace plans, with enterprise options via Google Cloud.
  • Enterprise users get 5x or more artifacts, VPC-SC and IAM controls, and data that isn't used to train models.
  • Answers cite your sources, and generated images and videos carry SynthID watermarks.

What Gemini Notebook is

Gemini Notebook is Google’s “AI research and thinking partner, grounded in the information you trust.” You create a notebook, add sources—PDFs, Google Docs and Slides, and other files—and then ask questions, summarize, compare and create, with answers tied back to citations in your sources.

Its standout feature is Audio Overviews: one click turns your documents into a listenable conversation, so you can learn on the go. Gemini Notebook also creates Video Overviews, reports and mind maps. It’s now included in all Google Workspace plans.

Key features

Source-grounded chat

Ask questions and get answers with citations for verification.

Audio and Video Overviews

Turn documents into listenable or watchable summaries.

Reports and mind maps

Generate executive summaries, extract metrics and map out topics visually.

Drive integration and sharing

Import Docs and Slides directly from Drive and share notebooks with your team.

Enterprise controls

Advanced sharing, VPC-SC, IAM controls, data regionalization and enterprise-grade data protection through Google Cloud.

Best use cases

  • Students: upload lecture notes and readings, then quiz yourself.
  • Executives: summarize financial reports and strategy documents.
  • HR and onboarding: new hires ask questions of manuals and policies.
  • Sales: create meeting plans from product specs and market research.

How to get started

  1. Go to Gemini Notebook and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Create a notebook around one topic or project.
  3. Add sources from your computer or Drive.
  4. Ask a question and click citations to verify answers.
  5. Generate an Audio Overview for a quick listen.
  6. Share the notebook with collaborators.

Tips and tricks

  • Keep notebooks focused; one topic per notebook gives sharper answers.
  • Ask for comparisons across sources to spot disagreements.
  • Customize Audio Overviews for a specific audience or focus.
  • Always check citations before relying on an answer.

Example prompt:

Using only these sources, create a one-page study guide with the 10 most important concepts, a definition for each, and three practice questions with answers.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Generous free tier
  • Citations make answers easy to verify
  • Audio Overviews are great for learning on the go
  • Imports Google Docs and Slides directly from Drive
  • Enterprise-grade protection on Workspace and Cloud

👎 Cons

  • Only as good as the sources you add
  • Higher limits require a Google AI plan or business plan
  • Not designed for open-ended web research
  • Mobile app availability varies by country

Who should (and shouldn't) use Gemini Notebook

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Need to digest long PDFs, reports or course readings
  • Want cited answers from your own documents
  • Already use Google Workspace

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want live web search answers (consider Perplexity or Gemini)
  • Need systematic academic literature search (consider Elicit or Consensus)
  • Can't upload documents to Google services

Gemini Notebook alternatives

  • Perplexity — AI answer engine with cited sources, Deep Research, the Comet browser and Perplexity Computer. (from $17/mo billed annually (Pro))
  • Elicit — AI research assistant that searches, summarizes and extracts data from scientific papers, including systematic reviews. (from $11/user/mo (Plus, billed annually))
  • Consensus — AI search engine for peer-reviewed research that gives cited, evidence-based answers. (from $12/mo (Pro, $144 billed annually); $20 monthly)
  • Google Gemini — Google's AI assistant for chat, research, image and video generation, built into Gmail, Docs and Android. (from $4.99/mo (Google AI Plus))
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)

FAQ

Is Gemini Notebook the same as NotebookLM?

Gemini Notebook is Google's source-grounded research notebook, the product previously known as NotebookLM, and it's documented in Google's Gemini Notebook Help Center.

Is Gemini Notebook free?

Yes. It's available in the browser to users above the age of consent in supported countries. Paid Google AI plans, Workspace and Google Cloud raise limits.

What do paid plans add?

Google AI Plus ($4.99/month) includes more Audio Overviews and notebooks, and Google AI Pro ($19.99/month) offers 5x more Audio Overviews, notebooks and more. Enterprise plans add 5x or more artifacts and advanced security.

What is an Audio Overview?

An Audio Overview turns your documents into a listenable, podcast-style conversation so you can understand the core content while multitasking.

Is my data used to train models?

For Gemini Notebook for enterprise through Google Cloud, Google says files, chats and outputs aren't reviewed by humans or used to improve generative AI models. Check the terms for your account type.
