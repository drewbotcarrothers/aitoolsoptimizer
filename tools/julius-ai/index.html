Julius AI review

AI workspace to analyze data, research, and create charts, presentations, reports, websites, images and video.

freemium⏱ 3 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Julius AI → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Julius AI makes data analysis approachable: upload a spreadsheet, ask a question and get charts and explanations, with frontier models doing the heavy lifting. The free plan is enough to try it; Plus at $16-$20 a month fits most individuals, and Pro adds more memory and credits. Teams with data warehouses get connectors on Business, though dedicated platforms like Hex offer more control.

Key takeaways

  • Free plan with daily credits; every plan can create presentations, websites and videos.
  • Plus: $20/month or $16/month billed annually, 2,000 monthly credits, 16 GB RAM.
  • Pro: $45/month or $37 annually, 5,000 credits, 32 GB RAM, unlimited parallel agents.
  • Business: $450/month or $375 annually, 60,000 credits, data connectors (Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake) and up to 50 members.
  • All paid plans add 100 daily refresh credits and frontier models.

What Julius AI is

Julius AI is an AI workspace that started as an “AI data analyst” and now covers research and creation too. You upload a spreadsheet, CSV or document—or connect a database on Business—and ask questions in plain English. Julius writes and runs the analysis behind the scenes and returns charts, tables and explanations you can turn into presentations and reports.

Its pricing page sums it up: “Analyze data and research, create presentations and reports, build websites, and generate images and video.”

Key features

Conversational analysis

Ask questions like “Which region grew fastest?” and get charts and answers.

Charts, presentations and reports

Turn findings into shareable visuals and slides.

Agents

Browser agents for research and web tasks, with unlimited parallel agents on Pro.

Data connectors

Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake and more on Business, with no table limits.

Creation tools

Publish websites on a custom Julius domain and generate images and video.

Best use cases

  • Students: analyze survey data for a class project.
  • Small businesses: find trends in sales spreadsheets.
  • Analysts: produce quick charts for a weekly report.
  • Researchers: clean and visualize datasets.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at julius.ai.
  2. Upload a spreadsheet or CSV.
  3. Ask a simple question about the data.
  4. Request a chart and refine it.
  5. Turn results into a presentation or report.

Tips and tricks

  • Describe your columns if names are unclear.
  • Ask Julius to show its method so you can verify results.
  • Break big questions into steps to save credits.
  • Remove sensitive data before uploading.

Example prompt:

Using this sales CSV, show monthly revenue by region for 2026 as a line chart, identify the fastest-growing region, and explain the result in three sentences.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • No coding required
  • Free plan to start
  • Combines analysis with presentations and reports
  • Access to frontier models
  • Database connectors for teams

👎 Cons

  • Credits can run out on large analyses
  • Business plan is a big jump in price
  • Results need checking for statistical correctness
  • Less governance than dedicated BI platforms

Who should (and shouldn't) use Julius AI

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Analyze spreadsheets without code
  • Need quick charts for reports or classes
  • Want analysis and slides in one tool

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Run a governed analytics stack (consider Hex)
  • Need general writing help (consider ChatGPT or Claude)
  • Must keep data fully on-premises

Julius AI alternatives

  • Hex — Analytics platform for humans and agents: SQL and Python notebooks, the Hex agent, interactive data apps and self-serve exploration. (from $36/editor/mo (Professional))
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Claude — Anthropic's AI assistant for writing, analysis, research, coding and long-running work, with Claude Code included on paid plans. (from $17/mo (Pro, annual; $20 monthly); Max from $100)
  • Google Gemini — Google's AI assistant for chat, research, image and video generation, built into Gmail, Docs and Android. (from $4.99/mo (Google AI Plus))

FAQ

Is Julius AI free?

Yes. The Free plan includes a daily credit allowance, and every plan can create presentations, websites and videos.

How much does Julius AI cost?

Plus is $20 per month or $16 billed annually, Pro is $45 or $37 annually, and Business is $450 or $375 annually. Enterprise is custom.

What data sources does Julius support?

You can analyze spreadsheets, documents and data files on any plan. Business adds connectors for Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake and more.

Which AI models does Julius use?

Paid plans include access to frontier AI models, which Julius lists as including GPT-6.1 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5.

Can teams use Julius?

Yes. Business lets you invite up to 50 team members and collaborate.
