Pitch review

Collaborative presentation software with AI slide generation, analytics and deal rooms.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Pitch → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Pitch is one of the nicest places for a team to build decks: a sleek editor, real-time collaboration and AI that drafts on-brand slides. The free plan covers small teams of up to five, and paid plans from $10 per seat a month (yearly) add AI credits, custom fonts and PowerPoint export, then analytics and deal rooms for sales. If you just need a one-off AI deck, a generator like Gamma may be faster.

Key takeaways

  • Free: $0 forever for 1–5 members with unlimited presentations, custom templates and 100 AI credits that don't renew.
  • Plus: $10/seat/month billed yearly for one member, with 500 AI credits per seat per month, custom fonts, video uploads and PPTX export.
  • Team: $15/seat/month (1–25 members) adds advanced links with analytics, teamspaces and pitch rooms.
  • Business: $20/seat/month for up to 200 members; Enterprise is required above 200 members (minimum 30 seats).
  • Yearly billing gives a 17% discount; commenters aren't charged.

What Pitch is

Pitch is collaborative presentation software. Pitch says 4M+ teams use it to “create and deliver winning slides together.” You can start from a blank canvas or template, or use AI to generate on-brand slides in seconds, then refine every detail in a polished editor with high-resolution video, animations and custom fonts.

Where Pitch stands out is what happens around the deck: live co-editing and slide assignments, shared templates and a brand library, personalized decks generated in bulk from customer data, deal rooms, and analytics that show who opened your deck and how long they spent on each slide.

Key features

Editor and AI

A modern slide editor with AI generation and AI actions powered by credits.

Collaboration

Live co-editing, comments, slide assignments and teamspaces.

Brand consistency

Custom templates, custom fonts and a brand library.

Sharing and analytics

Advanced links track visits, slide views and time per slide; pitch rooms bundle decks and resources in one link.

Sales personalization

Import customer data and generate personalized decks in bulk.

Export

PowerPoint (PPTX) export without Pitch branding on paid plans.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at pitch.com.
  2. Generate a first draft with AI or pick a template.
  3. Invite teammates and assign slides.
  4. Apply your brand fonts and colors.
  5. Share a link and review engagement analytics.

Tips and tricks

  • Build a custom template before scaling deck creation.
  • Use AI for structure, then edit for voice.
  • Track links to time sales follow-ups.
  • Add commenters free for reviews.

Example AI prompt:

Create a 10-slide seed pitch deck for a B2B scheduling app: problem, solution, market size, traction, business model, competition, team and ask, in our brand colors.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Beautiful, modern editor
  • Strong real-time collaboration
  • Free plan for small teams
  • Engagement analytics on links
  • Brand consistency tools

👎 Cons

  • Free AI credits don't renew
  • Plus is limited to one member
  • Analytics and rooms need Team or higher
  • Less AI-automated than dedicated deck generators

Who should (and shouldn't) use Pitch

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Teams co-creating decks
  • Sales teams tracking deck engagement
  • Brands needing consistent templates

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want a full deck from one prompt (consider Gamma)
  • Must stay in PowerPoint or Google Slides (consider Plus AI)
  • Need design templates for all media (consider Canva)

Pitch alternatives

  • Gamma — AI design partner that generates presentations, documents, websites and social posts from a prompt. (from $9/seat/mo (Plus))
  • Beautiful.ai — AI presentation software with auto-aligning Smart Slides that keep decks polished and on-brand. (from $14.50/mo (Pro, billed annually); $45 monthly)
  • Plus AI — Create and edit AI presentations directly inside Google Slides and PowerPoint. (from $10/user/mo billed annually (Basic))
  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))
  • Napkin AI — Turn text into infographics, diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps and slides. (from $9/person/mo (Plus))

FAQ

Is Pitch free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 forever for workspaces of 1–5 members, with unlimited presentations, custom templates, basic sharing links and 100 AI credits that don't renew once used.

How much does Pitch cost?

Billed yearly, Plus is $10 per seat per month, Team is $15 and Business is $20. Yearly subscriptions are 17% cheaper than monthly. Enterprise pricing is through sales.

Who is the Plus plan for?

Plus is for a single member—solo entrepreneurs, freelancers and students. It can't be selected for a workspace with more than one member.

How do Pitch AI credits work?

Credits are used for AI actions that speed up slide creation. Free includes 100 one-time credits; Plus includes 500 credits per seat per month that renew with your subscription and are shared across the team.

Can I export Pitch to PowerPoint?

Yes. Paid plans export presentations as PPTX files without Pitch branding.

Does Pitch track who views my deck?

Team and higher include advanced links that track visits, slide views and time spent per slide, plus pitch rooms for sharing decks and resources.
