Udio review

Create, extend, remix and edit AI songs with Voices and Styles.

freemium⏱ 3 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Udio → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Udio is a strong alternative to Suno for people who like to shape their songs: extend sections, remix, blend and reduce Styles, edit lyrics and music, and use Voices. The free plan's 10 daily credits are enough to experiment; Standard at $10 a month ($8 annually) unlocks Voices, uploads and editing; Pro is for heavy creators. Check Udio's terms on downloads and usage rights before commercial projects.

Key takeaways

  • Free: $0 with 100 credits per month (10 per day), 2 simultaneous generations and up to three 130-second songs per day.
  • Standard: $10/month or $8/month billed annually ($96/year) with 2,400 credits, Voices, audio uploads, premium Artist Styles and editing.
  • Pro: $30/month or $24/month billed annually ($288/year) with 6,000 credits, 5 simultaneous generations and song access permissions.
  • Extra credits: 100 for $3 or 1,000 for $25; student pricing is available.
  • Prices are shown in USD, GBP or EUR; tax rates vary by location.

What Udio is

Udio is an AI music generator. You describe a song—or start from a musical reference—and Udio creates a track with vocals and instruments, which you can then extend, remix and edit. Its toolset is built for iteration: create short 32-second clips or longer 130-second songs, add sections, change lyrics, blend styles and apply voices until the song sounds right.

Key features

Create songs

Generate 32-second and 130-second songs from prompts.

Extend and remix

Grow a song section by section or remix it in new directions (all plans).

Styles

Create with a musical reference on all plans; paid plans add premium Artist Styles and blending or reducing Styles.

Voices

Use Udio’s library of Voices and save and reuse your own (paid).

Edit

Edit music and lyrics, upload your own audio and create or upload cover art (paid).

Sharing controls

Adjust song access permissions on Pro.

Best use cases

  • Songwriters: test lyrics in different genres.
  • Producers: generate ideas to rework.
  • Content creators: custom background music.
  • Hobbyists: explore styles and voices for fun.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at udio.com.
  2. Write a prompt with genre, mood and topic.
  3. Generate a short clip and extend the best one.
  4. Try a Style reference for a different sound.
  5. Upgrade for Voices, uploads and editing.

Tips and tricks

  • Build in sections with Extend.
  • Use Styles to keep a consistent sound.
  • Edit lyrics instead of regenerating.
  • Buy credit packs for occasional bursts.

Example prompt:

Melancholic lo-fi hip-hop about a rainy night in Tokyo, soft female vocals, vinyl crackle, mellow Rhodes piano, 80 BPM.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Fine-grained editing tools
  • Styles and Voices for control
  • Free daily credits
  • Affordable annual plans
  • Credit packs for bursts

👎 Cons

  • Free plan limits song length and count
  • Many features locked to paid plans
  • Usage rights need checking
  • Quality varies by genre

Who should (and shouldn't) use Udio

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Iterate on songs section by section
  • Use a reference style for new music
  • Experiment with AI music on a budget

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want an all-in-one DAW workflow (consider Suno)
  • Need voiceovers (consider ElevenLabs)
  • Need text to speech (consider Speechify)

Udio alternatives

  • Suno — Make full songs from a prompt, then edit them in a browser-based DAW. (from $8/mo billed yearly (Pro))
  • ElevenLabs — Lifelike AI voices for text to speech, voice cloning, dubbing, music, sound effects and conversational voice agents. (from $6/mo (Starter); Creator $22)
  • Murf AI — Studio-quality AI voiceovers, a fast text-to-speech API, dubbing and voice agents. (from API from $0.01 per 1,000 characters; Studio plans on pricing page)
  • Speechify — Listen to anything with text to speech, type by talking, and ask a voice AI assistant. (from $29/mo billed monthly (Premium))
  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))

FAQ

Is Udio free?

Yes. The Free plan includes 100 credits per month with a 10-credit daily limit, 2 simultaneous generations and up to three 130-second songs per day, with no credit card required.

How much does Udio cost?

Standard is $10/month or $8/month billed annually ($96 a year). Pro is $30/month or $24/month billed annually ($288 a year). Student discounts are available.

What are Udio Voices?

Paid plans can create songs with Udio's library of Voices and save and reuse your own Voices.

What are Styles?

Styles let you create with a musical reference. Paid plans add premium Artist Styles and the ability to blend and reduce Styles.

Can I buy more credits?

Yes. Udio sells 100 credits for $3.00 and 1,000 credits for $25.00.

Can I upload my own audio?

Uploading your own audio files is available on Standard and Pro.
