Canva review

Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations.

freemium⏱ 5 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Canva → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Canva is the default design tool for non-designers for good reason: an excellent free plan, a huge template library and AI features woven into the editor. Pro is worth it once you need background removal, Magic Resize, brand kits and premium assets. Teams should compare Business pricing carefully, since it is billed per person and rose to $250 a year in 2026.

Key takeaways

  • Canva Free includes a drag-and-drop editor, 1,000+ design types, 1.6M+ templates and 4.7M+ assets.
  • Pro costs $18 per month or $180 per year for one person (US, before tax).
  • Business costs $25 per person per month or $250 per person per year, with team admin tools and more AI.
  • Paid plans get a larger monthly AI allowance (Pro 10x and Business 20x Canva Free); some standard AI tools don't count toward it.
  • K-12 schools and eligible nonprofits can get most premium features free.

What Canva is

Canva is a browser-based design platform used by individuals, schools and companies to create almost anything visual: social posts, presentations, documents, whiteboards, videos, websites, print materials and more. Canva says 6 million-plus teams use it. You start from one of millions of templates or a blank canvas, drag in photos, text and graphics, and export, print or publish.

Over the past few years Canva has layered AI throughout the editor under the Canva AI name, including Magic Write for text, image generation, background removal, translation, Magic Resize and Canva AI 2.0. Pro subscribers also get access to Affinity with AI.

Key features

Templates and assets

The free plan alone includes 1.6M+ templates and 4.7M+ assets. Pro expands that to 3.6M+ templates including premium ones and a much larger premium stock library.

Canva AI

Write and rewrite copy, generate images, remove backgrounds, translate designs and resize them for other formats. Paid plans include higher AI allowances, and Canva AI connectors link Canva to other tools.

Brand Kits and team tools

Save logos, colors and fonts as Brand Kits. Business adds team admin, approvals and collaboration controls.

Publishing

Schedule social posts, present slides, share links, publish simple websites or order prints (Business gets 10% off print orders).

Best use cases

  • Small business social media: a coffee shop designs a month of Instagram posts from one template and schedules them.
  • Presentations: a consultant drafts a deck with AI and applies the company Brand Kit.
  • Print: a nonprofit designs event posters and orders prints from Canva.
  • Education: teachers build worksheets and students create presentations in Canva for Education.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at canva.com with Google, Apple or email.
  2. Choose a design type, such as Instagram post or presentation.
  3. Pick a template and replace text, photos and colors.
  4. Try Canva AI to draft copy or generate an image.
  5. Set up a Brand Kit for logos, fonts and colors.
  6. Start a Pro trial if you need background removal, resize or premium assets.

Tips and tricks

  • Use Magic Resize to turn one design into every social format.
  • Keep a Brand Kit so every design starts on-brand.
  • Lean on standard AI tools like Translate and Magic Write, which don’t count toward the monthly AI allowance.
  • Customize templates with your own photos and fonts so designs don’t look generic.

Example Magic Write prompt:

Write three Instagram captions for a coffee shop launching a pumpkin spice cold brew. Friendly tone, under 150 characters each, include one emoji and a call to visit this weekend.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • One of the best free design plans available
  • Huge template and stock library
  • AI tools built directly into the editor
  • Works on every major platform with real-time collaboration
  • Free for K-12 education and eligible nonprofits

👎 Cons

  • Pro is limited to one person
  • Business is priced per person, which adds up for larger teams
  • Heavy AI users may need extra AI add-ons at additional cost
  • Designs can look template-like without customization

Who should (and shouldn't) use Canva

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Need social posts, presentations and flyers without design skills
  • Run a small business and want consistent branding
  • Teach or work at a nonprofit (free premium access)

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need professional vector illustration or print prepress control
  • Design product interfaces (Figma fits better)
  • Want a pure AI image generator

Canva alternatives

  • Adobe Express — All-in-one design app for social posts, flyers, videos and PDFs, with Firefly generative AI built in. (from $9.99/mo (Premium))
  • Figma — Collaborative interface design platform with an AI agent, Figma Make prototyping and Dev Mode. (from $16/mo per Full seat (Professional, billed annually))
  • Gamma — AI design partner that generates presentations, documents, websites and social posts from a prompt. (from $9/seat/mo (Plus))
  • Looka — AI logo maker and brand kit with social assets, business cards and an AI-generated website. (from $35 one-time (Basic Logo Package))

FAQ

Is Canva free?

Yes. Canva Free includes the drag-and-drop editor, 1,000+ design types, 1.6M+ templates and 4.7M+ photos, videos, graphics and audio files, plus limited AI use.

How much is Canva Pro in the US?

Canva Pro costs $18 per month or $180 per year for one person, before tax. Only one person can use a Pro account.

How much does Canva Business cost?

Canva Business is $25 per person per month or $250 per person per year in the US. It adds team admin and collaboration tools, ad performance insights, 500GB storage and 20x Canva Free's AI allowance.

How does AI usage work in Canva?

Some standard AI design tools, such as Translate and Magic Write, can be used abundantly without counting toward your monthly AI allowance. Other AI features use the allowance, which is larger on Pro (10x Free) and Business (20x Free). An AI Pass add-on is available at extra cost.

Is Canva free for teachers and students?

K-12 educational organizations and eligible not-for-profits can use most premium features for free.

Can I try Canva Pro before paying?

Yes. Canva offers a free trial of Pro and Business.
