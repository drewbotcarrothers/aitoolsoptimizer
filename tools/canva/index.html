Canva (Magic Studio)

Design platform with built-in AI tools.

freemium
Visit Canva (Magic Studio) → Official website · verified Oct 15, 2026

Overview

Easy design platform with AI tools (Magic Studio) for graphics, presentations, video and docs.

Who it's for

Everyone – small businesses, students, marketers, teachers

Key features

  • Magic Design
  • Magic Write
  • Background remover
  • AI images
  • Templates

Best use cases

  • Social graphics
  • Presentations
  • Marketing materials
  • AI image/edit tools

👍 Pros

  • Easy for non-designers
  • Huge template library
  • Lots of AI helpers

👎 Cons

  • Best AI features on Pro
  • Designs can look template-y

Tips & tricks

  1. Use Magic Resize to repurpose designs
  2. Set up a Brand Kit for consistent colors and fonts

Pricing

Freemium. Paid plans start at $18 (Pro); $12/mo annual (USD). Free plan: Yes.
