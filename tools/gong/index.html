What Gong is

Gong calls itself “the only Revenue AI OS that turns insight into action.” The platform captures customer interactions—calls, video meetings and emails—analyzes what’s working, and automates what happens next. Its foundation is the Revenue Graph, a rich dataset of customer interactions, on top of which a team of specialized AI agents and apps support every stage from prospecting to expansion.

Gong is designed for revenue teams: sales reps, managers, customer success, enablement and RevOps leaders who want to close deals faster, predict pipeline risk, automate handoffs, increase win rates and predict churn.

Best use cases

  • Coaching: managers review key moments in calls and coach reps.
  • Forecasting: leaders spot at-risk deals before quarter end.
  • Handoffs: sales-to-CS handoffs carry full context automatically.
  • Market insights: product and marketing teams learn what customers say about competitors.

How to get started

  1. Request a proposal on gong.io/pricing with your team size.
  2. Take the product tour to evaluate modules.
  3. Plan call-recording consent policies with legal.
  4. Connect CRM, email, calendar and conferencing tools.
  5. Roll out to a pilot team and define success metrics.

Tips and tricks

  • Agree on key topics such as competitors, pricing and objections to review from day one.
  • Build call libraries of great moments for onboarding.
  • Review forecasts weekly alongside deal activity.
  • Measure ramp time before and after rollout to prove ROI.

Pros and cons

  • AI agents automate follow-up and handoffs

  • Strong multilingual capabilities

👎 Cons

  • No public pricing; requires a sales process

  • More than small teams need

Who should (and shouldn't) use Gong

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Run a sizable B2B sales or CS team
  • Want data-driven coaching and forecasts
  • Need visibility into deal risk across the pipeline

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Are a solo seller or tiny startup (consider Fathom or Fireflies.ai)
  • Need prospect data and outreach mainly (consider Apollo.io)
  • Want transparent self-serve pricing

Gong alternatives

  • Fathom — AI notetaker that records, transcribes and summarizes meetings, with a free forever plan and CRM sync for teams. (from $16/mo (Premium, billed annually); $20 monthly)
  • Fireflies.ai — AI notetaker that transcribes, summarizes, searches and analyzes meetings, with free dictation via Fireflies Talk. (from $10/user/mo (Pro, annual); $18 monthly)
  • Apollo.io — B2B contact database, outreach sequences and AI sales agents in one go-to-market platform. (from $49 per seat/mo (Basic, billed annually))
  • HubSpot (Breeze AI) — Agentic customer platform with a free CRM, Breeze AI agents for marketing, sales and service, and usage-based HubSpot Credits. (from Free; Starter currently from $7/seat/mo (limited-time offer))
  • Read AI — AI meeting notes, enterprise search and an AI assistant, Ada, across meetings, email and messages. (from $15/user/mo billed annually (Pro))

FAQ

How much does Gong cost?

Gong doesn't publish prices. Licenses are priced per user, there's a platform fee based on the number of users, and integrating your existing tech stack is free. You request a custom proposal.

Does Gong have a free plan?

No. Gong is sold through custom quotes.

What does Gong do?

Gong captures customer interactions, analyzes what's working and automates next steps, helping teams close deals faster, predict pipeline risk, automate handoffs and coach reps.

What is the Gong Revenue Graph?

It's Gong's data layer of customer interactions and context that powers its AI agents and insights.

Who uses Gong?

Sales, customer success, enablement and RevOps teams across industries such as technology, financial services, healthcare and manufacturing.
