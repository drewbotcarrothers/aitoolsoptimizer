What Brisk Teaching is

Brisk Teaching describes itself as an “AI layer for every tool, website, and resource” teachers use. Rather than asking you to move work into a new app, Brisk adds a button inside Google Docs, Slides, YouTube, PDFs, websites and your learning management system. Click it and you can turn the article you’re reading into a quiz, a slide deck, a leveled version for struggling readers or a set of discussion questions.

Brisk says it is trusted in more than 10,000 districts and covers the full teaching cycle: planning, creating, giving feedback and supporting students.

Key features

Create

Generate lesson plans, quizzes, rubrics, inquiry worksheets, creative writing prompts and Google Slides presentations from any idea, article, website or video. Materials can be generated in 50+ languages.

Feedback

Leave personalized comments “in your voice, at your standard” using Glow & Grow, next-steps or rubric-criteria feedback. Batch feedback summarizes classwide strengths and trends (school plans).

Inspect Writing

See how a student’s document was written and revised over time, useful for understanding process and spotting large pasted blocks.

Differentiate

Change reading levels or translate content instantly to support every learner, including special education and ELL students.

Brisk Boost

Safe, teacher-guided AI activities for students, plus a student extension for instant writing feedback.

Best use cases

  • Prep in minutes: a middle school science teacher turns a news article into a leveled reading, a 10-question quiz and a slide deck.
  • Feedback at scale: an English teacher gives Glow & Grow comments on 120 essays and then personalizes the suggestions.
  • Differentiation: a teacher rewrites a history passage at three reading levels and translates it into Spanish.
  • District rollout: a curriculum office aligns Brisk outputs to state standards and monitors usage.

How to get started

  1. Install the free Brisk extension for Chrome or Edge from briskteaching.com.
  2. Sign in with your school Google or Microsoft account.
  3. Open a Google Doc, article or YouTube video and click the Brisk button.
  4. Pick a tool, such as Create a Quiz or Change Reading Level, and set grade and standards.
  5. Review and edit the output before sharing with students.
  6. Ask your school about Premium if you want standards alignment and student tools.

Tips and tricks

  • Include grade level, standard and time available in every request.
  • Use feedback as a draft. Add one personal sentence to each comment.
  • Turn videos into checks for understanding by running Brisk on YouTube.
  • Explore the prompt library in Brisk’s resources for subject-specific ideas.

Example request:

Create a 45-minute 7th grade lesson plan on the water cycle aligned to NGSS MS-ESS2-4, with a 5-minute warm-up, a hands-on activity using household materials, and a 4-question exit ticket.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Genuinely free for teachers
  • Works inside Docs, Slides, YouTube, PDFs and LMS pages
  • Feedback tools are designed to keep teacher voice and standards
  • Strong privacy focus for student data
  • Supports Google and Microsoft ecosystems

👎 Cons

  • School and district pricing is not published
  • Free plan has fewer tools (20+ vs 35+) and standard models
  • AI feedback still needs teacher review
  • Mostly browser-based; less useful outside Chrome or Edge

Who should (and shouldn't) use Brisk Teaching

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Teach K-12 and spend evenings on feedback and prep
  • Need to differentiate texts for mixed reading levels or ELL students
  • Lead a school or district rolling out AI safely

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want a student-facing tutor first (consider Khanmigo)
  • Use a browser other than Chrome or Edge
  • Need a tool for corporate training

FAQ

Is Brisk Teaching free?

Yes. Brisk is free for individual teachers through its Chrome and Edge extension. Schools and districts can buy Premium or Intelligence plans with custom pricing.

What can Brisk do for teachers?

Brisk has 30+ tools to generate lesson plans, quizzes, rubrics and Google Slides presentations, give Glow & Grow or rubric-based feedback, inspect student writing, change reading levels and translate materials.

Does Brisk work with Google Classroom and Microsoft?

Brisk works in Google Docs, Slides, YouTube and LMS pages, and offers Brisk for Google and Brisk for Microsoft integrations.

What is Brisk Boost?

Brisk Boost is a safe, teacher-guided AI experience for students, with activities and instant writing feedback. Schools can set admin-controlled access windows.

Is Brisk safe for student data?

Brisk says it earned a 95% Common Sense Privacy Rating, and school plans offer custom data privacy agreements and rostering.
