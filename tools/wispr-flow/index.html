Wispr Flow review

AI voice dictation that turns speech into polished text in any app.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew

⚡ Quick verdict

Wispr Flow is one of the best ways to replace typing with your voice: speak naturally and it produces clean, punctuated text in whatever app you're using, learning your names and jargon along the way. The free plan is a solid trial, Pro at $144 a year unlocks unlimited dictation, and eligible students get Pro features free. Teams can add SSO and a meeting notetaker with Growth.

Key takeaways

  • Free: limited dictation in any language and app; 2,000 words/week (up to 5,000) on Mac and Windows and 1,000 on mobile.
  • Pro: $15/user/month or $144/user/year ($12/month) with unlimited dictation and more meetings kept longer.
  • Student: $0/year for eligible students on Mac, Windows and iOS with a .edu email (everything in Pro).
  • Growth: $23/user/month ($216/year) for dictation with SAML SSO and usage reporting; Growth with Notetaker $33/user/month ($312/year).
  • Enterprise adds SCIM, MDM deployment and premium support; US card prices, with fixed local prices in 24 currencies.

What Wispr Flow is

Wispr Flow is an AI dictation app. Speak naturally and Flow turns your words into clean, punctuated, well-formatted text in whatever app you’re using—email, Slack, docs, code editors or AI chat boxes. It works in any language, learns names and jargon, and lets you add words to a personal dictionary.

For organizations, Wispr adds a meeting notetaker with speaker identification, SSO and admin controls on its Growth plans.

Key features

Dictation everywhere

Speak into any app on Mac, Windows, iOS and Android.

Smart formatting

Removes filler and formats text so it reads as written, not transcribed.

Personalization

Learns names and jargon and supports dictionary additions.

Meeting notetaker

On Growth with Notetaker, records meetings on Mac and Windows with speaker identification.

Team and security

SAML SSO, multiple email domains and usage reporting on Growth; SCIM, MDM and premium support on Enterprise; member data training always off on Growth and Enterprise.

Best use cases

  • Professionals: answer email and Slack by voice.
  • Writers: draft long documents quickly.

How to get started

  1. Download Flow for Mac, Windows, iOS or Android.
  2. Grant microphone and accessibility permissions.
  3. Dictate into your email or chat app.
  4. Add names and jargon to your dictionary.
  5. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited dictation.

Tips and tricks

  • Speak in full thoughts—Flow cleans up the rest.
  • Use it for AI prompts, which are faster to say than type.
  • Add custom words early.
  • Students: claim the free plan with your .edu email.

Example dictation:

“Hey team, quick update—the launch moves to Thursday, Priya owns the press release, and I’ll send the final deck by end of day.”

FAQ

Is Wispr Flow free?

Yes. The Free plan includes limited dictation in any language and app: a 2,000-word weekly allowance (5,000-word maximum) on Mac and Windows, and 1,000 words (1,500 maximum) shared across mobile.

How much does Wispr Flow Pro cost?

Pro is $15 per user per month or $144 per user per year ($12/month equivalent), based on US card list prices. Annual saves 20%.

Is Wispr Flow free for students?

Eligible students with a recognized .edu email can claim a free annual Student plan with everything in Pro on Mac, Windows and iOS. Where free routing doesn't apply, paid Student pricing is $7.50/month or $72/year.

What does the Growth plan include?

Growth (dictation only) is $23/user/month or $216/year and adds SAML SSO, multiple email domains and individual usage reporting. Growth with Notetaker is $33/user/month or $312/year and adds the meeting notetaker with speaker identification on Mac and Windows.

Which platforms does Wispr Flow support?

Mac, Windows, iOS and Android, with subscriptions syncing across devices.

Is Wispr Flow HIPAA compliant?

A HIPAA-ready Business Associate Agreement is available, subject to eligibility, for organizations on active Growth or Enterprise plans.
