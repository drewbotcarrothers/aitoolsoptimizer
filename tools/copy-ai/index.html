Copy.ai review

GTM AI platform for sales and marketing teams, with multi-model chat and automated content and prospecting workflows.

paid⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Copy.ai → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Copy.ai has moved well beyond its roots as a copywriting app into a GTM automation platform. The Chat plan is an affordable way for a small team to share multi-model AI chat, while the workflow plans are priced for companies automating content and sales processes at scale. Solo writers looking for a simple writing assistant will find better-fitting tools.

Key takeaways

  • Chat plan: $29 per month, or $24 per month billed annually ($288 per year), including 5 seats.
  • Chat includes unlimited words, unlimited chat projects and access to OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini models.
  • Workflow plans: Growth $1,000/mo, Expansion $2,000/mo and Scale $3,000/mo, billed annually.
  • Workflow plans include 20K, 45K or 75K workflow credits per month and 75 to 200 seats.
  • Use cases span content, prospecting, inbound lead processing, translation and deal insights.

What Copy.ai is

Copy.ai started as one of the first AI copywriting apps and has since repositioned as a GTM AI platform: one place to “infuse AI across your go-to-market engine” rather than juggling many copilots and point tools. It has two layers. Chat is a shared AI workspace with access to models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google. Workflows chain AI steps into automated processes, such as researching a list of accounts and drafting outreach, or turning a product brief into a set of SEO articles.

Copy.ai’s site features customers such as Lenovo and Juniper Networks, and a quote crediting it with “5x more meetings.”

Key features

Chat

Unlimited words and chat projects, and the ability to pick from several AI model families, shared across up to 5 seats on the Chat plan.

Workflows

Build multi-step automations that run on lists or triggers. Common templates include account research, content briefs, blog drafts, translation and lead enrichment.

Sales use cases

Research accounts and contacts to draft outreach, enrich and engage inbound leads quickly, and analyze sales transcripts to score deals and suggest next steps.

Marketing use cases

Generate SEO content, thought leadership, case studies and social posts, plus localized versions in other languages.

Best use cases

  • Content operations: a B2B marketing team turns one webinar transcript into a blog post, LinkedIn posts and an email newsletter.
  • Outbound research: SDRs run a workflow that researches 200 target accounts and drafts tailored first emails.
  • Speed-to-lead: inbound demo requests are enriched and get a personalized reply within minutes.
  • Localization: product pages are translated into several languages at native-speaker quality.

How to get started

  1. Choose the Chat plan at copy.ai and invite up to 4 teammates.
  2. Create a project for each campaign or account list and add brand guidelines.
  3. Test different models on the same prompt to see which writes best for you.
  4. Identify one repeatable task, such as weekly social posts, as a workflow candidate.
  5. Talk to Copy.ai’s team about workflow plans if you need automation at scale.

Tips and tricks

  • Store brand voice and product facts in your project so every chat starts with context.
  • Compare models on important content; different models excel at different tasks.
  • Keep a human editor in the loop for published content.
  • Measure outcomes (meetings booked, traffic) to justify workflow plans.

Example chat prompt:

Using our brand voice notes and the attached case study, write a 150-word LinkedIn post for our CMO announcing the results. Lead with the customer’s outcome, avoid buzzwords and end with a question.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Chat plan includes 5 seats at a low price
  • Access to several leading AI model families
  • Workflows automate multi-step GTM processes
  • Guided onboarding and enterprise-grade security on larger plans

👎 Cons

  • Big jump from Chat to workflow plans ($1,000+/mo)
  • No permanent free plan listed on the pricing page
  • Less focused on long-form creative writing than some rivals
  • Getting value from workflows takes setup time

Who should (and shouldn't) use Copy.ai

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Run a small marketing or sales team that wants shared AI chat
  • Need to automate high-volume content or prospecting at a larger company
  • Want one platform instead of many point AI tools

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Are a solo blogger or novelist (consider Jasper, Sudowrite or Grammarly)
  • Need a free tool
  • Only want SEO content optimization (consider Surfer or Frase)

Copy.ai alternatives

  • Jasper — AI platform for marketing teams: on-brand content with Canvas, marketing agents, brand IQ and AI search visibility. (from $59/seat/mo billed yearly; $69 monthly (Pro))
  • Writesonic — Track and improve your brand's visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and more. (from $79/mo billed annually (Starter))
  • Clay — Go-to-market data platform that combines many data providers, AI research agents and workflows to build and enrich lead lists. (from $167/mo (Launch, billed annually))
  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)

FAQ

How much does Copy.ai cost?

The Chat plan costs $29 per month billed monthly or $24 per month billed annually ($288 per year) and includes 5 seats. Workflow plans run $1,000, $2,000 and $3,000 per month billed annually.

Does Copy.ai have a free plan?

Copy.ai's pricing page doesn't list a permanent free plan; plans start with Chat.

What AI models does Copy.ai use?

The Chat plan provides access to OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini models.

What are Copy.ai workflow credits?

Workflow credits are consumed when automated workflows run. Growth includes 20K per month, Expansion 45K and Scale 75K.

Is Copy.ai good for sales teams?

Yes. Copy.ai workflows can research accounts and contacts, draft personalized outreach, enrich inbound leads and analyze sales call transcripts.
