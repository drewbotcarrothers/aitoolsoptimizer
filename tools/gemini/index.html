Google Gemini

Google's AI assistant integrated with Search and Workspace.

freemium
Visit Google Gemini → Official website · verified Oct 15, 2026

Overview

Google's AI assistant integrated across Android, Search, Workspace and a standalone app.

Who it's for

Google/Android users, students, Workspace users

Key features

  • Gemini 3.1 Pro
  • Gemini Live voice/camera
  • Image generation (150M+ images/day)
  • Flow/Veo video access
  • 40+ app actions on Android

Best use cases

  • Everyday Q&A & planning
  • Voice/camera help (Gemini Live)
  • Image & video creation
  • Gmail/Docs help
  • Android app automation

👍 Pros

  • Tight Google Workspace integration
  • Good multimodal features
  • Generous free tier

👎 Cons

  • Advanced features need Google One AI plan
  • Answers vary in depth

Tips & tricks

  1. Use @-mentions to pull in Gmail or Drive content
  2. Ask it to double-check answers with Google Search

Pricing

Freemium. Paid plans start at $19.99 (Google AI Pro); Ultra $99.99–$199.99 per month (USD). Free plan: Yes.
