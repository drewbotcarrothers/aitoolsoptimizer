Google Gemini review

Google's AI assistant for chat, research, image and video generation, built into Gmail, Docs and Android.

freemium⏱ 4 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Google Gemini → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Gemini is the strongest choice if you live in Google's ecosystem: it works inside Gmail, Docs and Chrome, and Google AI Pro bundles 5 TB of storage and other perks that make $19.99 a month easy to justify. The free tier is solid for everyday help. Ultra is for developers and heavy creators. If you don't use Google services, ChatGPT or Claude may feel more focused.

Key takeaways

  • Free with a Google account: Flash-Lite model, image generation and editing, limited Nano Banana Pro access and 15 GB storage.
  • Google AI Plus ($4.99/mo): 2x usage vs. Free, video generation, 200 Flow credits and 400 GB storage.
  • Google AI Pro ($19.99/mo): 4x usage vs. Free, Gemini 4 Argon access, 1,000 Flow credits, Gemini in Gmail and Docs and 5 TB storage.
  • Google AI Ultra: $99.99/mo (5x Pro limits) or $199.99/mo (20x), 20 TB+ storage and YouTube Premium.
  • More than 1 billion monthly users, according to Google.

What Google Gemini is

Gemini is Google’s AI assistant, available at gemini.google.com, in mobile apps and inside Google products like Gmail, Docs, Vids and Chrome. Google says it’s the fastest-growing product in its history, with more than 1 billion monthly users.

You can use Gemini to answer questions, write and edit, plan, summarize documents, generate and edit images, create videos and help with code. Paid Google AI subscriptions bundle higher usage, advanced models such as Gemini 4 Argon, creative credits for Google Flow, cloud storage and perks like YouTube Premium.

Key features

Chat and writing

Everyday help for work, school and life, with higher limits on paid plans.

Advanced models

Pro and Ultra get higher access to Gemini 4 Argon and agentic capabilities.

Image and video generation

Image creation and editing with Nano Banana, plus video generation on paid plans and Google Flow credits.

Gemini in Google apps

Use Gemini directly in Gmail, Docs, Vids and more on Plus and above.

Agents and automation

Chrome auto browse completes tasks for you (Pro and above), and developers get limits for Google’s coding agent and Antigravity.

Best use cases

  • Inbox management: summarize long Gmail threads and draft replies.
  • Students: explain concepts and build study plans.
  • Creators: generate images and short videos for social posts.
  • Developers: use Google’s coding agents with higher Ultra limits.

How to get started

  1. Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Ask a simple question to get a feel for responses.
  3. Install the mobile app for voice and camera help.
  4. Try image generation with a descriptive prompt.
  5. Upgrade to Google AI Pro if you hit limits or want Gemini in Gmail and Docs.

Tips and tricks

  • Reference your Google files when asking for summaries or drafts.
  • Be specific about format: tables, bullet lists or word counts.
  • Use Gemini Notebook for questions grounded in your own documents.
  • Ask follow-up questions to refine an answer instead of starting over.

Example prompt:

Plan a 5-day trip to Lisbon for two people in May on a $1,500 budget, excluding flights. Give a day-by-day table with neighborhoods, one free activity and one food recommendation per day.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Deep integration with Gmail, Docs, Drive and Android
  • Useful free tier
  • AI Pro bundles 5 TB storage and other Google perks
  • Strong image and video generation
  • Wide availability in 150+ countries

👎 Cons

  • Best features require Google AI Pro or Ultra
  • Plan tiers and perks are complex
  • Some features are US-only or limited by country
  • Less appealing if you don't use Google apps

Who should (and shouldn't) use Google Gemini

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Use Gmail, Docs and Drive every day
  • Want image and video generation in your assistant
  • Could use the bundled cloud storage

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Prefer Microsoft 365 (consider Microsoft Copilot)
  • Want a writing-focused assistant (consider Claude)
  • Need cited web research answers (consider Perplexity)

Google Gemini alternatives

  • ChatGPT — OpenAI's AI assistant for chat, research, writing, coding, images, voice and agentic work. (from $8/mo (Go); Plus $20; Pro $100–$500)
  • Claude — Anthropic's AI assistant for writing, analysis, research, coding and long-running work, with Claude Code included on paid plans. (from $17/mo (Pro, annual; $20 monthly); Max from $100)
  • Microsoft Copilot — Microsoft's AI assistant across Windows, Edge and Microsoft 365 apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook). (from $9.99 (Microsoft 365 Personal incl. Copilot); Premium $19.99)
  • Perplexity — An AI answer engine that cites its sources. (from $20 (Pro; ~$17/mo annual))
  • Gemini Notebook — Google's AI research and thinking partner (formerly NotebookLM), grounded in your sources, with Audio and Video Overviews. (from Free; higher limits from $4.99/mo (Google AI Plus))

FAQ

Is Google Gemini free?

Yes. The free plan includes the Flash-Lite model, image generation and editing, limited access to Nano Banana Pro and 15 GB of storage.

How much is Google AI Pro?

Google AI Pro costs $19.99 per month and includes 4x higher usage than Free, Gemini 4 Argon access, 1,000 Google Flow credits, Gemini in Gmail and Docs, and 5 TB of storage.

What is Google AI Plus?

Google AI Plus is a $4.99 per month plan with 2x higher usage than Free, video generation, 200 Flow credits, more Gemini Notebook use and 400 GB of storage.

How much is Google AI Ultra?

Ultra costs $99.99 per month for 5x the Pro usage limits or $199.99 per month for 20x, and includes 20 TB or more of storage and YouTube Premium.

Is Gemini available in my country?

Google AI Plus is available in more than 160 countries and territories, and Pro and Ultra in more than 150. Some features are limited to the US or select countries.
