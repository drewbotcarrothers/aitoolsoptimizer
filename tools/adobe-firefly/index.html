Adobe Firefly review

Adobe's generative AI app for images, video, audio and vectors, with partner models and commercially safe Firefly models.

freemium⏱ 5 min readChecked October 16, 2026By Andrew
Visit Adobe Firefly → Official website

⚡ Quick verdict

Adobe Firefly is the most business-friendly AI image and video generator for teams already in Adobe's world. Its own models are trained on licensed and public-domain content, paid plans give unlimited standard image generations, and credits cover premium video, audio and partner models. Pure image enthusiasts may get more style variety elsewhere, but for commercial work and Creative Cloud integration it is hard to beat.

Key takeaways

  • Paid plans: Standard $9.99, Pro $19.99, Pro Plus $49.99 and Premium $199.99 per month (US).
  • Monthly credits: 2,000 (Standard), 4,000 (Pro), 10,000 (Pro Plus) and 50,000 (Premium).
  • All paid plans include unlimited standard image and vector generations; credits are spent on premium features such as video, audio and partner models.
  • Pro and above also include Adobe Express Premium and Photoshop on web and mobile.
  • Free users get a limited number of daily generations that refresh each day.

What Adobe Firefly is

Adobe Firefly is two things: a family of Adobe-built generative models, and the Firefly app (web and mobile) where you use them. Firefly powers features across Creative Cloud, including Generative Fill in Photoshop, Generative Extend in Premiere and text-to-image in Adobe Express. In the Firefly app itself you can generate images, video, audio, sound effects and vectors, and you can also pick partner models from companies such as Google, OpenAI, ElevenLabs and Topaz.

Adobe positions Firefly as commercially safe: the company says its Firefly models were trained on licensed content such as Adobe Stock and on public-domain content where copyright has expired. That matters for agencies and brands that need to know where their assets come from.

Key features

Image generation and editing

Text to image with Firefly Image models (including Image 4 Ultra and Image 5 as premium options), style and structure references, and generative editing. On paid plans, standard image generations are unlimited.

Video

Generate Video turns text or an image into short clips. In Premiere, Generative Extend adds frames to the start or end of a clip. Video is a premium feature and uses credits per second.

Audio and translation

Translate Video and Translate Audio dub speech into other languages, and Generate Sound Effects and Generate Music create audio. Text to speech is available with Firefly Speech or ElevenLabs.

Partner models

Pick third-party models inside Firefly. They count as premium features and use your credits.

Custom models (beta) and Firefly Graph

Train a custom model on your own assets (500 credits per training, 20 per generation) and build node-based workflows in Firefly Graph.

Best use cases

  • Campaign imagery: a marketing team generates lifestyle backgrounds for a product launch, then finishes them in Photoshop.
  • Social video: a creator turns a product still into a 5-second animated clip for Reels.
  • Localization: an online course creator translates a lesson video into Spanish and French with Translate Video.
  • Sound design: a podcaster generates short sound effects for transitions.

How to get started

  1. Go to firefly.adobe.com and sign in with an Adobe account.
  2. Try Text to image on the free tier to learn prompting.
  3. Choose a model from the model picker; note which ones are premium.
  4. Use reference images for composition and style to keep results consistent.
  5. Check your credit balance in your Adobe Account menu before generating video.
  6. Upgrade to Standard or Pro when you need unlimited standard images or video.

Tips and tricks

  • Test video at 540p first (20 credits per second), then render the final at 1080p.
  • Use Pro instead of separate subscriptions if you also want Express Premium and Photoshop on web and mobile.
  • Write prompts like a shot list: subject, setting, lighting, camera angle, mood.
  • Check beta labels before using outputs in client work.

Example image prompt:

Close-up product photo of a matte black wireless earbud case on wet slate, dramatic rim lighting, shallow depth of field, cinematic teal and orange color grade, 16:9

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Firefly models trained on licensed content such as Adobe Stock and public-domain content
  • Unlimited standard image generations on every paid plan
  • One app gives access to Adobe and partner models
  • Deep integration with Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and Express
  • Failed generations are typically refunded to your credit balance

👎 Cons

  • Credit math for video is steep (for example, 100 credits per second at 1080p)
  • Credits don't roll over month to month
  • Partner models consume premium credits
  • Pricing and plan names change often

Who should (and shouldn't) use Adobe Firefly

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Work commercially and want clear guidance on using AI output
  • Already use Photoshop, Premiere or Express
  • Need video translation or sound effects alongside images

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Want the most stylized, artistic image results above all (consider Midjourney)
  • Only need occasional images (the free tier or Express may suffice)
  • Need to generate long-form video on a small budget

Adobe Firefly alternatives

  • Midjourney — Artistic AI image and video generation, now testing its V8 model. (from $10/mo (Basic) or $8/mo billed annually)
  • ChatGPT Images (formerly DALL·E) — OpenAI's image generation inside ChatGPT, now powered by GPT Image models after DALL·E's retirement. (from Included with ChatGPT Free (limited), Go $8, Plus $20)
  • Ideogram — AI image generator known for accurate text in images, with editing, characters, batch generation and an API. (from $15/mo billed yearly (Plus); $20 monthly)
  • Leonardo.Ai — AI image and video generation platform with personal model training, Realtime Canvas and team plans, now part of Canva. (from $12/mo (Essential, monthly))
  • Adobe Express — All-in-one design app for social posts, flyers, videos and PDFs, with Firefly generative AI built in. (from $9.99/mo (Premium))

FAQ

Is Adobe Firefly free?

Yes. Free users get a limited number of daily generations across Firefly features on a curated set of models, refreshed each day. Paid plans unlock more models, credits and unlimited standard generations.

How much does Adobe Firefly cost?

In the US, Firefly Standard is $9.99 per month, Pro is $19.99, Pro Plus is $49.99 and Premium is $199.99. Adobe sometimes runs first-year discounts on the higher tiers.

What are Firefly generative credits used for?

On paid Firefly plans, standard image and vector features don't use credits. Credits are spent on premium features such as Generate Video, Translate Video and Audio, Generate Sound Effects, the Firefly Image 4 Ultra and Image 5 models and partner models.

How many credits does a Firefly video use?

Adobe's credits FAQ lists Generate Video at 20 credits per second at 540p, 50 per second at 720p and 100 per second at 1080p (24 fps).

Can I use Adobe Firefly images commercially?

Yes, for features without a beta label. Beta outputs can also be used commercially unless the product says otherwise, but for eligible business customers beta outputs are not indemnified.

Do Firefly credits roll over?

No. Generative credits reset to your plan allocation each month and unused credits do not carry over.
