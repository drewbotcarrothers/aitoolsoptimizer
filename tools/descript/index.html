What Descript is

Descript is a video and audio editor built around the transcript. Drop in footage, a script or a Zoom call and Descript transcribes it in seconds. From then on, editing the words edits the video: delete a sentence and that moment disappears, move a paragraph and the footage moves with it. A traditional timeline is still there for fine control.

On top of that core idea, Descript has added a large set of AI tools and an AI co-editor called Underlord. Descript says more than 6 million creators and teams use it, including companies such as Amazon, Canva and Spotify.

Key features

Text-based editing

Edit video like a doc. Cut filler words and retakes in a click, copy-paste and drag-and-drop sections, and use the timeline when you need precision.

Underlord, the AI co-editor

Tell Underlord what you want (“tighten this to five minutes and add captions”) or ask it what to do. Descript says it can turn a raw recording into a tight cut in minutes.

Sound good

Studio Sound removes background noise and enhances voices. Other tools include Edit for Clarity, Remove Filler Words, Remove Retakes and Regenerate Speech.

Look good

Eye Contact correction, Green Screen, Automatic Multicam, Video Regenerate and Brand Studio for templates and guardrails.

Promote and repurpose

Create Clips for social, AI Avatars, YouTube descriptions, show notes and translation. You can also reformat one video for YouTube, TikTok and LinkedIn.

Record and generate

A built-in screen recorder, Rooms for remote podcast and video recording, AI speech with voice clones and stock voices, and generated video, images and music to fill gaps. An API and MCP connection let you edit through code or an AI assistant.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at descript.com (no card needed).
  2. Import a recording or record your screen or camera.
  3. Read the transcript and delete what you don’t need.
  4. Run Studio Sound and Remove Filler Words.
  5. Ask Underlord for captions, clips or a tighter cut, then export or share a review link.

Tips and tricks

  • Fix the transcript first so search and cuts are accurate.
  • Use Remove Retakes on long takes before manual editing.
  • Keep an eye on media hours: long raw files use them up quickly.
  • Use review links to collect comments directly on the video.

Example Underlord instruction:

Cut this 40-minute interview to a 10-minute highlight, remove filler words, add captions in our brand style, and create three 30-second vertical clips for TikTok.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

👎 Cons

  • Free plan exports carry limits; 4K needs Creator or higher
  • Prices are per person, so teams add up
  • Less suited to cinematic, effects-heavy editing

Who should (and shouldn't) use Descript

FAQ

Is Descript free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0, needs no credit card and includes 60 media minutes (1 hour) per month for recording, editing and publishing.

How much does Descript cost?

Hobbyist is $16 per person per month billed annually ($24 monthly), Creator is $24 annually ($35 monthly) and Business is $50 annually ($65 monthly). Enterprise is custom through sales.

What are media hours and AI credits?

Media hours are the amount of imported or recorded media Descript processes for you each month per editor. AI credits are spent on AI tools. Hobbyist includes 10 hours and 400 credits, Creator 30 hours and 800 credits, and Business 40 hours and 1,500 credits.

What is Underlord?

Underlord is Descript's AI video co-editor. You tell it what you want, and it can turn a raw recording into a tight cut, among other edits. Hobbyist has access, and Creator and above get full access.

Can Descript translate videos?

Yes. Business includes translation and dubbing into 30+ languages with proofreading, and transcription supports 25 languages on all plans.

Can I record in Descript?

Yes. Descript includes a screen recorder and Rooms for recording podcasts and video with remote guests, so you can record and edit in one place.
