Gamma review

⚡ Quick verdict

Gamma is one of the fastest ways to go from an idea to a polished deck or web page. Generation quality is strong, and its card-based format works well on screens. The free plan is a trial more than a long-term option, since credits don't refresh, but Plus at $9 is cheap and Pro at $18 unlocks the API and advanced AI. Teams needing strict brand templates in PowerPoint may prefer Plus AI or Beautiful.ai.

Key takeaways

  • Plans: Free, Plus ($9), Pro ($18), Ultra ($90) per seat per month, plus Business and Enterprise for teams.
  • Monthly credits: Plus 1,000, Pro 4,000, Ultra 20,000. Free credits don't refresh.
  • Plus and Pro can be billed annually ($108 and $216 per seat per year).
  • Generating with AI costs about 1-3 credits per card; images cost 2-125 credits depending on the model.
  • API keys are available on Pro, Ultra, Teams and Business; connectors work on all plans.

What Gamma is

Gamma is an AI-powered tool for creating presentations, documents, websites and social posts. You type a prompt, paste notes or import a file, and Gamma generates a complete, designed result: structure, copy, layouts and images. You can then edit with AI (“make this more concise,” “try a new layout”) or by hand.

Instead of fixed slides, Gamma uses flexible “cards” that look great on screens and adapt to different devices, which is why it also works for one-page websites and docs.

Key features

Generate from a prompt or file

Create a full deck or document in seconds, metered per card in credits.

AI editing

Rewrite, expand, condense or restyle content, and try new layouts.

AI images

Standard, advanced, premium and ultra image models (higher tiers unlock more models).

Websites and docs

Publish a simple site or share a doc by link.

API and connectors

Automate generation from other tools; API keys on Pro and above.

Best use cases

  • Founders: draft a pitch deck from a one-page memo.
  • Teachers and trainers: turn lesson notes into visual slides.
  • Consultants: produce client-ready reports quickly.
  • Small businesses: publish a simple one-page website.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at gamma.app.
  2. Choose Generate and paste a prompt or your notes.
  3. Review the outline before generating.
  4. Pick a theme, then generate.
  5. Edit cards with AI or manually.
  6. Share by link with your audience.

Tips and tricks

  • Edit the outline first; it’s cheaper than regenerating cards.
  • Paste real content for better, more specific decks.
  • Use standard image models to save credits.
  • Set a custom theme with your brand colors and fonts.

Example prompt:

Create a 10-card pitch deck for a meal-prep delivery startup targeting busy nurses. Include problem, solution, market size, business model, traction, competition, team and the ask. Keep text under 30 words per card.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Very fast from idea to polished output
  • Cheap Plus tier
  • Creates decks, docs and sites in one tool
  • API for automating content generation
  • No charge for AI errors

👎 Cons

  • Free credits don't refresh
  • Card format isn't identical to traditional slides
  • Advanced image models use many credits
  • Ultra is monthly-only and expensive

Who should (and shouldn't) use Gamma

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Need a first-draft deck in minutes
  • Build simple landing pages or one-pagers
  • Want to automate decks via API

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Must work natively inside PowerPoint or Google Slides (consider Plus AI)
  • Need pixel-perfect brand design (consider Figma or Canva)
  • Present rarely and want everything free

Gamma alternatives

  • Beautiful.ai — AI presentation software with auto-aligning Smart Slides that keep decks polished and on-brand. (from $14.50/mo (Pro, billed annually); $45 monthly)
  • Plus AI — Create and edit AI presentations directly inside Google Slides and PowerPoint. (from $10/user/mo billed annually (Basic))
  • Pitch — Collaborative presentation software with AI slide generation, analytics and deal rooms. (from $10/seat/mo billed yearly (Plus))
  • Canva — Drag-and-drop design platform with templates, Canva AI and team tools for social, print, video and presentations. (from $18/mo or $180/yr (Pro, one person))
  • Napkin AI — Turn text into infographics, diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps and slides. (from $9/person/mo (Plus))

FAQ

Is Gamma free?

Yes, there's a free plan, but its AI credits don't refresh. Free accounts can hold up to 2,000 credits and earn more through referrals.

How much does Gamma cost?

Gamma Plus is $9, Pro is $18 and Ultra is $90 per seat per month. Plus and Pro can be billed annually at $108 and $216 per seat per year; Business and Enterprise are for teams.

How do Gamma credits work?

Credits power AI features. Creating with AI uses about 1-3 credits per card, and images cost 2-125 credits depending on the model. Gamma uses plan credits first, then purchased credits, then workspace credits.

Does Gamma have an API?

Yes. API keys are available on Pro, Ultra, Teams and Business plans, and connectors are available on every plan.

Who is Gamma best for?

Gamma suits founders, teachers, consultants and marketers who need polished decks, docs or simple web pages quickly without design skills.
