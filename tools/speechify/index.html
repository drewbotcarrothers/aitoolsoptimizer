Speechify review

Listen to anything with text to speech, type by talking, and ask a voice AI assistant.

⚡ Quick verdict

Speechify is the most polished way to listen to your reading: PDFs, articles, emails and books in natural voices at high speed, on every device. It's grown into a voice assistant with voice typing, summaries and AI podcasts. The free plan is basic (robotic voices, 1.5x); Premium is $29 a month billed monthly, and yearly billing saves 60%, which is the way to go if you'll use it daily.

  • Yearly billing saves 60% compared with monthly, according to Speechify's pricing page.
  • Voice typing works across apps like Google Docs, Gmail, Slack, Outlook and Cursor on desktop.
  • Listen up to 4.5x speed, summarize PDFs, create podcasts from documents and ask the Voice AI Assistant questions.

What Speechify is

Speechify started as a text-to-speech reader and has become a broader “Voice AI Assistant.” It reads anything aloud—PDFs, web pages, emails, documents—in lifelike voices, so you can listen instead of reading. You can also talk instead of type with voice typing across your apps, summarize documents, turn any document or description into a podcast, and ask the assistant questions about what you’re reading.

It suits students, busy professionals and anyone who prefers listening to reading.

Key features

Text to speech

1,000+ voices in 60+ languages, at up to 4.5x speed.

Voice typing

Dictate polished text in Google Docs, Gmail, Slack, Outlook, Cursor and other apps on Mac and Windows.

PDF and document tools

Upload a PDF to the web app to have it read aloud, summarized or turned into a podcast.

Voice AI Assistant

Ask questions about anything you’re reading, in the browser side panel or on mobile.

Everywhere

Web app, Chrome and Edge extensions, iOS, Android, Mac and Windows.

Best use cases

  • Students: listen to readings and lecture notes.
  • Professionals: catch up on reports during a commute.
  • Writers: proofread drafts by ear.
  • Accessibility: reading support for dyslexia and low vision.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at speechify.com.
  2. Install the Chrome or Edge extension.
  3. Open an article and click play.
  4. Upload a PDF to the web app.
  5. Upgrade to Premium for natural voices and higher speeds.

Tips and tricks

  • Increase speed gradually to build listening stamina.
  • Use summaries before listening to long PDFs.
  • Try voice typing for first drafts.
  • Pick a voice you enjoy for long sessions.

Example use:

Upload a 40-page research PDF, ask for a five-bullet summary, then listen to the methods section at 2x on your commute.

Pros and cons

👍 Pros

  • Natural, high-quality voices
  • Works on every major platform
  • Voice typing across apps
  • Great for accessibility
  • Free plan available

👎 Cons

  • Monthly Premium is pricey
  • Free voices sound robotic
  • Best features require Premium
  • Many features may overwhelm simple users

Who should (and shouldn't) use Speechify

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Listen to long documents and articles
  • Dictate instead of typing
  • Need reading support for dyslexia or low vision

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need voiceovers for videos (consider ElevenLabs or Murf)
  • Only need dictation (consider Wispr Flow)
  • Need meeting transcription (consider Otter.ai)

Speechify alternatives

  • ElevenLabs — Lifelike AI voices for text to speech, voice cloning, dubbing, music, sound effects and conversational voice agents. (from $6/mo (Starter); Creator $22)
  • Wispr Flow — AI voice dictation that turns speech into polished text in any app. (from $12/mo billed annually (Pro, $144/year))
  • Murf AI — Studio-quality AI voiceovers, a fast text-to-speech API, dubbing and voice agents. (from API from $0.01 per 1,000 characters; Studio plans on pricing page)
  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))
  • Otter.ai — AI meeting agent that transcribes, summarizes and answers questions about your meetings. (from $8.33/user/mo billed annually (Pro))

FAQ

Is Speechify free?

Yes. The free plan lets you listen at speeds up to 1.5x with 10 robotic-sounding voices and includes text-to-speech features only.

How much does Speechify Premium cost?

Premium is $29 per month when billed monthly. Speechify says yearly billing saves 60%; check the pricing page for the exact yearly amount.

How many voices and languages does Speechify have?

Premium includes 1,000+ high-quality, natural voices in 60+ languages.

Can Speechify type for me?

Yes. Voice Typing turns your speech into polished text in apps such as Google Docs, Gmail, Slack, Outlook and Cursor.

Which devices does Speechify support?

Web, Chrome and Edge extensions, iOS, Android, Mac and Windows.

How fast can Speechify read?

Up to 4.5x speed on supported apps; the free plan is limited to 1.5x.
