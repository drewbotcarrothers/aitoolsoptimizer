Synthesia review

Create training and business videos with AI avatars, dubbing, roleplays and surveys.

⚡ Quick verdict

Synthesia is the enterprise standard for AI avatar video: polished avatars, easy script-to-video editing, translation into 140+ languages and learning features like roleplays, surveys and SCORM export. The free Basic plan is a genuine trial (watermarked), Starter suits individuals, and Pro at $89 a month is the practical team tier. The new credit system pools usage across features, so estimate your monthly minutes before choosing.

Key takeaways

  • Basic: $0 with 10 minutes of video creation or dubbing, 3 survey responses and 500 credits per month; videos carry a watermark.
  • Starter: $29/month billed monthly (shown as $19/month with yearly billing) with 1,250 credits, downloads and logo removal.
  • Pro: $89/month billed monthly with 6,000 credits; higher credit tiers go up to 48,000/month ($676).
  • Enterprise: custom credits, unlimited video minutes, 1-click translation into 140+ languages, SAML/SSO, Brand Kits and SCORM export.
  • Credits are a shared currency: video costs about 100 credits per minute and dubbing about 76 per minute, beyond the first 10 included minutes.

What Synthesia is

Synthesia is an AI video platform for creating presenter-led videos without cameras, studios or actors. You write or paste a script, choose an AI avatar and voice, add slides, media and templates, and Synthesia renders a video where the avatar presents your content. It’s widely used for training, onboarding, product explainers and internal communications.

Synthesia has moved beyond one-way video with interactive roleplays and surveys, plus dubbing and translation, and now uses credits as a shared currency across all AI usage-based features.

Key features

AI avatars

125+ stock avatars on paid plans (240+ on Enterprise) and personal avatars of yourself.

Script-to-video editor

Script-based editing with templates and pre-built libraries.

Dubbing and translation

Dub existing videos and, on Enterprise, translate into 140+ languages in one click.

Roleplays and surveys

Interactive practice scenarios and survey questions for learners.

Sharing and export

Share links on all plans, downloads on paid plans and SCORM export on Enterprise.

Collaboration and brand

Live team collaboration, Brand Kits and SAML/SSO on Enterprise.

Best use cases

  • L&D teams: turn training scripts into narrated videos.
  • HR: onboarding and policy videos.
  • Product marketing: feature explainers that are easy to update.
  • Global companies: localize videos into many languages.

How to get started

  1. Sign up free at synthesia.io.
  2. Pick a template and paste your script.
  3. Choose an avatar, voice and language.
  4. Add images, media and screen recordings.
  5. Generate, review and share or download.

Tips and tricks

  • Write conversationally and keep sentences short.
  • Add pauses and on-screen text for key points.
  • Reuse templates for consistent branding.
  • Estimate credits using minutes and dubbing needs.

Example script opening:

Hi, I’m Alex. In the next three minutes, you’ll learn how to request time off in Workday and what happens after you submit.

Who should (and shouldn't) use Synthesia

✅ A good fit if you…

  • Produce training videos without filming
  • Translate videos into many languages
  • Update videos quickly when content changes

⛔ Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need cinematic generative video (consider Runway)
  • Want avatar video with lower entry cost (consider HeyGen)
  • Edit real footage (consider Descript)

Synthesia alternatives

  • HeyGen — AI video platform for realistic avatars, voice cloning and video translation in 175+ languages. (from $29/mo (Creator))
  • invideo AI — AI video creation platform with frontier image and video models and agents, priced by credits per seat. (from $10/seat/mo billed annually (Starter); $20 monthly)
  • Descript — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, with an AI co-editor called Underlord. (from $16/person/mo billed annually (Hobbyist))
  • Runway — AI creative suite for video, images and audio with Gen-4.5 and models from other labs. (from $12/mo billed annually (Standard))
  • Murf AI — Studio-quality AI voiceovers, a fast text-to-speech API, dubbing and voice agents. (from API from $0.01 per 1,000 characters; Studio plans on pricing page)

FAQ

Is Synthesia free?

Yes. The Basic plan is free with 10 minutes of video creation or dubbing, 3 survey responses and 500 credits per month. Videos carry a Synthesia watermark.

How much does Synthesia cost?

Starter is $29/month billed monthly (the page shows $19/month with yearly billing) with 1,250 credits. Pro is $89/month billed monthly with 6,000 credits, with higher tiers up to 48,000 credits for $676/month. Enterprise is custom. Yearly billing saves up to 3 months.

How do Synthesia credits work?

Credits are a shared currency across AI usage features. Video creation uses about 100 credits per minute and dubbing about 76 credits per minute, with the first 10 minutes included on every plan; survey responses use 350 credits each after the first 3.

How many languages does Synthesia support?

Enterprise includes 1-click translations into 140+ languages.

Can I create my own avatar?

Yes. Personal avatars are available, and Enterprise includes unlimited personal avatars.

Can I export to an LMS?

Enterprise includes SCORM export for learning management systems. Learner seats cost $29 per learner per month.
