What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels (web beacons) and device identifiers. "First-party" cookies are set by this site; "third-party" cookies are set by other companies, such as Google.

Consent

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, a consent message asks for your choice before personalized advertising and non-essential cookies are used.

How to control cookies

  • Opt out of personalized Google ads: Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of some third-party vendors' personalized-advertising cookies: www.aboutads.info/choices (US) or www.youronlinechoices.com (EU/UK).
  • Opt out of Google Analytics: Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect how some sites work.
