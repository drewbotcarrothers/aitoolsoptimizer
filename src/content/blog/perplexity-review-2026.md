**[Perplexity](/tools/perplexity/) is an answer engine that searches, writes a short answer, and attaches citations you are supposed to open.** That habit is the product. Pro at $17/month billed annually adds higher limits, model choice, files, and Perplexity Computer. The question of whether those extras are worth $17 is a different article: [is Perplexity Pro worth it](/blog/is-perplexity-pro-worth-it-2026/). This one is how to use the desk you already have.

Annual prices and the help-center limits below were read October 24, 2026 for that companion piece. We did not score answer accuracy against ChatGPT or Gemini. A citation is a link. It is not a fact until you open it.

## The workflow, before the upgrade

Use this order on the free plan. Do not start by buying Pro.

1. Ask one question a person could look up. Name the kind of source you want: a company's pricing page, a regulator, a paper, a date range.
2. Read the answer once for shape, not for numbers.
3. Open every citation attached to a number, a name, or a date. If a number has no citation, delete it from your notes.
4. Ask a follow-up in the same thread when the first answer missed a constraint. A new thread throws away the correction.
5. Stop when you have the links. Perplexity is a way to assemble a reading list with a draft on top. It is not the filing.

## What to use each surface for

**Basic search** is the everyday desk. The help-center comparison lists practically unlimited basic searches on Free, with citations. **Pro Search** is the heavier mode: 3 a day on Free, and a weekly allowance described as average use on Pro. **Research** queries are rarer still: 1 a month on Free. Spend Pro Search on the question you will quote. Spend basic search on the tour.

**Spaces** keep a project’s threads together. Use one Space per client, course, or article. Do not keep a year of unrelated questions in one thread and hope the model remembers.

**Files** matter when the source is a PDF you already trust. Pro adds higher upload limits. For a class PDF that should not be mixed with the open web, [Gemini Notebook](/tools/gemini-notebook/) stays on your uploads. The study workflow is [how to study with Gemini Notebook](/blog/gemini-notebook-study-workflows/). Perplexity is the worse tool when the whole point is "only these documents."

**Model choice and Model Council**, on Pro and Max, let you pick a model or compare answers. Use that on a question you are about to repeat in public. Do not use it on a lunch recommendation. Model names on the site change. Buy the right to choose, not a model string from a screenshot.

**Premium data** such as PitchBook and Statista is a Pro reason for market questions. It does not cover a number you failed to click.

**Comet** is Perplexity's browser, with an assistant on paid plans. It is optional. You can do the citation workflow in the ordinary app.

**Perplexity Computer** is the agent that performs tasks rather than only answering. It is the feature that makes Pro feel larger than search, and the feature that makes Max ($167/month billed annually) a capacity plan. If you will not hand over a task, do not buy Max, and read the worth-it article before you buy Pro for Computer alone.

## FAQ

### Is Perplexity a search engine or a chatbot?

It answers in prose, like a chatbot, and it attaches web sources, like a search tool. Use it when you want both. Use Claude or ChatGPT when you want a draft and you already have the sources. Use Consensus or Elicit when the sources must be papers.

### Do I need Pro to do research?

No. Free includes cited basic search, 3 Pro Searches a day, and 1 research query a month. Pro is for higher limits, files, model choice, premium data, and Computer. The upgrade math is in the companion article.

### Are Perplexity's citations reliable?

They are links. Some are relevant and some are not. Open the page, and do not repeat a figure you did not see there. This review does not claim an accuracy rate.
