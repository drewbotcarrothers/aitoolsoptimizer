**Buy Plus at $20/month if ChatGPT is a work tool. Buy Go at $8/month if you mostly chat, upload, and make images and you can live with ads. Buy Pro only after Plus runs out on the specific feature you use every day.** OpenAI does not publish a fixed message count for these plans. The useful comparison is which features each tier unlocks, plus the context sizes on the plans page.

US prices below come from OpenAI's Help Center and plan documentation. The feature matrix was read from OpenAI's plans page on October 24, 2026. Go at $8 and the "may include ads" line are on the current Go help article and on that plans page. Pro at $100, $200, and $500 is on the Pro tiers help article. Plus at $20 is the US price in OpenAI's pricing documentation we checked October 16, 2026, and in OpenAI's January 16, 2026 Go announcement, which still describes Plus as the $20 plan beside Go. We did not benchmark answer quality. "Worth it" means the published feature matches a repeated job.

## What OpenAI actually publishes about limits

Free and Go both include unlimited everyday text chats, subject to abuse guardrails. Go raises limits on tools: uploads, images, voice, data analysis, and memory. The plans page gives approximate context sizes for GPT Instant:

| | Free | Go | Plus | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPT Instant total context | 27K | 54K | 54K | 128K |
| Approximate input the user can fill | ~12 pages | ~40 pages | ~40 pages | ~250 pages |
| GPT Reasoning total context | Varies | 256K | 256K | 400K |
| Approximate reasoning input | Varies | ~320 pages | ~320 pages | ~680 pages |

OpenAI says those page counts are approximations. The window also holds instructions, memories, and the model's own work, so your paste is smaller than the headline number. Go and Plus share the same Instant and reasoning window sizes in this table. The reason to pick Plus is the model and the feature list, not a larger Instant window.

The plans page also shows scheduled tasks on Go and Plus, and not on Free. Deep research and apps for deep research stay limited on Free and Go. Interactive tables and charts, image generation with Thinking, developer mode, and Sites are Plus and Pro. Create-and-share for plugins is on Go. Sharing plugins with a workspace is Pro.

There is no annual discount on Go, Plus, or Pro. OpenAI's plans FAQ says monthly billing for those individual plans, with annual billing on Business and Enterprise. If a checkout screen offers a year of Plus, read it carefully. It was not the published individual offer on the pages we used.

## Go: the $8 plan, with the ad caveat

Go is the right plan when Free's upload, image, or voice cap is the thing that stops you, and your work is not "reasoning model, research report, or code agent." OpenAI's pricing page says the plan may include ads. The Go help article says OpenAI may test ads on Go. If an ad in the product is a dealbreaker, the next step is Plus, not a hope that the test skips you.

Go does not include the advanced GPT-6 reasoning row, GPT-6 Astra, or GPT-6.1 Sol on the October 24 matrix. It does list GPT-6 Luna. Treat model names as a lineup OpenAI can reshuffle. The stable fact is the row labels: advanced reasoning is a Plus feature, and Pro reasoning is a Pro feature.

Voice with video starts at Go. Free has voice and does not have voice with video, on that same matrix.

## Plus: the default for work

Plus at $20 is the plan OpenAI describes as advanced work: GPT-6 reasoning, expanded deep research, projects, scheduled tasks, custom GPTs, expanded Codex, and ChatGPT Work on desktop, web, and mobile. The Office and Google Sheets extensions move from limited to included. That is the feature that matters if your day is a workbook, which is covered in [best AI for Excel and Google Sheets](/blog/best-ai-for-excel-and-google-sheets-2026/).

Plus is worth $20 when at least one of these is true most weeks:

- You wait on a reasoning answer, not a quick draft.
- You run deep research and check the sources.
- You use Codex or ChatGPT Work on something other than a one-off desktop trial.
- You want the spreadsheet or document extension inside the file's workflow.

Plus is a poor upgrade from Go if you only wanted more pictures. Go already raises image limits. Plus is also a poor second subscription if you already pay for Claude Pro and the two tools are doing the same writing job.

Business is the team product, for 2 or more users, with admin controls. OpenAI's pricing documentation on October 16, 2026 listed Business at $20/user/month billed annually or $25 billed monthly, and said Business does not train on business data by default. The October 24 plans page confirms Business exists and is priced per user. Confirm the live seat price before you send a team the October 16 number. Go, Free, and Plus are individual plans.

OpenAI also lists **ChatGPT for Teachers**, free for verified U.S. K–12 educators through June 2027. That is a school plan, not a discount on Plus. Teachers choosing among classroom tools should read [best AI tools for teachers](/blog/best-ai-tools-for-teachers-2026/).

## Pro: three prices, and Ultrafast is only on the $500 tier

The Pro help article, current as of this check, lists:

| Tier | Monthly price | Ultrafast |
|---|---|---|
| Pro 100 | $100 | Not included |
| Pro 200 | $200 | Not included |
| Pro 500 | $500 | Included, for GPT-6 Astra Ultrafast and GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast |

Pro 200 includes more usage than Pro 100. Pro 500 includes the most. The plans page describes the family as "your choice of 3 usage tiers," with Pro reasoning powered by GPT-6 Astra, longer Codex and Work sessions, maximum deep research, and maximum memory. Dots, OpenAI's always-on agent, is listed on Pro and not on Plus. Our directory page for [OpenAI Dots](/tools/openai-dots/) is the product note.

Ultrafast is not a hidden toggle on the cheaper Pro tiers. The help article says it draws on included usage first at **8×** the standard rate for the selected model, then on credits at **6×** the standard credit rate. Buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast.

One dated exception: customers with an active Pro 200 subscription at any point from September 22, 2026 through 10 a.m. Pacific on September 29, 2026 keep the previous allowance through October 29, 2026. After that, the subscription moves to the lower included allowance and the price stays $200. If you are reading this after October 29, 2026, ignore the grandfathering window and use the allowance shown in your account.

New subscriptions that are not grandfathered include that lower Pro 200 allowance. OpenAI says the price stays $200 because the models got more efficient. "Pro" on a receipt from 2025 is not a guarantee of the same usage in late 2026.

## FAQ

### Is ChatGPT Go worth $8?

It is worth $8 when Free blocks images, uploads, voice, or longer memory, and you are fine that the plan may include ads. It is not the plan for advanced reasoning or expanded Codex. Those start at Plus.

### Does Plus include more context than Go?

On the October 24 plans page, Go and Plus show the same GPT Instant window (54K) and the same reasoning window (256K). Pro is larger (128K Instant, 400K reasoning). Plus is a feature upgrade, not a context upgrade over Go.

### Which Pro tier should I buy?

None, until Plus is the constraint. If you are already there, Pro 100 is the first capacity step. Pro 200 is more usage at $200, with the lower allowance for new subscribers. Pro 500 is the only tier with Ultrafast, and Ultrafast spends the allowance faster because it is billed at a multiple of the standard rate.
