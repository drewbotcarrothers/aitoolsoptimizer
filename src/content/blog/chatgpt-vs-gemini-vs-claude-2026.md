## How the three assistants differ

**ChatGPT** is OpenAI’s general assistant on the web, Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android. The current consumer lineup is built on the GPT-6 family. Free accounts get unlimited text chats with GPT-6, subject to abuse guardrails, plus limited uploads, image creation, voice, deep research, memory, and Codex. Plus adds advanced reasoning, projects, scheduled tasks, custom GPTs, and expanded Codex usage.

**Claude** is Anthropic’s assistant, also on web, desktop, and mobile, with a Chrome extension and Microsoft 365 support on paid plans. It is built around Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku models, Projects, Artifacts, Research, and file creation. Claude Code, Anthropic’s coding agent, is included with Pro and Max. Anthropic lists model training as opt-out on Free, Pro, and Max.

**Gemini** is Google’s assistant, available with a Google account and inside Google apps on paid plans. Google says the Gemini app has more than 1 billion monthly users. Free includes the Flash-Lite model, image generation and editing, and 15 GB of storage. Paid Google AI plans add higher usage, video generation, Google Flow credits, and more storage.

A useful fourth option sits beside them rather than instead of them. [Perplexity](/tools/perplexity/) is an answer engine: it searches the web and attaches citations. It is the better default when the output is a researched answer, not a draft.

## Which $20 plan is the default?

For a single professional subscription, the three plans that compete directly are **ChatGPT Plus ($20)**, **Claude Pro ($20, or $17 annual)**, and **Google AI Pro ($19.99)**.

- Choose **ChatGPT Plus** when you want images, voice, custom GPTs, and Codex without a second subscription. See [Cursor vs Claude Code vs Codex](/blog/cursor-vs-claude-code-vs-codex-2026/) if coding is the main reason you would upgrade.
- Choose **Claude Pro** when you live in long documents, want Artifacts and Projects, and like that Claude Code is included. Usage limits are the trade-off heavy users report hitting on Pro; Max exists for that, at $100 or $200.
- Choose **Google AI Pro** when the storage and the Gmail/Docs integration are worth as much as the model. Five terabytes of storage is part of the plan, which changes the math if you already pay Google for storage.

Do not buy all three. If you are deciding how many subscriptions to keep, use [which AI subscription is worth paying for](/blog/which-ai-subscription-is-worth-paying-for/).

## What the free plans are actually for

Free ChatGPT is the most capable zero-dollar assistant in this set for open-ended chat, because OpenAI includes unlimited GPT-6 text chats. The limits show up on tools: uploads, images, voice, deep research, and Codex.

Free Claude is a real writing and analysis tool: web search, file creation, code execution, memory, and Artifacts are on the free plan, with usage limits. It is a weak choice if you need image or video generation.

Free Gemini is the right free plan if you already have a Google account and want image editing plus the assistant. Video generation starts on Google AI Plus, not Free.

[DeepSeek](/tools/deepseek/) is a separate free chat app with a very low API price. It is a budget option, not a substitute for the feature sets above. [Grok](/tools/grok/) has a free tier and a paid Plus plan at $8/month through The X Subscription, which also covers Cursor.

## How to choose in one afternoon

1. Write down the three tasks you will actually repeat next week.
2. Run each task in the free tier of ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. Paste the same source document.
3. Check one factual claim in each answer against a primary source. If you skip this, you are grading style, not accuracy.
4. Note which app you had to correct least, and which one already sits inside your email and docs.
5. Subscribe to that one plan for a month. Cancel if you do not open it on workdays.

## FAQ

### Is ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini better in 2026?

None of the three is better at every job. ChatGPT is the broadest consumer toolkit. Claude is the stronger default for long-form writing and document analysis. Gemini is the stronger default inside Google’s apps, and it is the one of the three with a full video-generation path on paid plans.

### Can I use only the free plans?

Yes, for light use. You will hit tool limits: ChatGPT limits advanced tools, Claude limits how much you can do in a session, and Gemini’s free model is Flash-Lite rather than Gemini 4 Argon. Pay when those limits block a task you do every week.

### Do these assistants cite sources?

Perplexity cites sources on answers by design. ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini can browse and run deeper research modes, but a normal chat answer is not a bibliography. Treat unsourced numbers, quotes, and medical or legal claims as unverified.

### Which plan includes a coding agent?

Codex is included with ChatGPT, with light access on Free and Go and expanded access on Plus and Pro. Claude Code is included with Claude Pro and Max. Gemini’s coding-agent limits rise on higher Google AI plans. A dedicated comparison is in [Cursor vs Claude Code vs Codex](/blog/cursor-vs-claude-code-vs-codex-2026/).
