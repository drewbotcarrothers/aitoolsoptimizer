**Students should start with free [Grammarly](/tools/grammarly/) plus free [Claude](/tools/claude/) or [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/), and pay only if a cap blocks real assignments.** Professionals who need clean writing everywhere they type should look at Grammarly Pro ($12/member/month billed annually). Teams that need a governed marketing system should look at [Jasper](/tools/jasper/), not a chatbot alone. Novelists should use [Sudowrite](/tools/sudowrite/).

This is a job guide, not a blind ranking. Prices are US list prices last verified October 16, 2026. We did not score prose quality in a bake-off. “Strong” means the vendor sells the tool for that job.

## Pick by the page you are writing

| You are writing | Use this | Why this, not a generic chatbot |
|---|---|---|
| Essays, email, and anything you type in the browser | [Grammarly](/tools/grammarly/) Free, then Pro | It edits in place. A chatbot makes you copy and paste |
| A paper, memo, or long draft from source notes | [Claude](/tools/claude/) | Projects, long context, and file creation are the product |
| A mixed day of drafts, plus images or research | [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) | Broadest toolkit if writing is only half the work |
| Paraphrase and clarity, especially if English is not your first language | [QuillBot](/tools/quillbot/) | The free plan paraphrases 100 words; Premium removes that cap |
| On-brand marketing at team scale | [Jasper](/tools/jasper/) | Brand voice, knowledge, and audiences are plan features |
| Notes and docs that stay in one workspace | [Notion AI](/tools/notion-ai/) | AI sits on the pages you already keep |
| Fiction | [Sudowrite](/tools/sudowrite/) | Built for story, not blog posts |
| A post that also has to rank | [Writesonic](/tools/writesonic/) or a dedicated SEO tool | Writesonic is now an AI-search visibility product. See the [SEO tools guide](/blog/best-ai-seo-tools-2026/) |

## Students

You do not need a $20 assistant to get unstuck. You do need a rule your school will accept.

**Start here**

- [Grammarly](/tools/grammarly/) Free covers basic correctness in the browser, docs, and email.
- [Claude](/tools/claude/) Free or [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) Free can explain a reading, outline a response, and critique a draft you wrote.
- [QuillBot](/tools/quillbot/) Free paraphrases up to 100 words in two modes. That is a sample, not a workflow. Premium is $8.33/month billed annually if you rewrite every day. QuillBot says the annual plan was shown at 58% off versus shorter billing; checkout currency can vary.
- [Gemini](/tools/gemini/) Free is the better student default if your school uses Google Docs.

**Grammarly Pro** is $144/member/year ($12/month), $60 per quarter, or $30 month-to-month. Pro adds rewrites, tone, and 2,000 AI prompts. Enterprise, sold via Superhuman Go, is the unlimited-prompt tier. There is no lifetime plan.

**Do not** paste an assignment prompt into a chatbot and submit the output. Use the assistant to quiz you, list counterarguments, or mark unclear sentences in your own draft. Keep the draft in your words. If your course bans AI, the best tool is none.

Sudowrite’s Hobby & Student plan is $10/month billed annually ($19 monthly) with 225,000 credits, but it is a fiction studio. It is the wrong subscription for essays.

## Professionals who write every day

Most knowledge workers need an editor that follows them and one drafter.

| Tool | Price to know | Documented strength | Documented limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grammarly Pro | $12/month annual | Rewrites and tone across apps | 2,000 AI prompts on Pro, not unlimited |
| Claude Pro | $20/month or $17 annual | Long documents, Projects, Artifacts | Usage caps; weak at image generation |
| ChatGPT Plus | $20/month | Drafts plus research, images, and custom GPTs | Not an in-line editor in every text box |
| Notion Business | $20/seat/month | Notion Agent, AI meeting notes, connected-app search | AI value depends on living in Notion |
| QuillBot Premium | $8.33/month annual | Unlimited paraphrase modes, citations support via plagiarism tools on Premium | Not a full drafting environment |

Notion’s Free and Plus plans are not the AI product. Notion’s pricing page puts Notion Agent and AI Meeting Notes on **Business at $20 per seat per month**. Custom Agents are a separate credit add-on at $10 per 1,000 credits on Business and Enterprise.

If your writing is mostly slides, switch guides: [Gamma vs Beautiful.ai vs Plus AI vs Canva](/blog/best-ai-presentation-makers/). If your writing is mostly code, use [Cursor vs Claude Code vs Codex](/blog/cursor-vs-claude-code-vs-codex-2026/).

### A professional stack that stays cheap

1. Grammarly Free or Pro for the last pass.
2. One drafter: Claude Pro **or** ChatGPT Plus, not both, until one of them fails a job you repeat. The choice is spelled out in [ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude](/blog/chatgpt-vs-gemini-vs-claude-2026/).
3. Nothing else until a team asks for brand control or a CMS workflow.

A handbook page that must reuse locked sentences is [ChatGPT vs Claude for handbook pages](/blog/chatgpt-vs-claude-for-handbook-pages-2026/). The person who keeps the canonical copy in the wiki, and does not publish it, is [best AI tools for docs ops](/blog/best-ai-tools-for-docs-ops-2026/).

## Marketing teams

[Jasper](/tools/jasper/) Pro is $69/seat/month billed monthly or $59/seat/month billed yearly, with a 7-day trial. The documented Pro bundle includes Canvas, core agents, 2 Brand Voices, 5 Knowledge assets, and 3 Audiences. Business is custom and adds an agent builder and admin controls. Jasper is worth it when several people must sound like the same company. It is expensive if you are one person writing tweets.

[Copy.ai](/tools/copy-ai/) is a go-to-market workflow product, not a student editor. Chat is $29/month or $24/month billed annually for 5 seats. The workflow plans start at Growth for $1,000/month billed annually. Do not land there from a blog-post use case.

[Writesonic](/tools/writesonic/) no longer makes sense as “the cheap AI writer.” Starter is $79/month billed annually and is priced for tracking how a brand shows up in ChatGPT and other answer engines. If that is the job, use the [AI SEO tools comparison](/blog/best-ai-seo-tools-2026/). If it is not, skip it.

## Fiction

[Sudowrite](/tools/sudowrite/) Hobby & Student is $10/month annual or $19 monthly (225,000 credits). Professional is $22 annual or $29 monthly (1,000,000 credits). Max is $44 annual or $59 monthly (2,000,000 credits, with rollover). There is a free trial and no permanent free plan in the docs we checked. Use it when the unit of work is a scene or a chapter. Use Claude when the unit of work is “edit this chapter I already wrote” and you do not want another subscription.

## How to use any of them without publishing junk

- Give the audience, the point, and a sample of your own writing.
- Ask for an outline or a critique before you ask for a full draft.
- Keep facts, numbers, and citations in your source notes. The model does not know your course reading or your company’s numbers.
- Run a final pass yourself. Grammarly will not catch a wrong claim that is grammatically perfect.

## FAQ

### What is the best free AI writing tool?

Grammarly Free plus Claude Free or ChatGPT Free covers more real writing than any single paid niche tool. QuillBot Free is only a 100-word paraphrase sample. Jasper and Sudowrite are paid (trial only).

### Is Grammarly Pro worth $12 a month?

It is worth it if you want rewrites and tone advice in the places you already type, and 2,000 prompts is enough. If you need unlimited prompts and admin controls, that is Enterprise, not Pro. If you mostly want a drafter, a $20 chatbot does a different job.

### Can I use AI writing tools for school?

Only inside your school’s rules. A defensible use is feedback on a draft you wrote. Submitting generated text as your own work can violate an academic integrity policy even when the tool is free.
