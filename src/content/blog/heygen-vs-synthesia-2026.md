## HeyGen, when the video is a marketing or training asset you ship yourself

HeyGen turns a script into a video of a stock avatar or a custom avatar built from a short clip or a reference image. Voice cloning starts on Creator. Free includes Avatar IV and one custom video avatar, capped at 3 videos a month and 1 minute each, in 30+ languages. That is enough to see whether an avatar belongs in the format. It is not a content calendar.

Creator at $29/month is the plan most solo users actually need: longer videos (up to 30 minutes), 1080p, no watermark, cloning, and 175+ languages and dialects, with 600 credits. Pro at $49 adds 4K and 1,000 credits. Business at $149 is where SSO, SCORM, LMS integrations, and interactive pieces such as quizzes show up, with videos up to 60 minutes and extra seats at $20/month. Yearly billing exists. Use the monthly price until the format sticks.

HeyGen is the better documented self-serve path for **multilingual marketing** (175+ languages on the paid plans, not only on Enterprise) and for a custom avatar on a $29 plan. It is also the path with a published Business price when a training team wants SCORM without a sales call.

## Synthesia, when the system around the video matters

Synthesia is built for presenter-led training and internal communication. Basic is a real free allotment: 10 minutes and 500 credits a month, plus 3 survey responses, with a watermark. Starter removes the logo and adds downloads. The yearly price shown on the page was $19/month against $29 monthly. Pro at $89/month with 6,000 credits is the practical team tier if you are not ready for Enterprise. Yearly billing saves up to 3 months at the rates Synthesia published.

Credits are shared across features. Video is about 100 credits a minute and dubbing about 76 a minute, beyond the first 10 minutes included on every plan. Survey responses are 350 credits after the first 3. A 6-minute training module is on the order of 600 credits if you are past the included minutes, which is a small slice of Pro's 6,000 and a large slice of Starter's 1,250 if you also dub it. Estimate minutes before you pick the pack. The jump to 48,000 credits at $676/month is a volume plan, not a default.

**1-click translation into 140+ languages, Brand Kits, SAML/SSO, unlimited video minutes, and SCORM export are Enterprise features** on the page we checked. Personal avatars exist, and Enterprise includes them without a tight cap. Roleplays and surveys are part of the learning product, not a marketing afterthought. Learner seats are a separate $29 per learner per month.

Synthesia is the better fit when procurement, an LMS, and a brand team are in the room. It is an expensive fit when one person needs a 60-second ad and HeyGen Creator already covers 1080p and watermark removal.

## What both tools still require from you

- A script that is true. The avatar will say the wrong price as confidently as the right one.
- Consent for a likeness. A custom or personal avatar is a person's face. Have permission, especially for an employee who might leave.
- A disclosure when your platform or your audience requires one. Realism is the sales pitch. It is also how a viewer gets misled.
- A credit estimate. "Unlimited avatars" is not the same sentence as "unlimited minutes." Synthesia's unlimited minutes are an Enterprise line. HeyGen's minute caps are plan caps (1, 30, or 60).

[invideo AI](/tools/invideo-ai/) is the alternative when you want a generated edit from a prompt and you do not need a consistent presenter. Starter is $10/seat/month on annual billing. It is a different category, noted here so a marketing team does not buy an avatar seat for a job a timeline tool does.

## FAQ

### Which is cheaper, HeyGen or Synthesia?

At the first paid tier, both list $29/month on monthly billing. Synthesia shows Starter at $19/month with yearly billing. HeyGen's next step is Pro at $49. Synthesia's next step is Pro at $89. HeyGen Business at $149 is a published training plan. Synthesia's comparable controls are Enterprise and custom. Cheaper depends on whether you need those controls.

### Which is better for translating training videos?

HeyGen puts 175+ languages on Creator and above. Synthesia puts 1-click translation into 140+ languages on Enterprise. For a solo creator, HeyGen's language list arrives earlier in the price ladder. For a company that also wants SSO and SCORM in the same contract, Synthesia Enterprise is the bundle that lists them together.

### Is the free plan enough?

Enough to judge the workflow. HeyGen Free is three one-minute videos. Synthesia Basic is 10 watermarked minutes and 500 credits. Neither is a production plan for a monthly series.
