## Start with one annoying task

Pick a task you do every day — like copying form submissions into a spreadsheet — and automate just that.

## Zapier or Make?

Zapier is quicker to learn; Make offers more control and often better value for complex flows.

## Add an AI step

Use an AI step to summarise emails, tag support tickets or draft replies for review.
