**A user interview guide written with AI is the question list for a decision you already named: who you will talk to, what you will not ask, the tasks you will watch, and how you will know you are done.** The model can draft questions from that brief. It cannot invent a persona, a quote, or a finding. Those do not exist yet. The notes after the call are a different workflow.

## What the guide contains

| Section | Who owns it | The model may |
|---|---|---|
| Decision | You, one sentence | Repeat it. It may not widen the study |
| Who | The recruiting rule you can actually run | Draft a screener from that rule. It may not add a demographic you did not name |
| Tasks | The product path a person verified | List steps. It may not add a feature |
| Questions | Open questions tied to the decision | Draft them. It may not add leading praise |
| Do not ask | Legal, privacy, and the topics you are not studying | Repeat the list |
| Done when | A number of sessions or a stop rule you set | Not declare the research finished |

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  decision["You write the decision and the do-not-ask list"] --> draft["Claude or ChatGPT drafts the guide"]
  draft --> cut["You remove leading questions and any persona that was not in the brief"]
  cut --> wiki["Notion holds the guide"]
  wiki --> call["You run the interview"]
  call --> notes["Notes are a separate step, after consent"]
```

A question that asks the user to confirm your launch is a pitch. Cut it. A fake quote in a readout later is [a hallucination](/blog/what-is-ai-hallucination-2026/). The check is [how to fact-check AI writing](/blog/how-to-fact-check-ai-writing-2026/).

"Draft an interview guide for the decision in my notes. Include who we will recruit, tasks, open questions, and the do-not-ask list I pasted. Do not add personas, sample quotes, or findings. Flag any question that leads the participant. If the decision is missing, stop and say Missing."

## FAQ

### How many questions is enough?

Enough to make the decision, few enough that a person can talk. If the model returns thirty questions, group them under the tasks and cut until the session fits the time you booked.

### Can AI write the screener?

Yes, from the recruiting rule you paste. No, from "find me users who love us." A screener that seeks praise will fill the calendar with the wrong people.

### Should I paste old interview transcripts into the guide prompt?

Only as background you label as past research, and only if you are allowed to. The new guide should not treat old quotes as this study's result.

### Can the guide update itself after each call?

The questions stay stable unless you write down why you changed them. A guide that mutates every session is not a study. Put changes in the log with a date.
