## What “ranking in ChatGPT” means

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Google’s AI Overviews do not publish a keyword rank tracker the way Google Search Console does. Vendors now sell **AI visibility** or **GEO** (generative engine optimization): you pick prompts, they record whether your brand is mentioned or cited, and they suggest pages to create or fix.

That measurement is useful. It is not a leaderboard you can buy your way up with a plugin. From what these products actually implement, the work is:

1. **Be the page that answers a specific question.** A comparison with prices, dates, and named entities is easier to quote than a vague intro. This article is an example of the shape, not a guarantee anyone will cite it.
2. **Use stable names.** Product, company, and plan names should match what people type.
3. **Put facts where a model can lift them.** Tables, definitions, and “last verified” dates are concrete. Unsupported superlatives are not.
4. **Earn ordinary web visibility too.** These suites still treat classic SEO as the base. Writesonic, Frase, Surfer, and Semrush all still audit sites and pages. An unknown domain with no clear pages will not become an AI citation because a tracker is installed.
5. **Check the citation.** If a tool says you were mentioned, open the answer and see whether the link is yours. Screenshots in a vendor dashboard are a lead, not a ranking.

No tool in this list can promise a ChatGPT placement. Anyone who sells that promise is ahead of the evidence.

**Pay for Semrush** when the questions are competitive: who ranks, on which terms, with which pages. **Skip it** if you only write four articles a month and you do not use the data. Frase Starter is a third of the annual SEO price.

## A page structure that is easy to cite

Use this on the URL you hope an answer engine will quote. It is a writing pattern, not a ranking factor we measured.

- Say the answer in the first paragraph, with the entities named.
- Follow with a table a model can lift without inventing cells.
- Put a date on every price, and link the vendor page.
- Do not invent statistics, test scores, or “we analyzed 10,000 prompts.”
- Add an FAQ only for questions people actually ask. Three precise answers beat ten padded ones.
- Interlink your own explanations so the site agrees with itself.

If the asset is a slide rather than a URL, the citation problem is different. Build the deck with a tool from [Gamma vs Beautiful.ai vs Plus AI vs Canva](/blog/best-ai-presentation-makers/) and keep the source page as the thing you want cited.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI SEO tool in 2026?

There isn’t one winner. Semrush is the best fit for a full dataset. Surfer is the best fit for optimizing articles. Frase is the best fit for a publish-and-monitor loop at a lower entry price ($39/month yearly). Writesonic is the best fit when AI-answer tracking is the primary product you are buying.

### How do I show up in ChatGPT answers?

Publish a specific, sourced page, make the entity names unambiguous, and earn ordinary mentions and links. Then measure with an AI-visibility feature (Semrush One, Surfer AI Search Analytics, Frase, or Writesonic) and fix pages where you are absent on prompts you care about. No vendor on this list guarantees inclusion.

### Is Writesonic still an AI writer?

It still generates articles, but the plans are priced around prompt tracking and AI visibility, starting at $79/month on annual billing. For general writing, look at the [writing-assistant guide](/blog/best-ai-writing-assistants/) instead of Starter.
