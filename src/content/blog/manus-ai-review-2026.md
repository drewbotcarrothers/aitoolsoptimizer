**Manus is a general-purpose agent: you give it a goal, it spends credits, and it returns a deliverable such as research, a site, or slides.** The free plan is 300 refresh credits a day, one task at a time, and two scheduled automations. Pro starts at $20/month for 4,000 credits and 20 concurrent tasks. It is worth a trial when the output you want is a finished artifact. It is the wrong tool when you need the same steps, in the same apps, every Tuesday.

This review uses Manus's help center, updated the day before this article and read October 24, 2026. We did not run a scored bake-off against ChatGPT or Claude. "Can really do" means the jobs Manus lists on that pricing article, plus the limits that decide whether a task finishes.

## Plans and what the credits buy you in capacity

| Plan | Price | Credits and concurrency | Listed extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 300 refresh credits daily. 1 concurrent task. 2 scheduled automations | Chat, and a way to try basic tasks |
| Pro, entry | From $20/month. Annual billing saves 17% | From 4,000 credits/month. 20 concurrent tasks. 20 scheduled automations | Advanced research, website deployment, slide generation, Wide Research, early access to beta features |
| Pro, next | From $40/month, with a 7-day free trial. Annual saves 17% | From 8,000 credits/month | The same feature list at a higher credit floor |
| Team | From $20 per seat/month. Annual saves 17% | Pro features, priced per seat | SSO, a data-training opt-out, usage analytics, internal access control, shared slide templates |

The help center says higher credit tiers exist and that the pricing page is the source of truth. It also says the older Basic and Plus names were renamed to Pro, and that existing members keep their renewal price and can move. A separate **Cue** phone-number subscription is not part of the membership. Phone use draws a weekly Cue allowance, not Manus credits.

## What Manus says it will produce

The Pro feature list on the help article is specific:

- **Advanced research** on a topic you name
- **Wide Research**, described as the better mode for high-intensity jobs
- **Professional website deployment**
- **Slide generation** with a structured deck
- **Schedules**, up to 20 on Pro, 2 on Free
- **Concurrency**, 20 tasks at once on Pro, 1 on Free

Manus's product pages also describe images, music, and video among the things the agent can generate. Treat those as additional outputs that spend credits, not as a reason to cancel a dedicated video or music tool. A HeyGen clip or a Suno track has a license line you can read. An agent-attached media file needs the same check before you publish it.

Slides are the output most likely to overlap a tool you already know. [Gamma](/tools/gamma/) Plus is $9/seat/month and is a slide product with a published credit table. Manus is the better pitch when the slides are the last step of a research task ("find the five competitors and put them in a deck"). Gamma is the better pitch when you already have the outline. The comparison of slide tools is [Gamma vs Canva](/blog/gamma-vs-canva-presentations/) and [best AI presentation makers](/blog/best-ai-presentation-makers/).

## What an agent session actually requires from you

Manus will plan the steps. You still have to:

1. Name the deliverable (a memo, a one-page site, a ten-slide deck) and the length.
2. Name the sources that count, and the ones that do not.
3. Expect to check every figure. Research output from an agent is a draft with links, not a filing.
4. Watch the first small task's credit use before you schedule a daily run. Three hundred daily credits on Free is a sample. A wide research job can be a different order of magnitude, and Manus does not publish a credit-per-task rate in the help article we used. The pricing page's "everything is subject to what is displayed" line is doing real work.

Free is the right plan for that first measured task. The $40 tier's 7-day trial is the right plan if the first task clearly needed more than the daily refresh and you want a week of the higher credit floor before a renewal. The $20 tier is the right plan if 4,000 credits and 20 scheduled jobs match a weekly rhythm you already have.

Team is the right plan when several people share templates and someone needs SSO and a training opt-out. Those controls are listed on Team, not on individual Pro. Do not assume Pro includes the opt-out.

## How to judge it in an afternoon

1. Sign up on Free. Give it one narrow task with a format and a source list. Example: "Compare three named vendors on price and plan limits, in a table, and link each pricing page. Do not add vendors I did not name."
2. Read every link. Count how many claims had no link.
3. Look at the credits that task used. Multiply by the number of times you would run it in a month.
4. If the monthly total fits in 4,000 with room for retries, Pro's entry tier is the documented match. If the task was really a search, stop and use Perplexity. If the task was really a Zap, stop and use an automation tool.

That afternoon is the review. A leaderboard score would pretend we published a hundred tasks. We did not.

## FAQ

### Is Manus free?

Yes. The help center lists a $0 plan with 300 daily refresh credits, one concurrent task, and two scheduled automations. Pro starts at $20/month for 4,000 credits. Annual billing saves 17%.

### What can Manus do that ChatGPT cannot?

Manus is sold as an agent that researches, deploys a site, builds slides, and runs scheduled tasks on a credit meter, including a wide mode for larger research. ChatGPT is a subscription assistant with its own agent features on higher tiers. Use Manus when the deliverable is the artifact and you want that credit model. Use ChatGPT when you want a conversation and a draft in the same thread.

### Does the Team plan include privacy controls?

The help center lists SSO, a data-training opt-out, usage analytics, and internal access control on Team. Individual Pro's list in that article does not include the opt-out. Read the current pricing page before you rely on a control for client data.
