**Most agents do not need a "real estate AI." They need a writing pass, a design pass, and a follow-up pass, with the listing facts kept outside the model.** Use [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) or [Claude](/tools/claude/) to draft from facts you supply, [Grammarly](/tools/grammarly/) on the email, [Canva](/tools/canva/) for the post, and [Gamma](/tools/gamma/) or [HeyGen](/tools/heygen/) only when the deliverable is a deck or a presenter video.

Prices are US list prices from vendor pages checked October 16, 2026, except where a later date is named. Nothing here is a fair-housing opinion, a brokerage policy, or a test of which model "writes more luxurious" copy. Fit labels follow the job each product sells.

## Listing copy: give the model a fact sheet, not a vibe

Paste a structured note, then ask for two lengths: MLS remarks under the character limit your form actually has, and a shorter post. A usable fact sheet is boring on purpose:

- Address or "do not include the address"
- Status, price, beds, baths, interior square footage, lot size, year built, and the source of each figure (tax record, measurement, seller)
- What the buyer can verify on a tour
- What you are not allowed to say (school quality as a proxy, "safe area," family status, religion, and anything your brokerage has banned)
- Words you want kept (a view, a shop, a transit stop) only if they describe the property rather than the people who live nearby

[Claude](/tools/claude/) is the better default when the listing package is a long PDF plus your notes. Projects hold the style guide. [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) Plus is the better default when you also want images or a custom GPT that always asks for the fact sheet first. Free tiers are enough to learn the prompt. Plus or Pro is worth it when you are writing listings several days a week and the cap interrupts you. The buying rule is in [which AI subscription is worth paying for](/blog/which-ai-subscription-is-worth-paying-for/).

If your office is Microsoft 365, [Copilot](/tools/microsoft-copilot/) in Word on Personal ($9.99/month), Family ($12.99/month, AI for the owner only), or Premium ($19.99/month) drafts where the remarks already live. Those dollar prices are from Microsoft's US pages as checked October 16, 2026.

### The check that has to happen before you paste into the MLS

- Every number matches the source you named. Models invent square footage.
- No steering. In the United States, the Fair Housing Act restricts advertising that indicates a preference or limitation based on protected characteristics. This article is not legal advice. Your brokerage's counsel and your MLS rules outrank a chatbot.
- No claim about a permit, a renovation, or a school boundary unless you have the document.
- Read it aloud. If it could describe any house on the street, it will not help this seller.

## Follow-ups: short, specific, and sent by you

The useful AI email is four lines: what you saw together, the one question the buyer asked, the fact you owe them, and the next time on the calendar. Generate that from your notes. Do not generate a "just checking in" sequence that could go to anyone.

[Grammarly](/tools/grammarly/) Pro is $144/member/year ($12/month), $60 a quarter, or $30 month-to-month. The support article, re-read October 24, 2026, confirms those prices and that Grammarly is part of Superhuman. Pro is worth it for agents who live in email and want rewrites in the browser. It is optional if Claude or ChatGPT already produced a clean draft and you only need a spellcheck. The longer buying note is [Is Grammarly Pro worth it in 2026?](/blog/is-grammarly-pro-worth-it-2026/).

[HubSpot](/tools/hubspot/) belongs in this list when the follow-up has to be logged against a contact, not when you need a nicer adjective. [Zapier](/tools/zapier/) is the glue if the form, the CRM, and the inbox are separate products. Compare that glue with Make and n8n in [Zapier vs Make vs n8n](/blog/zapier-vs-make-vs-n8n/) before you add another monthly seat. A solo agent with 15 active clients can run the follow-up from a checklist. Automation pays when the same handoff happens every day and someone keeps missing it.

## Social posts and property video

[Canva](/tools/canva/) Free is the default for a single photo, a price, and a line of type. Pro at $18/month or $180/year is the upgrade when you also need background removal, brand kit, and more AI uses. Put your own photos in the frame. An AI exterior that is not the house is a misleading ad, whatever the caption says.

[Gamma](/tools/gamma/) Plus at $9/seat/month is enough for a seller presentation you can send as a link, if the comps are yours. Gamma's free credits do not refresh on a schedule. The full credit table is in [best AI presentation makers](/blog/best-ai-presentation-makers/). Use Gamma for the meeting. Use Canva for the square crop. They are not substitutes, which is the point of [Gamma vs Canva](/blog/gamma-vs-canva-presentations/).

[HeyGen](/tools/heygen/) Creator at $29/month makes a presenter video from a script. Use it for a market-update series or a listing walkthrough you narrate honestly. Free is 3 videos a month, up to 1 minute, which is a test, not a listing calendar. [CapCut](/tools/capcut/) is the cheaper path when you already shot the house on a phone: Free for a basic cut, Pro at $19.99/month or $179.99/year when you want the paid templates and AI credits. Say on camera, or in the description your brokerage requires, when a voice or a face is synthetic.

## A weekly stack that stays under $30

1. One assistant at $0 until a cap hits a day you are writing listings. Then one of Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus, not both.
2. Canva Free.
3. Grammarly Free, and Pro only if email is the bottleneck.
4. No avatar tool until you have a script you would read yourself.

That is a complete public-facing stack. Add Gamma when seller meetings are weekly. Add HeyGen when video is weekly. Add a CRM automation when leads are slipping, not because a thread called it a growth hack.

## FAQ

### What AI writes the best real estate listings?

The one you feed a fact sheet and then edit. Claude and ChatGPT are both capable drafters. The listing gets better when the facts are complete and the prohibited phrases are in the prompt. It does not get better because the model has a real-estate mode.

### Can I let AI answer buyer inquiries automatically?

You can draft. Sending without a read is how wrong prices and fair-housing problems leave the office. If a CRM such as HubSpot is going to send anything on a trigger, the trigger should be a template you approved, with the property fields filled from your database, not a fresh generation per lead.

### Is there a free way to make listing graphics?

Yes. Canva Free, your photos, and a caption you wrote or lightly edited. Pay for Canva Pro when the free AI allowance or the brand kit is what you are actually missing.
