## What not to automate

Do not let a model update the price field in the CRM from a chat. Paste the approved number, or leave the field alone. Do not generate a one-off claim for a single rep's deal. That claim becomes the next rep's template. Do not describe HubSpot's promo as the ongoing price after the offer ends. Checkout on the day you buy is the price.

## FAQ

### Is revenue enablement the same as enablement?

On this site, enablement writes the script, the card, and the deck. Revenue enablement gets those words into the CRM, the sequence, and the call review. One person may do both. The tools differ. A beautiful doc nobody opens is an enablement win and a revenue-enablement miss.

### Do we need Gong before we have a script?

No. Gong reviews conversations you record. If the approved sentence does not exist, the library will show the improvisation. Write the sentence. Then decide if you need the library.

### Can Apollo replace the CRM?

Not in this workflow. Apollo can sequence and hold contacts. The system the company treats as the account record should be the one you already run, such as HubSpot's CRM. Two account records will disagree about the price you quoted.

### What if the rep needs a different sentence for one industry?

Write it on the source page, with an owner, or do not say it. A model-local variant in a personal chat is how the unapproved claim ships. The page can have a section per industry. It should not have a section per rep.
