## What is an AI image generator?

You describe an image in words, and the model creates it. The quality of your description makes a huge difference.

## A simple prompt formula

`subject + setting + style + lighting + camera/composition`

Example: *a fox reading a book in a cozy library, watercolor style, warm lamp light, wide shot*.

## Which tool should you start with?

Canva is easiest, ChatGPT's image tool is conversational, and Midjourney gives the most artistic results.
