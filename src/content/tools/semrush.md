## What Semrush is

Semrush calls itself "the leading platform to grow and measure brand visibility—from search results to AI answers." It started as an SEO keyword and competitor tool and now spans toolkits for SEO, AI visibility, traffic and market research, local, content, social, advertising and AI PR, plus enterprise solutions and an app center.

Its newest bundle, **Semrush One**, combines traditional SEO with AI search: you can track how your brand appears in AI answers, monitor custom prompts and measure AI market share against competitors.

## Key features

### SEO toolkit
Keyword research across billions of keywords, backlink analysis, rank tracking and an AI-ready Site Audit.

### AI visibility
Prompt-level visibility tracking, AI visibility reports for any domain and AI market share analysis.

### Traffic and market
Benchmark competitors, track trends and get audience insights.

### Content
Find top-ranking topic ideas and score and optimize content in real time.

### Data in AI assistants
Bring live Semrush data into chat assistants for data-backed workflows.

### Other toolkits
Local, social, advertising and AI PR toolkits, plus enterprise options.

## Pricing breakdown

From Semrush's pricing page, checked October 16, 2026 (USD). Annual billing saves up to 17%.

| Plan | Monthly | Billed annually | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 1 demo project, 10 reports/day |
| SEO | $139/mo | $117.33/mo | Freelancers and small businesses |
| Semrush One Starter (SEO + AI Search) | $199/mo | $165.17/mo | Small teams and agencies |
| Semrush One Pro+ (SEO + AI Search) | $299/mo | $248.17/mo | Growing teams across markets; unlimited daily prompts |

**Add-ons:** additional users from $45/month, Lead Generation $90/month, Base Report $10/month and Pro Report $20/month. Other toolkits (local, content, social, advertising) are priced separately; Enterprise is via sales. A free trial is available on paid plans.

## Best use cases

- **Agencies:** audit prospects and report to clients.
- **SEO managers:** find keyword gaps against competitors.
- **Content teams:** plan topics with search demand.
- **Brand teams:** monitor visibility in AI answers.

## How to get started

1. Create a free account at semrush.com.
2. Add your domain as a project and run Site Audit.
3. Check competitors in Domain Overview.
4. Start a trial of the plan you need.
5. Set up rank and AI prompt tracking.

## Tips and tricks

- **Fix audit errors first**, then chase keywords.
- **Use keyword gap** against 3–4 competitors.
- **Track a handful of AI prompts** that matter to buyers.
- **Avoid unnecessary add-ons** until you need them.

Example AI prompt to track:

> "What is the best project management software for small construction companies?"
