## What Gong is

Gong calls itself "the only Revenue AI OS that turns insight into action." The platform captures customer interactions—calls, video meetings and emails—analyzes what's working, and automates what happens next. Its foundation is the **Revenue Graph**, a rich dataset of customer interactions, on top of which a team of specialized AI agents and apps support every stage from prospecting to expansion.

Gong is designed for revenue teams: sales reps, managers, customer success, enablement and RevOps leaders who want to close deals faster, predict pipeline risk, automate handoffs, increase win rates and predict churn.

## Key features

### Conversation intelligence
Record and analyze customer conversations to understand what top performers do differently.

### Gong Engage
Prioritize and personalize sales engagement, keeping communications targeted.

### Gong Forecast
Unbiased, centralized analytics for forecast accuracy.

### Gong Enable
Coaching and enablement grounded in real customer interactions.

### AI agents
Tuned agents power workflows with governed agentic execution.

## Best use cases

- **Coaching:** managers review key moments in calls and coach reps.
- **Forecasting:** leaders spot at-risk deals before quarter end.
- **Handoffs:** sales-to-CS handoffs carry full context automatically.
- **Market insights:** product and marketing teams learn what customers say about competitors.

## How to get started

1. Request a proposal on gong.io/pricing with your team size.
2. Take the product tour to evaluate modules.
3. Plan call-recording consent policies with legal.
4. Connect CRM, email, calendar and conferencing tools.
5. Roll out to a pilot team and define success metrics.

## Tips and tricks

- **Agree on key topics** such as competitors, pricing and objections to review from day one.
- **Build call libraries** of great moments for onboarding.
- **Review forecasts weekly** alongside deal activity.
- **Measure ramp time** before and after rollout to prove ROI.
