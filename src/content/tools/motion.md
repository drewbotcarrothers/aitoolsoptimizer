## What Motion is

Motion is an AI productivity platform that bills itself as an all-in-one workspace for "AI Projects, AI Tasks, AI Calendar, AI Meetings, AI Docs, AI Notes, AI Reports, AI Workflows, and more." Its core idea: a due date isn't a plan. Motion takes everything on your plate and schedules it into real time slots on your calendar, then re-plans automatically when meetings move or priorities change.

For teams, Motion builds an optimal plan for every member based on tasks, deadlines, assignees and dependencies, and keeps statuses and reports up to date. Motion claims projects get done 32% faster by eliminating idle time between tasks.

## Key features

### AI calendar and prioritization
Motion's AI detects your most urgent and important tasks and schedules them around your meetings, warning you when something is at risk.

### AI project builder
Describe a project and add docs; Motion builds the whole project with tasks, deadlines, assignees and stages. Motion says it's over 90% accurate out of the box, needing only a review.

### Task capture
Forward emails from Gmail, Outlook or iCloud, convert Zoom, Meet or Teams meetings and Slack or Teams messages into tasks, or use Siri.

### Team visibility
Automatic status updates, next-step assignment, delay flags and project reports.

### Collaboration
Comment and ask questions inside projects, tasks and docs so discussion stays with the work.

## How to get started

1. Start a free trial at usemotion.com.
2. Connect your Google or Outlook calendar.

## Tips and tricks

- **Always add durations**—Motion schedules by time.
- **Mark hard deadlines** so they're protected.
- **Forward emails** instead of retyping tasks.
- **Review the plan each morning** for a minute.
