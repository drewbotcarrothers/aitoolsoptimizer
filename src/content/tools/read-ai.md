## What Read AI is

Read AI's pitch is simple: "Every meeting should have outcomes." It joins meetings on Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams, records and transcribes them, and produces summaries enriched with context from your email and calendar, plus AI agents that recommend what happens next.

Read AI is independent and platform-agnostic, so it also summarizes email and messaging and provides **enterprise search** across connected tools. **Ask Read** answers questions like "what was decided and who owns it?" with citations, and **Ada** acts as an AI executive assistant.

## Key features

### Meeting summaries and transcripts
Automatic recaps, action items and searchable transcripts in 20+ languages.

### Ask Read and enterprise search
Ask about decisions, owners and open items across meetings and messages, with citations.

### Speaker coach
A personalized meeting coach with feedback on how meetings went.

### Topic Readouts
Summaries organized by topic across conversations.

### Playback and highlights
Audio and video playback and video highlights on Enterprise.

### Integrations
Basic integrations on Free; Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Confluence, Zapier and webhooks on Pro.

## Best use cases

- **Managers:** catch up on missed meetings in minutes.
- **Sales teams:** push call notes to Salesforce or HubSpot.
- **Project leads:** find decisions and owners across weeks of meetings.
- **Executives:** get summaries of email and chat alongside meetings.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at read.ai and connect your calendar.
2. Let Read AI join your next meeting.
3. Review the summary and coaching feedback.
4. Try Ask Read on a past decision.
5. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited transcripts and integrations.

## Tips and tricks

- **Let attendees know** Read AI is present.
- **Use Ask Read** before weekly planning.
- **Connect your CRM** on Pro to avoid manual notes.
- **Review coaching metrics** to improve meetings.

Example Ask Read question:

> What did we decide about the Q3 launch date in the last three product meetings, and who owns the open items?
