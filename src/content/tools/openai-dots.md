## What OpenAI Dots are

Dots are OpenAI's always-on agents, built into ChatGPT. OpenAI describes them as "remarkably capable, always-on agents built to handle everything," powered by **GPT-6 Astra**. Where a ChatGPT chat answers and stops, a dot takes on ongoing responsibility: it works out next steps as things change, follows up between conversations and reaches out with results or decisions that need you.

Each dot lives in the cloud with **its own computer and browser**, so it can keep working when your devices are off. It starts with context from your ChatGPT memory, uses Codex and your connected tools, and can connect to over 4,000 apps through plugins. OpenAI also previewed "specialist dots" for organizations, with their own identity and deep integrations.

## Key features

### Its own cloud computer
Dots research, analyze data, prepare documents and run software on a cloud computer. You can open it and select **Take over** to step in, then **Return control**.

### Memory and context
Your dot starts with your ChatGPT memory and preferences and learns from feedback over time.

### Apps and plugins
Connect email, calendar, files and other apps through plugins. You can also connect your own computer so local files, code and apps are available.

### Messaging
Message or call your dot in ChatGPT on web, mobile and desktop, or connect a contact method such as Slack or Teams.

### Permissions and safety
Custom Rules let you allow, require approval for or block specific actions. Actions that could affect accounts or share information go through Auto-review, and steps like changing your password stay with you.

## Pricing breakdown

Dots don't have a separate price. OpenAI says your first dot is included in Pro or Business Premium at no extra cost, with an allowance for deeper work. Plan prices from OpenAI's help center, checked October 16, 2026 (USD):

| Plan | Price | Dots access |
|---|---|---|
| Pro 100 | $100/mo | Rolling out (18+, outside EEA, Switzerland, UK) |
| Pro 200 | $200/mo | Rolling out (same) |
| Pro 500 | $500/mo | Rolling out (same) |
| Business Premium seat | $125/user/mo monthly, $100 annually | Rolling out |
| Enterprise / Edu / Healthcare | Contact sales | Beta, off by default |

Pro is billed monthly only. OpenAI says it plans to let you add more dots and scale their speed or monthly workload in the future; pricing for that hasn't been published.

## Best use cases

- **Ongoing projects:** keep track of a project, work out next steps and follow up.
- **Research and analysis:** gather information and prepare documents while you do other things.
- **Software:** delegate build tasks to Codex.
- **Updates in your tools:** get progress reports in ChatGPT, Slack or Teams.

## How to get started

1. Check that you're on an eligible plan and region.
2. Open dots in the ChatGPT mobile app, desktop app or a desktop browser.
3. Name your dot and customize its appearance.
4. Connect plugins for apps like email, calendar and files (optional).
5. Give it a goal and the context it needs.

## Tips and tricks

- **Set Custom Rules early** to decide what it can do without approval.
- **Give it ongoing responsibilities,** not just one-off questions.
- **Use Take over** when a site needs a human step.
- **Keep the ChatGPT app open** if your dot is using your connected computer.

Example request:

> Keep track of our Q4 product launch. Check the shared launch doc and my email each morning, list anything overdue, and draft follow-ups for me to approve.
