## What Julius AI is

Julius AI is an AI workspace that started as an "AI data analyst" and now covers research and creation too. You upload a spreadsheet, CSV or document—or connect a database on Business—and ask questions in plain English. Julius writes and runs the analysis behind the scenes and returns charts, tables and explanations you can turn into presentations and reports.

Its pricing page sums it up: "Analyze data and research, create presentations and reports, build websites, and generate images and video."

## Key features

### Conversational analysis
Ask questions like "Which region grew fastest?" and get charts and answers.

### Charts, presentations and reports
Turn findings into shareable visuals and slides.

### Agents
Browser agents for research and web tasks, with unlimited parallel agents on Pro.

### Data connectors
Postgres, BigQuery, Snowflake and more on Business, with no table limits.

### Creation tools
Publish websites on a custom Julius domain and generate images and video.

## Best use cases

- **Students:** analyze survey data for a class project.
- **Small businesses:** find trends in sales spreadsheets.
- **Analysts:** produce quick charts for a weekly report.
- **Researchers:** clean and visualize datasets.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at julius.ai.
2. Upload a spreadsheet or CSV.
3. Ask a simple question about the data.
4. Request a chart and refine it.
5. Turn results into a presentation or report.

## Tips and tricks

- **Describe your columns** if names are unclear.
- **Ask Julius to show its method** so you can verify results.
- **Break big questions into steps** to save credits.
- **Remove sensitive data** before uploading.

Example prompt:

> Using this sales CSV, show monthly revenue by region for 2026 as a line chart, identify the fastest-growing region, and explain the result in three sentences.
