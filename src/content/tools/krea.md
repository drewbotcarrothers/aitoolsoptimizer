## What Krea is

Krea is a creative AI platform that gathers leading image and video models in one interface and adds its own tools for iteration and production. It became known for **real-time generation**—images update as you type or draw—and has grown into a full suite with upscaling, LoRA fine-tuning, video style transfer, node-based workflows and an agent. Krea says it's trusted by 40 million creatives, enterprises, agencies and studios.

Its in-house models (Krea 2 and the newer Krea 3) sit alongside third-party models such as Nano Banana, Seedance and Veo, all paid for with compute units.

## Key features

### Real-time generation
Iterate instantly as you type or sketch.

### Images and video
Generate with many models; Pro unlocks all video models.

### Upscale and enhance
Upscale up to 2K on free and up to 22K with paid upscalers like Topaz Standard.

### LoRA training
Personalize models with your own characters, styles or objects.

### Krea Nodes, App Builder and Agent
Build visual workflows, turn them into reusable apps, or brief the Krea Agent to plan and iterate across models.

### Plugins, API and MCP
Use Krea in Photoshop, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, 3D tools and chat assistants.

## Best use cases

- **Concept artists:** sketch and see variations in real time.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at krea.ai.
2. Try real-time generation with a simple prompt.
3. Explore the model picker for images and video.
4. Upscale a favorite result.
5. Upgrade to Pro for all video models and more units.

## Tips and tricks

- **Use real-time mode** to find a composition, then render with a premium model.
- **Train a LoRA** for consistent brand characters.
- **Use relaxed generations** to save units on paid plans.
- **Pick yearly billing** to save 40%.

Example prompt:

> People running through a city park at dawn, risograph poster style, two-color print in teal and orange, grainy texture
