## What Speak is

Speak is a language-learning app built around speaking out loud. Its tagline is "Don't just learn languages, start speaking them." Instead of tapping multiple-choice answers, you repeat phrases, answer prompts and hold AI-driven conversations, and Speak gives real-time feedback on pronunciation and phrasing at your level.

Speak says it has been downloaded more than 15 million times worldwide. It has a formal partnership with OpenAI and is backed by the OpenAI Startup Fund.

## Key features

### AI conversation practice
Free-talking practice with an AI partner, without the anxiety of being judged by a person.

### Real-time feedback
Corrections on pronunciation and phrasing as you speak.

### Lessons matched to your level
Structured lessons that adapt to what you know.

### Speak Tutor
The full Speak Tutor experience is part of Premium Plus.

### Personalized review
Premium Plus builds custom review from your mistakes and creates Made for You lessons.

### Languages
Spanish, French, Korean, Japanese, Italian and Simplified Chinese for English speakers.

## Best use cases

- **Travelers:** practice phrases before a trip.
- **Beginners:** build confidence speaking from day one.
- **Intermediate learners:** get comfortable with free conversation.
- **Busy people:** fit short speaking sessions into the day.

## How to get started

1. Go to speak.com and click Get Started.
2. Start your 7-day trial.
3. Download the app and log in.
4. Do a lesson a day and try a free conversation.
5. Choose Premium or Premium Plus before the trial ends.

## Tips and tricks

- **Speak out loud every time**, even when it feels awkward.
- **Repeat lessons** with low pronunciation scores.
- **Use free talk** to practice real situations.
- **Set a daily reminder** to build the habit.

Example practice scenario:

> Practice ordering at a café in Spanish: greet the barista, order a large coffee with oat milk and a croissant, and ask how much it costs.
