## What Fathom is

Fathom is an AI notetaker for video meetings. Connect it to your calendar and it joins your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams calls, records and transcribes them, and delivers a summary within moments of hanging up. You can search across past calls, clip key moments and share them, and on team plans, push meeting insights straight into your CRM.

Fathom built its reputation on a generous free plan with unlimited recordings. It's now part of Superhuman, the email and productivity company.

## Key features

### Recording and transcription
Unlimited recordings and transcripts on every plan, including Free.

### AI summaries and action items
Instant summaries on Free; Premium adds advanced summaries and AI-generated action items.

### Clips, playlists and search
Highlight moments, build playlists (for example, customer objections) and search across all calls.

### Team collaboration
Comments, folders and keyword alerts on Team plans.

### CRM sync and Deal View
Business maps meeting insights into specific CRM fields automatically and summarizes deals across calls.

## Best use cases

- **Sales:** a rep runs discovery calls and Fathom fills in CRM fields like budget and timeline.
- **Customer success:** a CSM shares a clip of a customer's feature request with product.
- **Recruiting:** interviewers search past interviews for answers to the same question.
- **Internal meetings:** a manager gets action items emailed after every one-on-one.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at fathom.ai with Google or Microsoft.
2. Connect your calendar and choose which meetings Fathom should join.
3. Tell participants the meeting is being recorded.
4. Review the summary after your first call and edit if needed.
5. Connect your CRM or Slack on the relevant plan.

## Tips and tricks

- **Announce recording** at the start of calls to stay compliant and build trust.
- **Use keyword alerts** for competitor names or pricing mentions.
- **Build clip playlists** for onboarding new reps.
- **Use LLM integrations** to ask questions across your call history.
