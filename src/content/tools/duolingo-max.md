## What Duolingo Max is

Duolingo is the world's most popular language-learning app, built on short, gamified lessons, streaks and leaderboards. **Duolingo Max** is its top subscription tier. It launched as one of the first consumer products built on OpenAI's GPT-4, adding AI features on top of Super Duolingo: **Explain My Answer** (why your answer was right or wrong), **Roleplay** (practice conversations in realistic scenarios) and later **Video Call with Lily**, a spoken conversation with one of Duolingo's characters.

In 2026, things changed. According to Class Central's report on Duolingo's Q2 2026 earnings call, the cost of a Video Call dropped from about 30 cents to under a cent after Duolingo switched to open-source models, so the company moved Video Call into the cheaper Super plan. CEO Luis von Ahn said openly that he didn't yet know what Max would be for, listing options from limiting calls on Super to adding new Max features or sunsetting Max.

## Key features

### Video Call with Lily
Real-time speaking practice that adapts to your level. Now included in Super for most new subscribers, with plans to extend it to existing ones.

### Roleplay
Practice conversations, such as ordering at a café or planning a trip, and get feedback afterward.

### Explain My Answer
A short explanation of the grammar or vocabulary behind a correction, so you learn from mistakes instead of just repeating them.

### Core Duolingo experience
Lessons in dozens of languages, streaks, leagues and practice reviews. Paid plans remove ads and give unlimited Energy.

## Pricing breakdown

Duolingo doesn't list prices on its website; they vary by country, app store and promotions. The figures below are what Class Central observed in a US account during 2026 and are a guide only.

| Plan | What it includes | Observed price |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Core lessons with ads and Energy limits | $0 |
| Lite (testing) | Ads, double Energy that refills faster | $35.99/yr |
| Super / Individual | No ads, unlimited Energy, Video Call for most new subscribers | $119.99/yr |
| Super Family | Up to 6 accounts | $119.99/yr |
| Max | Super plus AI features; not offered in Class Central's test account | Varies |

## Best use cases

- **Speaking confidence:** a traveler uses Video Call for five minutes a day before a trip to Spain.
- **Habit building:** a busy parent keeps a daily streak with 10-minute lessons.
- **Understanding errors:** a learner reviews explanations for repeated grammar mistakes.

## How to get started

1. Download Duolingo free and take the placement test.
2. Set a realistic daily goal (5–15 minutes).
3. Open the upgrade screen to see which plans and prices your account is offered.
4. Check whether Super already includes Video Call before considering Max.
5. Use a free trial if offered, and set a reminder before it renews.

## Tips and tricks

- **Speak out loud** during lessons, even when not required.
- **Use Video Call after lessons** to apply new vocabulary.
- **Pair Duolingo with input** like podcasts or TV in your target language.
- **Compare platform prices**; app-store pricing can differ from web.
