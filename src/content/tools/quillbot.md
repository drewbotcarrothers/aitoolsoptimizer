## What QuillBot is

QuillBot is an AI writing toolkit from Learneo. It started as a paraphrasing tool—paste a sentence and get alternative phrasings in different styles—and has grown into a suite that includes a Grammar Checker, AI Detector, Plagiarism Checker, AI Humanizer, AI Chat, AI Image Generator, Translator, Summarizer and Citation Generator. It works on the web, through browser extensions such as QuillBot for Chrome, and in apps.

## Key features

### Paraphraser
10+ modes from Academic to Simple. Premium unlocks unlimited modes and lets you create your own—"sound like a CEO, pro scholar, or bestselling author."

### Grammar Checker
Basic fixes on Free; advanced recommendations on Premium.

### AI Detector and Humanizer
Check whether text reads as AI-generated and rewrite it to sound more natural; full access on Premium.

### Plagiarism Checker
Helps prevent unintentional plagiarism (Premium).

### Summarizer, Translator and Citations
Basic or custom summaries, translation and citation generation.

## Pricing breakdown

From QuillBot's Premium page and Help Center, checked October 16, 2026.

| Plan | Price | Highlights |

| Premium | $8.33/mo billed annually | Unlimited modes, advanced grammar, full humanizer and AI Detector, custom summaries, plagiarism prevention |
| Team Plan | Contact / see page | Usage metrics, user management, centralized billing, data control, priority support |

Premium is available on monthly, quarterly and annual plans; the annual plan is the best value (the page showed "58% off" for annual). QuillBot notes prices may adjust by location and local currency at checkout, and usage limits apply.

## Best use cases

- **Students:** rephrase notes and summarize readings (within academic rules).
- **ESL writers:** make sentences sound natural.
- **Professionals:** tighten emails and reports.
- **Researchers:** generate citations and summaries.

## How to get started

1. Open quillbot.com and sign up free.
2. Paste text and try Standard and Simple modes.
3. Run the Grammar Checker on your final draft.
4. Install the Chrome extension for in-browser help.
5. Upgrade to Premium for longer text and all modes.

## Tips and tricks

- **Paraphrase sentence by sentence** to keep meaning.
- **Try several modes** to compare tone.
- **Always reread** output for accuracy.
- **Cite your sources** with the Citation Generator.

Example use:

> Paste: "Our team is currently in the process of reviewing the proposal." Choose Concise mode to get a shorter version like "Our team is reviewing the proposal."
