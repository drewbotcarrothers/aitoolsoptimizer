## What Speechify is

Speechify started as a text-to-speech reader and has become a broader "Voice AI Assistant." It reads anything aloud—PDFs, web pages, emails, documents—in lifelike voices, so you can listen instead of reading. You can also **talk instead of type** with voice typing across your apps, summarize documents, turn any document or description into a podcast, and ask the assistant questions about what you're reading.

It suits students, busy professionals and anyone who prefers listening to reading.

## Key features

### Text to speech
1,000+ voices in 60+ languages, at up to 4.5x speed.

### Voice typing
Dictate polished text in Google Docs, Gmail, Slack, Outlook, Cursor and other apps on Mac and Windows.

### PDF and document tools
Upload a PDF to the web app to have it read aloud, summarized or turned into a podcast.

### Voice AI Assistant
Ask questions about anything you're reading, in the browser side panel or on mobile.

### Everywhere
Web app, Chrome and Edge extensions, iOS, Android, Mac and Windows.

## Best use cases

- **Students:** listen to readings and lecture notes.
- **Professionals:** catch up on reports during a commute.
- **Writers:** proofread drafts by ear.
- **Accessibility:** reading support for dyslexia and low vision.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at speechify.com.
2. Install the Chrome or Edge extension.
3. Open an article and click play.
4. Upload a PDF to the web app.
5. Upgrade to Premium for natural voices and higher speeds.

## Tips and tricks

- **Increase speed gradually** to build listening stamina.
- **Use summaries** before listening to long PDFs.
- **Try voice typing** for first drafts.
- **Pick a voice** you enjoy for long sessions.

Example use:

> Upload a 40-page research PDF, ask for a five-bullet summary, then listen to the methods section at 2x on your commute.
