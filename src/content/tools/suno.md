## What Suno is

Suno makes AI music creation accessible to everyone: whether you have a melody in your head, lyrics you've written or just a feeling, you describe it and Suno generates a complete song—instruments, arrangement and vocals. Suno says its newest model is its most capable yet, "with fuller vocals, tighter arrangements and more of the detail you asked for."

Suno has also grown into a production tool, with **Suno Studio**, voice recording, stems, remixing and custom models.

## Key features

### Song generation
Create full tracks from a prompt or your own lyrics.

### Suno Studio
A browser-based DAW to arrange, edit and mix every part of a song.

### Your voice
Record a few seconds of your voice and hear it sing anything you write (Pro and Premier).

### Stems
Split songs into clean, time-aligned stems for your own DAW.

### Remix and restyle
Change a song's style, rewrite lyrics or rebuild it around your recording.

### Custom models
Train a model on your recordings so every song starts from your sound.

## Best use cases

- **YouTubers and podcasters:** original intro music and beds.
- **Songwriters:** turn lyrics into a demo.
- **Producers:** generate stems to rework in a DAW.
- **Marketers:** quick jingles for campaigns (with commercial rights).

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at suno.com.
2. Describe a style, mood and topic, or paste lyrics.
3. Generate a couple of versions and pick a favorite.
4. Open it in Studio to edit sections.
5. Upgrade to Pro for downloads and commercial use.

## Tips and tricks

- **Name genre, tempo and instruments** in the prompt.
- **Write your own lyrics** for more control over the song.
- **Iterate on sections** rather than regenerating whole songs.
- **Export stems** for final mixing.

Example prompt:

> Upbeat indie-pop song about a summer road trip along the coast, female vocals, jangly guitars, 120 BPM, catchy singalong chorus.
