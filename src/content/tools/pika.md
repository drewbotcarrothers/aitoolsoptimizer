## What Pika is

Pika began as one of the first popular text-to-video tools. In 2026 it relaunched as "an AI creative platform made for and by Creatives," bringing video, image and sound creation into one place. Rather than a blank prompt box and a list of model names, the new Pika offers **25+ creative apps**—some build a complete project, others solve a focused task—and automatically picks the best model underneath.

Pika's pricing philosophy is also a selling point: every user gets every model and app, and plans differ only by credits and how many generations can run at once. "No bait and switch. No fake unlimited," as Pika puts it.

## Key features

### Video Studio
Attach up to 50 characters, products, settings and styles and @mention them in prompts to make a clip or a multi-shot video up to 30 seconds, with sound.

### Relight Media
Rebuild light and shadow in a photo or video while preserving subject, framing and motion.

### Product Shot
Turn a phone photo of a product into professionally lit campaign imagery.

### Camera Director
Reshoot an existing scene from new angles, from extreme wide to close-up.

### More apps
Ads, short films, post-production changes, concept images, motion, color, characters and soundtracks.

### Model access
Seedance, GPT Image, MiniMax, Pika's own models and more—chosen automatically or manually.

## Best use cases

- **E-commerce:** product shots from simple photos.
- **Marketers:** short ads with sound.
- **Filmmakers:** concept scenes and camera explorations.
- **Social creators:** quick, stylized clips.

## How to get started

1. Sign up at pika.art.
2. Pick an app that matches your task.
3. Upload references (products, characters) or write a prompt.
4. Generate, then refine with relight or camera apps.
5. Upgrade to Creator for commercial use.

## Tips and tricks

- **Use references** for consistent characters and products.
- **Let apps choose the model** unless you need a specific look.
- **Generate in parallel** to compare options.
- **Plan credits** for longer videos.

Example prompt:

> @sneaker on a rain-soaked city rooftop at night, neon reflections, slow orbit camera move, 8 seconds, with ambient city sound.
