## What Khanmigo is

Khanmigo is "an AI-powered personal tutor and teaching assistant from trusted education nonprofit Khan Academy." For learners, it acts like a patient tutor: when a student types a homework question, Khanmigo asks guiding questions so they discover the answer themselves. For teachers, it's a free assistant that cuts planning time.

Because it's tied to Khan Academy's library, Khanmigo can tutor across math, science, writing, coding, SAT prep and more, with privacy and safety controls designed for schools and families.

## Key features

### Socratic tutoring
Personalized help that builds understanding rather than giving away answers.

### Writing Coach
Guided prompts and immediate feedback for writing and debate practice.

### Teacher tools
Lesson plans, rubrics, exit tickets, learning objectives and student work summaries.

### Family controls
Add up to 10 children, view their chat history and get moderation alerts.

### District tools
Classroom access, data dashboards, SSO and automatic rostering.

## Best use cases

- **Homework help:** a student gets guided through an algebra problem step by step.
- **Lesson planning:** a teacher generates a differentiated lesson and exit ticket.
- **Homeschooling:** parents use it as a tutor and monitor progress.
- **Writing:** students practice essays with feedback.

## How to get started

1. Teachers: sign up free at khanmigo.ai.
2. Families: create a parent account and choose monthly or annual.
3. Add your children and review settings.
4. Have students start with a subject they're working on.
5. Check history and alerts regularly.

## Tips and tricks

- **Encourage students to explain their thinking** to Khanmigo.
- **Teachers: paste your unit objectives** for better lesson plans.
- **Pick annual billing** to save a little.
- **Review chat history** together with younger kids.
