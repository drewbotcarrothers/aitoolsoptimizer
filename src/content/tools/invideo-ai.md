## What invideo AI is

invideo AI is a video creation platform that turns a prompt into a finished video draft and gives you access to leading image and video models in one place. Recent plans highlight models like **Nano Banana Pro** for images and **Seedance 2** for video, plus "frontier agents" that handle more of the creative work.

It's built for creators, marketers and small teams who want to publish videos quickly without traditional editing skills.

## Key features

### Prompt to video
Describe your video and let invideo generate a complete draft you can refine.

### Image and video models
Use popular frontier models under one credit balance.

### Agents
Plus and higher include access to frontier agents (Lite, Pro and Ultra tiers).

### Seats and collaboration
Per-seat plans, with free guest seats on Plus.

## Best use cases

- **YouTubers:** produce explainer or listicle videos from a topic.
- **Marketers:** generate product promos and social ads.
- **Small businesses:** create videos without hiring an editor.
- **Designers:** generate images for thumbnails and posts.

## How to get started

1. Sign up at invideo.io.
2. Describe your video: topic, length, audience and platform.
3. Review the generated draft and edit with prompts.
4. Export and check credit usage.
5. Choose annual billing if you'll use it regularly.

## Tips and tricks

- **Be specific about length and format** (e.g., 60-second vertical).
- **Draft with cheaper models**, then use premium models for key shots.
- **Edit the script before generating** visuals.
- **Check rights and licensing** for commercial use.

Example prompt:

> Create a 60-second vertical video explaining three ways to save on groceries, upbeat tone, female voiceover, captions on screen, ending with a call to follow for more tips.
