## What Microsoft Copilot is

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant. On its own, at copilot.microsoft.com and in the Copilot apps, it's a chatbot that helps you research, create images and get answers—free with a Microsoft account. With a Microsoft 365 subscription, Copilot also shows up inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, where it can draft, summarize and analyze your own documents and email.

For organizations, **Microsoft 365 Copilot** adds work-grounded AI with enterprise security, adoption analytics and model choice. Microsoft is also rolling out **Copilot Notebooks** to Copilot Chat users.

## Key features

### Copilot chat
Ask questions, brainstorm and research. Paid consumer plans raise usage limits, and Premium adds advanced Copilot chat with extensive usage.

### Copilot in Office apps
Draft and rewrite in Word, analyze data and build formulas in Excel, turn outlines into PowerPoint decks, and summarize threads in Outlook and meetings in Teams.

### Visual assistance
Get help from what's on your screen or camera, with higher usage on Premium.

### Image creation
Generate images in Copilot, with higher usage than free on paid plans.

### Business controls
Business plans add identity and access management, analytics to measure adoption and impact, and LLM model choice.

## Pricing breakdown

From Microsoft's US pricing pages, checked October 16, 2026.

**Individuals and families**

| Plan | Price | Copilot highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Copilot (free) | $0 | Chat, images, research with usage limits |
| Microsoft 365 Personal | $9.99/mo | Copilot with higher usage than free, Office apps |
| Microsoft 365 Family | $12.99/mo | 1–6 people; AI for owner only |
| Microsoft 365 Premium | $19.99/mo | Advanced Copilot chat, extensive usage |
| Microsoft 365 Pro | $99.99/mo (monthly only) | Highest usage, agentic AI, 5 TB storage (not available in all markets) |

Annual billing on consumer plans saves up to 17%.

**Business (per user/month, paid yearly)**

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 Copilot Business | From $18 (promotional; originally $21) |
| Microsoft 365 Business Standard with Copilot | From $23.50 |
| Microsoft 365 Business Premium with Copilot | $32 |

Monthly billing options cost more; free trials are available on some plans.

## Best use cases

- **Office workers:** draft reports in Word and summarize long email threads.
- **Analysts:** explain and chart Excel data.
- **Managers:** get Teams meeting recaps and action items.
- **Households:** plan, write and create images with the free app.

## How to get started

1. Go to copilot.microsoft.com and sign in with a Microsoft account.
2. Try chat and image creation for free.
3. If you have Microsoft 365, open Word or Excel and click Copilot.
4. Upgrade to Premium if you hit usage limits.
5. Businesses can start a trial of Copilot Business.

## Tips and tricks

- **Reference files** in Microsoft 365 ("summarize /Q3 report").
- **Ask for structure**: tables, bullet summaries, slide outlines.
- **Iterate** on drafts instead of starting over.
- **Check numbers** in Excel analysis before sharing.

Example prompt:

> Using the attached sales spreadsheet, find the three regions with the biggest quarter-over-quarter decline, chart them, and draft a five-bullet summary for my manager.
