## What Luma AI is

Luma AI is a creative AI company that builds its own models and a platform for creative teams. "Speed like this doesn't come from a wrapper," Luma says. "We build the models underneath Luma." Its mission statement goes further, aiming to build general intelligence that can generate, understand and operate in the physical world.

For creators, the headline models are **Ray 3.2**, which lets you "direct video the way you would direct a shoot," and **Uni-1**, which "understands your brand as deeply as it generates for it," carrying style and character references. **Luma Agents** research, generate and refine across video, image, audio and text.

## Key features

### Ray 3.2 video
Frame-level direction, continuity across cuts and cinematic finishing.

### Uni-1
Brand intelligence at the model level, with style and character references.

### Luma Agents
Explore many creative directions quickly across media types.

### Third-party models
Plans include Luma and third-party image and video models.

### Collaboration
Edit access for guest collaborators; Team plans add projects, sharing and SSO.

## Best use cases

- **Agencies:** generate many campaign directions before a client meeting.
- **Brands:** keep characters and style consistent across assets.
- **Filmmakers:** previsualize scenes with directed camera work.

## How to get started

1. Create an account at lumalabs.ai.
2. Subscribe to Plus to get credits.
3. Upload brand references for Uni-1.
4. Brief an agent or generate with Ray 3.2.
5. Invite collaborators to review.

## Tips and tricks

- **Provide style and character references** for consistency.
- **Think like a director**: specify shots, angles and transitions.
- **Let agents explore broadly** before refining one direction.
- **Choose yearly billing** to save up to 20%.
