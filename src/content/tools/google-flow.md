## What Google Flow is

Google Flow is Google's AI creative studio for making cinematic scenes and stories. Built by Google Labs, it brings Google's generative video and image models into one workspace where you can generate clips from text or images, edit generated or uploaded videos, create images and get help from the **Google Flow Agent**.

Flow runs on credits. Everyone gets a daily free allowance, and Google AI subscribers receive monthly credits on top, making Flow one of the perks of Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra.

## Key features

### Veo 3.1 video
Generate 4, 6 or 8-second clips with Veo 3.1 Lite, Fast or Quality, trading speed and cost for quality.

### Gemini Omni Flash
Create 4-10 second videos at 360p or 720p and edit uploaded and generated videos of any length and resolution.

### Images
Generate images (with Nano Banana models) for concepts, frames and assets.

### Flow Agent
Get help planning and creating scenes; available even to free users during peak hours.

### Credit management
Track remaining credits, refresh dates and usage history (subscribers), and share AI credits with your family group.

## Best use cases

- **Ads:** create several short product clips to test on social media.
- **Filmmakers:** visualize a scene or storyboard before shooting.
- **Educators:** illustrate historical or scientific concepts with short clips.
- **Marketers:** generate B-roll for explainer videos.

## How to get started

1. Go to labs.google/flow and sign in with your Google account.
2. Check your credit balance under your profile picture.
3. Start with Veo 3.1 Lite to test ideas cheaply.
4. Refine prompts, then switch to Quality for final shots.
5. Subscribe to a Google AI plan for more credits and peak-hour access.

## Tips and tricks

- **Draft cheaply, finish in Quality**: test with Lite or Fast first.
- **Describe camera moves and lighting** explicitly.
- **Use your first generation wisely**; it starts your daily refresh clock.
- **Generate outside peak hours** if you're on the free tier.

Example prompt:

> Slow dolly-in on a steaming cup of coffee on a rainy cafe windowsill at dawn, warm interior light, raindrops on glass, shallow depth of field, soft jazz ambience
