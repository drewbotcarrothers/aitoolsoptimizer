## What tl;dv is

tl;dv ("too long; didn't view") is an AI meeting recorder and note taker. It joins your calls, records and transcribes them, and delivers AI-generated summaries right after each meeting. tl;dv describes itself as the place where teams "capture knowledge, find answers, and automate meeting workflows," sending meeting insights to the apps you already use.

Its strengths are post-call automation—logging notes and outcomes in your CRM, drafting follow-ups—and analysis across many meetings, such as tracking feature requests across customer calls.

## Key features

### Recording and transcription
Unlimited recordings and transcription in 30+ languages, even on Free.

### AI notes
Instant summaries with custom templates—sales frameworks like MEDDIC, Smart AI Topics or your own format, in many languages.

### CRM and follow-ups
Automatically log call notes and outcomes; AI drafts follow-up emails and next-step tasks.

### Ask AI
Ask questions about your meetings.

### Multi-meeting insights and reports
Analyze many calls at once and receive scheduled AI reports (Business).

### Sales coaching
AI playbook monitoring and objection handling (Business).

## Tips and tricks

- **Create note templates** for each meeting type.
- **Tell participants** they're being recorded.
- **Schedule weekly reports** on topics like objections.
- **Use Ask AI** to find quotes quickly.

> Across last month's discovery calls, what were the top three objections about pricing, and which accounts raised them?
