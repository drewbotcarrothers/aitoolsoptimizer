## What Jasper is

Jasper is an AI platform designed for marketers. It began as one of the first AI copywriting tools and has since grown into a system for producing on-brand marketing content at scale, with "purpose-built agents that execute end-to-end marketing workflows" and governance that keeps every asset aligned with your brand.

A newer focus is AI search: Jasper helps you "win the new front door of search" by measuring how your brand performs across major AI answer engines, identifying content gaps and generating content AI tools are more likely to cite.

## Key features

### Canvas
A workspace for creating and refining on-brand content quickly.

### Marketing agents
Agents for core workflows on Pro, and complex ones (GEO, translation, deep research) on Business.

### Brand IQ
Brand Voices analyze your tone and set rules; Knowledge assets and Audiences give the AI context about products and personas.

### AI visibility
Track brand presence, citation rates and sentiment across AI answer engines.

### Jasper Grid and custom agents
Systematic content execution at scale and a no-code builder for custom agents (Business).

## Best use cases

- **Campaign launches:** turn a brief into on-brand emails, ads and landing page copy.
- **SEO and GEO:** create content designed to rank and be cited by AI.
- **ABM:** personalize content for target accounts.
- **Localization:** translate campaigns with Business agents.

## How to get started

1. Start the 7-day Pro trial at jasper.ai.
2. Set up a Brand Voice from your existing content.
3. Add Knowledge assets about your products.
4. Define your key Audiences.
5. Run an agent on a real campaign brief and review the output.

## Tips and tricks

- **Invest in Brand Voice setup**; it drives quality across every asset.
- **Keep Knowledge current** when products or pricing change.
- **Review AI visibility monthly** to guide content priorities.
- **Edit for facts and claims** before publishing.

Example brief:

> Create a launch email, three LinkedIn posts and two Google ad headlines for our new expense app for freelancers. Audience: US freelancers. Tone: friendly, confident. Key benefit: track expenses in 10 seconds.
