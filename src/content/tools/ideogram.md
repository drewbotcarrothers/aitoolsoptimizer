## What Ideogram is

Ideogram is an AI image generator built with a particular strength: putting accurate, readable text into images. That makes it a favorite for posters, flyers, social graphics, YouTube thumbnails, logo concepts and ad mockups, where many image models still garble lettering.

Beyond generation, Ideogram includes editing tools (remove backgrounds and objects), consistent **Characters**, collections and sharing, JSON prompting for precise control, an iOS app and an API. Its documentation is unusually thorough, with guides on prompt structure, text in images and fixing common problems.

## Key features

### Text in images
Put the words you want in quotes and Ideogram renders them as typography in the design.

### Editing tools
Edit images, remove backgrounds and objects, and upscale (these use credits).

### Characters and collections
Keep characters consistent and organize work in collections.

### Batch generation
Generate many variations at once on Pro.

### Private generation
Plus and higher keep your generations private.

### API and pay as you go
Developers can integrate Ideogram; personal accounts can fund a wallet without a subscription.

## Best use cases

- **Event promotion:** generate a concert poster with the band name and date.
- **Small business:** draft logo concepts and storefront signage ideas.
- **Creators:** make thumbnails with bold, readable titles.
- **Marketers:** produce several ad variations with different headlines.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at ideogram.ai.
2. Write a prompt with the exact text in quotes.
3. Choose an aspect ratio and style.
4. Generate, then refine or edit the best result.
5. Upgrade to Plus if you need privacy and more credits.

## Tips and tricks

- **Keep on-image text short**; fewer words render more reliably.
- **Describe font style and placement** ("bold sans-serif title at top").
- **Use JSON prompting** when you need precise control.
- **Check the cost on the run button** before generating with partner models.

Example prompt:

> Retro travel poster of Lake Louise at sunrise, flat illustration style, the text "LAKE LOUISE" in bold cream letters at the top and "Banff National Park" smaller at the bottom
