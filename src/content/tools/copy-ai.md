## What Copy.ai is

Copy.ai started as one of the first AI copywriting apps and has since repositioned as a **GTM AI platform**: one place to "infuse AI across your go-to-market engine" rather than juggling many copilots and point tools. It has two layers. **Chat** is a shared AI workspace with access to models from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google. **Workflows** chain AI steps into automated processes, such as researching a list of accounts and drafting outreach, or turning a product brief into a set of SEO articles.

Copy.ai's site features customers such as Lenovo and Juniper Networks, and a quote crediting it with "5x more meetings."

## Key features

### Chat
Unlimited words and chat projects, and the ability to pick from several AI model families, shared across up to 5 seats on the Chat plan.

### Workflows
Build multi-step automations that run on lists or triggers. Common templates include account research, content briefs, blog drafts, translation and lead enrichment.

### Sales use cases
Research accounts and contacts to draft outreach, enrich and engage inbound leads quickly, and analyze sales transcripts to score deals and suggest next steps.

### Marketing use cases
Generate SEO content, thought leadership, case studies and social posts, plus localized versions in other languages.

## Best use cases

- **Content operations:** a B2B marketing team turns one webinar transcript into a blog post, LinkedIn posts and an email newsletter.
- **Outbound research:** SDRs run a workflow that researches 200 target accounts and drafts tailored first emails.
- **Speed-to-lead:** inbound demo requests are enriched and get a personalized reply within minutes.
- **Localization:** product pages are translated into several languages at native-speaker quality.

## How to get started

1. Choose the Chat plan at copy.ai and invite up to 4 teammates.
2. Create a project for each campaign or account list and add brand guidelines.
3. Test different models on the same prompt to see which writes best for you.
4. Identify one repeatable task, such as weekly social posts, as a workflow candidate.
5. Talk to Copy.ai's team about workflow plans if you need automation at scale.

## Tips and tricks

- **Store brand voice and product facts** in your project so every chat starts with context.
- **Compare models** on important content; different models excel at different tasks.
- **Keep a human editor** in the loop for published content.
- **Measure outcomes** (meetings booked, traffic) to justify workflow plans.

Example chat prompt:

> Using our brand voice notes and the attached case study, write a 150-word LinkedIn post for our CMO announcing the results. Lead with the customer's outcome, avoid buzzwords and end with a question.
