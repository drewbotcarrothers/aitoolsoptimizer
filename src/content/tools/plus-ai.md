## What Plus AI is

Plus AI is an AI presentation maker that lives inside the tools most companies already use: Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint. You describe the presentation or upload a document, and Plus generates a full deck of native, editable slides. You can then insert single slides in a specific format, rewrite text or remix a slide's layout—for example turning a paragraph into three columns.

Because the output is a real Slides or PowerPoint file, decks fit corporate templates, sharing and approval workflows without exporting or rebuilding.

## Key features

### Deck generation
Create AI presentations in Google Slides and PowerPoint from a prompt.

### Slide-level control
Insert single slides, rewrite and remix layouts.

### Document to deck
Upload PDF, docx or txt files and use 100K+ character prompts (Pro and up).

### AI images
Generate images on Pro; advanced image models on Team.

### Branding and presets
Team plans customize logos, colors and fonts, share templates and save custom AI instructions so Plus remembers company context.

### AI Agent
The Max plan targets power users and teams who use the AI Agent every day.

## Best use cases

- **Consultants:** turn a report into a client deck.
- **Sales teams:** produce branded proposals quickly.
- **Managers:** create monthly business reviews.
- **Trainers:** build course slides from outlines.

## How to get started

1. Sign up with Google or Microsoft at plusai.com.
2. Install the Slides add-on or PowerPoint add-in.
3. Describe your deck or upload a document.
4. Review the outline, then generate slides.
5. Remix slides and apply your template.

## Tips and tricks

- **Start with an outline** for better structure.
- **Remix instead of regenerate** to save credits.
- **Save presets** for recurring deck types.
- **Set brand templates** before rolling out to a team.

Example prompt:

> Build a 12-slide quarterly business review from the attached PDF: highlights, KPIs vs targets, wins, risks, customer feedback and next-quarter priorities.
