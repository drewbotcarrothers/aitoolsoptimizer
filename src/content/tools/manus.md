## What Manus is

Manus is a general-purpose AI agent. Rather than answering one prompt at a time, it takes a goal—"research the top competitors in meal-kit delivery and build me a comparison website"—then plans the steps, browses, analyzes and delivers finished work. Its motto is "less structure, more intelligence."

Manus can produce in-depth research reports, websites, slides, images, music and video, and run tasks on a schedule. **Wide Research** spreads big research jobs across many parallel agents.

## Key features

### Autonomous tasks
Describe the outcome and Manus plans and executes multi-step work.

### Research
In-depth research for everyday tasks, and Wide Research for large-scale jobs.

### Websites and slides
Professional websites (with analytics on higher tiers) and slide decks.

### Scheduling and concurrency
Up to 20 concurrent and 20 scheduled tasks on Pro.

### Integrations
Slack integration and team collaboration.

## Best use cases

- **Consultants:** compile market research into a report and deck.
- **Founders:** create a landing page from a short brief.
- **Marketers:** schedule a weekly competitor roundup.
- **Students:** gather and summarize sources on a topic (then verify them).

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at manus.im.
2. Start with a small, well-defined task.
3. Watch how it plans and how many credits it uses.
4. Refine instructions and try a bigger task.
5. Upgrade to Pro for monthly credits and scheduling.

## Tips and tricks

- **State the deliverable format** (report, website, slides).
- **Give constraints** like sources, length and deadline.
- **Check facts and links** in research outputs.
- **Use schedules** for recurring reports.

Example task:

> Research the five largest US meal-kit companies, compare pricing, menu size and delivery areas in a table, and build a one-page website summarizing the findings with sources.
