## What Magnific is

Magnific is the name Freepik now uses for its AI creative suite. It started as Freepik, a stock-content site, and grew into a platform that bundles dozens of AI models for images, video, audio and 3D with more than 250 million Premium photos, vectors, PSDs and videos. The Magnific name comes from the popular AI upscaler Freepik acquired; the original magnific.ai site remains available as a legacy service.

## Key features

### Image creation and editing
An AI image generator with many models, an image editor, upscaler, extender, variations, cinematic shot, background remover, skin enhancer, relight and change-camera tools.

### Video
A video generator, video project editor, clip editor, video upscaler, video relight and **Speak** for talking video.

### Audio and 3D
Text to speech, sound effects and music generators, plus a 3D generator, 3D scenes and 3D 360.

### Design helpers
Designer, auto layers, mockup generator and icon generator.

### Workflows
**Spaces** for node-based creative workflows, agents, plugins, an MCP server, desktop and mobile apps, and API access with credit-based usage.

## Pricing breakdown

From the Freepik/Magnific pricing page, checked October 16, 2026. **The page showed us prices in Canadian dollars**, excluding VAT and local taxes; US visitors should see USD on the same page. We have not listed USD prices because we couldn't confirm them.

| Plan | Shown to us (billed annually) | Credits | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Premium | CA$20/mo | 240K/year | Spaces, 250M+ Premium assets, all image/video/audio models |
| Premium+ | CA$45/mo | 600K/year | Unlimited generations on selected models, commercial AI license + music rights, Magnific & Topaz upscalers |
| Pro | CA$109.50/mo | 1.5M or 4M/year | Everything in Premium+, more credits and Nano Banana Pro unlimited options |

All paid plans include no model training on your data, credits valid for a year (annual), top-ups, early access to AI features and metadata export. Generation costs vary by model, e.g. the page lists Kling 2.5 720p at 140 credits per 5 seconds.

## Best use cases

- **Marketers:** produce campaign images and short ads in multiple formats.
- **Designers:** upscale and relight assets, then add stock elements.
- **E-commerce:** create mockups and product scenes.
- **Content creators:** generate B-roll and music for videos.

## How to get started

1. Create an account at freepik.com.
2. Try the image generator and compare a few models.
3. Upscale a favorite result with the Magnific upscaler.
4. Check credit costs per model before longer video jobs.
5. Choose Premium+ if you'll generate a lot on the unlimited models.

## Tips and tricks

- **Pick models deliberately**: credit costs differ widely.
- **Use Spaces** to chain generation, upscaling and variations.
- **Prefer unlimited models** for exploration, premium models for finals.
- **Check license terms** before commercial use.
