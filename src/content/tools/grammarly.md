## What Grammarly is

Grammarly is an AI writing assistant that checks your writing as you type—in your browser, email, documents and chat apps—and suggests fixes for grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity and tone. Over the years it has added generative AI, so it can also rewrite whole sentences, adjust tone and draft text from prompts.

Grammarly is now part of **Superhuman**, a suite of AI tools that includes Superhuman Go, Grammarly, Coda and Superhuman Mail. For organizations, Grammarly is delivered through Superhuman Go with proactive AI that works in every app and tab.

## Key features

### Correctness
Catch grammar, spelling and punctuation mistakes in real time.

### Clarity and tone
Make sentences concise and adjust tone to sound confident, friendly or formal.

### Full-sentence rewrites (Pro)
Rewrite awkward sentences in one click.

### Generative AI
Draft, reply and brainstorm with AI prompts (2,000 on Pro, unlimited on Enterprise).

### Team tools
Brand tones, analytics, roles and permissions, and data loss prevention for organizations.

Regional pricing is available in euros, pounds and rupees at checkout. Grammarly doesn't offer lifetime or gift plans.

## Best use cases

- **Professionals:** send clear, polished emails and reports.
- **Students:** proofread essays before submission (follow your school's AI policy).
- **Non-native speakers:** write confidently in English.
- **Teams:** keep brand tone consistent across customer communications.

## How to get started

1. Create a free account at grammarly.com.
2. Install the browser extension and desktop app.
3. Set your writing goals (audience, formality).
4. Write normally and review the underlined suggestions.
5. Upgrade to Pro (annual) if you want rewrites and more AI prompts.

## Tips and tricks

- **Set goals per document** so tone suggestions fit the audience.
- **Don't accept everything**; keep your voice where it matters.
- **Turn off Grammarly** for sensitive documents if your policy requires it.
- **Choose annual billing** to save 60% vs. monthly.

Example AI prompt:

> Rewrite this paragraph to sound confident but friendly, under 80 words, for a client who missed a payment deadline.
