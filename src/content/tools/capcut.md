## What CapCut is

CapCut is a video editor from ByteDance, the company behind TikTok. It runs on Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and in the browser, and it is built for the way social video is made today: vertical formats, quick cuts, captions, trending effects and templates.

The basic editor is free. Paid plans unlock premium templates and features and add monthly AI credits for generative tools, including AI video generation with the models listed on CapCut's pricing page.

## Key features

### Editing and templates
A multi-track timeline with transitions, effects, filters, keyframes and speed controls, plus a large library of templates you can drop your clips into.

### AI helpers
Auto captions (transcribe audio to text), background removal, text-to-speech and AI voices, and tools that add voice to video. Many free tools work without credits; generative features use credits.

### AI video generation
Generate short clips from text or images. CapCut's pricing lists different models and per-second costs, with lower-resolution options that use fewer credits.

### Collaboration
Teams plans add shared spaces, a brand kit and Pro features for every seat. CapCut's help center lists seat options of 10, 15 or 30.

## Best use cases

- **Short-form content:** a fitness coach cuts a 60-second Reel from a longer workout video, adds auto captions and a trending template.
- **E-commerce promos:** a shop owner turns product photos into a quick promo with text animations.
- **Talking-head videos:** a consultant records on a phone, removes the background and adds captions.
- **AI b-roll:** a creator generates a few seconds of AI footage to fill a gap in a story.

## How to get started

1. Download CapCut for desktop or mobile, or open the web editor at capcut.com.
2. Start a new project or pick a template.
3. Import your clips and trim them on the timeline.
4. Add auto captions and review the transcript for errors.
5. Export in the aspect ratio of your platform (9:16 for TikTok and Reels).
6. Upgrade to Pro only if you need premium templates or AI generation.

## Tips and tricks

- **Check the price in your account** on the platform where you'll subscribe.
- **Proofread auto captions**, especially names and brand terms.
- **Use lower-resolution AI previews** to save credits, then generate the final clip.
- **Hook viewers in the first two seconds** with a bold caption.

Example AI video prompt:

> Slow cinematic push-in on a steaming cup of coffee on a wooden table by a rainy window, cozy warm lighting, 5 seconds, vertical 9:16
