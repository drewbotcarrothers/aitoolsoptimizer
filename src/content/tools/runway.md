## What Runway is

Runway is one of the pioneers of AI video. Its creative suite is an all-in-one cloud platform to generate and edit video, images and audio in one workspace, for individuals and teams. Alongside its own **Gen-4.5** and **Gen-4** models, Runway now offers leading third-party models—Seedance, Kling, Nano Banana Pro, Wan and more—plus an agentic creative collaborator that helps plan and produce work.

Runway describes its broader mission as building "Real-World Intelligence," with an API platform for developers and **GWM-1**, a General World Model for simulation.

## Key features

### Video generation
Text- and image-to-video with Gen-4.5, Gen-4 and Gen-4 Turbo, plus third-party models.

### Image generation
Gen-4 Images and other models such as Nano Banana Pro.

### Agentic creative collaborator
An AI collaborator to help develop and execute creative ideas (all paid plans).

### Voice and audio
Custom voice clones (1 on Pro, up to 3 on Max).

### Upscaling
Unlimited 4K video upscaling and Topaz AI Upscale on Pro and above.

### Workflow tools
Runway MCP, projects, Brand Kits (Max) and an API.

## Best use cases

- **Filmmakers:** previsualize scenes and generate B-roll.
- **Agencies:** produce ad variations quickly.
- **Musicians:** create music-video sequences.
- **Designers:** animate stills and upscale to 4K.

## How to get started

1. Sign up at runway.com and use the free credits.
2. Generate a few images to lock in a look.
3. Animate the best one with Gen-4 Turbo to save credits.
4. Render finals with Gen-4.5.
5. Upscale to 4K and assemble in your editor.

## Tips and tricks

- **Draft with cheaper models**, finalize with Gen-4.5.
- **Use reference images** for consistent characters.
- **Describe camera motion** explicitly.
- **Track credits per shot** before big projects.
