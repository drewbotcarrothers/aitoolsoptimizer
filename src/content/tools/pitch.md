## What Pitch is

Pitch is collaborative presentation software. Pitch says 4M+ teams use it to "create and deliver winning slides together." You can start from a blank canvas or template, or use AI to generate on-brand slides in seconds, then refine every detail in a polished editor with high-resolution video, animations and custom fonts.

Where Pitch stands out is what happens around the deck: live co-editing and slide assignments, shared templates and a brand library, personalized decks generated in bulk from customer data, deal rooms, and analytics that show who opened your deck and how long they spent on each slide.

## Key features

### Editor and AI
A modern slide editor with AI generation and AI actions powered by credits.

### Collaboration
Live co-editing, comments, slide assignments and teamspaces.

### Brand consistency
Custom templates, custom fonts and a brand library.

### Sharing and analytics
Advanced links track visits, slide views and time per slide; pitch rooms bundle decks and resources in one link.

### Sales personalization
Import customer data and generate personalized decks in bulk.

### Export
PowerPoint (PPTX) export without Pitch branding on paid plans.

## Pricing breakdown

From Pitch's pricing page and Help Center, checked October 16, 2026 (yearly billing; yearly saves 17%).

| Plan | Price | Members | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 forever | 1–5 | 100 one-time AI credits, unlimited presentations, 2 external guests |
| Plus | $10/seat/mo | 1 | 500 AI credits/seat/month, custom fonts, video uploads, PPTX export, 5 guests |
| Team | $15/seat/mo | 1–25 | 25 advanced links, 3 teamspaces, 2 pitch rooms, 25 guests |
| Business | $20/seat/mo | 1–200 | Broader sharing and roles |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | 30+ seats (required above 200) | Custom |

Pitch charges for all members of a paid workspace except commenters.

## Best use cases

- **Startups:** craft investor decks together.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at pitch.com.
2. Generate a first draft with AI or pick a template.
3. Invite teammates and assign slides.
4. Apply your brand fonts and colors.
5. Share a link and review engagement analytics.

## Tips and tricks

- **Build a custom template** before scaling deck creation.
- **Use AI for structure**, then edit for voice.
- **Track links** to time sales follow-ups.
- **Add commenters free** for reviews.

> Create a 10-slide seed pitch deck for a B2B scheduling app: problem, solution, market size, traction, business model, competition, team and ask, in our brand colors.
