## What Poe is

Poe is Quora's AI chat app. Instead of committing to one assistant, you can chat with many—Poe lists GPT-6-Astra, Claude-Opus-5.5, Claude-Fable-5.1, Gemini-4-Argon, Grok-4.7 "and thousands of others"—privately or in group chats with friends and coworkers. Anyone can also create bots with custom instructions and share them.

Usage is measured in **compute points**. Each bot shows how many points a message costs, so cheaper models stretch your allowance and premium models use more.

## Key features

### Multi-model chat
Switch models mid-conversation or compare answers on the same prompt.

### Group chats
Chat with AI and people together in one thread.

### Community and custom bots
Use thousands of bots for writing, images, video and more, or build your own.

### Points and transparency
Per-bot rate cards show costs before you send a message; check usage history in Settings.

### Shared subscriptions
Invite a limited number of group members to use your points.

### API
API access to Poe's bots, including major models.

## Best use cases

- **Writers:** compare drafts from different models.
- **Researchers:** cross-check answers between models.
- **Creators:** access image and video bots without separate accounts.
- **Teams:** brainstorm with AI in group chats.

## How to get started

1. Sign up at poe.com or download the app.
2. Try a few models on the same question.
3. Check each bot's point cost.
4. Subscribe if free daily points run out.
5. Create a custom bot for a repeated task.

## Tips and tricks

- **Use cheaper models** for drafts and premium ones for finals.
- **Compare bots** on important questions.
- **Watch point costs** for image and video bots.
- **Create bots** with saved instructions.

Example prompt to compare models:

> Explain the difference between a Roth IRA and a traditional IRA for a 30-year-old earning $70,000, in five bullet points.
