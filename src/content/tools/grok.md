## What Grok is

Grok is the AI assistant built by xAI. You can use it at grok.com, in its mobile apps and directly inside X, where it can draw on real-time conversation and trends. Grok answers questions, writes and edits, helps with research and code, and creates images and video through **Grok Imagine**.

Paid Grok access is now sold through **The X Subscription**, which "brings together Grok, Cursor and X." Paid individual plans include X Premium benefits when you connect your X account, and one shared usage pool covers Grok models across Grok and Cursor.

## Key features

### Chat and research
General-purpose assistant for questions, writing, analysis and planning.

### Grok Imagine
Generate images and video from prompts.

### X integration
Use Grok inside X for context on posts and trends.

### Shared usage with Cursor
Included usage covers Grok models in Cursor; third-party models in Cursor use additional credits.

### Grok Bot
Included on Premium, Super and Ultra, drawing from the same weekly usage pool.

## Pricing breakdown

From xAI's X Subscription docs, checked October 16, 2026 (USD, excluding tax).

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Included usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Limited |
| Plus | $8 | $80 ($7/mo) | Base |
| Premium | $30 | $300 ($25/mo) | 4x |
| Super | $100 | $1,000 ($84/mo) | 15x |
| Ultra | $200 | $2,000 ($167/mo) | 50x |

Team seats: Premium $30, Super $100 and Ultra $200 per seat per month. Top-ups buy extra usage credits beyond what's included, with spending caps and alerts available.

## Best use cases

- **News and trends:** ask Grok to explain what's happening in a conversation on X.
- **Writing:** draft posts, emails and summaries.
- **Creative work:** generate images and short videos with Grok Imagine.
- **Developers:** use one plan for Grok and Cursor coding.

## How to get started

1. Go to grok.com or open Grok in X and sign in.
2. Try the free tier with a few everyday questions.
3. Experiment with Grok Imagine for images.
4. Choose a plan at x.ai/pricing if you need more usage.
5. Connect your X account to get X Premium benefits.

## Tips and tricks

- **Pick annual billing** to save roughly two months.
- **Watch the shared usage pool** if you also code heavily in Cursor.
- **Ask for sources** and verify facts on important topics.
- **Set spending caps** before enabling top-ups.

> Summarize the main arguments for and against four-day work weeks in five bullets each, then suggest three questions a manager should ask before piloting one.
