## What AdCreative.ai is

AdCreative.ai is an AI platform built for one job: producing ad creatives at scale. You give it your brand assets (logo, colors, fonts, product images) and it generates banners, headlines, product photography and short videos sized for each ad platform. The company says more than 4.2 million users have used it and that its models learned from patterns in "$35B+ in ad spend data."

Where general design tools focus on making one great image, AdCreative.ai focuses on **variation and testing**: generating dozens of versions, scoring them and helping you keep only the ones that work. It groups tools into Generate, Analyse, Predict and Automate.

## Key features

### Generate
- **Ad Creatives and Instant Ads:** conversion-focused banners in every format and size, from templates or your own custom templates.
- **Ad Copy Generation:** headlines and body text to pair with each creative.
- **Product and Fashion Photoshoots:** turn plain product photos into styled e-commerce scenes, or place clothing on AI-generated models.
- **Video tools:** AI video generation, product and fashion videoshoots, storytelling videos and UGC-style videos made from product photos.
- **AI Image Generation:** stock-style images the company describes as safe for commercial use.

### Analyse and predict
- **Creative Scoring** predicts how a creative may perform before launch.
- **Creative Insights** identify your top performers from connected ad accounts.
- **Competitor Insights** surfaces competitors' best-performing ads.
- **Compliance Checker** flags brand, platform and policy issues.
- **Buyer Personas** builds AI audience profiles for targeting.

## Pricing breakdown

AdCreative.ai prices plans by billing period. Figures below are from the plan cards on AdCreative.ai's own pages, checked October 16, 2026.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Quarterly billing | Yearly billing | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $39/mo | $29/mo | $20/mo | 20 credits a month, 1 brand, 1 user |
| Professional | $249/mo | $185/mo | $125/mo | 300 credits a month, up to 10 users |

Every listed plan includes all AI assets, unlimited generations, ad platform integrations and Creative Insight AI. Credits renew monthly and are consumed only when you download a creative. Larger credit bundles are available within each plan at higher prices. The 7-day trial includes 10 credits.

## Best use cases

- **Paid social testing:** an apparel brand generates 30 square and story-format variations for a new collection, scores them, and launches the top five on Meta.
- **Product photography on a budget:** a skincare shop turns white-background bottle shots into lifestyle scenes for ads and product pages.
- **Agency production:** an agency manages several client brands, each with saved colors and fonts, and exports full size sets for Google Display.
- **Video ads without a shoot:** a DTC gadget brand turns three product photos into UGC-style clips for TikTok and Reels.

## How to get started

1. Start the 7-day free trial at adcreative.ai (sign in with Google).
2. Add a brand: upload your logo, set brand colors and fonts.
3. Choose a tool, such as Ad Creatives, and upload a product image.
4. Write a short headline and call to action, or let the copy tool suggest options.
5. Generate a batch, review the scores, and download only the winners (each download uses a credit).
6. Connect your ad accounts to use Creative Insights on real performance data.
7. Cancel before day 7 if it doesn't fit; otherwise pick monthly, quarterly or yearly billing.

## Tips and tricks

- **Generate freely, download selectively.** Since previews are unlimited, shortlist creatives before spending credits.
- **Test against a control.** Run your best existing ad alongside AI variants so you can judge lift yourself.
- **Use high-resolution product photos** with clean backgrounds for better photoshoots.
- **Build custom templates** for recurring campaigns so output stays on-brand.
- **Check the Compliance Checker** before launching in regulated categories.

Example copy brief for the ad copy tool:

> Product: insulated steel water bottle, keeps drinks cold 24 hours. Audience: hikers aged 25-40. Tone: energetic, no exaggeration. Give 5 headlines under 30 characters and 3 descriptions under 90 characters with a clear call to action.
