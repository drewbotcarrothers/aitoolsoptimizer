## What Udio is

Udio is an AI music generator. You describe a song—or start from a musical reference—and Udio creates a track with vocals and instruments, which you can then extend, remix and edit. Its toolset is built for iteration: create short 32-second clips or longer 130-second songs, add sections, change lyrics, blend styles and apply voices until the song sounds right.

## Key features

### Create songs
Generate 32-second and 130-second songs from prompts.

### Extend and remix
Grow a song section by section or remix it in new directions (all plans).

### Styles
Create with a musical reference on all plans; paid plans add premium Artist Styles and blending or reducing Styles.

### Voices
Use Udio's library of Voices and save and reuse your own (paid).

### Edit
Edit music and lyrics, upload your own audio and create or upload cover art (paid).

### Sharing controls
Adjust song access permissions on Pro.

## Pricing breakdown

From Udio's pricing page, checked October 16, 2026 (USD; also shown in GBP and EUR, tax varies by location).

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Credits | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 100/month (10/day) | 2 simultaneous generations, 3 long songs/day |
| Standard | $10 | $8/mo ($96/yr) | 2,400/month, no daily limit | Voices, uploads, Artist Styles, editing, 3 simultaneous |
| Pro | $30 | $24/mo ($288/yr) | 6,000/month | 5 simultaneous (10 songs), access permissions |

Add-on credits: 100 for $3.00 or 1,000 for $25.00. A free trial is available for Standard (some features not included in trials), and student pricing is offered.

## Best use cases

- **Songwriters:** test lyrics in different genres.
- **Producers:** generate ideas to rework.
- **Content creators:** custom background music.
- **Hobbyists:** explore styles and voices for fun.

## How to get started

1. Sign up free at udio.com.
2. Write a prompt with genre, mood and topic.
3. Generate a short clip and extend the best one.
4. Try a Style reference for a different sound.
5. Upgrade for Voices, uploads and editing.

## Tips and tricks

- **Build in sections** with Extend.
- **Use Styles** to keep a consistent sound.
- **Edit lyrics** instead of regenerating.
- **Buy credit packs** for occasional bursts.

Example prompt:

> Melancholic lo-fi hip-hop about a rainy night in Tokyo, soft female vocals, vinyl crackle, mellow Rhodes piano, 80 BPM.
